## 1. Overview & Revelation Context
Sūrat al-Baqarah is the longest sūrah of the Holy Qurʾān and the first major comprehensive revelation received following the Hijrah to Medina (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:376). It systematically establishes the theological, legal, and social foundation of the emerging Islamic state, addressing the three distinct communities of Medina: the faithful believers (**[[al-Muhājirūn]]** and **[[al-Anṣār]]**), the Jewish tribes of Yathrib (**[[Banū Qaynuqāʿ]]**, **[[Banū al-Naḍīr]]**, and **[[Banū Qurayẓah]]**), and the faction of hypocrisy (**[[ʿAbdullāh b. Ubayy b. Salūl]]** and his confederates) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:376).

---

## 2. The Turning of the Qiblah (Taḥwīl al-Qiblah)
The central historical event detailed in Sūrat al-Baqarah during the second year of the Hijrah is the redirection of the prayer direction from Jerusalem (**[[Bayt al-Maqdis]]**) to the Sacred House in Mecca (**[[al-Kaʿbah]]**) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:370–372).
- **The Longing of the Prophet ﷺ**: For approximately sixteen or seventeen months after arriving in Medina, the Muslims prayed facing Bayt al-Maqdis, while the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ continually raised his gaze toward the heavens yearning for the Qiblah of his forefather Ibrāhīm ﵊ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:370).
- **The Divine Command**:
  > ﴿قَدْ نَرَىٰ تَقَلُّبَ وَجْهِكَ فِي السَّمَاءِ ۖ فَلَنُوَلِّيَنَّكَ قِبْلَةً تَرْضَاهَا ۚ فَوَلِّ وَجْهَكَ شَطْرَ الْمَسْجِدِ الْحَرَامِ﴾
  > *"We have seen the turning of your face toward heaven; now We shall turn you toward a Qiblah that pleases you. So turn your face toward the Sacred Mosque..."* (Sūrat al-Baqarah 2:144; *Subul al-Hudā*, 3:370).
- **The Reassurance Concerning Past Prayers**: When the Qiblah was redirected, Muslims inquired about the validity of the prayers performed by their brethren who had passed away prior to the change. Allāh revealed:
  > ﴿وَمَا كَانَ اللَّهُ لِيُضِيعَ إِيمَانَكُمْ ۚ إِنَّ اللَّهَ بِالنَّاسِ لَرَءُوفٌ رَّحِيمٌ﴾
  > *"And Allāh would never let your faith [i.e., your past prayers toward Jerusalem] be lost. Truly Allāh is full of kindness and mercy toward mankind."* (Sūrat al-Baqarah 2:143; *Subul al-Hudā*, 3:372).

## 3. Polemics with the Rabbinate of Medina
Al-Ṣāliḥī dedicates extensive chapters (*Jimāʿ Abwāb...*, vol. 3, pp. 376–420) to the theological debates and inquiries posed by the rabbis of Medina, which occasioned the revelation of substantial sections of the sūrah:
- **Hostility toward Jibrīl ﵊**: When Jewish scholars questioned the Prophet ﷺ regarding the identity of the angelic emissary who brought revelation to him, he ﷺ named Jibrīl ﵊. They claimed: *"He is our enemy among the angels who brings punishment and war!"* In response, Allāh revealed:
  > ﴿مَن كَانَ عَدُوًّا لِّجِبْرِيلَ فَإِنَّهُ نَزَّلَهُ عَلَىٰ قَلْبِكَ بِإِذْنِ اللَّهِ مُصَدِّقًا لِّمَا بَيْنَ يَدَيْهِ وَهُدًى وَبُشْرَىٰ لِلْمُؤْمِنِينَ﴾
  > *"Say: Whoever is an enemy to Gabriel, it is he who has brought down this revelation to your heart by Allāh's permission, confirming what went before it..."* (Sūrat al-Baqarah 2:97; *Subul al-Hudā*, 3:380, 402).
