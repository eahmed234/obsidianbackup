# ʿAbdullāh b. Ubayy b. Salūl (عبد الله بن أبي بن سلول) ☠
*Abū al-Ḥubāb al-Khazrajī al-ʿAwfī — Raʾs al-Nifāq (The Chief of the Hypocrites in Medina)*

## 1. Lineage & Lost Kingship
He is ʿAbdullāh b. Ubayy b. Mālik b. al-Ḥārith b. ʿUbayd al-Khazrajī al-ʿAwfī (kunya: Abū al-Ḥubāb; Salūl was his mother from the Khuzāʿah tribe) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:360). In pre-Islamic Yathrib, he was the paramount aristocrat of the Khazraj. Following the devastating civil war of Buʿāth, both the Aws and Khazraj had agreed to crown him sovereign king of Yathrib, stringing beads and precious stones to fashion his royal diadem (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:360).

When the Prophet's arrival upon **[[al-Hijrah ilā al-Madīnah]]** unified the warring tribes under Islam, his kingship dissolved overnight. Bitterly jealous and resentful, he outwardly feigned Islam following the Muslim victory at **[[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]]** to preserve his tribal prestige, while organizing covert subversion as *Raʾs al-Nifāq* ("Head of the Hypocrites") (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:171).

His son, **[[ʿAbdullāh b. ʿAbdillāh b. Ubayy]]** ﵁, was by contrast a devoted, heroic veteran of Badr and Uḥud (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:93).

## 2. Key Treacheries in the Sīrah
- **Intervention for Banū Qaynuqāʿ (2 AH)**: When the Jewish artisan clan of **[[Banū Qaynuqāʿ]]** surrendered after breaching their treaty, Ibn Ubayy aggressively grasped the Prophet's armor at his collar, demanding pardon for his seven hundred armed allies until the Prophet ﷺ ordered them expelled rather than executed (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:171).
- **The Defection at Uḥud (3 AH)**: During the march to **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]**, Ibn Ubayy orchestrated a catastrophic mutiny at al-Shawt, pulling out with one-third of the army—three hundred hypocrites—protesting: *“He obeyed young men and disobeyed me! Why should we kill ourselves and our children?!”* attempting to induce panic in the Muslim ranks (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:186).
- **The Treason of al-Muraysīʿ & Sūrat al-Munāfiqūn (5 AH)**: Following the expedition of Banū al-Muṣṭaliq, when a quarrel erupted at a well, Ibn Ubayy declared to the Anṣār:
  > *"Have they dared do this?! They crowd us and outnumber us in our own land! By God, the parable of ourselves and these destitute Qurashī vagabonds is as the proverb says: 'Fatten your dog and it will devour you!' By Allāh, if we return to Medina, the more honorable will surely expel therefrom the more humble!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:60).
  A young companion, Zayd b. Arqam ﵁, reported these treasonous words to the Prophet ﷺ. When Ibn Ubayy swore false oaths denying it, Allāh exposed his deceit by revealing **Sūrat al-Munāfiqūn** (63:1–8), directly quoting his vile words (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:60–62). His own son ʿAbdullāh drew his sword at the gates of Medina, blocking his father and declaring: *“By Allāh, you shall not enter Medina until the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ permits you, so that you know who is the honorable and who is the humble!”* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:62).
- **The Slander of ʿĀʾishah (*Ḥadīth al-Ifk*) (5 AH)**: He was the mastermind who fabricated and circulated the vicious slander against Umm al-Muʾminīn **[[ʿĀʾishah bint Abī Bakr]]** ﵂ when she was accidentally left behind on the road, seeking to destroy the moral integrity of the Prophet's household (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:65–68). Allāh condemned him in Sūrat al-Nūr: *«And he who took upon himself the greater portion thereof, for him is a tremendous punishment»* (24:11) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:70).
- **Collusion at Tabūk (9 AH)**: Encamped his hypocrite followers separately beneath Mount Dhubāb during **[[Ghazwat Tabūk]]** and refused to march, attempting to dissuade believers from participating during the intense summer heat (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:445).

## 3. Demise & The Revelation Forbidding Prayers
In Dhū al-Qaʿdah 9 AH, Ibn Ubayy fell fatally ill in Medina (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:488). His pious son ʿAbdullāh implored the Prophet ﷺ to give his shirt to shroud his father and to perform the funeral prayer over him, hoping it might lessen his punishment (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:488).

Out of boundless mercy and magnanimity, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ gifted his inner garment. When the Prophet ﷺ stepped forward to lead the funeral prayer, **[[ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb]]** ﵁ sprang forward, grasping the Prophet's robe and reminding him of Ibn Ubayy’s innumerable treasons. The Prophet ﷺ gently smiled and said:
> «أَخِّرْ عَنِّي يَا عُمَرُ! إِنِّي خُيِّرْتُ فَاخْتَرْتُ، قِيلَ لِي: ﴿اسْتَغْفِرْ لَهُمْ أَوْ لَا تَسْتَغْفِرْ لَهُمْ إِنْ تَسْتَغْفِرْ لَهُمْ سَبْعِينَ مَرَّةً فَلَنْ يَغْفِرَ اللَّهُ لَهُمْ﴾ [التوبة ٨٠]، وَلَوْ أَعْلَمُ أَنِّي لَوْ زِدْتُ عَلَى السَّبْعِينَ غُفِرَ لَهُ لَزِدْتُ!»
> *"Step back from me, O ʿUmar! For I was given a choice and I made my choice. It was said to me: 'Ask forgiveness for them or do not ask forgiveness for them; if you ask forgiveness for them seventy times, Allāh will never forgive them!' And had I known that by adding beyond seventy he would be forgiven, I would have added!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:489; *Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī*, no. 1269).

The Prophet ﷺ prayed over him and walked to his grave until he was buried. Immediately thereafter, divine revelation descended settling the matter decisively in **[[Sūrat al-Tawbah]]**:
> «وَلَا تُصَلِّ عَلَىٰ أَحَدٍ مِنْهُمْ مَاتَ أَبَدًا وَلَا تَقُمْ عَلَىٰ قَبْرِهِ ۖ إِنَّهُمْ كَفَرُوا بِاللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِ وَمَاتُوا وَهُمْ فَاسِقُونَ»
> *"And never pray over any of them who died, nor stand at his grave! Truly, they disbelieved in Allāh and His Messenger and died while they were defiantly disobedient!"* (Qurʾān 9:84; *Subul al-Hudā*, 5:490).
Never again did the Prophet ﷺ perform funeral prayers over a hypocrite.
