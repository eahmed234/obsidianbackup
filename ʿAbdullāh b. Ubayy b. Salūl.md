# ʿAbdullāh b. Ubayy b. Salūl (عبد الله بن أبي ابن سلول)
*Chieftain of the Khazraj — Raʾs al-Munāfiqīn (Chief of the Hypocrites)*

## 1. Motive for Enmity
Prior to the [[al-Hijrah]], the people of Yathrib had gathered jewels to crown him king. The arrival of the Prophet ﷺ eclipsed his ambition, breeding lifelong resentment.

## 2. Subversion in the Sīrah
- Defected with 300 men on the morning of **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]** (3 AH).
- Conspired with Banū al-Naḍīr and Banū Qurayẓah against the state.
- Fabricated and fanned the slander against ʿĀʾishah (*Ḥadīth al-Ifk*) after **[[Ghazwat al-Muraysīʿ]]**.
- Declared: *"If we return to Medina, the more honorable will expel the more humiliated!"* Prompting [[Sūrat al-Munāfiqūn]] (63:8).
- Upon his death, his righteous son **[[ʿAbdullāh b. ʿAbdillāh b. Ubayy]]** requested the Prophet's shirt as a shroud. The Prophet ﷺ prayed over him in sublime mercy, prompting the revelation prohibiting prayer over hypocrites (Qurʾān 9:84) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:465).
