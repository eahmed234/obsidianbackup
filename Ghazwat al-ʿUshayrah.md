# Ghazwat al-ʿUshayrah (غزوة العشيرة)
*The Interception at Yanbuʿ — The Precursor Expedition to Badr*

## 1. Strategic Context & Departure
In Jumādā al-Ūlā 2 AH (sixteen months after the Hijrah), intelligence reached Medina that a major mercantile caravan belonging to **[[Quraysh]]**, heavily laded with the pooled wealth of the Meccan aristocracy, had departed Makkah bound for Syria (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:17).

The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ mobilized between one hundred and fifty and two hundred Muhājirūn, leaving Abū Salamah b. ʿAbd al-Asad as deputy over Medina. The white army standard was entrusted to **[[Ḥamzah b. ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib]]** ﵁ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:17). The men alternated riding across thirty camels, marching along the Malal route until reaching **al-ʿUshayrah** in the valley of Yanbuʿ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:17).

## 2. Developments at al-ʿUshayrah
Upon arriving at al-ʿUshayrah, the Prophet ﷺ discovered that the Meccan caravan, commanded by **[[Abū Sufyān b. Ḥarb]]**, had traversed the coastal pass a few days prior (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:17). 

This identical caravan was the precise target that the Prophet ﷺ set out to intercept several months later on its return journey from Syria in Ramaḍān 2 AH, which directly precipitated **[[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]]** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:17).

While encamped at al-ʿUshayrah through the remainder of Jumādā al-Ūlā and the start of Jumādā al-Ākhirah, the Prophet ﷺ concluded a formal treaty of peace and mutual non-aggression with the local tribe of **Banū Mudlij** and their allies of Banū Ḍamrah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:17). It was also during this encampment that the Prophet ﷺ affectionately conferred the agnomen **Abū Turāb** upon **[[ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib]]** ﵁ when finding him resting on the ground with dust clinging to his back (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:17; 11:288).

## 3. Return & Significance
The Prophet ﷺ returned to Medina without military combat, having consolidated the alliances along the northern trade route and laid the operational groundwork for the watershed confrontation at Badr (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:17–18).
