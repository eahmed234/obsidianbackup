# Dustūr al-Madīnah (دستور المدينة / صحيفة المدينة)
*The Charter of Medina — The World's First Written Constitutional Framework*

## 1. Overview
Drafted by the Prophet [[Muḥammad b. ʻAbdullah|Muḥammad]] ﷺ, *Dustūr al-Madīnah* (The Charter of Medina) established a multi-faith, pluralistic political commonwealth (*Ummah wāḥidah*). It codified mutual defense obligations, internal security, judicial sovereignty, and freedom of religion.

## 2. Key Constitutional Articles
1. **Unity of the Ummah**: *"Indeed, they are a single community (*Ummah wāḥidah*) distinct from all other people."*
2. **Religious Freedom**: *"To the Jews their religion, and to the Muslims their religion."*
3. **Collective Defense**: All signatories are obligated to defend the oasis of Medina against external aggression, sharing financial expenses of war.
4. **Supreme Judicial Sovereignty**: *"Whenever any dispute or controversy arises among the people of this document... it shall be referred to Allāh and to Muḥammad, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ."* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:380).
