# Mālik b. Sinān (مالك بن سنان) ﵁
*Father of Abū Saʿīd al-Khudrī — The Companion Who Drank the Blessed Blood of the Prophet ﷺ and Martyr of Uḥud*

## 1. Lineage, Clan & Household
- **Ancestry**: Mālik b. Sinān b. ʿUbayd b. Thaʿlabah b. al-Abjar (whose name was Khudrah) b. ʿAwf b. al-Ḥārith b. al-Khazraj al-Anṣārī al-Khazrajī al-Khudrī (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:538, no. 7651).
- **Clan**: Banū Khudrah, a prominent clan of the Khazraj.
- **Progeny**: Father of the illustrious junior Companion and prolific hadith narrator **Saʿd b. Mālik** (universally renowned by his kunyah, **[[Abū Saʿīd al-Khudrī]]** ﵁) (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:538).

## 2. Ghazwat Uḥud & The Prophetic Wounding (3 AH)
- Mālik b. Sinān marched with the Muslim army to **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]** alongside his young son Saʿd (whom the Prophet ﷺ turned back due to his tender age of thirteen) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:186).
- When the enemy assault pierced the defensive lines and Ibn Qamīʾah hurled a missile that broke the lower helmet ring of the Prophet ﷺ, driving two iron links into the Prophet's ﷺ cheek, blood flowed profusely over the Prophet's face and beard (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:200).
- Seeing the bleeding face of the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ, Mālik b. Sinān stepped forward, placed his lips directly over the wound on the cheek of the Prophet ﷺ, and sucked the blood until the wound cleared.
- The Prophet ﷺ said to him: *«Spit it out!»* but Mālik replied: *«No, by Allāh, I shall never spit it out!»* and he swallowed it (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:200; *al-Iṣābah*, 5:538).
- The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ then said: *«Whoever wishes to look upon a man whose blood has mingled with my blood, let him look upon Mālik b. Sinān»* (and in another narration: *«Whoever touched my blood, the Fire shall not touch him»*) (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:538; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:200).

## 3. Martyrdom at Uḥud
- Returning immediately to the fray, Mālik fought fiercely in front of the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ against the polytheist onslaught until he was struck down and attained martyrdom on the slopes of Mount Uḥud (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:538).
- He was interred among the seventy noble martyrs of Uḥud (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:200).
