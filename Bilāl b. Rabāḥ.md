# Bilāl b. Rabāḥ (بلال بن رباح الحبشي) ﵁
*Abū ʿAbdillāh al-Ḥabashī — Muʾadhdhin Rasūlillāh (The Caller of the Prophet ﷺ) and Icon of Monotheistic Steadfastness*

## 1. Lineage & Unshakeable Endurance in Mecca
He is Bilāl b. Rabāḥ (kunya: Abū ʿAbdillāh), born in Mecca into enslavement to the Banū Jumaḥ clan, his mother being Ḥamāmah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:307; 10:204). He was among the first seven persons on earth to declare Islam openly in Mecca (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:307).

His master, the arch-tyrant **Umayyah b. Khalaf** ☠, subjected him to barbaric tortures to compel apostasy: dressing him in heavy iron coats of mail under the midday desert sun, dragging him through the rocky ravines by ropes held by street urchins, and pinning him flat upon the incandescent stones with a massive boulder upon his chest (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:307). Through suffocating agony, Bilāl repeated his immortal proclamation:
> «أَحَدٌ! أَحَدٌ!»
> *"One! One [Alone]!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:307).

Passing by, the Prophet ﷺ saw his agony and said: *“The One (Allāh) shall deliver you, O Bilāl!”* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:307). **[[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]]** ﵁ purchased his freedom for a high sum and emancipated him for the sake of Allāh; the companions would declare: *“Abū Bakr is our master, and he emancipated our master!”* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:308).

## 2. Key Sīrah Milestones
- **The First Muʾadhdhin in Islam (1 AH)**: Following **[[al-Hijrah ilā al-Madīnah]]** and the erection of **[[Bināʾ al-Masjid al-Nabawī]]**, when ʿAbdullāh b. Zayd and ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb experienced identical dreams regarding the call to prayer, the Prophet ﷺ commanded:
  > «قُمْ مَعَ بِلَالٍ فَأَلْقِ عَلَيْهِ مَا رَأَيْتَ، فَلْيُؤَذِّنْ بِهِ، فَإِنَّهُ أَنْدَى صَوْتًا مِنْكَ!»
  > *"Stand with Bilāl and convey to him what you saw, that he may call the Adhān with it, for he has a more melodious and resonant voice than you!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:328–330; *Sunan Abī Dāwūd*, no. 499).
  Bilāl became the official lifelong Muʾadhdhin of the Prophet ﷺ.
- **Retribution at Badr (2 AH)**: Fought at **[[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]]** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:92). On the battlefield, he caught sight of his former torturer Umayyah b. Khalaf attempting to surrender under the protection of ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. ʿAwf. Bilāl cried out: *“The head of disbelief, Umayyah b. Khalaf! May I not survive if he survives!”* rallying the Anṣār until Umayyah was slain, executing divine justice (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:68–70).
- **Adhān atop the Kaʿbah at Fatḥ Makkah (8 AH)**: On the day of **[[Fatḥ Makkah]]**, following the purification of the Kaʿbah from three hundred and sixty idols, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ commanded Bilāl to ascend the roof of the Kaʿbah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:240). Bilāl called the Adhān from atop the Ancient House, declaring monotheism across the valley of Mecca before the assembled chieftains who had once tortured him in chains.
- **Footsteps in Paradise**: The Prophet ﷺ asked him: *“O Bilāl, tell me of the most hopeful deed you perform in Islam, for last night I heard the rustling sound of your footsteps before me in Paradise!”* Bilāl replied: *“I never perform ablution at any hour of day or night except that I pray with it whatever prayer was decreed for me”* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 10:204; *Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī*, no. 1149).

## 3. Demise & Legacy
Following the death of the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ, Bilāl found it unbearable to call the Adhān without weeping upon the words *“Ashhadu anna Muḥammadan Rasūl Allāh”*. He requested Caliph Abū Bakr to permit him to depart for military service in Syria.

Years later, when Caliph **[[ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb]]** ﵁ visited al-Jābiyah in Syria and requested Bilāl to call the Adhān once more, Bilāl agreed; when his voice echoed with the sacred call, the entire gathering of Companions wept uncontrollably, having not wept like that since the day the Prophet ﷺ died (*Subul al-Hudā*, 10:204).

Bilāl passed away in Damascus during the Caliphate of ʿUmar in 20 AH at over sixty years of age, and was laid to rest at the Bāb al-Ṣaghīr cemetery (*Subul al-Hudā*, 10:204).
