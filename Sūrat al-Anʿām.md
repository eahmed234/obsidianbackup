# Sūrat al-Anʿām (سورة الأنعام)
*The Grand Makkan Manifestation of Cosmic Monotheism and Angelic Escort*

## 1. Overview and Dignity
**Sūrat al-Anʿām** (The Cattle) is the sixth chapter of the Qurʾān, occupying a uniquely exalted station among the foundational revelations of the Makkan era (*Subul al-Hudā*, 10:298).

Unlike many sūrahs that descended in scattered sections according to emergent circumstances, Sūrat al-Anʿām was revealed as a magnificent, unified whole in a single night in Makkah, establishing the decisive theological arguments against paganism, star-worship, superstition, and erroneous dietary prohibitions (*Subul al-Hudā*, 10:298).

## 2. Occasion & Circumstances of Revelation
In his treatment of the special distinctions granted to the Prophet ﷺ and his Scripture (*Khaṣāʾiṣ al-Qurʾān*), Imām al-Ṣāliḥī records:
> «وَرَوَى الطَّبَرَانِيُّ وَابْنُ الْمُنْذِرِ عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ رَضِيَ اللهُ عَنْهُمَا قَالَ: نَزَلَتْ سُورَةُ الْأَنْعَامِ بِمَكَّةَ لَيْلًا جُمْلَةً، وَحَوْلَهَا سَبْعُونَ أَلْفَ مَلَكٍ يَجْأَرُونَ بِالتَّسْبِيحِ!»
> *"Al-Ṭabarānī and Ibn al-Mundhir narrated from Ibn ʿAbbās ﵄ that he said: 'Sūrat al-Anʿām was revealed in Makkah at night in its entirety, surrounded by seventy thousand angels raising their voices in celestial glorification (tasbīḥ)!'"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 10:298).

Al-Ṣāliḥī corroborates this transmission through parallel routes recorded by:
- Al-Ṭabarānī from **[[Anas b. Mālik]]** ﵁
- Ibn al-Mundhir from Abū Juḥayfah ﵁
- ʿAbd b. Ḥumayd from Ibn al-Munkadir
- Al-Firyābī and Isḥāq b. Rāhwayh from Shahr b. Ḥawshab
- Ibn Mardawayh from **[[ʿAbdullāh b. Masʿūd]]** ﵁
- Al-Ṭabarānī and Ibn Mardawayh from Asmāʾ bint Yazīd ﵂
- Al-Bayhaqī and al-Khaṭīb al-Baghdādī from **[[ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib]]** ﵁ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 10:298).

Addressing the critique of Imām al-Nawawī who had questioned whether Sūrat al-Anʿām was sent down as a single whole, al-Ṣāliḥī notes that al-Ḥāfiẓ **[[Ibn Ḥajar al-ʿAsqalānī]]** refuted al-Nawawī's skepticism in his *Amālī*, affirming the robust multiplicity of supporting chains (*Subul al-Hudā*, 10:298).

## 3. Theological Themes & The Prophetic Argument
Sūrat al-Anʿām gathers the foundational pillars of Islamic creed:
- **Divine Cosmic Authority**: Establishing Allāh as the sole Creator of the heavens, the earth, light, and darkness.
- **The Argument of Ibrāhīm ﵊**: Recounting the prophet Ibrāhīm's rigorous intellectual debate against the astrological polytheism of his people, culminating in his timeless declaration: «إِنِّي وَجَّهْتُ وَجْهِيَ لِلَّذِي فَطَرَ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضَ حَنِيفًا» (*"Indeed, I have turned my face toward He who created the heavens and the earth, inclined toward truth"*).
- **The Comprehensive Enjoinments (*al-Waṣāyā al-ʿAshr*)**: The ten universal ethical commandments codifying the inviolability of human life and property, prohibiting polytheism, mistreatment of parents, infanticide, approaching indecencies, unlawful killing, consuming the orphan's property, and dishonest weights and measures.
