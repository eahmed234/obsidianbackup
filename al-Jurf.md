# al-Jurf (الجُرُف)
*The Northern Military Assembly Ground of Medina*

## 1. Topography & Distance
Al-Jurf is an open plain situated approximately **three miles** north of **[[al-Madīnah al-Munawwarah]]** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:95). Its name is vocalized with ḍammah on the jīm and rāʾ (*al-Juruf*), or with sukoon on the rāʾ (*al-Jurf*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:95).

## 2. Military Role in the Sīrah
Al-Jurf served as the primary assembly and staging ground for Muslim military expeditions marching north toward the Syrian and Najd frontiers:
- **[[Sariyyat Dūmat al-Jandal]] (Shaʿbān 6 AH)**: The seven-hundred-man expedition commanded by **[[ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. ʿAwf]]** ﵁ encamped at al-Jurf prior to receiving the final military investiture and turban from the Prophet ﷺ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:94–95).
- **Expedition of Usāmah b. Zayd (11 AH)**: The army assembled and pitched their camp at al-Jurf awaiting departure under **[[Usāmah b. Zayd]]** ﵁ during the final illness of the Prophet ﷺ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 12:44).
