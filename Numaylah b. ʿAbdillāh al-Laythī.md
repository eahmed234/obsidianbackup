# Numaylah b. ʿAbdillāh al-Laythī (نميلة بن عبد الله الليثي)
*Deputy of Medina during al-Ḥudaybiyah, Governor of Khaybar, and Enforcer of Justice at the Conquest of Makkah*

> [!NOTE] Primary Sourcing & Biographical Provenance
> Sourced directly from *al-Iṣābah*, 6:373–374 (entry 8831), cross-referenced with *Subul al-Hudā*, 5:33, 224–225.

---

## 1. Lineage & Tribal Roots
Numaylah belonged to the prestigious northern tribe of **Kinānah**, specifically from the branch of **Banū Layth b. Bakr** (*al-Iṣābah*, 6:373).

- **Full Paternal Genealogy**: Numaylah b. ʿAbdillāh b. Fuqaym b. Ḥazn b. Sayyār b. ʿAbdillāh b. Kalb b. ʿAwf b. Kaʿb b. ʿĀmir b. Layth al-Laythī (*al-Iṣābah*, 6:373).
- **Clan Attribution**: He is occasionally styled *al-Kalbī*, which refers strictly to his ancestor Kalb b. ʿAwf of Banū Layth, not to the distinct southern tribe of Banū Kalb b. Wabrah (*al-Iṣābah*, 6:373).

---

## 2. Deputy Governor over Medina (6 AH)
In Dhū al-Qaʿdah 6 AH, when the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ departed Medina with fourteen hundred Companions to embark upon the expedition of al-Ḥudaybiyah (**[[Ṣulḥ al-Ḥudaybiyah]]**), he designated Numaylah b. ʿAbdillāh al-Laythī (or, according to parallel reports, **[[ʿAbdullāh b. Umm Maktūm]]** ﵁) as the official deputy governor (*istakhlafahu*) to maintain administrative order, lead congregational prayers, and secure the capital city (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:33).

Ibn Hishām records in his additions to the *Sīrah* that the Prophet ﷺ subsequently appointed Numaylah to administrative duties over **Khaybar** following its conquest in 7 AH (*al-Iṣābah*, 6:373).

---

## 3. The Execution of Miqyas b. Ṣubābah at Fatḥ Makkah (8 AH)
On the Day of the Supreme Conquest (**[[Fatḥ Makkah]]**, 20 Ramaḍān 8 AH), the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ proclaimed universal clemency for the citizens of Makkah, with the exception of a handful of individuals who had committed treacherous murder and apostasy (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:223–225).

### The Crime of Miqyas
Foremost among these was Miqyas b. Ṣubābah al-Laythī. Miqyas had previously feigned conversion in Medina, received the full blood money (*diyah*) from the Prophet ﷺ for his brother Hishām b. Ṣubābah (who had been killed mistakenly by an Anṣārī soldier during Ghazwat Dhī Qarad), and then treacherous murdered the Anṣārī killer while he slept, renounced Islam, and fled to Makkah composing boasting verses (*al-Iṣābah*, 6:373; *Subul al-Hudā*, 5:224).

### Execution Between al-Ṣafā and al-Marwah
When the Prophet ﷺ commanded that Miqyas be brought to justice, Numaylah b. ʿAbdillāh—being a fellow member of Banū Layth—stepped forward to vindicate the rule of law (*al-Iṣābah*, 6:373). Numaylah located Miqyas between Mount al-Ṣafā and Mount al-Marwah and executed him on the spot (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:225).

In response to his execution, Miqyas's sister composed verses lamenting his fate:
> «لَعَمْرِي لَقَدْ أَخْزَى نُمَيْلَةُ قَوْمَهُ ... فَفَجَّعَ أَضْيَافَ الشِّتَاءِ بِمِقْيَسِ»
> *"By my life, Numaylah has brought distress upon his folk, depriving the winter guests of Miqyas!"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 6:374).

Numaylah's action cleansed his tribe of the stain of treason and upheld divine justice in the Sacred Precinct.
