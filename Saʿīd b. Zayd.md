# Saʿīd b. Zayd (سعيد بن زيد العدوي) ﵁
*Abū al-Aʿwar al-ʿAdawī al-Qurashī — Son of the Pure Monotheist (Ḥanīf), Early Pioneer of Islam*

## 1. Lineage & Pure Monotheistic Heritage
He is Saʿīd b. Zayd b. ʿAmr b. Nufayl b. ʿAbd al-ʿUzzā al-ʿAdawī al-Qurashī (kunya: Abū al-Aʿwar) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:237, 317). His father, **Zayd b. ʿAmr b. Nufayl**, was one of the celebrated pre-Islamic monotheists (*al-Ḥunafāʾ*) who openly denounced the idolatry of Quraysh, refused to eat meats sacrificed upon pagan altars, and traveled through Syria seeking the pristine religion of Abraham ﵊ before dying prior to the Prophetic mission. The Prophet ﷺ said of his father:
> «يُبْعَثُ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ أُمَّةً وَحْدَهُ!»
> *"He will be resurrected on the Day of Judgment as a solitary nation by himself!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:317).

Saʿīd married Fāṭimah bint al-Khaṭṭāb (the sister of **[[ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb]]** ﵁). They embraced Islam together at the very inception of the Daʿwah before the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ entered **[[Dār al-Arqam]]**, making them among the foremost early pioneers (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:365; 11:317). It was in their home that ʿUmar heard the recitation of Sūrat Ṭā-Hā, leading to his monumental conversion (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:365).

## 2. Key Sīrah Milestones
- **The Intelligence Mission at Badr (2 AH)**: Prior to **[[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]]**, the Prophet ﷺ dispatched Saʿīd alongside **[[Ṭalḥah b. ʿUbaydillāh]]** ﵁ to the Syrian trade road at al-Ḥawrāʾ to intercept intelligence regarding Abū Sufyān's caravan (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:24; 11:317). When they returned to Medina, the battle had concluded; the Prophet ﷺ awarded both Saʿīd and Ṭalḥah their full portion of spoils and equal status among the Ahl Badr (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:24; 11:317).
- **Steadfastness Across the Campaigns**: Witnessed **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]**, standing firm beside the Prophet ﷺ, and fought in **[[Ghazwat al-Khandaq]]**, **[[Ṣulḥ al-Ḥudaybiyah]]**, **[[Ghazwat Khaybar]]**, and **[[Fatḥ Makkah]]** without missing any subsequent campaign (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:317).
- **The Answered Supplication**: Renowned for having his prayers answered directly by Allāh. When an envious woman named Arwā bint Uways falsely claimed to Marwān b. al-Ḥakam that Saʿīd had usurped a parcel of her land, Saʿīd prayed: *“O Allāh, if she is lying, blind her eyes and let her die upon her land!”* She lost her sight shortly thereafter, and while walking upon her property, fell into an open well and perished (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:318; *Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim*, no. 1610).

## 3. Demise & Legacy
Saʿīd fought heroically at the decisive Battle of Yarmūk (13 AH) during the conquest of the Levant, and was appointed the first Muslim governor of Damascus before resigning to fight as a common soldier in the ranks.

He withdrew to his agricultural estate at al-ʿAqīq outside Medina, passing away in 51 AH at over seventy-three years of age. His body was washed by **[[Saʿd b. Abī Waqqāṣ]]** ﵁, carried into Medina, and buried in [[Jannat al-Baqīʿ]] (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:318).
