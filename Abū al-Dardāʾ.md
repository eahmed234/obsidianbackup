# Abū al-Dardāʾ (أبو الدرداء)
*The Sage of the Ummah — Master of Asceticism and Collector of the Qurʾān*

## 1. Lineage & Conversion
ʿUwaymir b. Mālik (or b. Zayd) al-Anṣārī al-Khazrajī, renowned universally by his agnomen **Abū al-Dardāʾ**, belonged to the Banū al-Ḥārith clan of **[[al-Khazraj]]** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:360; 11:330). He was the last in his household to embrace Islam, converted shortly after the battle of Badr when his close friend **[[ʿAbdullāh b. Rawāḥah]]** ﵁ visited his house and broke his domestic idol, convincing him of the futility of polytheism (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:360).

Upon his conversion, the Prophet ﷺ paired him in the fraternal covenant (**[[al-Muʾākhāh]]**) with **[[Salmān al-Fārisī]]** ﵁ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:360). Salmān famously counselled him on maintaining spiritual balance between worship, bodily health, and family duties, a maxim confirmed by the Prophet ﷺ: «صَدَقَ سَلْمَانُ» (*"Salmān has spoken the truth"*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:360).

## 2. Key Sīrah Milestones
- **Military Service**: Commencing with **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]**, Abū al-Dardāʾ participated in all major military campaigns of the Prophet ﷺ, displaying noted chivalry (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:205, 365; 5:49).
- **Compiler of the Qurʾān**: Renowned for profound mastery of Qurʾānic memorization and recitation; he was one of the small group of Companions who completed the compilation and recitation of the entire Qurʾān during the lifetime of the Prophet ﷺ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:330).
- **The Sage of the Ummah**: Celebrated as *Ḥakīm al-Ummah* (The Sage of the Islamic Nation) for his boundless legal wisdom, deep contemplation, and ascetic detachment from worldly splendor (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:330).

## 3. Demise & Legacy
During the caliphate of **[[ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb]]** ﵁, Abū al-Dardāʾ was dispatched to Damascus as supreme judge (*Qāḍī*) and teacher of the Qurʾān, establishing the legendary Damascus Qurʾānic circle. He passed away in Damascus in 32 AH during the caliphate of **[[ʿUthmān b. ʿAffān]]** ﵁ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:330).
