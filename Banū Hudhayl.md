# Banū Hudhayl (بنو هذيل)
*Hudhayl b. Mudrikah of Muḍar — Custodians of Suwāʿ and the Host of the Ḥijāz Mountains*

## 1. Lineage and Ancestry
**Banū Hudhayl** is an ancient and prominent North Arabian tribal confederation of Muḍar (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:30; 6:198). 

Their complete ancestral line ascends: **Hudhayl b. Mudrikah b. Ilyās b. Muḍar b. Nizār b. Maʿadd b. ʿAdnān** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:30). Through Mudrikah b. Ilyās, they share close paternal kinship with **[[Banū Kinānah]]** and **[[Quraysh]]** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:280; 5:30).

Major branches (*buṭūn*) of Hudhayl include:
- **Banū Liḥyān**: Celebrated branch inhabiting the valleys around ʿUsfān and Ghurrān (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:30).
- **Banū Saʿd b. Hudhayl**: Renowned throughout Arabia for their unmatched mastery of classical Arabic poetry and marksmanship (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:40).

## 2. Pre-Islamic Devotion & The Idol Suwāʿ
In the chronicle of pre-Islamic idol worship, Imām al-Ṣāliḥī records that Banū Hudhayl took the ancient idol **Suwāʿ** as their chief tribal deity (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:178; 6:198):
> «وَاتَّخَذَتْ هُذَيْلُ بْنُ مُدْرِكَةَ سُوَاعًا، كَانَ بِرُهَاطَ»
> *"And Hudhayl b. Mudrikah adopted Suwāʿ, which was located at Ruhāṭ."* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:178; 6:198).

Suwāʿ was an idol fashioned in the likeness of a woman, stationed at the oasis settlement of Ruhāṭ on the Red Sea coastal road three miles from the pilgrim routes, where tribes of the region made pilgrimage and offerings to it (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:198).

## 3. Role Across Major Sīrah Milestones

### The Treachery of al-Rajīʿ
In the month of Ṣafar, tribesmen of Banū Liḥyān from Hudhayl sought retribution for the death of their chieftain Sufyān b. Khālid b. Nubayḥ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:40). They colluded with ʿAḍal and al-Qārah to lure an expedition of Muslim instructors from Medina. 

When the companions reached the well of al-Rajīʿ near ʿUsfān, approximately two hundred fighters of Banū Liḥyān ambushed them, resulting in the martyrdom of **[[ʿĀṣim b. Thābit]]**, **[[Marthad b. Abī Marthad al-Ghanawī]]**, and the capture and execution of **[[Khubayb b. ʿAdī]]** and Zayd b. al-Dathinah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:40–41).

### The Expedition of Banū Liḥyān (Ghazwat Banī Liḥyān)
Deeply grieved by the betrayal of his companions at al-Rajīʿ, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ marched personally at the head of two hundred warriors directly into the rugged mountain territory of Banū Liḥyān at Ghurrān near ʿUsfān (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:30). Upon hearing of the approaching Muslim force, Banū Liḥyān fled into the mountain summits (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:30).

### The Demolition of Suwāʿ (Sariyyat ʿAmr b. al-ʿĀṣ)
Following the Conquest of Makkah in Ramaḍān, the Prophet ﷺ dispatched **[[ʿAmr b. al-ʿĀṣ]]** ﵁ to eradicate the idol of Hudhayl at Ruhāṭ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:198). 

When ʿAmr arrived at the shrine, the hereditary guardian (*al-sādin*) warned him: *"You cannot destroy it; you will be prevented!"* ʿAmr retorted: *"Woe to you! Does it hear or see?"* ʿAmr shattered the idol into pieces, demolished the treasure house, and upon seeing its total impotence, the guardian renounced idolatry and embraced Islam (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:198).

## 4. Prominent Figures
- **[[ʿAbdullāh b. Masʿūd]]** ﵁: The venerable Companion, custodian of the Prophet's secrets, and master Qurʾānic exegete belonged to the Banū Hudhayl tribe (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:199; 11:400).
