# ʿArafāt (عرفات)
*The Plain of the Standstill, Mount of Mercy, and the Theater of the Supreme Pilgrimage Covenant*

## 1. Topography & Sanctity outside the Ḥaram
ʿArafāt is an expansive desert plain ringed by granite hills, situated approximately twenty kilometers east of Makkah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:201; 8:468). Unlike Minā and Muzdalifah which reside within the bounds of the sacred precinct (*al-Ḥaram*), ʿArafāt lies completely within the profane perimeter (*al-Ḥill*), marking the boundary where the Ḥaram ends at Baṭn ʿUranah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:201; 8:468).

In the pre-Islamic era (*al-Jāhiliyyah*), Quraysh—boasting of their status as the guardians of the sanctuary (*Ahl al-Ḥaram* or *al-Ḥums*)—arrogantly refused to exit the sacred perimeter, halting instead at Muzdalifah, while other Arab pilgrims proceeded out to ʿArafāt (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:468, 473). In the tenth year of the Hijrah, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ dissolved this pagan tribal privilege and revived the primordial Abrahamic rite, advancing beyond the Ḥaram boundaries directly into the plain of ʿArafāt (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:468, 473).

## 2. The Halting at Namirah & The Universal Farewell Sermon
On the morning of Friday, the 9th of Dhū al-Ḥijjah 10 AH, the Prophet ﷺ departed Minā for ʿArafāt riding his camel *al-Qaṣwāʾ* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:468). He found a dome-tent of wool pitched for him at **Namirah** according to his prior instruction (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:468). After noon (*zawāl*), he rode into the valley basin of ʿUranah and delivered his historic **Khutbat ʿArafāt** (*Khutbat Ḥajjat al-Wadāʿ*) before a multitude of Companions, standing upright in his stirrups while Rabīʿah b. Umayyah b. Khalaf al-Jumaḥī ﵁ repeated his words with a booming voice across the ranks (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:468–471):
- **Sanctity of Human Life and Property**: Proclaiming that their blood and wealth were sacrosanct forever, akin to the inviolability of that sacred day, month, and city (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:468).
- **Abolition of Pagan Vendettas and Usury**: Striking down all matters of Jāhiliyyah beneath his feet, beginning by renouncing the blood vendetta for Ibn Rabīʿah b. al-Ḥārith b. ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib, and nullifying all interest-bearing transactions, beginning with the usury of his uncle **[[al-ʿAbbās b. ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib]]** ﵁ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:468–469).
- **Sanctity of Women's Rights**: Admonishing men to fear Allāh regarding women, reminding them that wives are held as trusts from Allāh (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:469).
- **The Two Weighty Legacies**: Exhorting the community to adhere steadfastly to the Book of Allāh and the Sunnah of His Prophet, guaranteeing they would never go astray (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:469).
- **Universal Brotherhood**: Affirming that every Muslim is the brother of a Muslim, and that mutual wealth is forbidden without willing consent (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:469).
- **The Divine Testimony**: Asking the vast assembly: *«And you shall be asked concerning me—what will you say?»* The Companions replied in unison: *«We testify that you have conveyed the message, fulfilled the trust, and given sincere counsel!»* The Prophet ﷺ raised his index finger to the sky and lowered it toward the people, supplicating thrice: *«اللَّهُمَّ اشْهَدْ، اللَّهُمَّ اشْهَدْ، اللَّهُمَّ اشْهَدْ»* (*"O Allāh, bear witness! O Allāh, bear witness! O Allāh, bear witness!"*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:470).

Following the sermon, **[[Bilāl b. Rabāḥ]]** ﵁ called the adhān and iqāmah, and the Prophet ﷺ combined the Ẓuhr and ʿAṣr prayers shortened to two units each with a single adhān and two iqāmahs (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:471).

## 3. The Standstill (*Wuqūf*) and the Revelation of Completion
After concluding prayer, the Prophet ﷺ rode onto the actual plain of ʿArafāt until he reached the foot of the rocky mount, positioning his she-camel *al-Qaṣwāʾ* against the large black boulders (*al-ṣakhrāt*) at the base of the hill, facing the Qiblah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:471). He announced to the believers:
> «وَقَفْتُ هَا هُنَا، وَعَرَفَةُ كُلُّهَا مَوْقِفٌ، إِلَّا بَطْنَ عُرَنَةَ»
> *"I have stood here, and all of ʿArafāt is a place of standstill, save the valley of ʿUranah!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:468, 471).

From midday until sunset, the Prophet ﷺ remained standing upon his mount, supplicating fervently with hands raised to his chest like a destitute petitioner pleading for mercy (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:471). He declared that the supreme supplication is the prayer offered on the Day of ʿArafah: *«خَيْرُ الدُّعَاءِ دُعَاءُ يَوْمِ عَرَفَةَ»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:471).

It was during this solemn standstill at ʿArafāt on the afternoon of Friday that Allāh revealed the momentous āyah of divine perfection and culmination (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:472):
> ﴿الْيَوْمَ أَكْمَلْتُ لَكُمْ دِينَكُمْ وَأَتْمَمْتُ عَلَيْكُمْ نِعْمَتِي وَرَضِيتُ لَكُمُ الْإِسْلَامَ دِينًا﴾
> *"This day I have perfected for you your religion and completed My favor upon you and have approved for you Islam as religion."* (al-Māʾidah 5:3).

## 4. The Departure (*al-Ifāḍah*) to Muzdalifah
As the solar disc vanished beneath the western horizon and the yellow glow disappeared, the Prophet ﷺ set out from ʿArafāt toward Muzdalifah, seating **[[Usāmah b. Zayd]]** ﵁ behind him on his mount (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:472). He pulled the reins of *al-Qaṣwāʾ* so tightly that her head brushed against his stirrup, restraining her speed while repeatedly calling out to the pilgrims with serenity and dignity:
> «أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ، عَلَيْكُمُ السَّكِينَةَ، فَإِنَّ الْبِرَّ لَيْسَ بِالإِيضَاعِ»
> *"O people, observe tranquility and calmness, for righteousness does not consist in galloping!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:472).

He continued along the pass of al-Maʾzimayn toward Muzdalifah, chanting the talbiyah unceasingly (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:472).
