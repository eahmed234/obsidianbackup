# ʿArafāt (عرفات)
*The Grand Plain of the Stand (al-Wuqūf), the Mountain of Mercy, and the Proclamation of the Universal Charter*

> [!NOTE]
> **Canonical Sourcing**: This topographical profile is established upon *[[Subul al-Hudā war-Rashād]]* by Imām Muḥammad b. Yūsuf al-Ṣāliḥī (vol. 8, pp. 468–472).

---

## 1. Topography & Geographic Uniqueness
- **Topography**: An expansive desert plain encircled by granite ridges approximately twenty kilometers east-southeast of **[[Makkah al-Mukarramah]]** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:468).
- **Location Beyond the Ḥaram**: Unlike **[[Minā]]** and **[[Muzdalifah]]**, the plain of ʿArafāt is geographically situated **outside the boundary of the Sacred Sanctuary (*al-Ḥill*)**, directly bordering the Ḥaram markers at Baṭn ʿUranah and Namirah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:468).
- **Repudiation of the Ḥums Practice**: In the pre-Islamic era, the aristocratic Quraysh (who termed themselves *al-Ḥums*) arrogantly refused to exit the Ḥaram boundary during the pilgrimage, halting only at Muzdalifah while common pilgrims were made to stand at ʿArafāt. In Islam, the Prophet ﷺ dismantled this tribal exceptionalism, proceeding directly onto the plain of ʿArafāt to fulfill the ancient Abrahamic ritual and affirming: *«الْحَجُّ عَرَفَةُ»* (*"Hajj is ʿArafah!"*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:468).
- **Jabal al-Raḥmah (Mount of Mercy)**: The prominent granite hill (*Jabal Īlāl*) rising in the eastern quadrant of the plain, at the foot of which the Prophet ﷺ made his historic station on his mount *al-Qaṣwāʾ* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:471).

---

## 2. The Great Day of ʿArafah at Ḥajjat al-Wadāʿ (10 AH)
On Friday, 9th Dhū al-Ḥijjah 10 AH, the Prophet ﷺ rode onto the plains of ʿArafāt, marking the spiritual apex of the Prophetic biography (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:468–472):
- **Encampment at Namirah**: A dome tent was pitched for the Prophet ﷺ at Namirah near the western edge of the plain, where he rested until midday (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:468).
- **The Farewell Sermon in Baṭn ʿUranah**:
  - Descending into the dry river basin of Baṭn ʿUranah, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ sat upon *al-Qaṣwāʾ* and delivered the historic **Farewell Address (*Khuṭbat al-Wadāʿ*)** to the gathered multitude of believers (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:468–471).
  - With **Rabīʿah b. Umayyah b. Khalaf** ﵁ shouting his words sentence by sentence to the assembly, the Prophet ﷺ proclaimed:
    1. The eternal sanctity and inviolability of human life, property, and honor.
    2. The total abolition of pre-Islamic blood vendettas and usury (*ribā*).
    3. The sacred charter of women's rights, kindness, and equity.
    4. The universal brotherhood of all believers regardless of race or tribe.
    5. The perpetual adherence to the two anchors: the Book of Allāh and the Prophetic Sunnah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:469–470).
  - The Prophet ﷺ called Allāh to witness the fulfillment of his prophetic trust:
    > «اللَّهُمَّ اشْهَدْ! اللَّهُمَّ اشْهَدْ! اللَّهُمَّ اشْهَدْ!»
    > *"O Allāh, bear witness! O Allāh, bear witness! O Allāh, bear witness!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:470).

---

## 3. The Standing (*al-Wuqūf*) & The Completion of Revelation
- **The Station at the Boulders**: After combining Ẓuhr and ʿAṣr prayers shortened at two rakʿahs each behind **[[Bilāl b. Rabāḥ]]'s** adhān, the Prophet ﷺ rode to the foot of the boulders (*al-ṣakhrāt*) of Jabal al-Raḥmah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:471).
- **Deep Supplication**: Facing the Qiblah, he remained standing in continuous, heartfelt duʿāʾ with hands raised to his chest until the sun set and its yellow disk completely disappeared (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:471). He instructed: *«قَدْ وَقَفْتُ هَا هُنَا، وَعَرَفَةُ كُلُّهَا مَوْقِفٌ»* (*"I have stood here, and all of ʿArafah is a place of standing!"*).
- **Revelation of the Final Seal (Qurʾān 5:3)**: While standing upon ʿArafāt on that Friday evening, the ultimate seal of divine guidance descended upon him:
  > ﴿الْيَوْمَ أَكْمَلْتُ لَكُمْ دِينَكُمْ وَأَتْمَمْتُ عَلَيْكُمْ نِعْمَتِي وَرَضِيتُ لَكُمُ الْإِسْلَامَ دِينًا﴾
  > *"This day I have perfected for you your religion and completed My favor upon you and have approved for you Islam as your religion."* (Sūrat al-Māʾidah 5:3; *Subul al-Hudā*, 8:472).
  Hearing this verse, **[[ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb]]** ﵁ wept, discerning that with divine completion comes the imminent departure of the Prophet ﷺ to the Highest Companion (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:472).
