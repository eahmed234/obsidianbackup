# Jabal Abū Qubays (جبل أبي قبيس)
*The Ancient Eastern Bastion of Makkah — Mount al-Amīn and Witness of Cosmic Signs*

## 1. Topography & Primordial Status
**Jabal Abū Qubays** is the venerable mountain rising directly east of **[[al-Masjid al-Ḥarām]]**, directly overlooking the Kaʿbah and Mount al-Ṣafā (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:263; 5:374). Together with Mount Quʿayqiʿān on the western side, the two heights are known as **al-Akhshabān** (the two rugged mountain bastions of Makkah) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:374).

In sacred topography, classical chronicles relate that Jabal Abū Qubays was the first mountain set firmly upon the earth by Allāh (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:141).

### The Trust of the Black Stone (*al-Amīn*)
Classically, Mount Abū Qubays was named **al-Amīn** (the Trustworthy) because when the Great Deluge inundated the earth in the era of Nūḥ ﵊, the heavenly **Black Stone** (*al-Ḥajar al-Aswad*) was divinely deposited inside the bedrock of Mount Abū Qubays for safe-keeping (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:263). It remained preserved in trust until the era of Ibrāhīm and Ismāʿīl ﵄, who were directed by Jibrīl to extract it when raising the foundations of the House (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:151; 3:263).

The Prophet ﷺ celebrated the spiritual weight of the Stone in relation to the mountain, stating:
> «يَأْتِي الرُّكْنُ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ أَعْظَمَ مِنْ أَبِي قُبَيْسٍ، لَهُ لِسَانٌ وَشَفَتَانِ يَشْهَدُ لِمَنِ اسْتَلَمَهُ بِحَقٍّ»
> *"The Corner [the Black Stone] shall come on the Day of Resurrection greater in size than Mount Abū Qubays, possessing a tongue and two lips testifying in truth for whoever touched it."* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:179).

## 2. Key Sīrah Milestones

### The Proclamation of Ḥilf al-Fuḍūl
Before the advent of Islam, when an influential chieftain of Banū Sahm defrauded an incoming merchant from the Yemeni tribe of Zabīd, the oppressed merchant climbed to the summit of Mount Abū Qubays at sunrise and recited stirring poetic verses summoning the noble conscience of the descendants of Fihr (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:154). 

His cry from the heights of Abū Qubays stirred the noble houses of Quraysh—led by Banū Hāshim, Banū al-Muṭṭalib, and Banū Zuhrah—to assemble at the residence of ʿAbdullāh b. Judʿān and ratify the historic justice alliance, **[[Ḥilf al-Fuḍūl]]**, which the Prophet ﷺ attended in his youth (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:154).

### The Miracle of the Splitting of the Moon (*Inshiqāq al-Qamar*)
When the leaders of Quraysh gathered around the Prophet ﷺ demanding a celestial proof of his divine messengership, Allāh caused the moon to split into two distinct parts (*Subul al-Hudā*, 9:430). 

As recorded in multiple authentic transmissions, the two separate halves of the moon were visibly observed by the inhabitants of Makkah positioned directly over the twin peaks, with one half resting over **Jabal Abū Qubays** and the other half over Mount Quʿayqiʿān (*Subul al-Hudā*, 9:430–432). The Prophet ﷺ proclaimed to the witnessing multitude:
> «اشْهَدُوا!»
> *"Bear witness!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 9:430).
