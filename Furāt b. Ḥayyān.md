# Furāt b. Ḥayyān (فرات بن حيان) ﵁
*Abū Sulaymān al-ʿIjlī — Master Navigator of Arabia, Confederate of Banū Sahm, Trusted Companion Entrusted to His Faith*

## 1. Lineage, Ancestry & Pre-Islamic Prominence
### Patrilineal Descent & Confederacy
He is **Furāt b. Ḥayyān b. Thaʿlabah b. ʿAbd al-ʿUzzā b. Ḥabīb b. Ḥayyah b. Rabīʿah b. Ṣaʿb b. ʿIjl b. Lujaym al-Rabaʿī al-Yashkurī al-ʿIjlī** (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:272; *Subul al-Hudā*, 6:32). 

- He belonged to the northern tribal branch of **Banū ʿIjl** (of the great Rabīʿah confederation of Bakr b. Wāʾil).
- In Makkah, he had formed a long-standing military and commercial confederacy (*ḥilf*) with the clan of **Banū Sahm** (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:272; *Subul al-Hudā*, 6:32).
- **Master Navigator**: Furāt was universally renowned among the Arab tribes as one of the finest desert pathfinders and navigational guides (*ahdā al-nās bi-al-ṭarīq*) (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:273). He served as the primary intelligence scout and guide for **[[Abū Sufyān b. Ḥarb]]** in the desert routes toward Iraq and Syria (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:273).

---

## 2. Capture at al-Qaradah & Conversion (3 AH)
During the early months of 3 AH, following the blockade of the western Red Sea trade routes after Badr, Quraysh hired Furāt to guide their high-value trade caravan through the desolate inland plateau of Najd (**[[Sariyyat al-Qaradah]]**) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:32).

When **[[Zayd b. Ḥārithah]]** ﵁ ambushed the caravan at the wells of al-Qaradah, the merchants fled and Furāt was taken captive (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:32). Furāt had previously been captured at Badr and had fled; facing execution as an enemy scout, his close pre-Islamic friend **[[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]]** ﵁ visited him in captivity and urged him to accept Islam. 

Furāt declared his faith sincerely before the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ, who accepted his Islam, emancipated him, and spared his life (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:273; *Subul al-Hudā*, 6:32).

---

## 3. Prophetic Commendation & Scholarship
Furāt became an exemplary, pious Companion, studying the Qurʾān and attaining profound legal discernment (*faqiha fī al-dīn*) (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:273). 

The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ praised the unshakeable sincerity of his heart:
> «إِنَّ مِنْكُمْ رِجَالًا نَكِلُهُمْ إِلَى إِيمَانِهِمْ، مِنْهُمْ فُرَاتُ بْنُ حَيَّانَ!»
> *"Indeed, among you are men whom we entrust to their faith, among whom is Furāt b. Ḥayyān!"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:273; Sunan Abī Dāwūd).

- The Prophet ﷺ granted him an agricultural land endowment (*iqṭāʿ*) in Yamāmah that yielded an annual revenue of four thousand two hundred dirhams (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:273).
- **The Portent of al-Rajjāl b. ʿUnfuwah**: The Prophet ﷺ once observed Abū Hurayrah, Furāt b. Ḥayyān, and al-Rajjāl b. ʿUnfuwah departing his presence, warning that one among them would suffer apostasy and whose molar in Hell would be larger than Mount Uḥud. Furāt and Abū Hurayrah lived in constant terror of this portent until al-Rajjāl apostatized and joined the false prophet Musaylimah, being slain at Yamāmah, whereupon Furāt and Abū Hurayrah immediately fell in prostration of gratitude (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:273).

---

## 4. Migration to Kūfah & Passing
Following the Islamic conquests of Iraq, Furāt migrated to **Kūfah**, constructing a family residence there and leaving prominent scholarly progeny (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:273). Prominent early authorities, including Ḥārithah b. Muḍarrib, Qays b. Zuhayr, and the master scholar **al-Ḥasan al-Baṣrī**, transmitted traditions from him (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:273). 

Furāt passed away in Kūfah during the era of Muʿāwiyah (~50 AH) (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:273).
