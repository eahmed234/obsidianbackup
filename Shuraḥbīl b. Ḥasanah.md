# Shuraḥbīl b. Ḥasanah (شرحبيل بن حسنة الكندي)
*Early Emigrant of Abyssinia, Scribe of Revelation, and Supreme Commander of the Conquest of the Levant*

> [!NOTE] Primary Sourcing & Biographical Provenance
> Sourced directly from *al-Iṣābah fī Tamyīz al-Ṣaḥābah*, 3:265–266 (entry 3888), and *Subul al-Hudā war-Rashād*, 1:58, 11:197.

---

## 1. Lineage & Maternal Appellation

Shuraḥbīl was an early Companion of southern Arabian origin who achieved renown under his mother's name (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:265).

- **Full Lineage**: Shuraḥbīl b. ʿAbdillāh b. al-Muṭāʿ b. ʿAbdillāh al-Ghiṭrīf b. ʿAbd al-ʿUzzā b. Jaththāmah b. Mālik al-Kindī (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:265).
- **Maternal Appellation**: Named after his noble mother **Ḥasanah**, a client of Maʿmar b. Ḥabīb al-Jumaḥī (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:265).
- **Confederacy**: Following the migration, Shuraḥbīl entered into a close confederacy (*ḥilf*) with **[[Banū Zuhrah]]** in Makkah (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:266).

---

## 2. Early Islam & The Abyssinian Migration

- **Early Conversion**: Shuraḥbīl embraced Islam in the earliest dawn of the prophetic mission in Makkah alongside his maternal brothers Jābir and Junādah (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:265–266).
- **Migration to Abyssinia**: He undertook the perilous migration across the Red Sea to Abyssinia in the Second Hijrah, remaining there steadfast in his faith (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:266).
- **Return with the Bride of the Prophet ﷺ**: When the Negus solemnized the marriage of [[Umm Ḥabībah (Ramlah bint Abī Sufyān)]] ﵂ to the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ, he dispatched Shuraḥbīl b. Ḥasanah to escort the noble Mother of the Believers safely to Medina (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:197).
- **Scribe of Divine Revelation**: Upon settling in Medina, Shuraḥbīl was appointed by the Prophet ﷺ among the official scribes who recorded the divine revelation (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:58).

---

## 3. Leadership in the Syrian Campaigns

Following the demise of the Prophet ﷺ, Caliph **[[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]]** ﵁ appointed Shuraḥbīl as one of the four supreme army commanders dispatched for the conquest of the Levant (*Futūḥ al-Shām*) (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:266).
- Assigned command over the fourth army directed toward Palestine and Jordan, Shuraḥbīl exhibited exceptional tactical brilliance.
- Under Caliph **[[ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb]]** ﵁, he was confirmed as governor over a quarter of the Syrian provinces and completed the peaceful capitulation and conquest of **Tiberias** (*Ṭabariyyah*) and the surrounding districts of Jordan (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:266).

---

## 4. Martyrdom in the Plague of ʿAmwās

During the caliphate of ʿUmar, the devastating Plague of ʿAmwās (**[[Ṭāʿūn ʿAmwās]]**) swept across the military camps of the Levant (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:266).
- Shuraḥbīl and the supreme commander **[[Abū ʿUbaydah b. al-Jarrāḥ]]** ﵁ were stricken on the very same day (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:266).
- He passed away as a martyr at the age of sixty-seven years (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:266).
