# al-ʿAbbās b. ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib (العباس بن عبد المطلب) ﵁
*Abū al-Faḍl — Paternal Uncle of the Prophet ﷺ, Custodian of the Siqāyah, and Progenitor of the ʿAbbāsids*

## 1. Lineage, Birth & Maternal Roots
- **Ancestry**: al-ʿAbbās b. [[ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib]] b. Hāshim b. ʿAbd Manāf b. Quṣayy al-Qurashī al-Hāshimī (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:511). Paternal uncle of the Prophet ﷺ and full elder brother of Ḍirār and Qutham (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:103).
- **Mother**: Nutaylah bint Janāb b. Kalb. When al-ʿAbbās was lost as a small child in Mecca, she vowed that if Allāh returned him safely to her, she would clothe the Sacred House in silk; upon finding him, she fulfilled her vow, becoming the first woman to clothe the Kaʿbah in silk (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:511).
- **Birth**: Born in Mecca approximately two years prior to the Prophet ﷺ (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:511).
- **Appearance & Demeanour**: He was tall, handsome, fair-complexioned (*ṭawīlan jamīlan abyaḍ*), possessing an exceptionally powerful and resonant voice (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:512).

## 2. Offices of the Sanctuary: *Siqāyah* and *ʿImārah*
- Inheriting ancient custody from Hāshim b. ʿAbd Manāf, al-ʿAbbās held the custodianship of the pilgrim water supply (*al-Siqāyah*) and the maintenance of the Sacred Precinct (*al-ʿImārah*) throughout the Jāhiliyyah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:271; *al-Iṣābah*, 3:511).
- Following the Conquest of Mecca, the Prophet ﷺ confirmed his perpetual tenure over the *Siqāyah* and granted him an explicit dispensation (*rukhṣah*) from encamping overnight at Minā during the Hajj rites so he could remain in Mecca attending to the water provision for pilgrims:
  > *al-Bāb … fī iʿṭāʾihi ﷺ lil-ʿAbbās al-siqāyah wa-rukhṣatihi lahu fī tark al-mabīt bi-Minā li-ajlihā* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:103).

## 3. Narrator of the *Dalāʾil al-Nubuwwah*
Al-Ṣāliḥī relies heavily on al-ʿAbbās’s direct testimony for events surrounding the Prophet's ﷺ early life:
- The expedition of [[ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib]] to Yemen and the prognostications of the Jewish scholars and rabbis regarding the emerging Prophet (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:325).
- The signs accompanying his early childhood and nursing, including the account of the infant Prophet ﷺ addressing the moon in his cradle (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:349).

## 4. Key Sīrah Milestones
- **The Second Pledge of al-ʿAqabah (622 CE)**: Prior to declaring his Islam, al-ʿAbbās accompanied his nephew to the secret rendezvous at al-ʿAqabah. He addressed the seventy-three representatives of the Aws and Khazraj, demanding ironclad guarantees for the protection and security of the Prophet ﷺ before any pact was sealed (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:203; *al-Iṣābah*, 3:511).
- **The Battle of Badr (2 AH)**: Compelled to march out among the Qurayshite nobles under duress, he was captured by the Muslims. Held alongside the prisoners of war, he ransomed himself and his nephew **[[ʿAqīl b. Abī Ṭālib]]** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:55; *al-Iṣābah*, 3:511). When ransomed, the divine assurance of [[Sūrat al-Anfāl]] (8:70) was revealed regarding captives harboring inward faith (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:70).
- **Intelligence in Mecca & Hijrah**: Returning to Mecca, he concealed his faith, acting as a confidential informant transmitting critical intelligence regarding Qurayshite movements to the Prophet ﷺ in Medina (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:511). He completed his open emigration (*hijrah*) shortly before the Conquest of Mecca (*Fatḥ Makkah*), encountering the advancing Muslim army at al-Juḥfah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:208; *al-Iṣābah*, 3:511).
- **Fatḥ Makkah & Ghazwat Ḥunayn (8 AH)**: Present during the triumphal entry into Mecca. At the severe crisis of **[[Ghazwat Ḥunayn]]**, when the vanguard broke under the Hawāzin ambush, al-ʿAbbās remained steadfastly clutching the bridle of the Prophet’s ﷺ mule; at the Prophet’s ﷺ command, he utilized his thunderous voice to summon the fleeing warriors:
  > «يَا مَعْشَرَ الْأَنْصَارِ! يَا أَصْحَابَ السَّمُرَةِ!»
  > *"O assembly of the Anṣār! O companions of the Tree of the Pledge!"*
  re-rallying the Muslim ranks to complete victory (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:330–335; *al-Iṣābah*, 3:511).

## 5. Merits & Prophetic Praise
- The Prophet ﷺ exalted his status, affirming:
  > «مَنْ آذَى الْعَبَّاسَ فَقَدْ آذَانِي، فَإِنَّمَا عَمُّ الرَّجُلِ صِنْوُ أَبِيهِ»
  > *"Whoever harms al-ʿAbbās has harmed me, for a man’s paternal uncle is the twin of his father."* (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:511).
- Saʿd b. Abī Waqqāṣ ﵁ narrated that the Prophet ﷺ said:
  > «هَذَا الْعَبَّاسُ أَجْوَدُ قُرَيْشٍ كَفّاً وَأَوْصَلُهَا»
  > *"This al-ʿAbbās is the most generous of Quraysh in hand and the most maintaining of kinship bonds."* (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:512).

## 6. Death & Burial
He passed away in Medina in Rajab (or Ramaḍān) of the year thirty-two AH during the caliphate of **[[ʿUthmān b. ʿAffān]]** ﵁ and was buried in **[[al-Baqīʿ]]** (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:512). His descendants through his son **[[ʿAbdullāh b. ʿAbbās]]** would later establish the ʿAbbāsid Caliphate.
