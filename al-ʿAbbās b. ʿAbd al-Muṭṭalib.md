# al-ʿAbbās b. ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib (العباس بن عبد المطلب)
*Abū al-Faḍl — Paternal Uncle of the Prophet ﷺ and Narrator of the Signs of Prophethood*

## 1. Lineage
Son of [[ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib]] and thus paternal uncle of the Prophet ﷺ, of the [[Banū Hāshim]]. He was
close in age to his nephew and remained a figure of standing in Makkah, holding the office of
provisioning the pilgrims (*siqāyah*).

## 2. Narrator of the *Dalāʾil*
Al-Ṣāliḥī repeatedly transmits reports of the signs preceding and surrounding the Prophet's ﷺ birth
and infancy on al-ʿAbbās's authority — including the account of the Yemeni journey and the rabbi's
recognition (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:325), and the report of the infant Prophet ﷺ addressing the moon in
his cradle (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:349). His narrations are a principal channel for the
*dalāʾil al-nubuwwah* material in Volume 1.

## 3. Badr and Conversion
He was present at **[[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]]** on the Meccan side and was taken captive. When the
seventy prisoners were held for ransom, he and others claimed inward faith — the occasion of the
revelation of [[Sūrat al-Anfāl]] (8:70), which guaranteed restitution to any captive in whose heart
Allāh knew good (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:70). He later declared Islam openly and was present at
**[[Fatḥ Makkah]]** (8 AH) and **[[Ghazwat Ḥunayn]]**, where he was among the small band who held
firm beside the Prophet ﷺ.

## 4. Legacy
He died in Medina in 32 AH. The ʿAbbāsid caliphate would later trace its legitimacy to his line.
