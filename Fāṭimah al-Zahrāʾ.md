# Fāṭimah al-Zahrāʾ (فاطمة الزهراء بنت رسول الله) ﵂
*Umm Abīhā (The Mother of Her Father) — Sayyidat Nisāʾ Ahl al-Jannah (Mistress of the Women of Paradise)*

## 1. Lineage & Early Devotion in Mecca
She is Fāṭimah bint Muḥammad b. ʿAbdillāh ﷺ, the youngest and most beloved daughter of the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ and **[[Khadījah bint Khuwaylid]]** ﵂ (kunyas: Umm al-Ḥasanayn and *Umm Abīhā*, "The Mother of Her Father", for her tender care of the Prophet ﷺ; titles: *al-Zahrāʾ*, "The Luminous", and *al-Batūl*, "The Pure and Chaste") (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:37). Born in Mecca around the rebuilding of the Kaʿbah, she resembled the Prophet ﷺ more closely in gait, speech, and mannerism than anyone else (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:37, 43).

As a young girl in Mecca, when the polytheist leaders threw the filthy fetal membrane and entrails of a slaughtered camel upon the Prophet's back as he prostrated before the Kaʿbah, Fāṭimah ran forward weeping, cast off the filth, and confronted the mocking aristocrats of Quraysh with fearless courage (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:330; 11:37).

## 2. Marriage to ʿAlī & The Prophetic Lineage
In Dhū al-Qaʿdah 2 AH, following the victory of Badr, the Prophet ﷺ wed her to his cousin **[[ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib]]** ﵁ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:38). Their household lived in noble ascetic poverty, with Fāṭimah grinding grain by hand until blisters formed on her palms and carrying heavy waterskins until bruises marked her shoulders (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:48).

Through Fāṭimah alone did Allāh preserve the direct biological lineage of the Prophet ﷺ, bearing the masters of the youth of Paradise, **al-Ḥasan** and **al-Ḥusayn**, and their daughters Zaynab and Umm Kulthūm (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:55).

## 3. Exalted Prophetic Status
The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ bestowed upon her unparalleled praise:
- **Part of the Prophet's Being**:
  > «فَاطِمَةُ بَضْعَةٌ مِنِّي، فَمَنْ أَغْضَبَهَا أَغْضَبَنِي، وَمَنْ آذَاهَا فَقَدْ آذَانِي!»
  > *"Fāṭimah is a part of me; whosoever angers her angers me, and whosoever causes her pain causes me pain!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:42; *Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī*, no. 3714).
- Whenever she entered his presence, the Prophet ﷺ would stand up to receive her, kiss her forehead, take her by the hand, and seat her in his own place (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:43).
- **Mistress of the Women of Paradise**:
  > «يَا فَاطِمَةُ، أَمَا تَرْضَيْنَ أَنْ تَكُونِي سَيِّدَةَ نِسَاءِ أَهْلِ الْجَنَّةِ، وَإِنَّكِ أَوَّلُ أَهْلِي لُحُوقًا بِي؟»
  > *"O Fāṭimah, are you not pleased to be the mistress of the women of Paradise, and that you will be the first of my household to join me?!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:41, 45; *Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī*, no. 3624).
- **Service at Uḥud (3 AH)**: Rushed to the battlefield of **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]** upon hearing of the Prophet's injury; seeing blood pouring from his face, she burned a scrap of woven straw mat into ashes and pressed it upon the wound, which staunched the bleeding (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:210; 11:44).

## 4. Final Secret & Passing (11 AH)
During the Prophet's final illness (**[[Wafāt al-Nabī]]**), he called Fāṭimah close and whispered in her ear; she wept profusely. He then whispered a second time, and she smiled brightly (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:45; 12:216). When **[[ʿĀʾishah bint Abī Bakr]]** ﵂ asked her concerning this, Fāṭimah revealed after the Prophet's demise: *“He whispered to me that he would die from this illness, so I wept; then he whispered that I would be the first of his family to join him, and that I am the mistress of the women of Paradise, so I smiled!”* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:45).

Overcome with profound grief after her father’s departure, she lived only six months after him, never once seen smiling during that period. She passed away in Medina on Tuesday, 3 Ramaḍān 11 AH, at approximately twenty-nine years of age. She was washed by Asmāʾ bint ʿUmays ﵂ and ʿAlī ﵁, and buried at night in **[[Jannat al-Baqīʿ]]** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:52–53).
