# Usayd b. Ḥuḍayr (أسيد بن حضير الأشهلي) ﵁
*Abū Yaḥyā al-Ashhalī al-Awsī — Chieftain of Banū ʿAbd al-Ashhal, Naqīb of al-ʿAqabah, and The Master of Recitation Attended by Angels*

## 1. Lineage, Clan Associations & Pre-Islamic Eminence
He is Usayd b. al-Ḥuḍayr b. Simāk b. ʿAtīk b. Imriʾ al-Qays b. Zayd b. ʿAbd al-Ashhal b. Jusham b. al-Ḥārith b. al-Khazraj b. al-Nabīt b. Mālik b. al-Aws al-Anṣārī al-Ashhalī (kunyas: Abū Yaḥyā and Abū ʿAtīk) (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:234). His father, Ḥuḍayr, was the paramount knight and leader of the Aws during the pre-Islamic battle of Buʿāth (*fāris al-Aws wa-raʾīsuhum yawma Buʿāth*) (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:234). 

Usayd was among the hereditary noblemen of Banū ʿAbd al-Ashhal. In pre-Islamic Yathrib, he was accorded the title *al-Kāmil* ("The Complete") by his people because he had mastered the three rare accomplishments of the nobility: fluent writing in Arabic, expert swimming, and martial archery (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:234).

## 2. Conversion to Islam & The Pledges of al-ʿAqabah
When **[[Muṣʿab b. ʿUmayr]]** ﵁ and **[[Asʿad b. Zurārah]]** ﵁ began calling the people of Yathrib to Islam in an orchard of Banū Ẓafar, the chieftain **[[Saʿd b. Muʿādh]]** ﵁ sent Usayd with his lance to confront and turn them back (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:198).

Usayd approached aggressively, planting his spear in the ground and rebuking them (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:198). Muṣʿab gently said to him: *“Will you not sit and listen? If you are pleased with what you hear, you may accept it; and if you dislike it, we shall desist.”* Usayd replied: *“You have spoken fairly”* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:198). As Muṣʿab recited the Qurʾān, the radiance and ease of Islam became visibly manifest upon Usayd's face (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:198). Overwhelmed, Usayd exclaimed: *“How beautiful is this speech!”* and asked how one enters the faith. Following Muṣʿab's instruction, he bathed, purified his garments, bore witness to the truth, and prayed two rakʿahs (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:198–199).

Usayd then returned to Saʿd b. Muʿādh and his clan council. When Saʿd saw him approaching, he swore: *“By Allāh, Usayd has returned with a countenance completely different from that with which he left!”* Usayd shrewdly devised a way to bring Saʿd to the same gathering, leading directly to the conversion of Saʿd and the entire clan of Banū ʿAbd al-Ashhal that very afternoon (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:199). 

Usayd subsequently attended [[Bayʿat al-ʿAqabah al-Thāniyah]], where the Prophet ﷺ appointed him as one of the twelve chosen representatives (**[[al-Nuqabāʾ]]**) representing the Aws (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:234). Following the Hijrah, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ established fraternal brotherhood (**[[al-Muʾākhāh]]**) between Usayd and **[[Zayd b. Ḥārithah]]** ﵁ (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:234).

## 3. Key Sīrah Milestones
- **The Battle of Badr (2 AH)**: Classical authorities differed over his presence at **[[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]]**; Ibn al-Sakan counted him among those who witnessed Badr, while other authorities stated he was among those who remained behind in Medina not anticipating a general war, a fact for which he expressed deep regret (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:234).
- **Steadfastness at Uḥud (3 AH)**: During **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]**, when the Muslim lines buckled under the polytheist cavalry assault, Usayd stood unwaveringly in defense of the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ, sustaining seven severe wounds during the engagement (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:234).
- **Subsequent Expeditions**: He participated steadfastly in **[[Ghazwat al-Khandaq]]**, **[[Ṣulḥ al-Ḥudaybiyah]]**, and all later campaigns alongside the Prophet ﷺ.

## 4. Angelic Canopy at His Recitation
Usayd was endowed with an extraordinarily melodious and resonant voice in reciting the Qurʾān. While reciting Sūrat al-Baqarah late at night near his tethered mare, his infant son Yaḥyā lay sleeping nearby (*Subul al-Hudā*, 10:47). Whenever he recited, his horse reared and bolted excitedly; when he paused, the mare calmed (*Subul al-Hudā*, 10:47). 

Looking up into the night sky fearing for his child, Usayd beheld a wondrous luminous canopy resembling a cloud studded with radiant lamps, ascending until it disappeared into the heavens (*Subul al-Hudā*, 10:47). When he informed the Prophet ﷺ the following morning, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ declared:
> «تِلْكَ الْمَلَائِكَةُ دَنَتْ لِصَوْتِكَ، وَلَوْ قَرَأْتَ لَأَصْبَحَ النَّاسُ يَنْظُرُونَ إِلَيْهَا لَا تَتَوَارَى مِنْهُمْ!»
> *"Those were the angels who drew near to your voice; and had you continued reading, the people would have looked upon them in the morning without them being veiled from sight!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 10:47).

## 5. Virtues, Character & Demise
- **Prophetic Praise**: The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ affirmed:
  > «نِعْمَ الرَّجُلُ أُسَيْدُ بْنُ حُضَيْرٍ»
  > *"What an excellent man is Usayd b. Ḥuḍayr!"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:235).
- **Eminence in Banū ʿAbd al-Ashhal**: ʿĀʾishah ﵂ stated:
  > *"Three of the Anṣār were such that none could surpass them in virtue, all of them from Banū ʿAbd al-Ashhal: Saʿd b. Muʿādh, Usayd b. Ḥuḍayr, and ʿAbbād b. Bishr"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:235).
- **Spiritual Aspiration**: Usayd used to remark:
  > *"Were I to remain continuously in the state I experience during three moments, I would be of the most virtuous: when I hear the Qurʾān or recite it; when I listen to the sermon of the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ; and when I attend a funeral"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:235).
- **Precedence Among Companions**: Ṭalḥah b. ʿAbdillāh al-Taymī reported that the Caliph **[[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]]** ﵁ never gave precedence to anyone from the Anṣār over Usayd b. Ḥuḍayr (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:235).
- **Demise & Burial**: Usayd passed away in Shaʿbān 20 AH (or 21 AH according to al-Madāʾinī) during the Caliphate of **[[ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb]]** ﵁ (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:235). Upon his death, ʿUmar personally intervened with his creditors, leasing the fruit of Usayd's date orchards for three years to pay off all outstanding debts, proclaiming: *“I shall not leave my brother’s children destitute!”* (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:235). ʿUmar accompanied his bier until he was buried in **[[Jannat al-Baqīʿ]]**.
