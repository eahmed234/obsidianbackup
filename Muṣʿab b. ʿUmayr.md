# Muṣʿab b. ʿUmayr (مصعب بن عمير)
*Abū ʿAbdillāh al-ʿAbdarī — The First Ambassador of Islam, The Martyr of Uḥud*

## 1. Overview
The most elegantly groomed youth of Makkah who sacrificed noble status and immense luxury for Islam. 

## 2. Key Sīrah Milestones
- Dispatched to Medina after **[[Bayʿat al-ʿAqabah al-Ūlā]]**; his tactful preaching brought the tribal aristocracy to Islam.
- Carried the Great White Standard at **[[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]]** (2 AH).
- Carried the Muslim standard at **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]** (3 AH). When his right arm was severed, he gripped the standard with his left; when his left was severed, he clasped it to his chest with his stumps until martyred (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:228).
- Buried in a short shroud that exposed his feet if his head was covered, prompting the Prophet ﷺ to weep and recite: *«مِّنَ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ رِجَالٌ صَدَقُوا مَا عَاهَدُوا اللَّهَ عَلَيْهِ»* (Qurʾān 33:23).
