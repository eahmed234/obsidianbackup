# ʿAbdullāh b. Jubayr (عبد الله بن جبير)
*Commander of the Archers at Uḥud — Who Held His Post to the Death*

## 1. Identity
An Anṣārī of **[[al-Aws]]**; al-Ṣāliḥī gives his genealogy and vocalizes the name against misreading
— *Ibn Jubayr, bi-ḍamm al-jīm wa-fatḥ al-muwaḥḥadah* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:218). He fought at
**[[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]]**.

## 2. Uḥud: The Archers' Hill
At **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]** the Prophet ﷺ stationed fifty marksmen atop Mount ʿAynayn under his command,
with orders admitting no exception:
> «احْمُوا ظُهُورَنَا … فَلَا تُشْرِكُونَا!»
> *"Guard our backs! … do not abandon your post until I summon you!"*
> (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:205)

When the Meccan lines broke and forty of the archers left the hill for the spoils, **ʿAbdullāh b.
Jubayr held it with the remaining ten**. [[Khālid b. al-Walīd]] ﵁ — then still fighting for Quraysh —
saw the gap, wheeled his cavalry around the rear, and destroyed them where they stood. Ibn Jubayr was
martyred at his post.

His obedience is the counterweight the sources set against the archers' error: the reversal at Uḥud
turned on a command disregarded, and he is the man who did not disregard it.
