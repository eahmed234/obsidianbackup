# Banū Qurayẓah (بنو قريظة)
*The Chieftains of the Southern Strongholds — The Breach of Covenant during the Siege of Medina*

## 1. Heritage & Territory
One of the three major Jewish tribes of Yathrib, inhabiting the heavily fortified towers and palm groves of the southern quarter of **[[al-Madīnah al-Munawwarah]]**. Prior to Islam, they were traditional confederates of **[[al-Aws]]**.

## 2. The Treaty of Medina & Treason at the Trench
Banū Qurayẓah had entered into a mutual defense pact with the Prophet ﷺ under the **[[Dustūr al-Madīnah|Constitution of Medina]]**, binding them to defend the oasis against external aggression.

During **[[Ghazwat al-Khandaq]]** (5 AH), while the Muslim army was pinned along the northern trench facing 10,000 confederates, Ḥuyayy b. Akhṭab (exiled chief of Banū al-Naḍīr) arrived at the fortress of Kaʿb b. Asad al-Quraẓī (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:373). After initial resistance, Kaʿb succumbed to Ḥuyayy's pressure, ripped up the written covenant, and aligned Banū Qurayẓah with the polytheist coalition (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:373–374).

This betrayal exposed the unprotected Muslim families, women, and children in the southern quarter of Medina to immediate slaughter from the rear, bringing the community into unprecedented existential terror (Qurʾān 33:10–11; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:374).

## 3. The Collapse of the Plot & Subsequent Siege
The treason of Banū Qurayẓah was neutralized through the psychological discord sown by **[[Nuʿaym b. Masʿūd]]** ﵁ and the divine hurricane (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:384–387).

Immediately following the retreat of the Confederates, the Prophet ﷺ was commanded by Jibrīl ﵊ to march upon their fortresses without delay, culminating in **[[Ghazwat Banī Qurayẓah]]** (Dhū al-Qaʿdah 5 AH) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:4).
