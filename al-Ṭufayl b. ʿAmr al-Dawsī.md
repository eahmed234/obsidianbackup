# al-Ṭufayl b. ʿAmr al-Dawsī (الطفيل بن عمرو الدوسي)
*Dhū al-Nūr — Chieftain of Daws, Prophetic Luminary, and Heroic Martyr of al-Yamāmah*

> [!NOTE] Canonical Sourcing
> Biographical account recorded in *al-Iṣābah fī Tamyīz al-Ṣaḥābah* (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:422–424, entry 4273) and *Subul al-Hudā war-Rashād* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:273).

---

## 1. Lineage & Pre-Islamic Prominence

**Al-Ṭufayl b. ʿAmr** b. Ṭarīf b. al-ʿĀṣ b. Thaʿlabah b. Sulaym b. Fahm b. Ghanm b. Daws al-Dawsī (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:422).

- **Epithet**: Endowed with the enduring title **Dhū al-Nūr** (*The Possessor of Light*) (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:422, 424).
- **Noble Status**: A prominent chieftain, poet, and dignitary of the great Yamanite tribe of [[Daws]].

---

## 2. Conversion in Makkah & The Miracle of Light

- **Qurayshite Warning and Cotton in Ears**: When al-Ṭufayl arrived in Makkah, the leaders of Quraysh cautioned him against listening to the Prophet [[Muḥammad b. ʻAbdullah|Muḥammad]] ﷺ, warning that his speech parted husbands from wives and brothers from brothers. Al-Ṭufayl stuffed his ears with cotton to avoid hearing him (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:424).
- **Listening at the Kaʿbah**: Approaching the Kaʿbah, he observed the Prophet ﷺ in prayer and reflected: *"I am an intelligent poet; I can distinguish good speech from bad. What prevents me from listening?"* When he listened, he followed the Prophet ﷺ home, heard the recitation of Sūrat al-Ikhlāṣ and the Muʿawwidhatayn, and embraced Islam immediately (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:424).
- **The Miracle of the Whip**: He asked the Prophet ﷺ for a miraculous sign (*āyah*) to assist his preaching among his people. A light initially shone between his eyes, but fearing people would view it as a disfigurement (*muthlah*), he prayed, and it transferred to the tip of his whip, illuminating the dark night like a lantern (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:424).

---

## 3. Missionary Labor, Khaybar Caravan & Martyrdom

- **Daʿwah to Daws**: Returning to his mountainous tribal homeland, his father and wife accepted Islam. Among his entire tribe, only [[Abū Hurayrah]] responded initially (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:424).
- **Prophetic Supplication for Daws**: When al-Ṭufayl returned to Makkah lamenting the disobedience of Daws, the Prophet ﷺ raised his hands and supplicated:
  > «اللَّهمّ اهد دوسا وائت بهم»
  > *"O Allah! Guide Daws and bring them forward!"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:423).
- **Arrival at Khaybar**: During [[Ghazwat Khaybar]] in 7 AH, al-Ṭufayl arrived in Madīnah leading seventy to eighty families of Daws who had embraced Islam, including [[Abū Hurayrah]] (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:423).
- **Demolition of Dhū al-Kaffayn**: After [[Fatḥ Makkah]], the Prophet ﷺ dispatched al-Ṭufayl to burn the wooden idol of his clan, **Dhū al-Kaffayn** (belonging to ʿAmr b. Ḥumamah). He set it ablaze while reciting:
  > «يا ذا الكفّين لست من عبّادكا ... ميلادنا أكبر من ميلادكا ... إنّي حشوت النّار في فؤادكا»
  > *"O Dhū al-Kaffayn, I am not of your worshippers! Our origin is older than your inception; verily I have stuffed fire into your very heart!"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:423).
- **Vision and Martyrdom at al-Yamāmah**: During [[Ḥurūb al-Riddah]] in the caliphate of [[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]], al-Ṭufayl dreamed his head was shaved, a bird flew from his mouth, a woman concealed him in her womb, and his son ʿAmr sought him in vain. He correctly interpreted this as his imminent martyrdom, his spirit soaring, the earth engulfing him, and his son surviving to attain martyrdom later. He fought with utmost valor and was martyred at the Battle of al-Yamāmah (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:423–424).
