# Ḥajjat al-Wadāʿ (حجة الوداع وخطبة البلاغ)
*The Farewell Pilgrimage — The Universal Manifesto of Human Dignity and the Completion of Islam*

## 1. Overview
In Dhū al-Ḥijjah 10 AH, the Prophet [[Muḥammad b. ʻAbdullah|Muḥammad]] ﷺ performed his sole pilgrimage in Islam accompanied by more than 100,000 Companions, demonstrating practical Hajj rituals (*«خُذُوا عَنِّي مَنَاسِكَكُمْ»*).

## 2. The Farewell Sermon (*Khuṭbat al-Wadāʿ*) on ʿArafāt
Standing upon his camel *al-Qaṣwāʾ* in the valley of ʿUranah on the 9th of Dhū al-Ḥijjah, the Prophet ﷺ proclaimed the charter of universal human rights:
- **Sanctity of Life and Property**: *"Indeed, your blood, your property, and your honor are inviolable until you meet your Lord, just as this day of yours, in this month of yours, in this land of yours is sacred!"*
- **Eradication of Racism**: *"O people! Your Lord is One, and your father is one. An Arab holds no superiority over a non-Arab, nor a non-Arab over an Arab; nor a white person over a black, nor a black over a white—except through taqwā (God-consciousness)!"*
- **Women's Rights**: *"Fear Allāh concerning women! For you have taken them under the trust of Allāh!"*
- **Abolition of Usury (*Ribā*) and Vendettas**: Every remnant of pre-Islamic blood-feuds and interest was cancelled under his feet (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:450).

At the sermon's climax, Allāh revealed:
$$\text{الْيَوْمَ أَكْمَلْتُ لَكُمْ دِينَكُمْ وَأَتْمَمْتُ عَلَيْكُمْ نِعْمَتِي وَرَضِيتُ لَكُمُ الْإِسْلَامَ دِينًا}$$
> *"This day I have perfected for you your religion and completed My favor upon you and have approved for you Islam as your religion."* (Qurʾān 5:3).
