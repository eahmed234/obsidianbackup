# Ghazwat Banī Qurayẓah (غزوة بني قريظة)
*The Siege and Trial of Banū Qurayẓah — Divine Justice, the Verdict of Saʿd b. Muʿādh, and the Liberation of Medina*

> [!NOTE] Scholarly Provenance & Primary Source
> This master entry is derived strictly from ***[[Subul al-Hudā war-Rashād|Subul al-Hudā war-Rashād fī Sīrat Khayr al-ʿIbād]]*** by **[[al-Ṣāliḥī|Imām Shams al-Dīn Muḥammad b. Yūsuf al-Ṣāliḥī al-Shāmī]]** (d. 942 AH), Volume 5, *al-Bāb al-ʿIshrūn fī Ghazwat Banī Qurayẓah* (pp. 3–29). Al-Ṣāliḥī synthesizes the primary accounts of [[Ibn Isḥāq]], [[Mūsā b. ʿUqbah]], [[al-Wāqidī]], [[Ibn Saʿd]], [[al-Balādhurī]], [[al-Bukhārī]], and [[Muslim]].

---

## 1. Context & The Celestial Order at Midday
In Dhū al-Qaʿdah 5 AH, following the retreat of the Confederates from **[[Ghazwat al-Khandaq]]**, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ returned to Medina, laid aside his armor, and proceeded to wash himself at the house of Umm Salamah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:3).

At noon, Jibrīl ﵊ appeared riding a mule draped with brocade, covered in battle-dust, and asked:
> «أَوَقَدْ وَضَعْتَ السِّلَاحَ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ؟ فَإِنَّ الْمَلَائِكَةَ لَمْ تَضَعِ السِّلَاحَ بَعْدُ! إِنَّ اللَّهَ تَعَالَى يَأْمُرُكَ بِالْمَسِيرِ إِلَى بَنِي قُرَيْظَةَ، فَإِنِّي عَامِدٌ إِلَيْهِمْ فَمُزَلْزِلٌ بِهِمْ!»
> *"Have you laid down weapons, O Messenger of Allāh? For indeed, the angels have not laid down their weapons! Allāh commands you to march against Banū Qurayẓah, and I am heading toward them to shake their strongholds!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:3).

The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ immediately instructed the public herald to proclaim across Medina:
> «مَنْ كَانَ سَامِعًا مُطِيعًا فَلَا يُصَلِّيَنَّ الْعَصْرَ إِلَّا فِي بَنِي قُرَيْظَةَ!»
> *"Whoever hears and obeys, let him not pray ʿAṣr except at Banū Qurayẓah!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:4).

When ʿAṣr approached on the road, the Companions differed in legal interpretation: some prayed on the road reasoning that the command intended urgency rather than missing the prayer's time, while others delayed it until reaching Qurayẓah after nightfall. When recounted to the Prophet ﷺ, he censured neither group, establishing a fundamental principle of Islamic jurisprudence (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:4).

---

## 2. The Advance Guard & Encampment
- The Prophet ﷺ appointed **[[ʿAbdullāh b. Umm Maktūm]]** ﵁ over Medina (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:5).
- He handed the grand white battle standard to **[[ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib]]** ﵁, who galloped forward with the vanguard (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:5).
- As ʿAlī approached their fortresses, the Jewish garrison stood atop their battlements hurling vile abuse against the Prophet ﷺ and his wives (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:5). When the Prophet ﷺ arrived riding his donkey with three thousand men, he halted at the well of **Anā** (*Biʾr Anā*) and called out to their chieftains:
  > «يَا إِخْوَةَ الْقِرَدَةِ وَالْخَنَازِيرِ وَعَبَدَةَ الطَّاغُوتِ! أَخَزَاكُمُ اللَّهُ وَأَنْزَلَ بِكُمْ نِقْمَتَهُ؟»
  > *"O brothers of apes and swine and servants of tyrants! Has Allāh disgraced you and brought down His vengeance upon you?"*
  They lowered their voices and replied: *"O Abū al-Qāsim, you were never insolent!"* (*mā kunta faḥḥāshan*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:5).

---

## 3. The 25-Day Siege & Internal Despair
The Muslim host entrenched themselves around the perimeter, blockading the strongholds for **twenty-five nights** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:5).
Inside the fortresses, despair mounted under the icy cold and siege. The paramount chieftain Kaʿb b. Asad presented his people with three options (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:6):
1. **Embrace Islam**: Follow Muḥammad ﷺ, for by Allāh they knew he was the Messenger foretold in their scriptures, thereby preserving their lives, properties, and families. The clan vehemently refused, declaring: *"We shall never abandon the Torah of Moses!"*
2. **Slay Their Women & Children**: Kill their families with their own hands so they could charge out with drawn swords free of encumbrance, either conquering or dying to the last man. They answered: *"How can we slay these innocents? What value is life after them?"*
3. **A Surprise Attack on the Sabbath**: Attack the Muslims on Saturday when they would not suspect offensive operations. They responded: *"We shall never desecrate the Sabbath!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:6–7).

---

## 4. The Mission and Repentance of Abū Lubābah
Realizing their doom, Banū Qurayẓah requested that the Prophet ﷺ send their ancient Ausite ally **[[Abū Lubābah b. ʿAbd al-Mundhir]]** ﵁ to advise them (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:8).
- When Abū Lubābah entered their fortress, women and children rushed around him weeping uncontrollably. Their men asked: *"O Abū Lubābah, shall we surrender to Muḥammad's judgement?"*
- Abū Lubābah replied: *"Yes,"* but pointed with his hand across his throat, indicating that unconditional surrender meant execution (*al-dhabḥ*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:8).
- Instantly, Abū Lubābah realized he had betrayed Allāh and His Messenger ﷺ (*khāna Allāha wa Rasūlah*). Stricken with guilt, he bypassed the Prophet's camp, went straight to the Prophet's Mosque in Medina, and bound himself with chains to a pillar (*Usṭuwānat al-Tawbah*), swearing never to eat, drink, or be untied until Allāh forgave him or he died (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:8).
- He remained tied for multiple days until Allāh revealed his forgiveness in **[[Sūrat al-Tawbah]]** (9:102). When the Prophet ﷺ came smiling to untie him, Abū Lubābah insisted that only the blessed hands of the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ should release his bonds (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:8–9).

---

## 5. The Three Youths of Banū Hadl
On the final night of the siege, three righteous youths of Banū Hadl residing with Qurayẓah—**Thaʿlabah b. Saʿyah**, **Usayd b. Saʿyah**, and **Asad b. ʿUbayd**—addressed their companions (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:8):
> *"O Jews! By Allāh, you know full well that he is the Prophet described to us by Ibn al-Hayyabān! Fear Allāh and follow him!"*
When the tribe refused, the three youths climbed down the walls in the dead of night, surrendered to the Prophet ﷺ, embraced Islam, and preserved their lives and wealth (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:206, 232; *Subul al-Hudā*, 5:8). Another man, ʿAmr b. Suʿdā, who had vehemently refused to participate in the betrayal of the Prophet ﷺ, walked out through the Muslim lines undisturbed, vanishing into the desert (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:8).

---

## 6. Unconditional Surrender & Appointment of Saʿd b. Muʿādh
In the morning, Banū Qurayẓah laid down their arms and surrendered to the judgement of the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:9).
- The men of the Aws tribe gathered around the Prophet ﷺ pleading: *"O Messenger of Allāh, they were our confederates (*ḥulafāʾ*), just as Banū Qaynuqāʿ were the confederates of the Khazraj, and you showed mercy to Qaynuqāʿ for Ibn Ubayy!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:9).
- The Prophet ﷺ looked at them and asked:
  > «أَلَا تَرْضَوْنَ يَا مَعْشَرَ الْأَوْسِ أَنْ يَحْكُمَ فِيهِمْ رَجُلٌ مِنْكُمْ؟»
  > *"Will you be satisfied, O assembly of Aws, if a man from among yourselves judges over them?"*
  They said: *"Certainly!"* He said: *«That belongs to Saʿd b. Muʿādh.»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:9).

---

## 7. The Judgement of Saʿd b. Muʿādh
**[[Saʿd b. Muʿādh]]** ﵁ lay wounded in a medical tent in the Prophet's Mosque, having been struck in the medial arm vein (*al-akḥal*) by an arrow at the Trench.
- He was mounted upon a donkey cushioned with leather and brought to Qurayẓah. As he approached, his Ausite kinsmen crowded around him urging clemency for their old allies (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:10).
- Saʿd declared: *"The time has come for Saʿd to care nothing for the blame of any critic in the service of Allāh!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:10).
- As Saʿd drew near, the Prophet ﷺ commanded the believers:
  > «قُومُوا إِلَى سَيِّدِكُمْ!»
  > *"Stand up for your leader!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:10).
- Saʿd asked: *"Do you accept my verdict upon them?"* The Muslims affirmed: *"Yes!"* He gestured toward the tent of the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ with lowered eyes: *"And upon the one who is here?"* The Prophet ﷺ replied: *«Yes.»*
- Saʿd b. Muʿādh then pronounced the historic sentence:
  > «إِنِّي أَحْكُمُ فِيهِمْ أَنْ تُقْتَلَ الْمُقَاتِلَةُ، وَتُسْبَى الذُّرِّيَّةُ وَالنِّسَاءُ، وَتُقْسَمَ الْأَمْوَالُ!»
  > *"I judge that the fighting men shall be executed, the women and children taken as captives, and their properties distributed!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:10).
- Hearing the verdict, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ exclaimed:
  > «لَقَدْ حَكَمْتَ فِيهِمْ بِحُكْمِ اللَّهِ مِنْ فَوْقِ سَبْعَةِ أَرْقِعَةٍ!»
  > *"You have judged concerning them with the judgement of Allāh from above seven heavens!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:10).

---

## 8. Execution of the Sentence & Fate of Antagonists
The captives were brought into Medina and detained in the quarters of Banū al-Najjār (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:11).
- Trenches were excavated in the marketplace of Medina. The combatants—between 600 and 750 men—were brought out in small groups and executed (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:11).
- **Kaʿb b. Asad**: As he was led to the trench, he was asked: *"What do you think is being done with you?"* He said: *"Do you not understand? The caller never pauses, and whoever goes forth does not return; by Allāh, it is death!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:11).
- **Ḥuyayy b. Akhṭab**: Captured in the fortresses, having bound himself to share Qurayẓah's fate after inciting them at the Trench. When brought before the Prophet ﷺ with his hands bound behind his neck, wearing a fine rose-colored robe torn to prevent looting, he sneered: *"By Allāh, I do not blame myself for opposing you, but whoever forsakes Allāh will be forsaken!"* Turning to the people, he said: *"O people! There is no harm in Allāh's decree; it is a destiny and slaughter ordained upon the Children of Israel!"* He sat by the trench, and ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib struck off his head (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:12).
- **The Woman Nubātah**: Only a single woman was executed: Nubātah, who had cast the millstone that martyred **[[Khallād b. Suwayd]]** ﵁ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:14).
- **Intercession for al-Zubayr b. Bāṭā**: The companion **[[Thābit b. Qays]] b. Shammās** ﵁ petitioned the Prophet ﷺ to spare the elder al-Zubayr b. Bāṭā, who had saved Thābit's life at the Battle of Buʿāth. The Prophet ﷺ granted him his life, his family, and his property. But when al-Zubayr learned that Kaʿb b. Asad and Ḥuyayy had been slain, he declared: *"There is no good in life after them! By my hand upon you, O Thābit, join me to my companions!"* He was executed at his own insistence (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:14–15).

---

## 9. Distribution of Spoils & Casualties
- **Casualties**:
  - Two Muslims were lost: **[[Khallād b. Suwayd]]** ﵁ (martyred by the millstone, granted the reward of two martyrs) and **[[Abū Sinān b. Miḥṣan]]** ﵁ (elder brother of ʿUkkāshah, who passed away during the siege) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:14–15).
- **Division of Spoils**:
  - The army numbered 3,000 men with 36 cavalry.
  - For the first time, cavalrymen were awarded **three shares** (two shares for the horse and one for the rider), while infantrymen received **one share** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:15).
  - The Prophet ﷺ allotted posthumous shares to Khallād b. Suwayd and Abū Sinān b. Miḥṣan (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:15).
  - From the captive women, the Prophet ﷺ chose **Rayḥānah bint Zayd**, who accepted Islam and whom he freed and married (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:15).

## 10. Scriptural Exegesis: Sūrat al-Aḥzāb (33:26–27)
Allāh commemorated the triumph over Banū Qurayẓah directly following the commentary on the Trench in **[[Sūrat al-Aḥzāb]]**:

| Ayah | Arabic Excerpt | Occasion & Strategic Meaning | Key Figures |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **33:26** | وَأَنزَلَ الَّذِينَ ظَاهَرُوهُم مِّنْ أَهْلِ الْكِتَابِ مِن صَيَاصِيهِمْ | The descent of Banū Qurayẓah from their fortified towers (*ṣayāṣī*) after aiding the Confederates. | Kaʿb b. Asad, Banū Qurayẓah |
| **33:26** | وَقَذَفَ فِي قُلُوبِهِمُ الرُّعْبَ فَرِيقًا تَقْتُلُونَ وَتَأْسِرُونَ فَرِيقًا | Divine terror cast into their hearts, resulting in the execution of combatants and capture of non-combatants. | Saʿd b. Muʿādh, Ḥuyayy b. Akhṭab |
| **33:27** | وَأَوْرَثَكُمْ أَرْضَهُمْ وَدِيَارَهُمْ وَأَمْوَالَهُمْ وَأَرْضًا لَّمْ تَطَئُوهَا | Bestowal of their lands, strongholds, and estates to the Muslims, prefiguring the conquest of Khaybar. | Muḥammad ﷺ, al-Muslimūn |
