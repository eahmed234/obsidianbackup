# al-Ḥudaybiyah (الحديبية)
*The Plain of the Sacred Covenant and the Manifest Victory*

## 1. Overview
A desert plain situated at the boundary between the sacred sanctuary (*al-Ḥill wal-Ḥaram*). Witnessed the miraculous overflowing of its dried well when the Prophet ﷺ dropped his blessed saliva into it, the swearing of **[[Bayʿat al-Riḍwān]]** under the acacia tree, and the signing of **[[Ṣulḥ al-Ḥudaybiyah]]**.
