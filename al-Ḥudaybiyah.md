# al-Ḥudaybiyah (الحديبية)
*The Plain of the Sacred Covenant and the Manifest Victory*

## 1. Topography & Halting of al-Qaṣwāʾ
Al-Ḥudaybiyah is an open desert plain situated approximately twenty-two kilometers west of Makkah along the coastal road toward Jeddah, straddling the borderline between the profane perimeter (*al-Ḥill*) and the sacred precinct (*al-Ḥaram*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:40).

In Dhū al-Qaʿdah 6 AH, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ marched from Medina with approximately 1,400 to 1,500 companions garbed in pilgrim ihram, intending only the lesser pilgrimage (*ʿUmrah*). As they approached the border pass of al-Ḥudaybiyah, his she-camel **al-Qaṣwāʾ** knelt down and refused to advance. When the men cried that she had grown stubborn (*khalaʾat*), the Prophet ﷺ corrected them:
> «مَا خَلَأَتِ الْقَصْوَاءُ وَمَا ذَاكَ لَهَا بِعَادَةٍ، وَلَكِنْ حَبَسَهَا حَابِسُ الْفِيلِ... وَالَّذِي نَفْسِي بِيَدِهِ لَا يَسْأَلُونِي الْيَوْمَ خُطَّةً يُعَظِّمُونَ فِيهَا حُرُمَاتِ اللَّهِ إِلَّا أَعْطَيْتُهُمْ إِيَّاهَا»
> *"Al-Qaṣwāʾ has not grown stubborn, nor is that in her nature; rather, she has been restrained by the Same Hand that restrained the Elephant from Makkah... By Him in Whose Hand is my soul, they shall not ask me today for any proposal wherein they venerate the sacred precincts of Allāh except that I shall grant it to them"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:40).

He then diverted his course and encamped at the farthest rim of al-Ḥudaybiyah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:40).

## 2. The Miracle of the Dry Well
The encampment possessed only a single shallow pit-well containing virtually no water (*thamad qalīl al-māʾ*), quickly drained by the thirst of the massive encampment in the scorching heat (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:40–41). When the companions complained of thirst:
1. The Prophet ﷺ drew an arrow from his quiver and handed it to Nājiyah b. al-Aʿjam ﵁.
2. He called for a single bucket of water, performed ablution, rinsed his mouth, and spat back into the bucket.
3. He commanded Nājiyah to descend into the dry well, pour the blessed water into the pit, and stir the muddy bottom with the arrow (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:41).
Instantly, the well surged and bubbled like a boiling kettle (*fārat kamā tafūru al-qadr*), rising until it reached the stone rim, allowing all 1,400 companions and their riding mounts to drink to absolute repletion (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:41).

## 3. Bayʿat al-Riḍwān and The Treaty of Peace
Al-Ḥudaybiyah served as the stage for two milestone turning points in Sīrah history:
- **[[Bayʿat al-Riḍwān]]**: Following rumors that the Prophet's envoy **[[ʿUthmān b. ʿAffān]]** ﵁ had been assassinated in Makkah, the Prophet ﷺ assembled the companions beneath an acacia tree (*samurah*) to pledge their lives unto death, a covenant immortalized by Allāh: ﴿لَّقَدْ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنِ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ إِذْ يُبَايِعُونَكَ تَحْتَ الشَّجَرَةِ﴾ (Sūrat al-Fatḥ 48:18; *Subul al-Hudā*, 5:49–50).
- **[[Ṣulḥ al-Ḥudaybiyah]]**: The historic ten-year non-aggression pact negotiated with Suhayl b. ʿAmr on behalf of Quraysh, which dismantled military hostilities, enabled the rapid propagation of Islam across the Arabian peninsula, and was declared by the Qurʾān as the *Manifest Victory* (*al-Fatḥ al-Mubīn*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:54–65; Sūrat al-Fatḥ 48:1).
