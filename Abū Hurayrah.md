# Abū Hurayrah (أبو هريرة الدوسي) ﵁
*The Foremost Preserver of Prophetic Traditions and Devoted Companion of the Suffah*

## 1. Lineage & Conversion (7 AH)
Hailing from the tribe of Daws in southern Arabia, he journeyed to Medina in Muḥarram 7 AH alongside eighty households of his people (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:136). Upon reaching Medina, they prayed the dawn prayer behind **Sibāʿ b. ʿUrfuṭah al-Ghifārī**, whom the Prophet ﷺ had appointed as his deputy over Medina during the expedition of Khaybar (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:115, 136). Hearing that the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ was at Khaybar, he declared: *"I shall not hear of him being in any place without coming to him!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:137).

## 2. Arrival at Khaybar
Reaching **[[Khaybar]]** while the Prophet ﷺ was besieging the fortresses of al-Katībah (or immediately following their surrender), the Prophet ﷺ consulted the army and allotted Abū Hurayrah and his companions a share of the expedition’s spoils (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:137, 143).

## 3. Prophetic Supplications & Devotion to Knowledge
- **Supplication for Unforgotten Knowledge**: In the Prophetic Mosque, while seated alongside **[[Zayd b. Thābit]]** ﵁ and another companion making supplication, the Prophet ﷺ entered and said *"Āmīn"* to their prayers. Abū Hurayrah supplicated: *"O Allāh, I ask You for the like of what my two companions asked, and I ask You for knowledge that is never forgotten!"* The Prophet ﷺ responded *"Āmīn"*. When the others sought the same, the Prophet ﷺ remarked:
  > «سَبَقَكُمَا الدَّوْسِيُّ»
  > *"The Dawsī has preceded you both!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 10:206).
- **Supplication for Mutual Love**: When his mother accepted Islam after the Prophet ﷺ supplicated for her guidance, Abū Hurayrah asked the Prophet ﷺ to supplicate that the believers love them:
  > «اللَّهُمَّ حَبِّبْ عَبْدَكَ هَذَا، وَأُمَّهُ إِلَى عِبَادِكَ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ، وَحَبِّبْهُمْ إِلَيْهِمَا»
  > *"O Allāh, endear this servant of Yours and his mother to Your believing servants, and endear them to both of them!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 10:206).
