# Abū Hurayrah (أبو هريرة الدوسي) ﵁
*The Foremost Preserver of Prophetic Traditions and Devoted Companion of the Suffah*

## 1. Lineage, Name & Kunyah
- **Tribal Lineage**: Hailing from the Yemeni tribe of [[Daws]], his lineage is recorded by Ibn al-Kalbī as Abū Hurayrah b. ʿĀmir b. ʿAbd Dhī al-Sharā b. Ṭarīf b. ʿIttāb b. Abī Ṣaʿb b. Munabbih b. Saʿd b. Thaʿlabah b. Sulaym b. Fahm b. Ghanm b. Daws b. ʿAdnān b. ʿAbdillāh b. Zahrān b. Kaʿb al-Dawsī (*al-Iṣābah*, 7:348).
- **Personal Name**: Classical authorities differed extensively over his personal name across dozens of variant transmissions; the most famous and widely relied upon view is that he was named **ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. Ṣakhr** in Islam, having previously been known as ʿAbd Shams or ʿAbd ʿAmr in the Jāhiliyyah (*al-Iṣābah*, 7:349–352).
- **Origin of the Kunyah**: He recounted that during the pre-Islamic days he grazed the livestock of his people and found a wild kitten (*hurayrah*); he would keep it safely in a tree by night and carry it in his sleeve by day, playing with it, wherefore his folk nicknamed him *Abū Hurayrah* (*al-Iṣābah*, 7:349). The Prophet ﷺ affectionately addressed him also as *Abū Hirr* (*al-Iṣābah*, 7:349).
- **Marital Network**: He married **Bisrah bint Ghazwān** (sister of the prominent companion ʿUtbah b. Ghazwān), for whom he had formerly laboured as a hired hand for food and provisions prior to the expansion of Islam (*al-Iṣābah*, 7:360).

## 2. Conversion & Arrival at Khaybar (7 AH)
- **Emigration with Daws**: In Muḥarram of 7 AH, alongside eighty households of his tribe of Daws, he arrived in Medina while the Prophet ﷺ was dispatching the campaign against Khaybar (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:136).
- **Prayer behind Sibāʿ**: Upon entering Medina at dawn, they prayed behind **Sibāʿ b. ʿUrfuṭah al-Ghifārī**, who had been appointed deputy over Medina (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:115, 136).
- **Joining at Khaybar**: Resolved never to stay behind, he marched forth immediately to **[[Khaybar]]**, declaring: *"I shall not hear of him being in any place without coming to him!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:137). He joined the army during the siege of al-Katībah, and the Prophet ﷺ consulted the Muslims and awarded them an equal share of the spoils (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:137, 143; *al-Iṣābah*, 7:354).

## 3. Companionship, Knowledge & Prophetic Supplications
- **Inseparable Companionship**: From the day of Khaybar until the Prophet’s ﷺ passing, Abū Hurayrah resided among the impoverished ascetics of the Suffah (*Aṣḥāb al-Ṣuffah*), clinging to the Prophet’s ﷺ company continuously upon contentment with bare sustenance (*ʿalā shibaʿ baṭnih*), accompanying him wherever he turned (*al-Iṣābah*, 7:354–355).
- **Supplication for Unforgotten Knowledge**: In the Prophet's Mosque, seated alongside **[[Zayd b. Thābit]]** ﵁ and another companion making supplication, the Prophet ﷺ entered and joined them, saying *"Āmīn"* to their prayers. When Abū Hurayrah petitioned:
  > «اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ مِثْلَ مَا سَأَلَكَ صَاحِبَايَ هَذَانِ، وَأَسْأَلُكَ عِلْمًا لَا يُنْسَى»
  > *"O Allāh, I ask You for the like of what my two companions have asked, and I ask You for knowledge that is never forgotten!"*
  The Prophet ﷺ said *"Āmīn"*. When his two companions sought to replicate the petition, the Prophet ﷺ informed them:
  > «سَبَقَكُمَا الدَّوْسِيُّ»
  > *"The Dawsī has preceded you both!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 10:206; *al-Iṣābah*, 7:354).
- **Supplication for His Mother**: His mother initially rejected Islam. When Abū Hurayrah sought Prophetic intercession, the Prophet ﷺ prayed for her heart, and she promptly accepted Islam. The Prophet ﷺ then supplicated:
  > «اللَّهُمَّ حَبِّبْ عَبْدَكَ هَذَا، وَأُمَّهُ إِلَى عِبَادِكَ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ، وَحَبِّبْهُمْ إِلَيْهِمَا»
  > *"O Allāh, endear this servant of Yours and his mother to Your believing servants, and endear them to both of them!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 10:206; *al-Iṣābah*, 7:355).
- **Devotion & Piety**: Abū ʿUthmān al-Nahdī related that Abū Hurayrah, his wife, and his servant divided the night into thirds, one taking over prayer as another rested. He completed twelve thousand tasbīḥ daily (*al-Iṣābah*, 7:360).

## 4. Later Life & Passing (57–59 AH)
- **Funeral Prayers**: In Medina, Abū Hurayrah led the funeral prayer over the Mother of the Believers **[[ʿĀʾishah bint Abī Bakr]]** ﵂ in Ramaḍān of 58 AH (*al-Iṣābah*, 7:362).
- **Death & Burial**: He passed away at his residence in al-ʿAqīq at the age of seventy-eight and was borne into Medina to be interred in **[[al-Baqīʿ]]** (*al-Iṣābah*, 7:362). The foremost transmitters, including Hishām b. ʿUrwah and Khalīfah b. Khayyāṭ, date his death to 57 AH, while others record 58 AH or 59 AH (*al-Iṣābah*, 7:362).
