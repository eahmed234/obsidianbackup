# Ghazwat Banī al-Muṣṭaliq (غزوة بني المصطلق)
*The Expedition of al-Muraysīʿ — The Unmasking of the Hypocrites and the Enfranchisement of Khuzāʿah*

## 1. Strategic Context & Intelligence
In Shaʿbān 5 AH (or 6 AH), reliable military intelligence reached Medina that **al-Ḥārith b. Abī Ḍirār**, the paramount chieftain of **Banū al-Muṣṭaliq** (a powerful branch of **[[Banū Khuzāʿah]]** dwelling near the Red Sea coast at al-Muraysīʿ), was actively mobilizing his warriors and buying warhorses to mount a preemptive assault against Medina (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:344).

To verify the reports, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ dispatched **Buraydah b. al-Ḥuṣayb al-Aslamī** ﵁ on a covert reconnaissance mission (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:344). Buraydah infiltrated the enemy camp, met directly with al-Ḥārith b. Abī Ḍirār pretending to be an Arab warrior seeking to join the anti-Muslim coalition, confirmed their war mobilization, and returned swiftly to Medina with detailed operational intelligence (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:344).

## 2. Mobilization & Surprise Advance
The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ immediately ordered rapid mobilization, marching out with his Companions (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:344). He appointed [[Zayd b. Ḥārithah]] ﵁ (or [[Abū Dharr al-Ghifārī]] or Numaylah b. ʿAbdillāh) as deputy governor over Medina (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:344).

A substantial contingent of hypocrites joined the expedition under **[[ʿAbdullāh b. Ubayy b. Salūl]]** ☠, seeking easy plunder after hearing of the vast herds possessed by Banū al-Muṣṭaliq (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:344).

The Prophet ﷺ marched with supreme speed along the coastal route, completely taking Banū al-Muṣṭaliq by surprise while their livestock were drinking at their historic watering place, **al-Muraysīʿ**, in the territory of Qudayd (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:344). 

The Muslim battle standards were entrusted to:
- **Muhājirūn**: **[[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]]** ﵁ (or [[ʿAmmār b. Yāsir]]) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:344).
- **Anṣār**: **[[Saʿd b. ʿUbādah]]** ﵁ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:344).

## 3. The Engagement at the Water of al-Muraysīʿ
The Prophet ﷺ offered the tribe the invitation to Islam. Confident in their position and archery, Banū al-Muṣṭaliq refused and opened fire with a volley of arrows (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:345).

The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ ordered his Companions to charge in unison. In a single decisive mounted assault, the Muslim lines crashed through the enemy positions, killing ten polytheists, routing their ranks, and capturing the remainder of the tribe along with their women, children, and herds (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:345). 

The spoils impounded were immense: two thousand camels, five thousand sheep, and hundreds of captives (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:345). Only one Muslim fell on the field: **Hishām b. Ṣubābah** ﵁, who was mistakenly struck down in the confusion of battle by an Anṣārī warrior who mistook him for an enemy combatant (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:345).

## 4. Marriage to Juwayriyah & Mass Emancipation
Among the captives brought to the camp was the daughter of the supreme chieftain al-Ḥārith b. Abī Ḍirār, **Barrah bint al-Ḥārith** (*al-Iṣābah*, 8:72). She fell into the lot of **[[Thābit b. Qays b. Shammās]]** ﵁, with whom she drafted a contract of emancipation (*kitābah*) (*al-Iṣābah*, 8:72; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:346).

She approached the Prophet ﷺ seeking financial assistance to purchase her freedom. The Prophet ﷺ offered to pay off her entire contract and take her in honorable marriage (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:346). She accepted with joy, and the Prophet ﷺ renamed her **[[Juwayriyah bint al-Ḥārith|Juwayriyah]]**, elevating her to the rank of Mother of the Believers (*al-Iṣābah*, 8:72).

When the Muslim army learned of the marriage, a wave of profound chivalry swept the camp:
> «أَصْهَارُ رَسُولِ اللهِ ﷺ فِي أَيْدِينَا؟!»
> *"Shall the in-laws of the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ remain captives in our hands?!"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 8:73; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:347).

Every Muslim soldier unconditionally liberated his captives. Over **one hundred households** of Banū al-Muṣṭaliq were instantly emancipated without ransom (*al-Iṣābah*, 8:73; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:347). 

Umm al-Muʾminīn **[[ʿĀʾishah bint Abī Bakr]]** ﵂ remarked:
> «فَمَا رَأَيْتُ امْرَأَةً كَانَتْ أَعْظَمَ بَرَكَةً عَلَى قَوْمِهَا مِنْهَا!»
> *"Never have I seen a woman who brought greater blessing upon her people than Juwayriyah!"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 8:73; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:347).

Touched by this moral elevation, al-Ḥārith b. Abī Ḍirār and the entire tribe entered Islam in sincerity (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:347).

## 5. The Scuffle at the Well & Tribal Sedition
While the army was encamped around the well of al-Muraysīʿ, **[[Jahjāh b. Saʿīd al-Ghifārī]]** ﵁ (a retainer hired by [[ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb]]) and **Sinān b. Wabar al-Juhanī** (an ally of Banū Sālim b. ʿAwf of the Khazraj) crowded each other at the water's edge (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:621; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:348). A physical fight erupted, and Jahjāh struck Sinān (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:348).

Sinān shouted: *«Yā lal-Anṣār!»* (O company of the Anṣār!), and Jahjāh shouted: *«Yā lal-Muhājirīn!»* (O company of the Muhājirūn!) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:348). Weapons were drawn and tribesmen rushed to support their kin.

The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ hurried to the scene and rebuked the gathering with fiery prophetic condemnation:
> «أَبِدَعْوَى الْجَاهِلِيَّةِ وَأَنَا بَيْنَ أَظْهُرِكُمْ؟! دَعُوهَا فَإِنَّهَا مُنْتِنَةٌ!»
> *"Is it with the calls of the Jāhiliyyah while I am yet among you?! Cast it aside, for it is foul and putrid!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:348).

The believers instantly laid down their weapons and embraced in brotherhood (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:348).

## 6. The Treason of Ibn Ubayy & Sūrat al-Munāfiqūn
Witnessing the rapid reconciliation of the Muhājirūn and Anṣār, the chief of the hypocrites, **[[ʿAbdullāh b. Ubayy b. Salūl]]** ☠, erupted in treasonous fury among his partisans:
> «سَمِّنْ كَلْبَكَ يَأْكُلْكَ! أَمَا وَاللهِ لَئِنْ رَجَعْنَا إِلَى الْمَدِينَةِ لَيُخْرِجَنَّ الْأَعَزُّ مِنْهَا الْأَذَلَّ!»
> *"Fatten your dog and it will devour you! By Allāh, if we return to Medina, the mightier will surely expel the lowlier from it!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:348).

He commanded his faction:
> «لَا تُنْفِقُوا عَلَى مَنْ عِنْدَ رَسُولِ اللهِ حَتَّى يَنْفَضُّوا مِنْ حَوْلِهِ!»
> *"Do not spend upon those who are with the Messenger of Allāh until they disperse from around him!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:348).

The young Anṣārī companion **[[Zayd b. Arqam]]** ﵁ was seated nearby, heard the words, and reported them to the Prophet ﷺ (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:488; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:348). When confronted, Ibn Ubayy swore false oaths by Allāh denying the charge, and people blamed Zayd for hasty mishearing (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:348).

While the army was on the march, Allāh sent down **[[Sūrat al-Munāfiqūn]] (63:1–8)**, verbatim quoting Ibn Ubayy's words and branding him a liar (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:354). The Prophet ﷺ took Zayd's ear with tenderness, twisted it gently, and declared:
> «وَفَتْ أُذُنُكَ يَا غُلَامُ، وَصَدَّقَكَ اللهُ!»
> *"Your ear has proved faithful and true, O youth, and Allāh has vindicated you!"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:488; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:354).

## 7. The Forced March & The Loyalty of Ibn Ubayy's Son
To neutralize the toxic friction and prevent gossiping factions from festering in camp, the Prophet ﷺ ordered an immediate, non-stop forced march at an unusual midday hour in blazing heat (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:349). 

The army marched without pause through the rest of the day, through the entire night, and through the following morning until the sun reached its zenith. When the Prophet ﷺ finally halted, the men collapsed onto the sand and fell into a deep, oblivious sleep, entirely purging the dispute from their minds (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:349).

### The Stance of ʿAbdullāh b. ʿAbdillāh b. Ubayy
When the army reached the outskirts of Medina, Ibn Ubayy's own son, the righteous Badri companion **[[ʿAbdullāh b. ʿAbdillāh b. Ubayy]]** ﵁, drew his sword and blocked his father at the gates of the city (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:355). 

He proclaimed to his father:
> «وَاللهِ لَا تَدْخُلُهَا حَتَّى يَأْذَنَ لَكَ رَسُولُ اللهِ ﷺ، فَتَعْلَمَ أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللهِ هُوَ الْأَعَزُّ وَأَنَّكَ أَنْتَ الْأَذَلُّ!»
> *"By Allāh, you shall never enter Medina until the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ permits you, that you may know with certainty that the Messenger of Allāh is the truly mighty and that you are the lowliest of the low!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:355).

Ibn Ubayy was forced to stand humiliated in the dust outside Medina until the Prophet ﷺ rode up and granted him permission to enter (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:355).

## 9. Strategic Outcomes
1. **Neutralization of the Northern Tihāmah Coast**: The entire tribe of Banū al-Muṣṭaliq entered Islam and became dedicated allies of the Madinan state (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:347).
2. **Definitive Exposure of the Munāfiqūn**: The internal fifth column in Medina was legally and socially unmasked by the revelation of Sūrat al-Munāfiqūn, permanently stripping Ibn Ubayy of any moral authority (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:354).
3. **Consolidation of the Ummah**: The prompt eradication of tribal chauvinism cemented the Islamic fraternity between Muhājirūn and Anṣār, demonstrating that the bond of faith reigned supreme over blood ties (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:348).
