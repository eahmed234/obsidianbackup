# Abū Dharr al-Ghifārī (أبو ذر الغفاري جندب بن جنادة) ﵁
*The Fearless Proclaimer of Monotheism and Exemplar of Prophetic Asceticism*

## 1. Lineage & Early Conversion
He is Jundub b. Junādah al-Ghifārī (kunya: Abū Dharr), hailing from the Bedouin tribe of Banū Ghifār, who controlled the trade route between Mecca and Syria (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:315). Even before Islam, he had discarded the worship of idols and prayed to Allāh alone.

Upon hearing rumors of a Prophet arising in Mecca, he dispatched his brother Unays to investigate, then traveled to Mecca secretly. After meeting **[[ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib]]** and **[[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]]**, he met the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ in **[[Dār al-Arqam]]** and became the fourth or fifth person to enter Islam (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:315). Despite the Prophet's instruction to conceal his faith until returning to his people, Abū Dharr stood at the Kaʿbah and proclaimed the Shahādah openly; the polytheists beat him until **[[al-ʿAbbās b. ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib]]** intervened to save him (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:315–316). He then returned to his clan and converted half of Banū Ghifār to Islam (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:317).

## 2. Key Sīrah Milestones
- **Migration**: He remained preaching among his people until migrating to Medina following the Battle of the Trench, residing among the destitute ascetics of **[[Ahl al-Ṣuffah]]**.
- **Solitary March at [[Ghazwat Tabūk]] (9 AH)**: During the sweltering expedition to Tabūk, his weak camel lagged behind. Abū Dharr placed his gear upon his shoulders and marched across the searing desert on foot to rejoin the army. Seeing a lone silhouette approaching through the heat mirage, the Prophet ﷺ said: *“Be Abū Dharr!”* When recognized, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ proclaimed:
  > «يَرْحَمُ اللَّهُ أَبَا ذَرٍّ: يَمْشِي وَحْدَهُ، وَيَمُوتُ وَحْدَهُ، وَيُبْعَثُ وَحْدَهُ!»
  > *"May Allāh have mercy on Abū Dharr! He walks alone, he will die alone, and he will be resurrected alone!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:452).
- **The Most Truthful Tongue**: The Prophet ﷺ conferred upon him the highest testimony of sincere speech:
  > «مَا أَظَلَّتِ الْخَضْرَاءُ وَلَا أَقَلَّتِ الْغَبْرَاءُ مِنْ ذِي لَهْجَةٍ أَصْدَقَ مِنْ أَبِي ذَرٍّ»
  > *"No greener sky has shaded and no dustier earth has carried one with a tongue more truthful than Abū Dharr!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:452; 10:208).

## 3. Demise & Legacy
In his later years, Abū Dharr withdrew from public affairs to live in ascetic seclusion at al-Rabadhah, east of Medina. He passed away there in 32 AH in solitary simplicity; by divine orchestration, a traveling caravan from Iraq carrying **[[ʿAbdullāh b. Masʿūd]]** ﵁ arrived at that exact hour, wept over him, and buried him, fulfilling the Prophetic prophecy (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:453).
