# Ibn Isḥāq (محمد بن إسحاق بن يسار)
*Muḥammad b. Isḥāq — The Father of the Sīrah Genre*

## 1. Identity
**Muḥammad b. Isḥāq b. Yasār al-Madanī** (d. 150–151 AH), the first scholar to gather the Prophet's ﷺ
life into a single continuous, chronologically ordered work. Every later Sīrah — including
[[Subul al-Hudā war-Rashād]] — is in some measure downstream of him.

His original text does not survive independently. It reaches us chiefly through the recension of his
student's student **[[Ibn Hishām]]**, and in fragments preserved by [[al-Ṭabarī]] and others.

## 2. Standing
Al-Ṣāliḥī cites him constantly, usually at the head of a chain of collectors — *rawā Ibn Isḥāq
wa-Ibn Saʿd…* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:68, 3:370).

> [!NOTE] A Note on His Grading
> Ibn Isḥāq was contested among the ḥadīth critics: Mālik b. Anas criticized him sharply, while
> Shuʿbah and others defended him, and Ibn Ḥajar's considered verdict is broadly favourable for
> narrative *maghāzī* material while cautious for legal ḥadīth. He also transmits poetry and reports
> without always grading them. Al-Ṣāliḥī reports him fully but defers to
> [[al-Bukhārī]], [[Muslim]] and [[Ibn Ḥajar al-ʿAsqalānī]] where they conflict — the same posture
> this vault takes.
