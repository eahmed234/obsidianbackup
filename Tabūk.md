# Tabūk (تبوك)
*The Northern Limit of Prophetic Expeditions*

## 1. Overview
The northern frontier staging ground where the Prophet ﷺ encamped with 30,000 warriors during **[[Ghazwat Tabūk]]** (*Jaysh al-ʿUsrah*). Witnessed the miraculous gushing of its spring when the Prophet ﷺ washed his face and hands in it, providing water for the entire army (*Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim*, no. 706).
