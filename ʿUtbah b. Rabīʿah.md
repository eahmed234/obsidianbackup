# ʿUtbah b. Rabīʿah (عتبة بن ربيعة)
*The Qurashī Chief Who Counselled Against Badr — and Died in It*

## 1. Standing
A senior chief of Banū ʿAbd Shams and one of the most respected men of Quraysh, father of
**[[al-Walīd b. ʿUtbah]]** ☠ and of Hind bint ʿUtbah.

## 2. The Voice Against Battle
Alone among the Meccan leadership he argued to turn back once the caravan was safe, urging Quraysh
not to seek an engagement with their own kin — a counsel **[[Abū Jahl (ʿAmr b. Hishām)]]** ☠ threw
back at him as cowardice, taunting him publicly until he committed to the fight. Al-Ṣāliḥī records
the schism of Quraysh and Abū Jahl's arrogance as a distinct stage of the campaign
(*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:26 ff.).

## 3. The Duel and His Death
When the two armies faced each other, ʿUtbah went out with his brother Shaybah and his son al-Walīd
to call for champions. He was matched against **[[ʿUbaydah b. al-Ḥārith]]** ﵁ — each dealt the other
a mortal blow, and [[Ḥamzah b. ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib]] ﵁ and [[ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib]] ﵁ finished him.

His body was among those cast into **al-Qalīb**, the dry well of [[Badr]].

> [!NOTE] A Note on the Reports
> The sources treat ʿUtbah with a certain restraint: he is an opponent, but one whose judgment was
> sound and who was pressed into the battle against it. His is the case the Sīrah uses to show that
> Badr was Abū Jahl's war before it was Quraysh's.
