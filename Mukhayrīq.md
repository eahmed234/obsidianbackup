# Mukhayrīq (مخيريق) ﵁
*The Foremost of the Jews — Rabbi of Medina, First Endower of Prophetic Waqf, and Martyr of Uḥud*

## 1. Origins, Scholarly Rank & Lineage
- **Background**: Mukhayrīq al-Naḍrī al-Isrāʾīlī. Prominent classical authorities including al-Wāqidī and al-Balādhurī record that he belonged either to **[[Banū al-Naḍīr]]** or was from a remnant clan of **[[Banū Qaynuqāʿ]]** (or Banū al-Quṭayūn) (*al-Iṣābah*, 6:46–47, no. 7867).
- **Rabbinic Erudition**: He was an accomplished Jewish scholar (*ḥabr* / *ʿālim*) of Yathrib possessing comprehensive knowledge of the Torah and the prophetic signs (*al-Iṣābah*, 6:46).

## 2. Decision on the Sabbath & Ghazwat Uḥud (3 AH)
- On Saturday morning, 7 Shawwāl 3 AH, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ marched out to Mount Uḥud to confront the Quraysh polytheists.
- Mukhayrīq addressed his Jewish compatriots in Medina, declaring: *«O company of Jews! By Allāh, you know full well that aiding Muḥammad is an incumbent duty upon you according to our Scriptures!»* (*al-Iṣābah*, 6:47; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:212).
- They objected: *«Today is the Sabbath day (*yawm al-sabt*)!»*
- Mukhayrīq answered emphatically: *«There is no Sabbath for you today!»* (*lā sabta lakum!*) (*al-Iṣābah*, 6:47).

## 3. The Seven Orchards of Prophetic Waqf
- Before departing for the battlefield, Mukhayrīq drew up his last will and testament before witnesses: *«If I am slain today, my properties and wealth belong entirely to Muḥammad ﷺ, to place wherever Allāh directs him»* (*al-Iṣābah*, 6:46–47).
- His vast fortune comprised **seven fertile date-palm orchards** across Medina:
  1. **al-Mīthab** (or al-Mīthar)
  2. **al-Ṣāʾifah**
  3. **al-Dalāl**
  4. **Ḥusnā**
  5. **Barqah**
  6. **al-Aʿwāf** (or al-Maʿwān)
  7. **Mashrabat Umm Ibrāhīm** (*al-Iṣābah*, 6:46–47).
- When Mukhayrīq was slain, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ received these orchards, and they formed the foundational **first charitable religious endowments (*awqāf*) of Islam in Medina**, from which the Prophet ﷺ provided for the poor and travellers (*al-Iṣābah*, 6:46–47; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:212).

## 4. Martyrdom & The Prophetic Eulogy
- Mukhayrīq took his sword and armor, galloped out to Uḥud, and joined the ranks of the Muslim fighters (*al-Iṣābah*, 6:47).
- He fought valiantly against the Quraysh until he was mortally wounded and passed away on the field (*al-Iṣābah*, 6:47).
- Standing over his body, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ declared:
  > *«Mukhayrīq is the foremost of the Jews, Salmān is the foremost of the Persians, and Bilāl is the foremost of the Abyssinians»*
  > (*Mukhayrīq sābiq Yahūd, wa-Salmān sābiq Fāris, wa-Bilāl sābiq al-Ḥabashah*) (*al-Iṣābah*, 6:47).
- In another sound narration, the Prophet ﷺ eulogized him: *«Mukhayrīq is the best of the Jews!»* (*Mukhayrīq khayr Yahūd*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:212).
