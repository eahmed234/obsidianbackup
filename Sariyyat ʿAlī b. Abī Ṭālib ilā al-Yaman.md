# Sariyyat ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib ilā al-Yaman (سرية علي بن أبي طالب إلى اليمن)
*The Expedition of ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib to Yemen — The Conversion of Madhḥij and Hamdān, the Sublime Investiture, and the Renowned Hadith of Guidance*

> [!NOTE] Scholarly Provenance & Primary Source
> This master entry is derived strictly from *Subul al-Hudā*, Volume 6, *al-Bāb al-Thānī wal-Sabʿūn fī Sariyyat ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib rḍy Allāh taʿālā ʿanhu ilā al-Yaman al-Marrah al-Thāniyah* (pp. 238–240), synthesizing the transmissions of [[al-Wāqidī]] and [[Ibn Saʿd]], alongside the sound narrations of *Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī* and *Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim*, cross-referenced with *al-Iṣābah*, 4:269.

---

## 1. Context & The Prophetic Investiture

Prior to the deployment of ʿAlī ﵁, **[[Khālid b. al-Walīd]]** ﵁ had spent six months in Yemen inviting the tribes to Islam, but they had remained hesitant (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:238).

In the blessed month of Ramaḍān in the year ten AH, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ resolved to dispatch his cousin and son-in-law, **[[ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib]]** ﵁, ordering him to encamp at Qanāh until his forces fully assembled (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:238).

The Prophet ﷺ personally conducted a ceremonial military investiture (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:238):
- He tied a white banner (*liwāʾ*) with his own hands, took his turban, folded it into a square, fastened it to the head of the spear, and handed it to ʿAlī.
- He wound a turban around ʿAlī's head with three turns (*thalāthata akwār*), leaving an arm's length draped in front of him and a span behind him.

The Prophet ﷺ then commanded him:
> «امْضِ وَلَا تَلْتَفِتْ!»
> *"March forth, and do not turn back!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:238).

ʿAlī asked: "O Messenger of Allāh, what shall I do?" 

The Prophet ﷺ delivered the supreme code of Islamic warfare and daʿwah:
> «إِذَا نَزَلْتَ بِسَاحَتِهِمْ فَلَا تُقَاتِلْهُمْ حَتَّى يُقَاتِلُوكَ، وَادْعُهُمْ إِلَى أَنْ يَقُولُوا لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ مُحَمَّدٌ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ؛ فَإِنْ قَالُوا نَعَمْ فَمُرْهُمْ بِالصَّلَاةِ، فَإِنْ أَجَابُوا فَمُرْهُمْ بِالزَّكَاةِ، فَإِنْ أَجَابُوا فَلَا تَبْغِ مِنْهُمْ غَيْرَ ذَٰلِكَ!
> 
> فَوَاللَّهِ، لَأَنْ يَهْدِيَ اللَّهُ بِكَ رَجُلًا وَاحِدًا خَيْرٌ لَكَ مِمَّا طَلَعَتْ عَلَيْهِ الشَّمْسُ أَوْ غَرَبَتْ!»
> 
> *"When you alight in their territory, do not fight them until they fight you! Call them to testify that there is no god but Allāh and that Muḥammad is the Messenger of Allāh. If they accept, command them to perform the prayer; if they comply, command them to pay the zakāt; and if they agree, seek nothing further from them!*
> 
> *For by Allāh, that Allāh should guide through you a single man is better for you than everything upon which the sun rises or sets!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:238).

## 2. The Clash with Madhḥij & The Victory

ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib ﵁ set out at the head of three hundred cavalrymen—the first Muslim cavalry ever to ride deep into those southern lands (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:238). 

He placed **[[Buraydah b. al-Ḥuṣayb]]** ﵁ in charge of collecting and securing any acquired spoils (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:238). 

Upon encountering the assembled host of **Madhḥij**, ʿAlī ﵁ invited them to Islam. They refused obstinately, shooting arrows and hurling stones at the Muslim detachment (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:238). 

Seeing their insistence on war, ʿAlī drew up his ranks and handed the battle standard to **[[Masʿūd b. Sinān al-Sulamī]]** ﵁ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:238). A champion of Madhḥij stepped forth challenging for single combat; al-Aswad b. Khuzāʿī emerged from the Muslim ranks, slew the challenger in duel, and seized his arms (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:238).

ʿAlī then led his three hundred horsemen in a coordinated charge. They struck down twenty enemy warriors, completely routing their ranks (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:238). Demonstrating extraordinary restraint, ʿAlī ordered his men to halt and refrain from pursuing the fleeing warriors (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:238). 

He called them once more to Islam. Seeing the justice and nobility of the Muslims, the tribal leaders stepped forward, embraced Islam, surrendered their weapons, and declared:
> «نَحْنُ عَلَى مَنْ وَرَاءَنَا مِنْ قَوْمِنَا، وَهَٰذِهِ صَدَقَاتُنَا فَخُذْ مِنْهَا حَقَّ اللَّهِ تَعَالَى!»
> *"We pledge allegiance on behalf of those behind us of our people, and these are our alms, so take from them the due right of Allāh the Exalted!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:238).

---

## 3. The Conversion of Hamdān in a Single Day

ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib ﵁ then turned toward the great Yemeni confederation of **Hamdān** (*Hamdān*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:238). 

He gathered their tribes, stood before them, and recited the letter of the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ. In an astonishing demonstration of divine guidance, the entire tribe of Hamdān embraced Islam in a single day (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:238).

ʿAlī dispatched a courier, ʿAbdullāh b. ʿAmr b. ʿAwf al-Muzanī, to carry the glad tidings to Medina (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:238–239). When the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ read the letter announcing Hamdān's conversion, he prostrated to Allāh in profound gratitude (*sajdat al-shukr*), raised his head, and proclaimed twice:

> «السَّلَامُ عَلَى هَمْدَانَ! السَّلَامُ عَلَى هَمْدَانَ!»
> *"Peace be upon Hamdān! Peace be upon Hamdān!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:239).

---

## 4. Strict Financial Integrity & Rendition of the Fifth (*al-Khumus*)

ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib ﵁ divided the acquired wealth into five parts, casting lots and setting aside the Fifth (*al-Khumus*) for the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:238).

He divided the remaining four-fifths strictly among the soldiers. When some cavalrymen requested extra gifts (*nafl*) from the Fifth—as had occasionally occurred on other campaigns—ʿAlī refused with unbending scrupulousness, saying:
> «الْخُمُسُ أَحْمِلُهُ إِلَى رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَآلِهِ وَسَلَّمَ يَرَى فِيهِ رَأْيَهُ!»
> *"The Fifth I shall carry untouched to the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ so that he may exercise his divine judgment over it!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:238).

---

## 5. Joining the Prophet ﷺ at the Farewell Pilgrimage

The Prophet's courier returned to ʿAlī in Yemen carrying the command of the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ: to conclude his mission and join the Prophet ﷺ in Makkah for the Farewell Pilgrimage (**[[Ḥajjat al-Wadāʿ]]**) in Dhū al-Ḥijjah 10 AH (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:239).

Leaving **[[Abū Rāfiʿ]]** ﵁ in charge of the caravan and the secured treasury, ʿAlī rode swiftly ahead to Makkah, arriving just as the Prophet ﷺ entered the holy sanctuary (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:239). The Prophet ﷺ welcomed him with joy and embraced him, asking him about his sacral intention (*iḥrām*). ʿAlī replied: "I said: 'O Allāh, I enter the sacral state for whatever Your Messenger has entered it for!'" The Prophet ﷺ affirmed his pilgrimage and made him a full partner in his sacrificial offerings of one hundred camels (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:239).
