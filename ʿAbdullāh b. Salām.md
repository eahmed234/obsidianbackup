# ʿAbdullāh b. Salām (عبد الله بن سلام) ﵁
*Abū Yūsuf — The Paramount Sage of the Children of Israel, Righteous Scholar of Scripture, and Promised Inheritor of Paradise*

## 1. Lineage, Names & Pre-Islamic Rabbinate
- **Ancestry**: ʿAbdullāh b. Salām b. al-Ḥārith, traced genealogically to the lineage of the Prophet **Yūsuf b. Yaʿqūb** ﵊ (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:102). Kunyah: **Abū Yūsuf**.
- **Pre-Islamic Name**: In the Jāhiliyyah, he was named **al-Ḥuṣayn**; upon his conversion, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ renamed him **ʿAbdullāh** (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:102).
- **Tribal & Confederate Ties**: Originating from the tribe of **[[Banū Qaynuqāʿ]]**, he held a confederate alliance (*ḥilf*) with the Banū al-Qawāqil clan of **[[al-Khazraj]]** (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:102; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:179).
- **Rabbinic Eminence**: Prior to Islam, he was the supreme religious leader, rabbi, and most learned jurist among the Jewish communities of Yathrib (*sayyiduhum wa-aʿlamuhum*) (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:103).

## 2. Dramatic Conversion at the Hijrah (1 AH)
- **Recognition of Prophethood**: When the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ reached Qubāʾ and entered Medina during **[[al-Hijrah ilā al-Madīnah]]**, ʿAbdullāh b. Salām rushed alongside the crowds to scrutinize his features. He testified:
  > «فَلَمَّا تَبَيَّنْتُ وَجْهَهُ عَرَفْتُ أَنَّ وَجْهَهُ لَيْسَ بِوَجْهِ كَذَّابٍ!»
  > *"When I closely examined his countenance, I recognized that his face was not the face of a liar!"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:103).
- **The Three Questions**: In *Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī*, Anas b. Mālik ﵁ related that ʿAbdullāh b. Salām came to the Prophet ﷺ while working among his palm groves, saying: *"I shall question you regarding three matters that none knows save a Prophet: What is the first portent of the Hour? What is the first food consumed by the people of Paradise? And why does a child resemble its father or maternal kin?"* When the Prophet ﷺ answered each by revelation through Jibrīl ﵊, ʿAbdullāh proclaimed: *«I bear witness that you are indeed the Messenger of Allāh!»* (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:103).
- **The Exposure of Communal Slander**: ʿAbdullāh concealed himself in the Prophet's ﷺ quarters and requested him to inquire of the Jewish leaders concerning his standing. When summoned, they declared: *"He is our master and the son of our master, our most learned scholar and the son of our most learned scholar!"* ʿAbdullāh then emerged, declaring his Islam and calling them to believe. Instantly reversing their testimony, they shouted: *"He is the worst of us and the son of the worst of us!"* slandering him (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:103).

## 3. Glad Tidings of Paradise & Scriptural Attestations
- **Explicit Promise of Paradise**: Saʿd b. Abī Waqqāṣ ﵁ attested in *Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī* and *Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim*:
  > «مَا سَمِعْتُ النَّبِيَّ ﷺ يَقُولُ لِأَحَدٍ يَمْشِي عَلَى الْأَرْضِ: إِنَّهُ مِنْ أَهْلِ الْجَنَّةِ، إِلَّا لِعَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ سَلَامٍ»
  > *"I never heard the Prophet ﷺ say of any living person walking upon the earth: 'He is from the people of Paradise,' except for ʿAbdullāh b. Salām!"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:103).
- **Qurʾānic References**: Classical exegetes and Companions, including ʿAbdullāh himself, identified his testimony as the subject of the divine verse:
  > *«Say: Have you considered: if the Qurʾān was from Allāh, and you disbelieved in it while a witness from the Children of Israel has testified to the like thereof and believed, while you displayed arrogance? Indeed, Allāh guides not the wrongdoing people»* (Qurʾān 46:10; *al-Iṣābah*, 4:104).

## 4. Stand during the Fitnah & Demise (43 AH)
- During the tumultuous siege of the residence of Caliph **[[ʿUthmān b. ʿAffān]]** ﵁, ʿAbdullāh b. Salām stood at the gates defending the Caliph, reminding the insurgents that Medina was the sanctuary of the Prophet ﷺ and that unsheathing the sword against the lawful Caliph would cause division until the Day of Resurrection (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:104).
- He passed away peacefully in **[[al-Madīnah al-Munawwarah]]** in 43 AH during the caliphate of Muʿāwiyah (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:104).
