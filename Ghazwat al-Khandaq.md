# Ghazwat al-Khandaq (غزوة الخندق / غزوة الأحزاب)
*The Battle of the Trench — The Grand Confederate Siege and the Day the Earth Shook*

> [!NOTE] Scholarly Provenance & Primary Source
> This master entry is derived exclusively from *Subul al-Hudā*, Volume 4, *al-Bāb al-Tāsiʿ ʿAshar fī Ghazwat al-Khandaq* (pp. 363–396). Al-Ṣāliḥī synthesizes the primary accounts of [[Ibn Isḥāq]], [[Mūsā b. ʿUqbah]], [[al-Wāqidī]], [[Ibn Saʿd]], [[al-Bukhārī]], [[Muslim]], and the classical *maghāzī* authorities.

---

## 1. Instigation of the Grand Confederacy (*al-Aḥzāb*)

Following their expulsion from Medina, the disgraced chieftains of Banū al-Naḍīr — led by Ḥuyayy b. Akhṭab, Sallām b. Abī al-Ḥuqayq, and Kinānah b. al-Rabīʿ — established their base in the fortified northern oasis of **[[Khaybar]]** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:363). Driven by bitter animosity, they resolved to assemble a pan-Arab coalition to eradicate the Islamic state.

The delegation traveled first to [[Makkah al-Mukarramah]], inciting [[Quraysh]]:
> *"We shall stand with you as one hand until we uproot Muḥammad and his companions!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:364).

When Quraysh asked them to evaluate their idolatrous faith against Muḥammad's monotheism, the Jewish scholars declared that pagan polytheism was superior and closer to the truth than Islam, prompting Allāh's stern condemnation in the Qurʾān (4:51; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:364).

The Jewish emissaries then traveled to the fierce desert clans of Ghaṭafān, offering them the entire annual date harvest of Khaybar in exchange for their military mobilization (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:364). They secured the alliance of Banū Sulaym, Banū Fazārah, Banū Murrah, Banū Asad, and Ashjaʿ.

### The Two Armies
Al-Ṣāliḥī details the precise composition of the invading forces (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:364):
- **Quraysh and their Ahabīsh allies**: **4,000 warriors**, marching with **300 horses** and **1,500 camels**, under the supreme command of **[[Abū Sufyān b. Ḥarb]]**.
- **Banū Sulaym**: **700 warriors**, joining Quraysh at Marr al-Ẓahrān under Sufyān b. ʿAbd Shams.
- **Banū Fazārah**: **1,000 warriors**, commanded by ʿUyaynah b. Ḥiṣn.
- **Banū Murrah**: **400 warriors**, commanded by al-Ḥārith b. ʿAwf.
- **Ashjaʿ**: **400 warriors**, commanded by Masʿūd b. Rukhaylah.
- **Banū Asad**: Led by Ṭulayḥah b. Khuwaylid al-Asadī.
- **Total Confederate Strength**: **10,000 warriors** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:364).

Opposing this force, the Muslim army numbered exactly **3,000 defenders** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:371). Al-Ṣāliḥī explicitly clarifies:
> «وَكَانَ الْمُسْلِمُونَ فِيمَا قَالُوا: ثَلَاثَةَ آلَافٍ، وَوَهِمَ مَنْ قَالَ: إِنَّهُمْ كَانُوا سَبْعَمِائَةٍ»
> *"And the Muslims were, as they said, three thousand; and whoever asserted that they were seven hundred erred!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:371).

---

## 2. The Defensive Innovation: Digging the Trench (*al-Khandaq*)

When intelligence of the confederate march reached Medina, the Prophet ﷺ rode out with a contingent of Muhājirūn and Anṣār to survey the topography (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:365). Medina was naturally shielded by impassable volcanic lava fields (*ḥarrāt*) on its eastern and western flanks, and by dense date-palm groves and fortresses on the south. Only the broad, flat northern approach was exposed to mass cavalry assault.

**[[Salmān al-Fārisī]]** ﵁ stepped forward and proposed a military stratagem unknown to the Arabs:
> *"O Messenger of Allāh, in Persia, whenever we were besieged by cavalry, we dug a trench around us!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:365).

The Prophet ﷺ accepted the proposal. He selected the defensive perimeter, anchoring the army's back to **[[Jabal Salʿ]]** and digging the trench from al-Madhād on the west to Dhubāb and Rātij on the east (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:365).

### The Labor and Prophetic Companionship
The Prophet ﷺ apportioned the excavation at forty cubits (*arbaʿīna dhirāʿan*) for every ten men (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:365). The Muhājirūn and Anṣār vied for the inclusion of Salmān, the Muhājirūn declaring: *«Salmān is of us!»* and the Anṣār declaring: *«Salmān is of us!»* The Prophet ﷺ settled the noble rivalry, proclaiming:
> «سَلْمَانُ مِنَّا أَهْلَ الْبَيْتِ!»
> *"Salmān is of us, the Ahl al-Bayt!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:365).

Working under biting winter winds with empty stomachs, the believers carried earth upon their backs in wicker baskets (*al-makātil*), chanting verses of martial devotion (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:366–367):
> «نَحْنُ الَّذِينَ بَايَعُوا مُحَمَّدَا ... عَلَى الْإِسْلَامِ (الْجِهَادِ) مَا بَقِينَا أَبَدَا»
> *"We are those who have pledged allegiance to Muḥammad, unto Islam [and Jihad] as long as we remain alive!"*
To which the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ answered:
> «اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّ الْعَيْشَ عَيْشُ الْآخِرَهْ ... فَاغْفِرْ لِلْأَنْصَارِ وَالْمُهَاجِرَهْ»
> *"O Allāh, there is no true life except the life of the Hereafter; so forgive the Anṣār and the Muhājirah!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:366).

The Prophet ﷺ personally swung the pickaxe, carried dirt until dust covered his stomach, and shared the extreme privation of his men (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:367).

## 3. Miracles during the Excavation

Al-Ṣāliḥī records numerous evidentiary miracles (*dalāʾil*) witnessed during the excavation:

### 1. The Immovable Rock and the Triple Sparks
A gargantuan white boulder resisted all iron pickaxes, shattering their blades (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:367–368). The Prophet ﷺ descended into the trench, took the pickaxe from Salmān, and struck:
1. **The First Strike**: One-third shattered, emitting a blinding flash of light that illuminated the palaces of ash-Shām (Syria). The Prophet ﷺ cried: *«Allāhu Akbar! I have been given the keys of Syria; by Allāh, I behold its red palaces at this moment!»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:368).
2. **The Second Strike**: Another third shattered, flashing a radiant beam toward the east. The Prophet ﷺ cried: *«Allāhu Akbar! I have been given the keys of Persia; by Allāh, I behold the white palace of al-Madāʾin!»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:368).
3. **The Third Strike**: The remainder disintegrated into fine sand, flashing a beam southward. The Prophet ﷺ cried: *«Allāhu Akbar! I have been given the keys of Yemen; by Allāh, I behold the gates of Ṣanʿāʾ from this place!»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:368).

### 2. Multiplicative Feedings
- **The Feast of Jābir b. ʿAbdillāh**: Seeing the Prophet ﷺ tying a stone against his stomach to quell extreme hunger, Jābir slaughtered a small ewe (*ʿināq*) and his wife ground a measure of barley (*ṣāʿ*). Jābir invited the Prophet ﷺ with a small handful of men; the Prophet ﷺ called all three thousand trench-workers! He blessed the dough and the pot; the entire army ate until satisfied, while the meat pot remained bubbling full and dough remained plentiful (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:368–369).
- **The Dates of the Young Girl**: The daughter of Bashīr b. Saʿd brought a small handful of dates for her father and uncle; the Prophet ﷺ spread them upon a cloak and invoked blessings; the dates multiplied until they cascaded over the edges of the sheet, feeding the entire host (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:370).

---

## 4. Arrival of the Confederates & Treason of Banū Qurayẓah

The Confederates arrived, expecting open warfare in the plains. Halting in astonishment before the broad ditch, they exclaimed: *«By Allāh, this is a cunning stratagem that the Arabs never practiced!»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:372). They encamped in massive rings around the northern perimeter: Quraysh at Majmaʿ al-Asyāl and Ghaṭafān at Dhanab Naqamā (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:372).

### The Perfidious Breach by Banū Qurayẓah
While the believers stood vigilant along the trench under freezing conditions, Ḥuyayy b. Akhṭab stole into the fortress of Kaʿb b. Asad, chieftain of **[[Banū Qurayẓah]]** in southern Medina (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:373). After initial hesitation, Kaʿb tore up the pact (*al-ṣaḥīfah*) guaranteeing peace with the Muslims, declaring allegiance to the Confederate invasion (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:373–374).

When intelligence reached the Prophet ﷺ, he dispatched **[[Saʿd b. Muʿādh]]** ﵁ and **[[Saʿd b. ʿUbādah]]** ﵁ to verify the report. Returning, they used the agreed code-words: *«ʿAḍal wa-l-Qārah»* (recalling the treachery of Rajīʿ), confirming the betrayal (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:374). The Prophet ﷺ shouted: **«اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ! أَبْشِرُوا يَا مَعْشَرَ الْمُسْلِمِينَ!»** (*«Allāhu Akbar! Rejoice, O assembly of Muslims!»*), disguising panic from the ranks (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:374).

The encirclement was complete: ten thousand polytheists at their front, and treacherous enemies directly behind the defenseless households of their women and children. Of this existential trial, Allāh revealed:
$$\text{إِذْ جَاءُوكُم مِّن فَوْقِكُمْ وَمِنْ أَسْفَلَ مِنكُمْ وَإِذْ زَاغَتِ الْأَبْصَارُ وَبَلَغَتِ الْقُلُوبُ الْحَنَاجِرَ وَتَظُنُّونَ بِاللَّهِ الظُّنُونَا}$$
$$\text{هُنَالِكَ ابْتُلِيَ الْمُؤْمِنُونَ وَزُلْزِلُوا زِلْزَالًا شَدِيدًا}$$
> *"When they came at you from above you and from below you, and when eyes shifted [in fear], and hearts reached the throats, and you assumed doubts about Allāh. There the believers were tested and shaken with a severe shaking."* (Qurʾān 33:10–11; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:374).

The hypocrites openly sneered: *«Muḥammad promises us the treasures of Chosroes and Caesar, while none of us can even feel safe going to relieve himself!»* (Qurʾān 33:12; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:374).

---

## 5. Assaults across the Trench & Dueling of Ali

For nearly a month, the polytheists rained arrows across the ditch, mounting relentless probes (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:376). The fighting was so intense on certain days that the Prophet ﷺ and his Companions were prevented from performing the ʿAṣr prayer until after sunset, prompting the Prophet's supplication: *«May Allāh fill their houses and graves with fire, just as they distracted us from the middle prayer!»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:381).

### The Breach of the Trench and Duel with ʿAmr b. ʿAbd Wudd
A contingent of premier Meccan horsemen — including **ʿAmr b. ʿAbd Wudd al-ʿĀmirī** (esteemed as equivalent to a thousand horsemen), ʿIkrimah b. Abī Jahl, Hubayrah b. Abī Wahb, and Dirār b. al-Khaṭṭāb — discovered a narrow sector of the trench and spurred their horses across into the marshland below **[[Jabal Salʿ]]** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:377).

ʿAmr, a scarred veteran of Badr who wore an emblem of terror, trotted forward bellowing: *«Who will duel?!»*

**[[ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib]]** ﵁ volunteered: *«I will, O Messenger of Allāh!»* The Prophet ﷺ cautioned him: *«It is ʿAmr!»* Ali replied: *«Even if it is ʿAmr!»* The Prophet ﷺ gave him his own sword, fastened his turban, and prayed:
> «اللَّهُمَّ أَعِنْهُ عَلَيْهِ!»
> *"O Allāh, aid him against him!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:377).

Ali approached on foot, reciting martial poetry:
> «لَا تَعْجَلَنَّ فَقَدْ أَتَاكَ ... مُجِيبُ صَوْتِكَ غَيْرَ عَاجِزْ»
> *"Do not hasten, for there has come to you an answerer to your call, not helpless!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:377).

When ʿAmr recognized Ali, he sneered: *«Who are you?»* Ali replied: *«I am ʿAlī, son of Abū Ṭālib.»* ʿAmr said: *«O son of my brother, I do not wish to shed your blood; your father was a friend of mine!»* Ali retorted: *«But by Allāh, I desire to shed your blood!»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:378).

Incensed, ʿAmr leaped from his horse, hamstrung its legs, and slashed at Ali. Ali caught the blow upon his leather shield; ʿAmr's blade split the shield and nicked Ali's forehead. Ali instantly countered with a lightning stroke across ʿAmr's neck (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:378). Dust billowed up between them; suddenly, the takbīr of Ali resounded through the air: **«اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ!»** The Muslims answered with a deafening roar of takbīr. The remaining horsemen fled in terror, plunging back across the trench (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:378).

### The Wounding of Saʿd b. Muʿādh
During the arrow skirmishes, Ḥibbān b. Qays al-ʿAraqah shot a shaft that severed the medial arm vein (*al-akḥal*) of **[[Saʿd b. Muʿādh]]** ﵁ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:380). Blood gushed forth; Saʿd prayed:
> «اللَّهُمَّ لَا تُمِتْنِي حَتَّى تُقِرَّ عَيْنِي مِنْ بَنِي قُرَيْظَةَ!»
> *"O Allāh, do not cause me to die until You grant my eyes satisfaction concerning Banū Qurayẓah!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:380).
His bleeding miraculously paused, and the Prophet ﷺ pitched a medical tent for him inside the Mosque so he could attend to him closely (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:380).

## 6. Strategic Dissension by Nuʿaym b. Masʿūd

As the siege dragged on under relentless cold and famine, the Prophet ﷺ considered negotiating with the chieftains of Ghaṭafān (ʿUyaynah b. Ḥiṣn and al-Ḥārith b. ʿAwf) to withdraw their forces in exchange for one-third of Medina's date harvest (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:376). When he consulted **[[Saʿd b. Muʿādh]]** ﵁ and **[[Saʿd b. ʿUbādah]]** ﵁, asking if this was a Divine command or personal military judgment, the Prophet ﷺ explained: *«It is my judgment to break their coalition.»* The two Saʿds firmly replied:
> *"By Allāh, when we and these people were polytheists and idolaters, they never ate a single date of Medina except by purchase or as guests! Now that Allāh has honored us with Islam and guided us through you, shall we yield our wealth to them?! By Allāh, we shall give them nothing but the sword!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:377).
The Prophet ﷺ smiled and cancelled the treaty.

### The Mission of Nuʿaym
At that moment, **[[Nuʿaym b. Masʿūd]]** ﵁ of the Ashjaʿ clan of Ghaṭafān arrived secretly at the Prophet's command post, professing Islam (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:384). The Prophet ﷺ told him: *«Warfare is deception; dishearten them on our behalf if you can.»*

Nuʿaym went to **[[Banū Qurayẓah]]**, advising them not to fight alongside Quraysh and Ghaṭafān without demanding seventy noble hostages as insurance (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:384–385). He then went to Quraysh and Ghaṭafān, warning them that Qurayẓah had secretly agreed to seize seventy Meccan hostages and deliver them to Muḥammad to be executed (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:385). When Abū Sufyān demanded that Qurayẓah join battle on the Sabbath, the Jews demanded hostages; the Confederates cried that Nuʿaym had spoken the truth, shattering trust between the allies (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:386).

---

## 7. The Hurricane and the Reconnaissance of Ḥudhayfah

For three consecutive days (Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday), the Prophet ﷺ stood atop **[[Jabal Salʿ]]** at the site of Masjid al-Fatḥ, supplicating against the Confederates (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:383):
> «اللَّهُمَّ مُنْزِلَ الْكِتَابِ، سَرِيعَ الْحِسَابِ، اهْزِمِ الْأَحْزَابَ، اللَّهُمَّ اهْزِمْهُمْ وَزَلْزِلْهُمْ!»
> *"O Allāh, Revealer of the Book, Swift in Reckoning, defeat the Confederates! O Allāh, defeat them and shake them!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:383).
On Wednesday between the prayers of Ẓuhr and ʿAṣr, Divine victory descended (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:383).

### The Reconnaissance of Ḥudhayfah b. al-Yamān
On a night of bitter, bone-chilling cold and pitch-black darkness, Allāh unleashed upon the Confederate camp a ferocious easterly hurricane (*rīḥ al-ṣabā*) accompanied by invisible angelic hosts (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:386–387). The tempest uprooted their tents, blew their fires into the night, overturned their cooking cauldrons, and sent gravel flying into their faces.

The Prophet ﷺ asked: *«Who will go forth to bring me news of the enemy, and he shall be my companion in Paradise?»* None arose due to extreme cold and starvation (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:387). The Prophet ﷺ then called specifically: **«قُمْ يَا حُذَيْفَةُ!»** (*«Arise, O Ḥudhayfah!»*), commanding him: *«Bring me news of the people and do not alarm them against me!»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:387).

Ḥudhayfah ﵁ slipped across the trench into the enemy camp. He felt no cold as long as he carried out the mission. In the darkness, he saw Abū Sufyān warming his hands over a dying ember, exclaiming:
> *"O people of Quraysh! By Allāh, you are not in an enduring dwelling! The horses and camels have perished; Banū Qurayẓah have betrayed us; and we have met from this wind that which you see! Depart, for I am departing!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:388).

Abū Sufyān mounted his tethered camel and struck it; it bounded up on three legs before he untied its hock (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:388). Ḥudhayfah had an arrow notched and could have killed Abū Sufyān, but remembered the Prophet's instruction not to initiate anything (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:388).

Returning to the Prophet ﷺ, Ḥudhayfah found him praying enveloped in a mantle belonging to one of his wives; the Prophet ﷺ drew Ḥudhayfah beneath the cloak, listened to his report, and laughed until his teeth showed (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:388).

---

## 8. Aftermath & Strategic Turning Point

At dawn, the northern desert was empty. The Grand Confederacy had dissolved without a set-piece clash (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:389). Allāh commemorated this sovereign intervention:
$$\text{وَرَدَّ اللَّهُ الَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا بِغَيْظِهِمْ لَمْ يَنَالُوا خَيْرًا ۚ وَكَفَى اللَّهُ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ الْقِتَالَ ۚ وَكَانَ اللَّهُ قَوِيًّا عَزِيزًا}$$
> *"And Allāh repelled those who disbelieved in their rage; they gained no advantage. And Allāh sufficed the believers in combat, and Allāh is Powerful and Exalted in Might."* (Qurʾān 33:25; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:389).

As the Muslims cleaned their weapons, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ pronounced the decisive turning point in Islamic geopolitical history:
> «الْآنَ نَغْزُوهُمْ وَلَا يَغْزُونَنَا، نَحْنُ نَسِيرُ إِلَيْهِمْ!»
> *"Now we shall invade them, and they will never invade us! We shall march against them!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:389).

No sooner had the Prophet ﷺ laid down his weapons at noon in Medina than the Angel Jibrīl ﵊ appeared, covered in dust atop a mule with a saddlecloth of brocade, asking: *«Have you laid down your weapons, O Messenger of Allāh? By Allāh, the angels have not laid down their weapons! March forth to Banū Qurayẓah!»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:4).
