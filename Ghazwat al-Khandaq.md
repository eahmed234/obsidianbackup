# Ghazwat al-Khandaq (غزوة الخندق / غزوة الأحزاب)
*The Battle of the Trench — The Grand Confederate Siege of Medina*

## 1. The Confederate Coalition (*al-Aḥzāb*)
Instigated by the exiled chieftains of Banū al-Naḍīr in Khaybar (led by Ḥuyayy b. Akhṭab), an unprecedented confederacy of 10,000 polytheist warriors marched upon Medina to annihilate Islam once and for all.

## 2. The Digging of the Trench (*Khandaq*)
## The Two Forces, as the Book Gives Them
Quraysh *"went out in **four thousand**, and tied the standard in Dār al-Nadwah … and they led with
them **three hundred horses**, and with them were **one thousand five hundred camels**."* Banū Sulaym
met them at Marr al-Ẓahrān with **seven hundred**; Banū Fazārah came out **a thousand** under ʿUyaynah
b. Ḥiṣn; Ashjaʿ **four hundred**; Banū Murrah **four hundred** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:364).

Of the Muslims the book is explicit, and corrects a rival figure:

> «وَكَانَ الْمُسْلِمُونَ فِيمَا قَالُوا: ثَلَاثَةَ آلَافٍ، وَوَهِمَ مَنْ قَالَ: إِنَّهُمْ كَانُوا سَبْعَمِائَةٍ»
> *"And the Muslims were, as they said, **three thousand** — and **whoever said they were seven
> hundred erred**."* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:371)

The same page records that the standard of the Muhājirūn was with
**[[Zayd b. Ḥārithah]]** ﵁ and that of the Anṣār with **[[Saʿd b. ʿUbādah]]** ﵁, that
[[ʿAbdullāh b. Umm Maktūm]] was left over Medina, and that the women and children were placed among
the fortified houses.

## The Trench
Facing those odds, **[[Salmān al-Fārisī]]** ﵁ proposed a defensive siege innovation unknown to the Arabs: digging a deep defensive trench across the vulnerable, unfortified northern flatland of Medina. The Prophet ﷺ personally swung the pickaxe, breaking unyielding boulders; as sparks flew, he prophesied the future conquest of the Persian, Byzantine, and Yemeni empires (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:375).

## 3. The Betrayal of Banū Qurayẓah & Divine Wind
While freezing hunger and terror tested the believers to their limits, **[[Banū Qurayẓah]]** tore up their peace treaty and coordinated an assault from the southern rear. In response, **Nuʿaym b. Masʿūd** skillfully sowed discord between Quraysh and the Jews. 

Allāh then dispatched a freezing winter hurricane (*rīḥan wa-junūdan*) that tore down enemy tents, overturned cooking cauldrons, and shattered Meccan morale, forcing Abū Sufyān to order a humiliating retreat (Qurʾān 33:9).
