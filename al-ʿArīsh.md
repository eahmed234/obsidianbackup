# al-ʿArīsh (العريش)
*The Command Booth of the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ at Badr*

## 1. What It Was
A shelter of palm branches raised for the Prophet ﷺ on the nearer slope at **[[Badr]]**, on the
counsel of **[[Saʿd b. Muʿādh]]** ﵁ — who proposed it so that, should the day go against them, the
Prophet ﷺ would have a mount and a position from which to withdraw to the Anṣār still in Medina. Saʿd
and a guard stood over it.

## 2. What Happened There
It was inside al-ʿArīsh, with **[[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]]** ﵁ beside him, that the Prophet ﷺ made the
supplication of the battle — weeping and pressing his Lord until his cloak slipped from his
shoulders, and Abū Bakr ﵁ urged him to relent. The answer was the descent of the angelic legions
under Jibrīl ﵊ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:36–37).

This is the occasion of **[[Sūrat al-Anfāl]]** (8:9–10):
> ﴿إِذْ تَسْتَغِيثُونَ رَبَّكُمْ فَاسْتَجَابَ لَكُمْ﴾
> *"When you were seeking help of your Lord, and He answered you…"*

The dispute over the spoils afterward set the young men who had charged the enemy against those who
had guarded the standards and the ʿArīsh — the occasion of 8:1.
