# Abū Lubābah b. ʿAbd al-Mundhir (أبو لبابة بن عبد المنذر)
*The Companion of the Binding Repentance*

## 1. Deputy Over Medina
An Anṣārī of **[[al-Aws]]**. Dispatched with the army toward Badr, he was turned back at
**al-Rawḥāʾ** and appointed by the Prophet ﷺ over the civil affairs of Medina for the duration of the
campaign (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:23) — see **[[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]]**.

## 2. Banū Qurayẓah and His Repentance
During the siege of **[[Ghazwat Banī Qurayẓah]]** (5 AH), the beleaguered tribe — long-standing
confederates of his own tribe the Aws — asked for Abū Lubābah specifically, trusting that old
alliance. Asked whether they should submit to the Prophet's ﷺ judgment, he answered them with a
gesture toward his throat that they understood as slaughter.

He recognised at once that he had betrayed a confidence of the Prophet ﷺ. Al-Ṣāliḥī gives the episode
and its resolution a section of its own — *dhikr ṭalab Yahūd Abī Lubābah wa-mā waqaʿa lahu wa-nuzūl
tawbatihi*, "their asking for Abū Lubābah, what befell him, and the descent of his repentance"
(*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:8, with the glossary at 5:24). He bound himself to a pillar of the
**[[Bināʾ al-Masjid al-Nabawī|Prophet's Mosque]]** and refused to be released by any hand but the
Prophet's ﷺ, until his forgiveness was revealed.
