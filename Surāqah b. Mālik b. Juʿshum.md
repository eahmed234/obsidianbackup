# Surāqah b. Mālik b. Juʿshum (سراقة بن مالك بن جعشم)
*The Pursuer Who Became a Companion — and the Form Iblīs Borrowed at Badr*

## 1. The Pursuit of the Hijrah
A tracker of Banū Mudlij who set out after the Prophet ﷺ and
**[[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]]** ﵁ during **[[al-Hijrah ilā al-Madīnah]]**, drawn by the hundred-camel
bounty Quraysh had set. Three times his horse pitched him and its forelegs sank into firm ground.
Recognising what he was contending with, he called for terms, asked for a written guarantee of
safety, and turned back — thereafter shielding the Prophet's ﷺ trail from other pursuers.

He later accepted Islam, and al-Ṣāliḥī accordingly gives him the honorific: *qiṣṣat Surāqah ﵁*
(*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:248, with the commentary at 3:264).

## 2. Badr: Iblīs in His Likeness
At **[[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]]**, when Quraysh hesitated at the prospect of battle,
**[[Iblīs]]** appeared among them **in Surāqah's form** to steady their resolve, and fled when the
angelic descent became visible. Al-Ṣāliḥī titles the section *dhikr tabaddī Iblīs li-Quraysh fī ṣūrat
Surāqah b. Mālik* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:22) — the occasion of
**[[Sūrat al-Anfāl]]** (8:48).

> [!NOTE] Two Distinct Roles
> The Surāqah of the Hijrah pursuit and the *likeness* worn at Badr are the same man's form, but not
> the same agent: the second is Iblīs impersonating him, at a time when Surāqah himself was under the
> Prophet's ﷺ written guarantee. The vault links both because both appear in the sources under his
> name.
