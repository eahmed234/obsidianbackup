# Fatḥ Makkah (فتح مكة المكرمة)
*The Supreme Conquest of Makkah — The Triumph of Truth and Universal Clemency*

## 1. Breach of the Ḥudaybiyah Treaty
When Quraysh supplied weapons to Banū Bakr to massacre Banū Khuzāʿah (the allies of Medina) within the Sacred Sanctuary, the Treaty of Ḥudaybiyah collapsed. The Prophet ﷺ mobilized a secret force of 10,000 believers.

## 2. Entry in Sublime Humility
On the 20th of Ramaḍān, the army entered Makkah in four separate columns. The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ entered riding his camel *al-Qaṣwāʾ*, bowing his head so low out of sheer humility before Allāh that his beard touched the pommel of his saddle, reciting [[Sūrat al-Fatḥ]] (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:285).

## 3. Cleansing the Kaʿbah & The General Amnesty
Approaching the 360 idols encircling the Kaʿbah, the Prophet ﷺ struck them with his bow, reciting:
$$\text{وَقُلْ جَاءَ الْحَقُّ وَزَهَقَ الْبَاطِلُ ۚ إِنَّ الْبَاطِلَ كَانَ زَهُوقًا}$$
> *"And say: 'Truth has arrived, and falsehood has perished. Indeed, falsehood is bound to perish!'"* (Qurʾān 17:81).

Facing the petrified chieftains of Quraysh who had persecuted him for two decades, the Prophet ﷺ asked: *«مَا تَرَوْنَ أَنِّي فَاعِلٌ بِكُمْ؟»* (*"What do you think I shall do with you?"*). They replied: *"Noble brother, son of a noble brother!"* The Prophet ﷺ proclaimed:
> «لَا تَثْرِيبَ عَلَيْكُمُ الْيَوْمَ، يَغْفِرُ اللَّهُ لَكُمْ، وَهُوَ أَرْحَمُ الرَّاحِمِينَ! اذْهَبُوا فَأَنْتُمُ الطُّلَقَاءُ!»
> *"No blame shall be upon you today! May Allāh forgive you, and He is the Most Merciful of those who show mercy! Go, for you are entirely free (*al-Ṭulaqāʾ*)!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:295).
