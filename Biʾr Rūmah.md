# Biʾr Rūmah (بئر رومة)
*The Blessed Well of Medina Purchased and Endowed in Perpetuity by ʿUthmān b. ʿAffān*

## 1. Topography & Sanctified Status
**Biʾr Rūmah** is one of the celebrated historical wells of Medina, located along the route of Wādī al-ʿAqīq (*Subul al-Hudā*, 7:230).

It is counted by classical scholars—including Imām al-Ghazālī, al-Ḥāfiẓ al-ʿIrāqī, and al-Samhūdī—among the renowned seven historical wells of Medina from which the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ drank, performed ablutions, and imparted blessing (*Subul al-Hudā*, 7:230–231).

## 2. Acquisition & Perpetual Endowment (*Waqf*)
When the Muhājirūn arrived in Medina after the Hijrah, they suffered from the salinity of local water sources, finding palatable drinking water scarce (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:280).

A spring and well known as Rūmah was owned by a man of Banū Ghifār, who sold its water at the rate of a *mudd* of grain per waterskin (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:280). The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ addressed him:
> «بِعْنِيهَا بِعَيْنٍ فِي الْجَنَّةِ»
> *"Sell it to me in exchange for a spring in Paradise!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:280).

The owner excused himself, stating that he and his household possessed no other livelihood. When news reached **[[ʿUthmān b. ʿAffān]]** ﵁, he immediately purchased the well from him for thirty-five thousand dirhams (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:280).

ʿUthmān then came to the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ and asked: *"O Messenger of Allāh! Will you grant to me that which you promised to him—a spring in Paradise?"* The Prophet ﷺ replied: *"Yes."* ʿUthmān declared: *"Indeed, I have bought it and dedicated it as an endowment for the Muslims"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:280).

By this act of munificence, ʿUthmān established one of the earliest and most enduring perpetual public endowments (*awqāf*) in Islamic history, granting equal drinking rights to rich and poor, resident and traveler alike (*Subul al-Hudā*, 7:231, 11:280).
