# Mālik b. ʿAwf al-Naṣrī (مالك بن عوف النصري)
*The Chieftain of Hawāzin — Opponent at Ḥunayn and Stalwart Commander of the Faith*

## 1. Lineage & Chieftaincy
His full genealogy is **Mālik b. ʿAwf b. Saʿd b. Rabīʿah b. Yarbūʿ b. Wāthilah b. Dihmān b. Naṣr b. Muʿāwiyah b. Bakr b. Hawāzin al-Naṣrī** (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:551). 

He was the supreme young prince, orator, and paramount commander of the great northern confederation of **Hawāzin** (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:551). Classical authorities record that he was an audacious, fearless chieftain endowed with powerful poetic eloquence (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:551).

## 2. Supreme Command at Ghazwat Ḥunayn (8 AH)
In Shawwāl 8 AH, following the peaceful surrender of Makkah, Mālik b. ʿAwf marshaled a confederate host of twenty thousand warriors from Hawāzin, Thaqīf, and allied clans to crush the newly victorious Islamic state (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:310). 

Disregarding the counsel of the blind elder tactician Durayd b. al-Ṣimmah, Mālik made the controversial operational decision to deploy the army with their wives, children, and entire herds of camels and sheep arrayed directly behind the battle lines, seeking to ensure that no man would flee (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:312).

At the gorge of **Ḥunayn**, Mālik's ambush threw the Muslim vanguard into a temporary panic, but the resolute counter-charge led by the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ and his steadfast companions broke the confederate lines, routing Hawāzin and scattering their host (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:320).

## 3. Flight to al-Ṭāʾif & The Prophetic Covenant
Following the defeat at Ḥunayn, Mālik b. ʿAwf and a contingent of his horsemen fled to the stone-walled citadel of **al-Ṭāʾif**, seeking refuge alongside the chieftains of **Thaqīf** (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:551; *Subul al-Hudā*, 5:382). 

While the Prophet ﷺ was impounding the massive spoils of Ḥunayn at **al-Jiʿrānah**, a delegation of Hawāzin arrived seeking the return of their families. The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ conveyed a magnificent, magnanimous royal summons:
> «أَخْبِرُوا مَالِكًا أَنَّهُ إِنْ أَتَانِي مُسْلِمًا رَدَدْتُ عَلَيْهِ أَهْلَهُ وَمَالَهُ، وَأَعْطَيْتُهُ مِائَةً مِنَ الإِبِلِ!»
> *"Inform Mālik that if he comes to me having embraced Islam, I shall restore to him his entire family and all his property, and bestow upon him an additional one hundred camels!"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:551; *Subul al-Hudā*, 5:405).

Deeply moved by the Prophet's generosity, Mālik slipped away from the fortress of al-Ṭāʾif under cover of night, evading the guards of Thaqīf. He spurred his horse toward al-Jiʿrānah, entered the presence of the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ, and embraced Islam with sincere devotion (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:551; *Subul al-Hudā*, 5:405).

The Prophet ﷺ fulfilled his promise to the letter: he returned Mālik's wife and children, restored all his impounded property, gifted him one hundred choice camels, and appointed him as the supreme commander over all who had accepted Islam among Hawāzin, Thumalah, Salimah, and Fahm (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:551).

## 4. Poetic Homage to the Prophet ﷺ
Overcome by gratitude and spiritual reverence, Mālik b. ʿAwf recited his immortal ode before the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ:
> مَا إِنْ رَأَيْتُ وَلَا سَمِعْتُ بِوَاحِدٍ ... فِي النَّاسِ كُلِّهِمْ كَمِثْلِ مُحَمَّدِ  
> أَوْفَى فَأَعْطَى لِلْجَزِيلِ لِمُجْتَدِي ... وَمَتَى تَشَأْ يُخْبِرْكَ عَمَّا فِي غَدِ  
> وَإِذَا الْكَتِيبَةُ عَرَّدَتْ أَنْيَابُهَا ... بِالسَّمْهَرِيِّ وَضَرْبِ كُلِّ مُهَنَّدِ  
> فَكَأَنَّهُ لَيْثٌ عَلَى أَشْبَالِهِ ... وَسَطَ الْهَبَاءَةِ خَادِرٌ فِي مَرْصَدِ  
> *"Never have I seen nor heard of any single man among all humankind comparable to Muḥammad! Most faithful to his word, bestowing magnificent bounty upon the petitioner; and whenever you wish, he informs you of the unrevealed truths of tomorrow! And when the armored battalion bares its fangs with piercing spears and the clash of Indian steel, it is as though he is a fearless lion defending his cubs, stalking his prey in the swirling dust of battle!"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:551; *Subul al-Hudā*, 5:405).

The Prophet ﷺ was delighted by his poetry and clothed him in a noble mantle of honor (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:551).

## 5. Operations against Thaqīf & Later Conquests
Operating under the Prophet's direct commission, Mālik mobilized his newly converted tribesmen and imposed a tight counter-blockade upon **al-Ṭāʾif**, intercepting their pasture-herds and trade convoys until Thaqīf was compelled to surrender and dispatch their delegation to Medina to accept Islam (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:551).

During the Caliphate of **[[ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb]]** ﵁, Mālik b. ʿAwf marched at the head of Hawāzin contingents in the great Syrian campaigns, displaying heroic leadership during the conquest of **Damascus** (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:551). He established his permanent residence in Damascus, occupying a large compound that was commemorated throughout early Syrian history as **Dār Banī Naṣr** (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:551).
