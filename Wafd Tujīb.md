# Wafd Tujīb (وفد تجيب)
*The exemplary thirteen-man delegation of Tujīb who brought the surplus zakāt of their tribe, the supplication for the pious youth of Banū Abdhā, and their unshakeable steadfastness during the Riddah*

## 1. Arrival & The Presentation of Surplus Zakāt

In 9 AH, during the Year of Delegations (**[[ʿĀm al-Wufūd]]**), a delegation of **thirteen men** from **Tujīb**—an esteemed branch of al-Sukūn belonging to the great confederation of Kindah in Hadramawt and Yemen—arrived in Medina (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:285). 

Unlike delegations seeking material stipends or political alliances, the men of Tujīb brought with them the obligatory zakāt on their wealth (*ṣadaqāti amwālihim*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:285). When they presented the wealth, the Prophet ﷺ rejoiced at their sincerity, welcomed them honorably, and instructed:
> «ردّوها فاقسموها على فقرائكم»
> *"Take it back and distribute it among your own poor!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:285).

The delegates replied:
> «يا رسول الله ما قدمنا عليك إلا بما فضل من فقرائنا»
> *"O Messenger of Allāh, we have only brought to you what remained in surplus after fully satisfying the needs of our own poor!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:285).

Hearing this extraordinary manifestation of communal social welfare, [[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]] ﵁ remarked with profound admiration:
> «يا رسول الله ما قدم علينا وفد من العرب بمثل ما وفد به هذا الحي من تجيب!»
> *"O Messenger of Allāh, no delegation among the Arabs has ever arrived before us like this clan of Tujīb!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:285).

The Prophet ﷺ replied:
> «إن الهدى بيد الله عز وجل، فمن أراد الله به خيرا شرح صدره للإيمان»
> *"Verily, guidance is entirely in the Hand of Allāh, Mighty and Majestic; whomever Allāh wills good for, He expands his chest to true faith!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:285).

## 2. Diligence in Sacred Knowledge & Prophetic Hospitality

The delegates remained in Medina for several days, devoting every waking hour to questioning the Prophet ﷺ regarding the Qurʾān, the obligatory rulings, and the Sunnah (*wa-jaʿalū yasʾalūnahu ʿan al-Qurʾāni wal-sunan*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:285). Seeing their genuine thirst for knowledge, the Prophet's ﷺ love for them deepened, and he commanded **[[Bilāl b. Rabāḥ]]** ﵁ to lodge them with the finest hospitality (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:285).

When they prepared to return after a short stay, someone asked why they were departing so quickly. They answered: *"We hasten back to our people behind us, that we may share with them the blessing of seeing the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ and conveying what he taught us!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:285). 

When they bid farewell, the Prophet ﷺ instructed Bilāl to bestow upon them diplomatic travel gifts more generous than those customarily given to other delegations (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:285).

## 3. The Pious Youth of Banū Abdhā

Before their departure, the Prophet ﷺ noticed an omission and asked: *«Hal baqiya minkum aḥad?»* (*"Has anyone among you remained behind?"*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:285). They replied: *"Only a youth whom we left behind guarding our mounts and baggage, the youngest among us in years."* The Prophet ﷺ commanded: *«Arsilūhu ilaynā»* (*"Send him to us!"*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:285).

The young boy from **Banū Abdhā** hurried into the presence of the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ and said:
> «يا رسول الله، إني غلام من بني أبذى من الرّهط الذين أتوك آنفا، فقضيت حوائجهم فاقض حاجتي يا رسول الله! إن حاجتي ليست كحاجة أصحابي... وإني والله ما أعملني من بلادي إلا أن تسأل الله عز وجل أن يغفر لي ويرحمني وأن يجعل غناي في قلبي!»
> *"O Messenger of Allāh, I am a boy of Banū Abdhā, from the company who came to you just now; you fulfilled their worldly needs, so fulfill my need, O Messenger of Allāh! My need is not like the needs of my companions... By Allāh, nothing urged me from my homeland except that you supplicate to Allāh the Exalted to forgive me, have mercy on me, and place my wealth in my heart!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:285).

The Prophet ﷺ smiled and fervently supplicated:
> «اللهم اغفر له وارحمه واجعل غناه في قلبه»
> *"O Allāh, forgive him, have mercy upon him, and place his richness in his heart!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:285).

The Prophet ﷺ then ordered that he be given the exact same generous honorary gift awarded to each mature adult of the delegation (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:285).

## 4. Fulfillment of the Duʿāʾ & Steadfastness in the Riddah

The following year during the Farewell Pilgrimage (**[[Ḥajjat al-Wadāʿ]]**, 10 AH), the men of Banū Abdhā met the Prophet ﷺ at Minā (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:285). The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ remembered the boy and inquired about him (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:285). The delegates exclaimed:
> «يا رسول الله: والله ما رأينا مثله قط ولا حدّثنا بأقنع منه بما رزقه الله! لو أن الناس اقتسموا الدنيا ما نظر نحوها ولا التفت إليها!»
> *"O Messenger of Allāh! By Allāh, we have never seen the like of him, nor has anyone ever told us of a soul more contented with what Allāh provides than him! Even if all humanity were dividing the riches of the entire world before him, he would not cast a single glance toward it!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:285).

The Prophet ﷺ praised Allāh and said: *«Al-ḥamdu lillāh, innī la-arjū an yamūta jamīʿan»* (*"Praise be to Allāh; I hope that he dies with a unified, untorn soul, unscattered across the valleys of worldly ambitions!"*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:285).

When the Prophet ﷺ passed away and widespread apostasy convulsed Yemen and Kindah during **[[Ḥurūb al-Riddah]]**, that very youth stood firm among his people (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:285). He preached passionately, reminding them of Allāh, the prophetic covenants, and the reality of faith, with such profound eloquence that **not a single soul from Tujīb apostatized** (*fa-lam yarjiʿ minhum aḥad*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:285). 

Caliph Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq ﵁ remembered the prophetic supplication, monitored his noble conduct, and wrote an official decree to Ziyād b. Labīd al-Anṣārī (the governor of Hadramawt) commending the youth of Banū Abdhā to the highest honor (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:286).

---

## 5. Strategic Significance

1. **Self-Sufficient Communal Welfare**: Tujīb established the ideal prophetic paradigm for regional zakāt administration: satisfying local poverty first before remitting surplus wealth to the central treasury (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:285).
2. **Spiritual Preeminence over Worldly Ambition**: The supplication granted to the youth demonstrated that spiritual richness (*ghinā al-qalb*) was the ultimate fruit of the Prophetic encounter (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:285).
3. **An Anchor of Orthodoxy in Southern Arabia**: Tujīb's total immunity from apostasy preserved a critical loyalist stronghold in Hadramawt that enabled Muslim commanders to quell the rebellion across southern Arabia (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:285–286).
