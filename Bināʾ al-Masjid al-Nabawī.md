# Bināʾ al-Masjid al-Nabawī (بناء المسجد النبوي الشريف)
*The Construction of the Prophet's Mosque and the Quarters of the Believers*

## 1. Overview
Upon entering Medina, the Prophet's camel (*al-Qaṣwāʾ*) knelt at a date-drying yard (*mirbad*) belonging to two orphan youths, Sahl and Suhayl sons of ʿAmr of [[Banū al-Najjār]]. The Prophet ﷺ insisted on purchasing the land at fair market value and designated it for the central sanctuary of the community.

The Prophet ﷺ labored alongside his companions with his own hands, hauling adobe bricks and singing verses of devotional perseverance:
> «اللَّهُمَّ لَا عَيْشَ إِلَّا عَيْشُ الْآخِرَهْ ... فَاغْفِرِ لِلْأَنْصَارِ وَالْمُهَاجِرَهْ!»
> *"O Allāh! There is no true life except the life of the Hereafter; so forgive the Anṣār and the Muhājirah!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:335).

## 2. Multi-Functional Center of the Ummah
The Mosque served as:
- The spiritual sanctuary for communal prayer.
- The administrative seat and diplomatic court for receiving tribal delegations.
- A residential academy for the destitute scholarly Companions known as **[[Ahl al-Ṣuffah]]** (including [[Abū Hurayrah]]).
- Adjoining apartments (*ḥujarāt*) for the Mothers of the Believers (*Ummahāt al-Muʾminīn*).
