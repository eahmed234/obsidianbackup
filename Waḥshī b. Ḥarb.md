# Waḥshī b. Ḥarb (وحشي بن حرب)
*Abū Dasmah al-Ḥabashī — Who Slew the Best of Men and the Worst of Men*

## 1. Identity & Role at Uḥud
An Abyssinian enslaved youth belonging to Jubayr b. Muṭʿim. He was famed for his deadly accuracy with the Abyssinian javelin (*ḥarbah*), which he threw according to the method of his homeland and seldom missed (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:183).

Prior to the departure of Quraysh for **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]**, Jubayr b. Muṭʿim offered him his liberty:
> *"Go forth with the people; if you kill Ḥamzah, the uncle of Muḥammad, in revenge for my uncle Ṭuʿaymah [slain at Badr], you are a free man"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:183).

Throughout the march, Hind bint ʿUtbah incited him relentlessly, saying whenever she passed him: *«O Abū Dasmah, heal [our grief] and heal yourself!»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:183).

## 2. The Slaying of Ḥamzah ﵁
At Uḥud, Waḥshī took no part in the general combat, seeking only **[[Ḥamzah b. ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib]]** ﵁. He concealed himself behind a boulder while Ḥamzah was cutting down champions with two swords (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:216–217). As Ḥamzah felled Sibāʿ b. ʿAbd al-ʿUzzā, Waḥshī balanced his javelin, aimed, and hurled it into Ḥamzah's lower abdomen, penetrating between his thighs (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:217). When Ḥamzah collapsed and died, Waḥshī retrieved his spear and returned directly to the camp, taking no further spoils or action, having sought solely his manumission (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:217).

## 3. Conversion to Islam
Following the Conquest of Makkah (**[[Fatḥ Makkah]]**), Waḥshī fled to al-Ṭāʾif. When the delegation of Thaqīf resolved to surrender to the Prophet ﷺ, Waḥshī was counseled that the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ never executed anyone who accepted Islam (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:217).

Waḥshī arrived in Medina and stood before the Prophet ﷺ, reciting the Shahādah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:217). When the Prophet ﷺ recognized him, he requested: *«Sit and recount to me how you killed Ḥamzah.»* After hearing the full account, the Prophet ﷺ, deeply sorrowed, said to him:
> «وَيْحَكَ! غَيِّبْ وَجْهَكَ عَنِّي فَلَا أَرَاكَ!»
> *"Woe to you! Conceal your face from me, so that I may not see you!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:217).

Thereafter, Waḥshī deliberately avoided entering the direct gaze of the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ until his passing (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:217).

## 4. Atonement at the Battle of al-Yamāmah
During the Wars of Apostasy, Waḥshī joined the Muslim army against the false prophet Musaylimah al-Kadhdhāb, taking with him the very same javelin with which he had killed Ḥamzah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:217).

On the battlefield of al-Yamāmah, Waḥshī spotted Musaylimah, aimed his javelin, and struck him squarely, while simultaneously an Anṣārī warrior (identified as ʿAbdullāh b. Zayd b. ʿĀṣim) struck Musaylimah with his sword (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:217–218). Waḥshī used to declare:
> «قَتَلْتُ خَيْرَ النَّاسِ، وَقَتَلْتُ شَرَّ النَّاسِ!»
> *"I killed the best of mankind [after the Prophet ﷺ], and I killed the worst of mankind!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:217).
