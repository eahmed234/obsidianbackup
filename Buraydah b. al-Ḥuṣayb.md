# Buraydah b. al-Ḥuṣayb (بريدة بن الحصيب)
*Standard-Bearer of the Hijrah, Veteran of Sixteen Expeditions, and Last Surviving Companion in Khurasan*

> «بَرَدَ أَمْرُنَا وَصَلُحَ … سَلِمْنَا … خَرَجَ سَهْمُكَ يَا أَبَا بَكْرٍ!»
> *"Our affair has cooled and been set right! … We have been granted peace! … Your arrow of triumph has come forth, O Abū Bakr!"*
> — Prophetic auspicious omen (*tafāʾul*) upon meeting Buraydah b. al-Ḥuṣayb al-Aslamī (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:252)

---

## 1. Lineage, Clan, and Name

### Lineage
He is **Buraydah b. al-Ḥuṣayb b. ʿAbdillāh b. al-Ḥārith b. al-Aʿraj b. Saʿd b. Rizāḥ b. ʿAdiyy b. Sahm b. Māzin b. al-Ḥārith b. Salāmān b. Aslam b. Afṣā al-Aslamī** (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:418, no. 632). 

Through Banū Sahm of the tribe of **Aslam**, he belonged to the broader confederacy of Khuzāʿah/Afṣā. Abū ʿAlī al-Ṭūsī Aḥmad b. ʿUthmān (companion of Ibn al-Mubārak) related that his original given name was **ʿĀmir**, and *Buraydah* was an honorific nickname that came to supersede it (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:418).

### Kunya
- **Abū ʿAbdillāh** (أبو عبد الله) — the most recognized kunya (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:251).
- Also reported as **Abū Sāsān** (أبو ساسان).

## 2. The Meeting on the Hijrah Road and Auspicious Omen (1 AH)

When the Prophet ﷺ and **[[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]]** ﵁ drew near to Medina during **[[al-Hijrah ilā al-Madīnah]]**, they passed by al-Ghamīm. Quraysh had posted a bounty of one hundred camels for the capture of the Prophet ﷺ dead or alive, prompting tribal leaders along the coastal routes to watch the roads. 

Buraydah set out with **seventy men** of his kinfolk from Banū Sahm of Aslam to intercept the travelers. Upon intercepting the small caravan, the Prophet ﷺ turned to him and asked:
> *"Who are you?"*
> He replied: *"Buraydah [meaning 'coolness' or 'hail']."*
> The Prophet ﷺ smiled, turned to Abū Bakr, and drew an auspicious omen (*fāʾl ḥasan*):
> «بَرَدَ أَمْرُنَا وَصَلُحَ!» (*"Our affair has cooled and been set right!"*)
> 
> The Prophet ﷺ then inquired: *"From which tribe?"*
> He replied: *"From Aslam [meaning 'peace' or 'submission']."*
> The Prophet ﷺ said to Abū Bakr:
> «سَلِمْنَا!» (*"We have been granted peace and safety!"*)
> 
> The Prophet ﷺ asked: *"From which branch?"*
> He answered: *"From Banū Sahm [meaning 'arrow' or 'allotment of victory']."*
> The Prophet ﷺ exclaimed:
> «خَرَجَ سَهْمُكَ يَا أَبَا بَكْرٍ!» (*"Your arrow of victory has emerged, O Abū Bakr!"*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:252).

Buraydah then asked: *"And who are you?"* The Prophet ﷺ replied: *"I am Muḥammad b. ʿAbdillāh, the Messenger of Allāh."* Struck by the beauty, majesty, and divine composure of the Prophet ﷺ, Buraydah immediately proclaimed:
> «أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَأَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا عَبْدُهُ وَرَسُولُهُ»
> *"I bear witness that there is no god but Allāh, and that Muḥammad is His slave and His Messenger!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:252).

All **seventy men** accompanying Buraydah embraced Islam together on the spot. Buraydah joyfully declared: *"Praise be to Allāh Who guided Banū Sahm to Islam willingly without coercion!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:252).

### The Improvised Banner of the Hijrah
The following morning, Buraydah insisted that the Prophet ﷺ must not enter Medina as an ordinary traveler, saying:
> «يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، لَا تَدْخُلِ الْمَدِينَةَ إِلَّا وَمَعَكَ لِوَاءٌ!»
> *"O Messenger of Allāh, do not enter Medina except that you have a military standard (liwāʾ) with you!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:252).

Buraydah untied his white turban (*ʿimāmah*), bound it to the tip of his lance (*rumḥ*), and marched ahead of the Prophet ﷺ as his standard-bearer until the Prophet ﷺ made his triumphant entry into the dwellings of Medina (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:252).

## 3. Military Campaigns & Sīrah Milestones

Buraydah remained in the territory of his tribe at al-Ghamīm through the period of **[[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]]** and **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]**, before formally relocating to Medina (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:418). 

In *Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī* and *Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim*, it is authentically established from Buraydah himself that he campaigned directly alongside the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ in **sixteen military expeditions** (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:418).

Notable Sīrah contributions include:
- **[[Ṣulḥ al-Ḥudaybiyah]] & [[Bayʿat al-Riḍwān]]** (6 AH): Marched with the Prophet ﷺ in the vanguard, swearing allegiance beneath the tree (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:38).
- **[[Ghazwat Khaybar]]** (7 AH): Witnessed the siege of the Jewish citadels. When the Muslims suffered from severe fevers after consuming unripened dates, Buraydah recorded the Prophet's ﷺ medical directive to cool water in leather skins (*al-shinān*) between the two calls to prayer (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:121). He was also present at the storming of Fort al-Ṣaʿb b. Muʿādh and witnessed **[[ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib]]** ﵁ breaching the gate of Khaybar (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:128).
- **Campaign to Yemen**: Dispatched alongside ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib ﵁ to Yemen, transmitting foundational hadiths regarding leadership and loyalty.

## 4. Frontier Jihad in Khurasan and Death in Marw (63 AH)

Following the passing of the Prophet ﷺ, Buraydah settled in Basra upon its founding during the reign of **[[ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb]]** ﵁ (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:418).

During the Caliphate of **[[ʿUthmān b. ʿAffān]]** ﵁, Buraydah dedicated his remaining life to frontier jihad along the eastern expanses of Islam. He marched with the armies of conquest into **Khurasan** and eventually settled in the historic oasis city of **Marw** (مرو, in modern Turkmenistan) (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:418).

### Death and Burial
Buraydah lived in Marw until his death in the year **63 AH** during the Caliphate of Yazīd b. Muʿāwiyah (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:418). He was the last of the Prophet's ﷺ Companions to pass away in Khurasan. He requested in his final illness that two green palm stalks (*jarīdatayn*) be placed upon his grave, following the Sunnah of the Prophet ﷺ.

He narrated over one hundred and fifty hadiths from the Prophet ﷺ, transmitted by his sons ʿAbdullāh and Sulaymān, as well as renowned Tābiʿīn of Basra and Khurasan (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:418).
