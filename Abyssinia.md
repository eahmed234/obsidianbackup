# Abyssinia (بلاد الحبشة)
*The Realm of Righteous Sanctuary Across the Red Sea*

## 1. Topography & Prophetic Description
Abyssinia (*Bilād al-Ḥabashah*, corresponding to the ancient Christian Empire of Aksum in the Horn of Africa) was the chosen land of refuge across the Red Sea for the persecuted early Muslim community in Mecca (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:382).

When persecution intensified in the fifth year of Nubūwah, the Prophet ﷺ directed his companions to migrate there, praising its sovereign ruler **[[al-Najāshī (Aṣḥamah)]]** ﵁:
> «لَوْ خَرَجْتُمْ إِلَى أَرْضِ الْحَبَشَةِ، فَإِنَّ بِهَا مَلِكًا لَا يُظْلَمُ عِنْدَهُ أَحَدٌ، وَهِيَ أَرْضُ صِدْقٍ، حَتَّى يَجْعَلَ اللَّهُ لَكُمْ فَرَجًا مِمَّا أَنْتُمْ فِيهِ!»
> *"If you were to depart to the land of Abyssinia, for truly in it is a king in whose presence no one is wronged, and it is a land of truthfulness, until Allāh grants you relief from what you are suffering!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:382).

## 2. Key Sīrah Milestones
- **The First & Second Migrations**: The first cohort of twelve men and four women (including **[[ʿUthmān b. ʿAffān]]** and **[[Ruqayyah bint Rasūlillāh]]**) sailed from the port of Shuʿaybah in Rajab of the 5th year of Nubūwah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:382). The second larger cohort, comprising eighty-three men and nineteen women led by **[[Jaʿfar b. Abī Ṭālib]]** ﵁, followed shortly thereafter (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:385).
- **The Royal Audience at Aksum**: At the imperial court, Jaʿfar recited Sūrat Maryam before the Negus and his bishops. Al-Najāshī wept, rejected Meccan bribes, and granted the believers sovereign protection (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:388–390).
- **Return to Khaybar (7 AH)**: The remaining emigrants dwelled securely in Abyssinia until 7 AH, when the Negus outfitted two ships carrying Jaʿfar and the companions to rejoin the Prophet ﷺ at **[[Ghazwat Khaybar]]** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:135).
