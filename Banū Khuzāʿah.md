# Banū Khuzāʿah (بنو خزاعة)
*The Confidants of Tihāmah — Ancient Confederates and Allies of the Prophetic Mission*

## 1. Lineage & Territory
An ancient Arabian tribe inhabiting the valleys and lowlands of Tihāmah between Makkah and Medina, along the Red Sea littoral (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:310).

## 2. Role in the Sīrah
Throughout the Prophetic biography, the people of Khuzāʿah maintained an unbroken strategic and affectionate alliance with the Prophet ﷺ. As al-Ṣāliḥī records:
> *"Khuzāʿah — both their Muslims and their polytheists — were a repository of sincere counsel and confidentiality (*ʿaybat nuṣḥ*) for the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ in Tihāmah, bound in agreement with him and never concealing from him anything that transpired there"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:310).

During the march of Quraysh to **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]**, ʿAmr b. Sālim al-Khuzāʿī rode swiftly from Dhū Ṭuwā to inform the Prophet ﷺ of the Meccan mobilization (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:183). Following Uḥud, their chieftain **[[Maʿbad b. Abī Maʿbad al-Khuzāʿī]]** actively misled the Meccan army at al-Rawḥāʾ, frustrating Abū Sufyān's planned counter-attack on Medina (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:310–311).

Their treaty with the Muslims later served as the critical legal catalyst during the Truce of Ḥudaybiyah that precipitated **[[Fatḥ Makkah]]** (8 AH).
