# Ḥamrāʾ al-Asad (حمراء الأسد)
*The Plain of the Five Hundred Fires — Ground of Prophetic Resolve and Deterrence*

## 1. Topography & Distance
A desert locality situated approximately **eight miles** south of **[[al-Madīnah al-Munawwarah]]**, on the left side of the route leading past Dhū al-Ḥulayfah toward Makkah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:313). Its name derives from the feminine of *aḥmar* (red) attributed to the lion (*al-asad*).

## 2. Historical Significance
Site of **[[Ghazwat Ḥamrāʾ al-Asad]]** (8 Shawwāl 3 AH), where the Prophet ﷺ encamped with his wounded veterans of **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]** to demonstrate psychological deterrence against Quraysh (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:308–311). Across its heights, the Muslim army lit **five hundred fires** every night that were visible from great distances, striking terror into the retreating Meccan forces under **[[Abū Sufyān b. Ḥarb]]** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:310).

Two Muslim scouts from Aslam — Salīṭ and Nuʿmān, sons of Sufyān b. Ṭalq — were slain here by the Meccan rear-guard and buried together in a single grave by the Prophet ﷺ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:310).
