# Asʿad b. Zurārah (أسعد بن زرارة)
*Abū Umāmah — Chief of the Banū al-Najjār and Pioneer of the Anṣār*

## 1. Precedence Among the Anṣār
Al-Ṣāliḥī transmits (on the authority of [[Ibn Saʿd]]) that Asʿad came from Shām as a merchant with
forty men of his people, and there saw a dream foretelling the coming of a prophet — placing him
among the very first of Yathrib to be turned toward Islam (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:133).

## 2. Key Sīrah Milestones
- Among the earliest Anṣār to accept Islam and one of the *nuqabāʾ* (deputies) of the pledges at
  al-ʿAqabah — see [[Bayʿat al-ʿAqabah al-Ūlā]] and [[Bayʿat al-ʿAqabah al-Thāniyah]].
- Hosted and supported **[[Muṣʿab b. ʿUmayr]]** ﵁ during his mission teaching Islam in Yathrib
  before the Hijrah.
- Served as *naqīb* (chief) of the **[[Banū al-Najjār]]**.

## 3. Death and the Prophet's ﷺ Response
He died early in **1 AH**, shortly after the Hijrah. When the Banū al-Najjār came to the Prophet ﷺ
asking him to appoint a replacement as their chief, he declined to name one over them, answering:
*«أَنْتُمْ أَخْوَالِي»* — *"You are my maternal uncles"* — and took their leadership upon himself
(*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:530).
