# Dhū al-Ḥulayfah (ذو الحليفة)
*The Prophetic Mīqāt of Medina and the Sacred Oasis of al-Shajarah*

## 1. Topography & Sanctified Designation
**Dhū al-Ḥulayfah** (named after the *ḥalfa* plant) is the celebrated oasis and waystation located six miles south of the Prophet's Mosque in Medina along the overland highway to Makkah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:198, 8:79).

It was formally designated by the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ as the canonical pilgrimage entry point (**Mīqāt**) for the people of Medina and all travelers passing through their territory (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:451–454).

## 2. Historical & Ritual Significance in the Sīrah

### The Waystation of Ihram and Sacrificial Consecration
During his sacred journeys—the expedition of **[[Ṣulḥ al-Ḥudaybiyah]]**, the compensatory pilgrimage of **[[ʿUmrat al-Qaḍāʾ]]**, and the Farewell Pilgrimage (**[[Ḥajjat al-Wadāʿ]]**)—the Prophet ﷺ routinely departed Medina after the Ẓuhr prayer, traversed the valley, halted at Dhū al-Ḥulayfah to rest, and assumed the state of *iḥrām* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:190, 8:451–454).

At Dhū al-Ḥulayfah, the Prophet ﷺ performed the ritual washing (*ghusl*), scented his beard, performed two rakʿahs in the mosque beside the acacia tree (*Masjid al-Shajarah*), garlanded (*taqlīd*) and marked (*ishʿār*) the sacrificial camels (*hady*), and mounted his camel al-Qaṣwāʾ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:190, 8:451–454). As she rose upright and ascended the elevation of al-Baydāʾ, he raised his voice in the universal chant of the Talbiyah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:453–454).

### The Birth of Muḥammad b. Abī Bakr
While encamped at Dhū al-Ḥulayfah on the journey to **[[Ḥajjat al-Wadāʿ]]**, Asmāʾ bint ʿUmays ﵂, wife of **[[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]]** ﵁, gave birth to Muḥammad b. Abī Bakr. She sent word to the Prophet ﷺ inquiring regarding the ritual regulations for a woman in post-natal bleeding; the Prophet ﷺ instructed her to perform ritual purification, wrap herself with a cloth, enter into *iḥrām*, and perform all pilgrimage rites save circumambulating the House (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:453).
