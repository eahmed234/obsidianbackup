# al-Uṣayrim (الأصيرم) — ʿAmr b. Thābit ﵁
*The Ashhali Martyr Who Entered Paradise Without Having Offered a Single Prayer*

## 1. Lineage & Background
- **Ancestry**: ʿAmr b. Thābit b. Waqsh (or Uqaysh) b. Zughbah b. Zaʿūrāʾ b. ʿAbd al-Ashhal b. Jusham b. al-Khazraj b. ʿAmr b. Mālik b. al-Aws al-Anṣārī al-Awsī al-Ashhalī (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:501, no. 5797). Known by the diminutive **al-Uṣayrim** (*Uṣayrim Banī ʿAbd al-Ashhal*).
- **Family**: Son of the aged martyr **Thābit b. Waqsh** ﵁ and nephew of Rifāʿah b. Waqsh ﵁.
- **Reluctance to Islam**: Before Uḥud, he resisted Islam due to pre-Islamic usury debts (*ribā*) owed to him in Jāhiliyyah, which he was reluctant to forfeit until he had collected them (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:501).

## 2. Conversion and Battle at Uḥud (3 AH)
- On the morning of **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]**, while the Muslim army was departing Medina, Allāh cast faith into the heart of al-Uṣayrim (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:501; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:212).
- He enquired after his cousins and clansmen of Banū ʿAbd al-Ashhal: *«Where are my cousins?»* People replied: *«They are at Uḥud.»* He donned his coat of mail, mounted his horse, drew his sword, and galloped straight to the battlefield (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:501).
- When the Muslims saw him arriving, they shouted defensively: *«Keep away from us, O ʿAmr!»* believing he was still an idolater. He called out: *«Indeed, I have believed in Allāh and His Messenger and have submitted!»* (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:501).
- He plunged directly into the thick of combat, battling until he collapsed under mortal wounds.

## 3. The Prophetic Testimony of Paradise
- As the men of Banū ʿAbd al-Ashhal combed the field after the battle searching for their wounded, they found him gasping his final breaths. Stunned, they asked: *«What brought you here, O ʿAmr? Was it tribal zeal (*ḥamiyyah*) for your clan, or desire for Islam (*raghbatan fī al-Islām*)?»* (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:501; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:212).
- He replied: *«Rather, it was longing for Islam! I believed in Allāh and His Messenger, fought alongside the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ, and received what you see.»* He passed away immediately in their arms (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:501).
- When they informed the Prophet ﷺ, he declared: *«Indeed, he is among the people of Paradise!»* (*inna-hu lamin ahli al-Jannah*) (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:501; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:212).
- **Abū Hurayrah's Riddle**: **[[Abū Hurayrah]]** ﵁ used to say to the Companions: *«Narrate to me about a man who entered Paradise without having prayed a single prayer for Allāh!»* When they could not answer, he would reveal: *«It is al-Uṣayrim of Banū ʿAbd al-Ashhal: ʿAmr b. Thābit»* (*Ṣaḥīḥ Ibn Ḥibbān*; *al-Iṣābah*, 4:501).
