# Bashīr b. Saʿd (بشير بن سعد)
*Badri Pioneer, First of the Anṣār to Pledge Allegiance at Saqīfah, and Martyr of ʿAyn al-Tamr*

## 1. Lineage and Family

Bashīr b. Saʿd b. Thaʿlabah b. Julās b. Zayd b. Mālik b. Thaʿlabah b. Kaʿb b. al-Khazraj al-Anṣārī al-Khazrajī (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:442). He was a distinguished early convert from the Khazraj who took part in the Second Pledge of al-ʿAqabah and fought at [[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā|Badr]], [[Ghazwat Uḥud|Uḥud]], and all subsequent campaigns alongside the Messenger of Allah ﷺ (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:442).

He was married to ʿAmrah bint Rawāḥah al-Anṣāriyyah, the sister of the celebrated poet and commander [[ʿAbdullāh b. Rawāḥah]] ﵁ (*al-Iṣābah*, 8:244). Through her, he was the father of the prominent Companion al-Nuʿmān b. Bashīr ﵁, the first child born to the Anṣār after the Hijrah (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:442, 6:440).

## 2. Legal Precedent on Parental Equity

In *Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī* and *Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim*, it is narrated that Bashīr b. Saʿd gave a special gift—a servant—to his son al-Nuʿmān. His wife ʿAmrah bint Rawāḥah insisted: *"I shall not be pleased until you take the Messenger of Allah ﷺ as a witness to this."* 

Bashīr came to the Prophet ﷺ and said: *"O Messenger of Allah! I have bestowed a gift upon my son from ʿAmrah bint Rawāḥah, and she told me to take you as a witness."* The Prophet ﷺ asked:
> «أكل ولدك نحلت مثل هذا؟»
> *"Have you gifted all your children the like of this?"*

Bashīr replied in the negative. The Prophet ﷺ declared:
> «فاتقوا الله واعدلوا بين أولادكم»
> *"Fear Allah and be just among your children!"*

He said: *"Take not me as a witness, for I do not bear witness to injustice (*jawr*)."* Bashīr immediately retracted the gift (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:442, 8:244).

## 3. Expeditions of Fadak and Yemen (7 AH)

In Shaʿbān of 7 AH, the Messenger of Allah ﷺ dispatched Bashīr b. Saʿd as commander of a reconnaissance detachment of thirty men to Banū Murrah at [[Fadak]] (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:132). They engaged the enemy, who were reinforced by superior numbers. Bashīr fought tenaciously until his companions fell, and he was struck with severe wounds, left for dead on the battlefield until he recovered and returned to Medina (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:132).

Later in Shawwāl of 7 AH, the Prophet ﷺ appointed him commander of three hundred men towards Yaman and Jabbār (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:134). Bashīr marched covertly by night and concealed his force by day, successfully dispersing the enemy gatherings and capturing their livestock, returning safely to Medina (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:134).

## 4. The Decisive Pledge at Saqīfah (11 AH)

Following the passing of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, when the Anṣār assembled at [[Saqīfat Banī Sāʿidah]] to determine the succession, Bashīr b. Saʿd stepped forward and delivered an impassioned appeal urging unity behind Quraysh. 

When [[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]] ﵁ presented [[ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb]] ﵁ and [[Abū ʿUbaydah b. al-Jarrāḥ]] ﵁, Bashīr b. Saʿd was the very first man among the Anṣār to step forward and clasp the hand of Abū Bakr, swearing the oath of allegiance (*bayʿah*), which dissolved tribal factionalism and secured consensus across the community (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:442).

## 5. Campaigns in Iraq and Martyrdom (12 AH)

During the Caliphate of [[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]] ﵁, Bashīr joined the armies dispatched to Iraq under the supreme command of [[Khālid b. al-Walīd]] ﵁ (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:442). 

In 12 AH, while fighting at the Battle of ʿAyn al-Tamr alongside Khālid, Bashīr was struck down in combat and achieved martyrdom (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:442).
