# Bashīr b. Saʿd (بشير بن سعد الأنصاري)
*Badri Veteran of the Khazraj, Expeditionary Commander, and First of the Anṣār to Pledge at the Saqīfah*

> [!NOTE] Primary Sourcing & Biographical Provenance
> Sourced directly from *al-Iṣābah fī Tamyīz al-Ṣaḥābah*, 1:442 (entry 694), and *Subul al-Hudā war-Rashād*, 6:132, 134, 140.

---

## 1. Lineage & Early Islam

Bashīr b. Saʿd was an eminent leader of the Anṣār belonging to the tribe of al-Khazraj (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:442).

- **Full Paternal Lineage**: Bashīr b. Saʿd b. Thaʿlabah b. Julās b. Zayd b. Mālik b. Thaʿlabah b. Kaʿb b. al-Khazraj al-Anṣārī al-Khazrajī, Abū al-Nuʿmān (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:442).
- **Progeny**: He was the father of the prominent Companion **al-Nuʿmān b. Bashīr** ﵁, the first child born to the Anṣār after the Hijrah (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:442).
- **Pledge & Badr**: Bashīr participated in the Second Pledge of al-ʿAqabah and fought with distinction at **[[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]]**, **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]**, and the subsequent major campaigns (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:442).

---

## 2. Command of Expeditions

The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ entrusted Bashīr b. Saʿd with the command of key military expeditions:
- **Sariyyah to Banū Murrah at Fadak (Shaʿbān, 7 AH)**: Dispatched at the head of thirty men against Banū Murrah; his men fought valiantly against overwhelming numbers until nightfall; wounded severely, Bashīr was sheltered by a local ally and returned safely to Medina (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:132; *al-Iṣābah*, 1:442).
- **Sariyyah to Yemen and Jibār (Shawwāl, 7 AH)**: Dispatched at the head of three hundred Muslims against gathering bedouin forces of Ghaṭafān; he dispersed the assembly, secured significant spoils, and safely returned to Medina (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:134).

---

## 3. The Historic Stand at Saqīfat Banī Sāʿidah (11 AH)

Following the demise of the Prophet ﷺ, the Anṣār assembled at **[[Saqīfat Banī Sāʿidah]]** to deliberate over leadership (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:442). 

When discussions grew intense and arguments arose between the Muhājirūn and Anṣār, Bashīr b. Saʿd stepped forward and delivered an impassioned, unifying address to the Anṣār, reminding them of the Prophet's lineage and unity. He then stepped forward and was the very first Companion from among the Anṣār to clasp the hand of **[[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]]** ﵁ and swear allegiance, breaking the deadlock and securing the consensus of the community (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:442).

---

## 4. Martyrdom (12 AH)

During the caliphate of Abū Bakr, Bashīr joined the Iraqi campaign under the supreme command of **[[Khālid b. al-Walīd]]** ﵁ (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:442).
- In the year twelve AH, during the fierce assault on the fortress of **ʿAyn al-Tamr**, Bashīr b. Saʿd fought with exemplary gallantry and fell as a martyr in the path of Allāh (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:442).
