# Dūmat al-Jandal (دومة الجندل)
*The Gateway to Syria and the Northern Arabian Frontier*

## 1. Overview
A strategic fortified city and commercial hub in northern Arabia. Witnessed the successful diplomatic mission of **[[Sariyyat Dūmat al-Jandal]]** led by **[[ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. ʿAwf]]**, which brought King al-Aṣbagh b. ʿAmr and Banū Kalb into the fold of Islam.
