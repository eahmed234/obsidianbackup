# al-Ṭāʾif (الطائف)
*The Mountain Stronghold of Thaqīf, City of Orchards, and Sanctuary of Wajj*

> [!NOTE] Canonical Sourcing
> Topographical description and historical narratives extracted from *Subul al-Hudā war-Rashād* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:438–442, 5:413, 6:226–227, 302).

---

## 1. Topography & Geographic Setting

- **Location & Climate**: A high fertile mountain plateau situated southeast of Makkah in the Sarat mountains of the Ḥijāz, famous across pre-Islamic Arabia for its abundant fresh springs, cool climate, grape orchards (*ḥablāt*), and lush vineyards (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:438, 442, 5:413).
- **Wādī Wajj**: The principal fertile valley encompassed by al-Ṭāʾif is **Wajj** (وجّ), situated between the mountains of al-Muḥtariq and al-Uḥayḥidayn (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:302).
- **Dominant Inhabitants**: The ancestral citadel and homeland of the powerful tribe of [[Thaqīf]] (Thaqīf b. Munabbih b. Bakr b. Hawāzin), divided into the factions of Banū Mālik and al-Aḥlāf (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:351, 6:226).
- **Pre-Islamic Citadel**: Protected by massive stone walls and fortresses (*ḥuṣūn*), within which stood the grand temple of the idol **al-Lāt** (also referred to as *al-Rabbah* or *al-Ṭāghiyah*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:413, 6:226).

---

## 2. Key Sīrah Milestones

- **The Journey of Sorrow (10th Year of Prophethood)**: Following the death of [[Abū Ṭālib]] and [[Khadījah bint Khuwaylid]], the Prophet [[Muḥammad b. ʻAbdullah|Muḥammad]] ﷺ journeyed on foot to al-Ṭāʾif accompanied by [[Zayd b. Ḥārithah]] in Shawwāl of the 10th year of Prophethood, seeking refuge and tribal protection (*manʿah*) from the chiefs of Thaqīf—the brothers ʿAbd Yālayl, Masʿūd, and Ḥabīb (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:438).
  - The chieftains rejected him insolently and incited the city's rabble and slaves to pelt him with stones until his feet bled and Zayd was wounded in the head (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:438–442).
  - Taking refuge in the vineyard of ʿUtbah and Shaybah sons of Rabīʿah, the Christian youth [[ʿAddās]] recognized his prophethood (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:439, 442).
  - At Qarn al-Thaʿālib, the Angel of the Mountains offered to crush the persecutors beneath the two mountains of Makkah (*al-Akhshabān*), to which the Prophet ﷺ famously replied:
    > «بل أرجو أن يخرج اللَّه عز وجل من أصلابهم من يعبد اللَّه عز وجل ولا يشرك به شيئا»
    > *"Rather, I hope that Allah will bring forth from their loins those who worship Allah alone, associating nothing with Him."* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:440).
- **The Siege of al-Ṭāʾif (8 AH)**: Following the victory of [[Ghazwat Ḥunayn]], the defeated remnants of Hawāzin and Thaqīf retreated behind the fortifications of al-Ṭāʾif. The Prophet ﷺ laid siege to the city during [[Ghazwat al-Ṭāʾif]], employing catapults (*manjanīq*) and siege-troughs (*dabbābah*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:413).
- **Delegation of Thaqīf & Demolition of al-Lāt (9 AH)**: In 9 AH, [[Wafd Thaqīf]] arrived in Madīnah and accepted Islam. The Prophet ﷺ dispatched [[al-Mughīrah b. Shuʿbah]] and [[Abū Sufyān b. Ḥarb]] to al-Ṭāʾif, where they demolished the idol of al-Lāt to the ground (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:226–227).
