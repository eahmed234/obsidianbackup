# al-Najāshī: Aṣḥamah b. Abjar (النجاشي أصحمة بن أبجر) ﵁
*The Righteous Sovereign of Abyssinia and Royal Protector of the Early Believers*

## 1. Lineage & Early Reign
His personal name was Aṣḥamah b. Abjar (meaning *ʿAṭiyyah*, "Gift", in the Ge'ez tongue), holding the imperial royal title of **al-Najāshī** (the Negus), hereditary emperor of the Christian Kingdom of Aksum in Abyssinia (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:385).

When severe persecution in Mecca threatened the survival of the infant Muslim community, the Prophet ﷺ instructed his companions to seek refuge across the Red Sea, praising the Negus:
> «إِنَّ بِأَرْضِ الْحَبَشَةِ مَلِكًا لَا يُظْلَمُ أَحَدٌ عِنْدَهُ، فَالْحَقُوا بِبِلَادِهِ حَتَّى يَجْعَلَ اللَّهُ لَكُمْ فَرَجًا وَمَخْرَجًا مِمَّا أَنْتُمْ فِيهِ»
> *"Verily, in the land of Abyssinia there is a king in whose realm no one is wronged; join his realm until Allāh grants you relief and a way out from your affliction!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:385).

## 2. Sanctuary & Hearing the Qurʾān
During [[al-Hijrah ilā al-Ḥabashah]], over eighty Muslim emigrants arrived at his court. When Quraysh dispatched **[[ʿAmr b. al-ʿĀṣ]]** and ʿAbdullāh b. Abī Rabīʿah laden with gifts to bribe his generals and demand the refugees' return, the Negus summoned the Muslims to hear their testimony (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:388–389).

**[[Jaʿfar b. Abī Ṭālib]]** ﵁ delivered a masterly exposition of the darkness of Jāhiliyyah, the moral transformation brought by Islam, and recited the opening of Sūrat Maryam describing the miraculous birth of ʿĪsā (Jesus) ﵊ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:389). Al-Najāshī wept until his beard was soaked, and his bishops wept until their scrolls were wet. The Negus declared:
> «إِنَّ هَذَا وَالَّذِي جَاءَ بِهِ مُوسَى لَيَخْرُجُ مِنْ مِشْكَاةٍ وَاحِدَةٍ!»
> *"Truly, this and that which Moses brought emanate from the very same niche of divine light!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:389).

He expelled the Qurashī delegates, returned their gifts, and granted the Muslims perpetual sanctuary. When Quraysh returned the next day accusing Muslims of uttering grave things concerning Jesus, Jaʿfar answered: *“We say of him that he is the servant of Allāh, His Messenger, His spirit, and His word cast unto Maryam.”* The Negus picked up a small twig from the floor and proclaimed: *“By God, Jesus the son of Mary does not exceed what you have said by the length of this twig!”* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:390).

## 3. Secret Islam & Prophetic Letters
Al-Najāshī embraced Islam at the hands of Jaʿfar, testifying to the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:390). In 7 AH, the Prophet ﷺ dispatched ʿAmr b. Umayyah al-Ḍamrī with an official letter; the Negus placed the letter upon his eyes, descended from his throne onto the floor in humility, and arranged the two ships that conveyed Jaʿfar and the remaining emigrants safely back to the Prophet ﷺ at **[[Ghazwat Khaybar]]** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:135; 11:365).

## 4. Demise & Funeral Prayer in Absentia
The Negus passed away in Aksum in Rajab 9 AH. On the very day he died, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ in Medina received divine tidings and informed his companions:
> «قَدْ مَاتَ الْيَوْمَ رَجُلٌ صَالِحٌ، فَقُومُوا فَصَلُّوا عَلَى أَخِيكُمْ أَصْحَمَةَ»
> *"Today a righteous man has died; arise and perform the funeral prayer over your brother Aṣḥamah!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:400–401; 5:485; *Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī*, no. 1327).
The Prophet ﷺ led the companions out to the prayer ground (*al-Muṣallā*), lined them in rows, and performed the funeral prayer in absentia (*Ṣalāt al-Janāzah ʿalā al-Ghāʾib*) with four Takbīrs, asking forgiveness for him (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:401). It was related that celestial light continued to be seen emanating above his tomb in Abyssinia (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:402).
