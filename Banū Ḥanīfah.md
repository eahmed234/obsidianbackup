# Banū Ḥanīfah (بنو حنيفة)
*The Agrarian Masters of al-Yamāmah, the Chieftaincy of Thumāmah b. Uthāl, and the Trial of the False Prophet*

## 1. Lineage & Territory
**Banū Ḥanīfah** were a powerful, sedentary Arab tribe belonging to the great confederation of Bakr b. Wāʾil of Rabīʿah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:71, 6:326).

They inhabited the vast and fertile agricultural oases of **[[al-Yamāmah]]** in central-eastern Arabia, controlling the vital grain supply that sustained the towns of the Ḥijāz, including Makkah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:72).

## 2. Role in the Sīrah

### The Capture and Conversion of Thumāmah b. Uthāl
During the expedition of Muḥammad b. Maslamah ﵁ to al-Qurṭāʾ in 6 AH, a Muslim scouting party captured **[[Thumāmah b. Uthāl]]** ﵁, the paramount chieftain of Banū Ḥanīfah, who was traveling toward Makkah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:71).

Brought to Medina, he was bound to one of the pillars of the Prophet's Mosque. For three consecutive days, the Prophet ﷺ visited him, asking: *«What do you have, O Thumāmah?»* Thumāmah replied: *«If you execute me, you execute one with blood [to avenge]; if you show favor, you show favor to one who is grateful; and if you desire wealth, ask whatever you wish!»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:72).

On the third day, the Prophet ﷺ commanded: *«Release Thumāmah!»* Overwhelmed by Prophetic magnanimity, Thumāmah proceeded to a nearby date palm grove, bathed, returned to the mosque, and proclaimed the testimony of faith (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:72). He subsequently proceeded to Makkah and performed the first Islamic ʿumrah within the Kaʿbah, placing the pagan Quraysh under an economic embargo until the Prophet ﷺ requested him to permit grain shipments to reach them (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:72).

### The Delegation of Banū Ḥanīfah & Musaylimah's Claim (9–10 AH)
During the Year of Delegations (**[[ʿĀm al-Wufūd]]**), a delegation of Banū Ḥanīfah arrived in Medina (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:326).

Among them was Musaylimah b. Ḥabīb al-Kadhdhāb (the Liar) ☠. When the delegation met the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ—who sat holding a palm stalk (*ʿusīb*)—Musaylimah insolently demanded that authority after the Prophet be shared with him or bequeathed to his tribe. The Prophet ﷺ declared:
> «لَوْ سَأَلْتَنِي هَذَا الْعَسِيبَ الَّذِي فِي يَدِي مَا أَعْطَيْتُكَهُ!»
> *"Even if you were to ask me for this dry palm branch in my hand, I would not give it to you!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:326).

Upon returning to al-Yamāmah, Musaylimah apostatized, claimed partnership in prophethood, fabricated mock verses, and dispatched envoys to the Prophet ﷺ claiming joint jurisdiction over the earth (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:326–327).

### Aftermath in the Caliphate
Following the death of the Prophet ﷺ, Banū Ḥanīfah rallied in large numbers around Musaylimah during the Wars of Apostasy (**[[Ḥurūb al-Riddah]]**). The Caliph **[[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]]** ﵁ dispatched the Muslim army under **[[Khālid b. al-Walīd]]** ﵁ to al-Yamāmah, where in the catastrophic clash at the Garden of Death (*Ḥadīqat al-Mawt*), Musaylimah was slain by **[[Waḥshī b. Ḥarb]]** ﵁ and Abū Dujānah ﵁, and the surviving remnant of Banū Ḥanīfah returned to Islam (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:327).
