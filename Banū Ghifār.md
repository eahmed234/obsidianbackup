# Banū Ghifār (بنو غفار)
*The Kinānite Tribe of the Coastal Caravan Highway, Bedouin Highwaymen Turned Early Champions of the Faith*

## 1. Lineage and Geographic Sphere
Banū Ghifār is an ancient nomadic branch of the great northern confederation of **[[Banū Kinānah]]** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:239–241; 2:315). Their genealogy ascends to:
**Ghifār b. Mulayl b. Ḍamrah b. Bakr b. ʿAbd Manāt b. Kinānah b. Khuzaymah** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:315).

They controlled the strategic desert corridor along the Red Sea caravan highway between Makkah and Syria, encamping around the valleys of Waddān, al-Abwāʾ, and Badr (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:315). In the pre-Islamic era (*al-Jāhiliyyah*), they were feared throughout Arabia as fierce, daring highwaymen who raided passing trade caravans and violated the sacred months (*ashhur ḥurum*), making their conversion to the ascetic discipline of Islam one of the remarkable transformations of the Prophetic era (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:315–316).

## 2. The Conversion of Abū Dharr al-Ghifārī
In the earliest days of the Prophetic mission in Makkah, news of the new Prophet reached the tribal camp of Ghifār (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:314). **[[Abū Dharr al-Ghifārī]]** ﵁ (who had already abandoned idolatry and worshipped Allāh in private for three years) dispatched his brother Unays to investigate (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:314). Unays returned and reported: *«I found a man enjoining noble character and uttering speech that is neither poetry nor sorcery!»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:314).

Abū Dharr traveled secretly to Makkah, survived for thirty days drinking solely from the well of Zamzam, and was guided by **[[ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib]]** ﵁ into the presence of the Prophet ﷺ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:315). Upon declaring his faith, he became the very first person to greet the Prophet ﷺ with the greeting of Islam: *«السلام عليك يا رسول الله»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:315). 

When the Prophet ﷺ inquired of his tribal origin, Abū Dharr answered: *«From Ghifār.»* The Prophet ﷺ touched his hand to his forehead in wonder at a member of that notorious raiding tribe embracing Islam so early (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:315). Abū Dharr then openly shouted the Shahādah in **[[al-Masjid al-Ḥarām]]** before Quraysh and was severely beaten, until **[[al-ʿAbbās b. ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib]]** ﵁ rescued him by reminding Quraysh: *«Woe to you! Do you not know that he is from Ghifār, and your trade route to Syria passes directly through their territory?!»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:315).

## 3. The Mass Conversion of Ghifār
The Prophet ﷺ instructed Abū Dharr to return to his homeland and invite his kin to Islam (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:316). Upon his return, his brother Unays and their mother embraced Islam immediately (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:316). Abū Dharr began preaching tirelessly among the desert encampments of Banū Ghifār:
- Half the tribe embraced Islam immediately in their pastures (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:316).
- The remaining half declared: *«When the Messenger of Allāh arrives in Medina, we will embrace Islam!»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:316).

Following the **[[al-Hijrah ilā al-Madīnah|Hijrah]]**, delegations of Banū Ghifār arrived in Medina in full tribal strength, fulfilling their pledge and entering Islam (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:316). Witnessing their devotion, their confederates Banū Aslam stepped forward and declared: *«O Messenger of Allāh! They are our brothers, and we embrace that which they embraced!»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:316). The Prophet ﷺ was deeply moved and pronounced his celebrated blessing:
> «غِفَارُ غَفَرَ اللَّهُ لَهَا، وَأَسْلَمُ سَالَمَهَا اللَّهُ!»
> *"Ghifār—may Allāh forgive her! And Aslam—may Allāh keep her in peace!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:316).

## 4. Sīrah Milestones and Military Service
- **The Water Dispute at al-Muraysīʿ**: A hireling of ʿUmar from Banū Ghifār, Jahjāh b. Saʿīd al-Ghifārī, was involved in the altercation at the well during **[[Ghazwat Banī al-Muṣṭaliq]]** which prompted the sedition of Ibn Ubayy and the revelation of **[[Sūrat al-Munāfiqūn]]** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:348).
- **The Conquest of Makkah**: Banū Ghifār marched in the vanguard of the ten thousand believers during **[[Fatḥ Makkah]]**, carrying their tribal standards alongside Banū Aslam and Banū Juhaynah.
- **The Defense of Islam**: Chieftains and warriors of Ghifār fought with distinction at Ḥunayn, Tabūk, and the early conquests across Egypt and North Africa.
