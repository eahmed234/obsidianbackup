# Zunnīrah al-Rūmiyyah (زنيرة الرومية)
*Tortured by ʿUmar and Abū Jahl, Blinded, and Miraculously Restored*

## 1. Identity
Al-Iṣābah gives her name as **Zunnīrah** (with variant readings Zanbarah/Zunbarah), **al-Rūmiyyah**
("the Byzantine/Roman"), and states she was **among the earliest converts to Islam** and among those
tortured for their faith (*al-Iṣābah*, 8:150, no. 11222).

## 2. Torture by ʿUmar and Abū Jahl
Al-Ṣāliḥī records that both **ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb** (before his own conversion) and **Abū Jahl**
tortured her. Ḥassān b. Thābit ﵁ recalled seeing ʿUmar torment her alongside the slave-girl of Banū
al-Muʾammal, **[[Lubaybah]]**, during a pilgrimage visit to Makkah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:361; *al-Iṣābah*,
8:151).

## 3. Blindness and Its Restoration
Abū Jahl used to say: *"Do you not marvel at these people and their following of Muḥammad? Had what
Muḥammad brought been good and true, they would not have outstripped us to it — has Zunnīrah, then,
outstripped us to guidance, being what you see of her?"* Al-Ṣāliḥī records that she had been tortured
**until she lost her sight**; Abū Jahl told her, *"Al-Lāt and al-ʿUzzā have done this to you."* She
answered, unable to see: *"You lie — al-Lāt and al-ʿUzzā neither profit nor harm; this is a matter from
heaven, and my Lord is able to restore my sight."* The next morning her sight had been restored, and
Quraysh said, *"This is more of Muḥammad's sorcery"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:361).

Al-Iṣābah preserves a second version of this same wonder, via Saʿd b. Ibrāhīm: she was a Byzantine who
accepted Islam and **then** lost her sight; Quraysh said al-Lāt and al-ʿUzzā had blinded her, and she
replied, *"I disbelieve in al-Lāt and al-ʿUzzā,"* whereupon Allāh restored her sight. A further chain,
via Umm Hāniʾ bint Abī Ṭālib, places the loss of sight specifically **at the moment Abū Bakr freed
her**, with the same exchange and restoration following (*al-Iṣābah*, 8:150–151).

## 4. Freed by Abū Bakr
She was purchased and freed by **Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq** ﵁, one of **seven** whom he bought out of
torture for their faith (*al-Iṣābah*, 8:150; see **[[Lubaybah]]** for the fuller list).
