# Abū Saʿīd al-Khudrī (أبو سعيد الخدري) ﵁
*Saʿd b. Mālik b. Sinān — The Sage-Jurist of the Anṣār and Prolific Preserver of the Prophetic Sunnah*

## 1. Lineage, Clan & Upbringing
- **Full Lineage**: Saʿd b. Mālik b. Sinān b. ʿUbayd b. Thaʿlabah b. al-Abjar (Khudrah) b. ʿAwf b. al-Ḥārith b. al-Khazraj al-Anṣārī al-Khazrajī al-Khudrī (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:65, no. 3204).
- **Clan**: Banū Khudrah, a leading clan of al-Khazraj.
- **Paternal Household**: Son of the noble Companion **[[Mālik b. Sinān]]** ﵁, the hero who sucked the blood from the wounded face of the Prophet ﷺ and was martyred at Uḥud (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:65).
- **Youth at Uḥud**: Presented to the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ on the eve of **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]** at al-Shaykhayn, but the Prophet ﷺ turned him back because of his tender age (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:65; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:187).

## 2. Military Expeditions & Early Daʿwah
- Following the martyrdom of his father at Uḥud, Abū Saʿīd participated in subsequent military campaigns, beginning with the Battle of the Trench (*Ghazwat al-Khandaq*) and all expeditions thereafter (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:65).
- Swore the solemn pledge directly to the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ alongside a select group of Companions (including Abū Dharr, ʿUbādah b. al-Ṣāmit, and Muḥammad b. Maslamah) that they would never let the blame of any blamer turn them from the cause of Allāh (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:66).

## 3. Prophetic Counsel on Contentment & Self-Sufficiency
- Following the martyrdom of his father at Uḥud, the family was left without wealth. Abū Saʿīd approached the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ to ask for assistance. Before he spoke, the Prophet ﷺ said:
  > «من استغنى أغناه اللَّه، ومن يستعفّ يعفّه اللَّه، ومن يتصبّر يصبّره اللَّه»
  > *"Whoever seeks self-sufficiency, Allāh makes him self-sufficient; whoever seeks chastity and restraint, Allāh grants him chastity; and whoever practices patience, Allāh grants him patience."* (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:66).
- Abū Saʿīd returned without asking and relied upon Allāh, finding that sustenance opened for him thereafter.

## 4. Jurisprudence & Transmission of Knowledge
- Renowned as one of the most learned jurists among the younger Companions: *«لم يكن أحد من أحداث أصحاب رسول اللَّه صلى الله عليه وآله وسلم أفقه من أبي سعيد الخدريّ»* (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:66).
- Transmitted extensive hadith from the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ and from elder Companions including Abū Bakr, ʿUmar, ʿUthmān, ʿAlī, and Zayd b. Thābit (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:65).
- Counted among the prolific narrators (*al-mukthirūn*), taught major Companions (such as Ibn ʿAbbās and Ibn ʿUmar) and senior Tābiʿūn (including Saʿīd b. al-Musayyab and Mujāhid) (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:65–66).

## 5. Death & Legacy
- Passed away in Medina; biographical authorities record differing reports regarding his passing, including 63 AH, 64 AH, 65 AH, and 74 AH (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:67).
- Interred in the cemetery of al-Baqīʿ in Medina.
