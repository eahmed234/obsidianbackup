# Sariyyat al-ʿUraniyyīn (سرية العرنيين)
*The twenty-horseman punitive pursuit of the apostate renegades of ʿUkl and ʿUraynah, the recovery of the prophetic camels, and the revelation of the Verse of Ḥirābah (Qurʾān 5:33)*

## 1. Context & The Murder of Yasār

In late 6 AH (Shawwāl), eight men belonging to the tribes of **ʿUkl** (from Taym al-Ribāb) and **ʿUraynah** (from Bajīlah) arrived in Medina and outwardly professed Islam (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:115, 117). They were afflicted with severe illness, dropsy (*istisqāʾ*), swelling of their bellies, and extreme pallor (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:115). Pitying their condition, the Prophet ﷺ accommodated them among **[[Ahl al-Ṣuffah]]** and provided them food (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:115).

When they complained that the air and climate of Medina did not suit them (*ijtawaw al-Madīnah*), the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ permitted them to go out to the pasturing camels of the public treasury (*sarḥ / liqāḥ*) pasturing at **Fayfāʾ al-Khabār** in the direction of the Ḥimā (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:115). He instructed them to drink from their milk and urine as medicinal therapy (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:115).

Under the care of the Prophet's freedman **Yasār** ﵁, their health was completely restored (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:115). However, once healed, they committed treasonous apostasy, turned upon Yasār and the herdsmen, tortured Yasār brutally by amputating his hands and feet and piercing thorns into his tongue and eyes until he died, and drove off **fifteen milch-camels** (*khams ʿashrata liqḥah*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:115–117).

## 2. Dispatch of the Twenty Horsemen under Kurz b. Jābir

When news of the atrocity reached Medina via a woman rider from Banū ʿAmr b. ʿAwf, the Prophet ﷺ immediately mobilized an elite cavalry detachment of **twenty horsemen** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:116). He placed them under the supreme command of **[[Kurz b. Jābir al-Fihrī]]** ﵁, the former chieftain of Safawān who had embraced Islam (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:116; *al-Iṣābah*, 5:434–435).

Among the twenty horsemen assigned to the pursuit were seasoned veterans (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:116):
- **[[Salamah b. al-Akwaʿ]]** ﵁
- **Abū Ruhm al-Ghifārī** ﵁
- **[[Abū Dharr al-Ghifārī]]** ﵁
- **Buraydah b. al-Ḥuṣayb** ﵁
- **Rāfiʿ b. Makīth** and his brother **Jundab** ﵄
- **Bilāl b. al-Ḥārith al-Muzanī** ﵁
- **ʿAbdullāh b. ʿAmr b. ʿAwf al-Muzanī** ﵁
- **Jiʿāl b. Surāqah al-Thaʿlabī** ﵁
- **Suwayd b. Ṣakhr al-Juhanī** ﵁

The Prophet ﷺ dispatched an expert desert tracker (*qāʾif*) with them and supplicated against the perpetrators:
> «اللهم أعم عليهم الطريق واجعله عليهم أضيق من مسك جمل»
> *"O Allāh, blind the paths before them and make them tighter upon them than the skin of a camel!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:116).

## 3. The Desert Pursuit & Capture

Kurz and his company pursued the tracks through the basalt plains (*al-Ḥarrah*), camping overnight when darkness fell (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:116). In the morning, uncertain which ravine the renegades had taken, they intercepted a Bedouin woman carrying the roasted shoulder of a camel (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:116). She disclosed:
> «مررت بقوم قد نحروا بعيرا فأعطوني هذه الكتف، وهم بتلك المفازة، إذا وافيتم عليها رأيتم دخانهم»
> *"I passed by a group who slaughtered a camel and gave me this shoulder; they are in that desert expanse—if you reach it, you will see their smoke!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:116).

Galloping directly toward the rising smoke, the Muslim cavalry overtook the eight renegades just as they finished eating (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:116). Surrounded and cut off, every single one of the eight surrendered unconditionally without a single man escaping (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:116).

The company bound the captives, mounted them behind their saddles on the horses, and drove the recovered prophetic camels back to Medina, finding the Prophet ﷺ at **al-Raghābah** at the confluence of the torrent valleys (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:116).

## 4. Retribution (*Qiṣāṣ*) & Revelation of the Ḥirābah Verse

Because the perpetrators had committed armed robbery, murdered an innocent herdsman, mutilated him with horrific cruelty, and waged war against the community after outwardly entering the fold of Islam, the Prophet ﷺ ordered exact retribution (*qiṣāṣ bi-al-mithl*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:116). Their hands and feet were amputated from opposite sides, their eyes were blinded in reciprocity for the thorns thrust into Yasār's eyes, and they were cast out on the rugged volcanic stone of the Ḥarrah until they expired (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:116).

Following this judgment, Allāh revealed the canonical statute regulating armed brigandage and terrorism (**Āyat al-Ḥirābah**, Qurʾān 5:33) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:117):
> ﴿إِنَّمَا جَزَاءُ الَّذِينَ يُحَارِبُونَ اللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُ وَيَسْعَوْنَ فِي الْأَرْضِ فَسَادًا أَن يُقَتَّلُوا أَوْ يُصَلَّبُوا أَوْ تُقَطَّعَ أَيْدِيهِمْ وَأَرْجُلُهُم مِّنْ خِلَافٍ أَوْ يُنفَوْا مِنَ الْأَرْضِ ۚ ذَٰلِكَ لَهُمْ خِزْيٌ فِي الدُّنْيَا ۖ وَلَهُمْ فِي الْآخِرَةِ عَذَابٌ عَظِيمٌ﴾
> *«Indeed, the penalty for those who wage war against Allāh and His Messenger and strive upon earth to cause corruption is none other than that they be killed or crucified or that their hands and feet be cut off from opposite sides or that they be exiled from the land. That is for them a disgrace in this world; and for them in the Hereafter is a great punishment.»*

Subsequent to the revelation of this verse, the Prophet ﷺ permanently prohibited mutilation (*al-muthlah*) across all campaigns and commands, strictly confining capital penalties to the prescribed legal limits (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:117).

---

## 5. Strategic Significance

1. **Rule of Law & Zero Tolerance for Highway Banditry**: The rapid apprehension of the killers established that state borders and herds within the Medina territory were under unwavering military surveillance (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:116).
2. **Rehabilitation and Command of Kurz b. Jābir**: Entrusting Kurz—the very man who had raided Medina's pastures years earlier during [[Ghazwat Safawān]]—demonstrated the transformative integration of former adversaries into trusted military officers (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:434–435).
3. **Foundational Legal Jurisprudence**: The incident formed the bedrock of Islamic penal law concerning armed insurrection and highway robbery (*aḥkām al-muḥāribīn wal-quṭṭāʿ*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:117).
