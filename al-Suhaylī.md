# al-Suhaylī (عبد الرحمن بن عبد الله السهيلي)
*Commentator on the Sīrah of Ibn Hishām*

## 1. Identity
**ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. ʿAbdullāh al-Suhaylī** (508–581 AH), an Andalusian scholar of Málaga, blind from
youth, and a master of Arabic grammar and lexicography as well as Sīrah. He died in Marrakesh.

## 2. al-Rawḍ al-Unuf
His ***al-Rawḍ al-Unuf*** is the standard commentary on **[[Ibn Hishām]]**'s *Sīrah*. Its
distinctive contribution is philological: it explains the obscure vocabulary, the poetry, the place
names and the genealogies that Ibn Hishām's text presents without comment.

This is why al-Ṣāliḥī reaches for him on points of wording and disputed particulars — for instance on
who gave [[Khadījah bint Khuwaylid]] ﵂ in marriage (*Subul al-Hudā*, Muqaddimah p. 11), where he
weighs al-Suhaylī's report against the position most authorities settled on. The *sharḥ gharīb*
("explanation of the obscure") sections that recur throughout *Subul al-Hudā* work in this same
tradition.

He is named among the authorities in the provenance note of **[[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]]**.
