# al-Barāʾ b. Maʿrūr (البراء بن معرور الأنصاري) ﵁
*The Noble Chieftain of Banū Salimah and the First to Face the Kaʿbah*

## 1. Lineage & Leadership
He is al-Barāʾ b. Maʿrūr b. Ṣakhr b. Khansāʾ al-Khazrajī al-Salmī (kunya: Abū Bishr), supreme chieftain and revered elder of the Banū Salimah clan of the Khazraj in Yathrib (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:201, 215). He was the father of the steadfast companion and Khaybar martyr **[[Bishr b. al-Barāʾ]]** ﵁.

## 2. Preeminence at the Second Pledge of al-ʿAqabah
At **[[Bayʿat al-ʿAqabah al-Thāniyah]]** (13th year of Nubūwah), al-Barāʾ led the delegation of seventy-three men and two women from Yathrib to meet the Prophet ﷺ at the pass of Mina (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:201). When **[[al-ʿAbbās b. ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib]]** addressed the Anṣār urging them to understand the gravity of war against all Arabs, al-Barāʾ stepped forward as the spokesman of the delegation:
> *"By Him Who sent you with the Truth as a Prophet, we shall surely defend you from whatever we defend our own women and children! Take our covenant, O Messenger of Allāh, for by Allāh, we are men of war and inheritors of arms from generation to generation!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:202).

He was the very first person among the seventy to grasp the Prophet’s hand in fealty, and was appointed the head Naqīb representing Banū Salimah among the twelve Nuqabāʾ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:203, 215).

## 3. Historic Precedents in Islam
- **Facing the Kaʿbah Before Its Prescription**: While traveling to Mecca for the pledge, al-Barāʾ declared that he could not bear turning his back upon the Ancient House, so he prayed facing the Kaʿbah in Mecca while the rest of the Muslims faced Jerusalem (*Bayt al-Maqdis*). Upon consulting the Prophet ﷺ in Mecca, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ advised him to maintain patience upon the current Qiblah until divine command was issued (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:132, 215).
- **The First Bequest of One-Third**: In his final illness, he instructed that one-third of his wealth be bequeathed to the Prophet ﷺ to spend as he wished in the path of Allāh, becoming the first person in Islam to establish the bequest of one-third (*al-waṣiyyah bil-thuluth*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:215).

## 4. Demise & Prophetic Funeral Prayer
Al-Barāʾ fell fatally ill in Medina in Ṣafar 1 AH, just weeks before the Prophet's arrival. On his deathbed, he requested that his face be turned toward the Kaʿbah in his grave. Upon reaching Medina, the Prophet ﷺ went directly with his companions to the grave of al-Barāʾ in [[Jannat al-Baqīʿ]], lined up the believers, and performed the funeral prayer over him with four Takbīrs, praying:
> «اللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لَهُ، وَارْحَمْهُ، وَأَدْخِلْهُ جَنَّتَكَ، وَقَدْ فَعَلْتَ!»
> *"O Allāh, forgive him, have mercy upon him, and admit him into Your Paradise—and indeed You have done so!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:215, 279).
The Prophet ﷺ then restored the bequeathed third of his wealth to his children (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:215).
