# al-Barāʾ b. Maʿrūr (البراء بن معرور السلمي) ﵁
*Abū Bishr al-Khazrajī al-Salamī — Chieftain of Banū Salimah, Chief Naqīb of al-ʿAqabah, The First to Face the Kaʿbah, and The First to Bequeath One-Third*

## 1. Lineage, Clan Associations & Leadership
He is al-Barāʾ b. Maʿrūr b. Ṣakhr b. Khansāʾ b. Sinān b. ʿUbayd b. ʿAdī b. Ghanam b. Kaʿb b. Salimah b. Saʿd b. ʿAlī b. Asad b. Sāridah b. Tazīd b. Jusham b. al-Khazraj al-Anṣārī al-Khazrajī al-Salamī (kunya: Abū Bishr) (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:415). He was the supreme chieftain, venerable elder, and obeyed leader of the Banū Salimah clan of the Khazraj (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:201; *al-Iṣābah*, 1:415). 

He was the father of the noble Companion and warrior **[[Bishr b. al-Barāʾ]]** ﵁, who was later martyred alongside the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ following the eating of the poisoned sheep at Khaybar (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:427; *Subul al-Hudā*, 5:134).

## 2. The Second Pledge of al-ʿAqabah
At [[Bayʿat al-ʿAqabah al-Thāniyah]] (13th year of Nubūwah), al-Barāʾ set out from Medina as the elder and leader of the Yathrib delegation (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:201; *al-Iṣābah*, 1:415). When the meeting convened in secret at the mountain pass near Mina in the presence of the Prophet ﷺ and his uncle **[[al-ʿAbbās b. ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib]]**, al-ʿAbbās delivered an opening address emphasizing the solemnity and perils of the covenant (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:201–202).

Al-Barāʾ stepped forward as the official spokesman of the Anṣār, responding with resolute courage:
> *"We have heard what you said... By Him Who sent you with the Truth as a Prophet, we shall surely protect you from whatever we protect our own women and children! Take our covenant, O Messenger of Allāh, for by Allāh, we are people of war and inheritors of weapons from generation to generation!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:202).

When the pledge of allegiance was enacted, al-Barāʾ b. Maʿrūr was the very first person whose hand struck the hand of the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ in fealty (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:415; *Subul al-Hudā*, 3:203, 215). The Prophet ﷺ designated him as one of the twelve Nuqabāʾ (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:415).

## 3. Historic Precedents in Islam
Classical biographers record three historic precedents established by al-Barāʾ b. Maʿrūr in the early development of Islamic rites:
1. **The First to Strike the Prophetic Hand in Fealty**: According to the narration of Ibn Isḥāq and the consensus of the scholars of Sīrah, he was the very first pledger at the Second Pledge of al-ʿAqabah (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:415; *Subul al-Hudā*, 3:215).
2. **The First to Face the Kaʿbah in Prayer**: While traveling from Medina to Mecca prior to the pledge, al-Barāʾ announced that he refused to place the Ancient House behind his back in prayer, so he prayed facing directly toward the Kaʿbah while the rest of the Companions faced Jerusalem (*Bayt al-Maqdis*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:132, 215; *al-Iṣābah*, 1:415). When he inquired of the Prophet ﷺ in Mecca, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ advised him to adhere to the appointed Qiblah of the time until divine instruction descended (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:415).
3. **The First to Bequeath One-Third of Wealth**: In his final illness, al-Barāʾ established the legal precedent of the testamentary bequest of one-third (*al-waṣiyyah bil-thuluth*), instructing that one-third of his wealth be bequeathed to the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ to distribute as he pleased in the path of Allāh (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:415; *Subul al-Hudā*, 3:215).

## 4. Demise & Prophetic Funeral Prayer
Al-Barāʾ fell gravely ill in Medina in Ṣafar 1 AH, approximately one month prior to the arrival of the Prophet ﷺ during the Hijrah (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:416; *Subul al-Hudā*, 8:372). Upon his deathbed, he instructed his family that his face and his grave be directed toward the Kaʿbah (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:415).

When the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ arrived in Medina, he went with his Companions to the newly made grave of al-Barāʾ b. Maʿrūr in **[[Jannat al-Baqīʿ]]** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:372; *al-Iṣābah*, 1:415). The Prophet ﷺ aligned the ranks behind him and performed the funeral prayer over his grave, pronouncing four Takbīrs (*ṣallā ʿalā qabrihi wa-kabbara ʿalayhi arbaʿ takbīrāt*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:372; *al-Iṣābah*, 1:415). The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ accepted the one-third bequest al-Barāʾ had willed to him, and then generously restored the entire amount back to al-Barāʾ's children (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:415).
