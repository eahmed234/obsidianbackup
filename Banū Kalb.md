# Banū Kalb (بنو كلب)
*The Quḍāʿī Tribe of the Northern Marches*

## 1. Identity & Territory
A great tribe of **Quḍāʿah** occupying the northern desert marches toward Syria, centred on the oasis
of **[[Dūmat al-Jandal]]**. Partly Christianized before Islam and oriented toward the Byzantine
sphere, which made them strategically significant on that frontier.

## 2. Role in the Sīrah
- **[[Zayd b. Ḥārithah]]** ﵁ was of Banū Kalb by birth, seized as a boy from his mother's people and
  sold into Makkah.
- Their territory was the objective of **[[Sariyyat Dūmat al-Jandal]]** (Shaʿbān 6 AH), commanded by
  **[[ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. ʿAwf]]** ﵁ — the frontier operation that secured the northern approach.
- A delegation of Banū Kalb later came to the Prophet ﷺ; al-Ṣāliḥī gives their embassy its own
  chapter (*al-Bāb al-Thānī wal-Thamānūn fī wufūd Banī Kalb ilayhi ﷺ*)
  (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:401).
