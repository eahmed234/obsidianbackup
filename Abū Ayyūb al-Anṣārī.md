# Abū Ayyūb al-Anṣārī (أبو أيوب الأنصاري)
*Khālid b. Zayd — Host of the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ*

## 1. The Household of the Hijrah
An Anṣārī of **[[Banū al-Najjār]]**, the Prophet's ﷺ maternal clan in Yathrib. When the Prophet ﷺ
entered the city at the close of **[[al-Hijrah ilā al-Madīnah]]**, he lodged in Abū Ayyūb's house
while the **[[Bināʾ al-Masjid al-Nabawī]]** and the adjoining apartments were built
(*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:275) — the household where the Islamic commonwealth was, in effect, first
administered.

Al-Ṣāliḥī records among the Prophet's ﷺ signs the miraculous increase of Abū Ayyūb's food
(*al-Bāb al-Ḥādī ʿAshar fī takthīrihi ﷺ ṭaʿām Abī Ayyūb*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 9:482).

## 2. Key Sīrah Milestones
- Fought at **[[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]]**, **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]**, **[[Ghazwat al-Khandaq]]** and
  onward — he is recorded as having missed almost no campaign in his long life.
- Continued campaigning into extreme old age, and died on the expedition against Constantinople,
  asking to be buried as far forward beneath its walls as the army could reach.
