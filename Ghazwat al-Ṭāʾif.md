# Ghazwat al-Ṭāʾif (غزوة الطائف)
*The Siege of al-Ṭāʾif — The Trial of Siegecraft, the Liberation of the Enslaved, and the Heart-Stirring Covenant of the Anṣār*

## 1. Strategic Context & Deployment from Ḥunayn
Immediately following the decisive victory over Hawāzin at **[[Ghazwat Ḥunayn]]** and the subsequent mop-up operation at Awṭās in Shawwāl 8 AH, the surviving remnants of **Thaqīf** and their Hawāzin allies under **[[Mālik b. ʿAwf al-Naṣrī]]** fled directly to the mountainous redoubt of **al-Ṭāʾif** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:382).

There, behind the thick basaltic fortifications, high stone towers, and massive iron-reinforced gates of al-Ṭāʾif, the defenders barred the gates and laid in provisions sufficient for a full year of siege warfare (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:382). 

The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ marched from Ḥunayn via Nakhlah al-Yamāniyyah and Baḥrah al-Rughāʾ, arriving beneath the battlements of al-Ṭāʾif and establishing an encirclement camp that lasted between twenty and forty days (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:382, 387).

## 2. Advanced Siegecraft: Mangonels & Mantlets
Initial Muslim encampments were situated too close to the fortress walls. Thaqīf's archers unleashed an intense, unending hail of arrows from their elevated towers that pierced tents and wounded numerous soldiers, compelling the Prophet ﷺ to withdraw the camp back to the site where the Mosque of al-Ṭāʾif was later erected (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:383).

Confronted with impassable stone walls, the Prophet ﷺ introduced advanced Near Eastern siege technologies:
1. **The Mangonel (*al-Manjanīq*)**: The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ erected a heavy ballista stone-thrower, bombarding the battlements and bastions of Thaqīf with boulders (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:385).
2. **Siege Mantlets (*al-Dabbābah*)**: Muslim sappers entered under covered wooden siege shelters (tanks or mantlets) and moved toward the base of the fortress wall, attempting to breach the masonry with picks (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:385).

Thaqīf countered with lethal defensive ingenuity: they heated iron needles and spikes over roaring furnaces (*ḥasak al-ḥadīd al-muḥmāh*) and poured the molten metal down upon the mantlets from the parapets, setting the wooden structures ablaze. As the Muslim sappers were forced out from beneath the burning shelters, Thaqīf showered them with arrows, inflicting casualties (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:385).

## 3. Economic Warfare: The Vineyards of Thaqīf
To exert compelling pressure on the aristocracy of Thaqīf, the Prophet ﷺ ordered his soldiers to begin cutting down and burning the prized grape vineyards and date groves outside the walls, which constituted the entire agrarian wealth of al-Ṭāʾif (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:383).

Terrified by the permanent ruin of their agricultural wealth, the leaders of Thaqīf called out from the ramparts, begging the Prophet ﷺ:
> «يَا مُحَمَّدُ، لِمَ تَقْطَعُ أَمْوَالَنَا؟ دَعْ أَمْوَالَنَا لِلَّهِ وَلِلرَّحِمِ!»
> *"O Muḥammad, why do you destroy our property? Leave our orchards for the sake of Allāh and the sacred bonds of kinship!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:383).

Displaying prophetic mercy, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ immediately commanded his army to halt the destruction of the orchards: *«I leave them for the sake of Allāh and the kinship!»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:383).

## 4. The Proclamation of Emancipation for Enslaved Servants
Recognizing that the city's labor force was composed of enslaved servants yearning for liberty, the Prophet ﷺ instructed a herald to proclaim beneath the battlements:
> «أَيُّمَا عَبْدٍ نَزَلَ مِنَ الْحِصْنِ وَخَرَجَ إِلَيْنَا فَهُوَ حُرٌّ!»
> *"Whosoever among the enslaved servants descends from the ramparts of the fortress and comes out to us is unconditionally a free man!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:384).

Approximately twenty-three servants broke free from their masters and scaled the walls (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:384). Among them was **Nufayʿ b. al-Ḥārith**, who fastened a rope to a well pulley (*bakrah*) and slid down the outer wall into the Muslim camp (*al-Iṣābah*, 6:369; *Subul al-Hudā*, 5:384). The Prophet ﷺ received him with honor, emancipated him from bondage, and named him **[[Abū Bakrah al-Thaqafī|Abū Bakrah]]** (*al-Iṣābah*, 6:369).

When Thaqīf later accepted Islam and petitioned the Prophet ﷺ to return these fugitives to slavery, the Prophet ﷺ flatly refused:
> «لَا، أُولَئِكَ عُتَقَاءُ اللهِ وَعُتَقَاءُ رَسُولِهِ!»
> *"No! Those men are the freedmen of Allāh and the freedmen of His Messenger!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:384).

## 6. The Prophetic Vision & Lifting the Siege
While the siege persisted, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ saw a truthful vision in his sleep: he was presented with a bowl of fresh butter, which a hoopoe bird pecked at, spilling its contents on the earth (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:387). 

The Prophet ﷺ recounted the vision to [[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]] ﵁, who interpreted it: *«O Messenger of Allāh, I do not believe that you will achieve your desire against them on this expedition.»* The Prophet ﷺ replied: *«I too consider it thus.»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:387).

The Prophet ﷺ ordered [[ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb]] ﵁ to announce the lifting of the siege:
> «إِنَّا قَافِلُونَ غَدًا إِنْ شَاءَ اللهُ!»
> *"Indeed, we are breaking camp and departing tomorrow, if Allāh wills!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:387).

When some eager warriors pleaded: *«O Messenger of Allāh, supplicate against Thaqīf and curse them!»* the Prophet ﷺ raised his hands to the heavens and uttered his sublime supplication:
> «اللَّهُمَّ اهْدِ ثَقِيفًا وَأْتِ بِهِمْ!»
> *"O Allāh! Guide Thaqīf to the truth, and bring them [to Medina as Muslims]!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:388).

His prayer was fulfilled completely within the year, when Thaqīf surrendered unconditionally and embraced Islam as a unified tribe (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:388).

## 7. The Spoils of al-Jiʿrānah & Reconciliation with Hawāzin
The army withdrew to **al-Jiʿrānah**, where the colossal booty of Ḥunayn had been guarded under **[[Budayl b. Warqāʾ al-Khuzāʿī]]** ﵁ (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:409; *Subul al-Hudā*, 5:389). 

Shortly after their arrival, an official delegation of fourteen Hawāzin chieftains arrived, announcing their conversion to Islam and pleading for their captured women and children (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:390). The Prophet ﷺ magnanimously surrendered his own share and that of Banū Hāshim, and inspired the army to release all six thousand captives unconditionally (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:392).

### Enfranchising al-Muʾallafah Qulūbuhum
To soften the hearts of the newly converted Qurashī aristocrats and Bedouin tribal kings (*al-Muʾallafah Qulūbuhum*), the Prophet ﷺ bestowed astronomical gifts from the remaining herds (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:396):
- One hundred camels each to [[Abū Sufyān b. Ḥarb]] ﵁, his sons Yazīd and Muʿāwiyah, Ḥakīm b. Ḥizām, al-Ḥārith b. Hishām, Ṣafwān b. Umayyah, Suhayl b. ʿAmr, ʿUyaynah b. Ḥiṣn, and al-Aqraʿ b. Ḥābis (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:396–399).
- The conversion of Hawāzin's supreme general, **[[Mālik b. ʿAwf al-Naṣrī]]** ﵁, who surrendered, embraced Islam, received one hundred camels and his returned family, and was appointed commander over Hawāzin (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:551; *Subul al-Hudā*, 5:405).

## 8. The Grievance & Tears of the Anṣār
Observing the immense distributions of camels and silver to Quraysh and the Bedouin chiefs while their own hands received none of the livestock, some young Anṣārī warriors felt wounded in their sentiments, murmuring: *«May Allāh forgive the Messenger of Allāh: he gives to Quraysh and abandons us, while our swords are still dripping with their blood!»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:402).

Their paramount chieftain, **[[Saʿd b. ʿUbādah]]** ﵁, came to the Prophet ﷺ and reported the feelings of his people. The Prophet ﷺ commanded Saʿd:
> «اجْمَعْ لِي قَوْمَكَ فِي هَذِهِ الْحَظِيرَةِ»
> *"Gather your people for me in this enclosure!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:402).

When the Anṣār were assembled in seclusion, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ stood before them and delivered what classical historians regard as the most moving speech in the entire biography:
> «يَا مَعْشَرَ الْأَنْصَارِ، مَا قَالَةٌ بَلَغَتْنِي عَنْكُمْ، وَجِدَةٌ وَجَدْتُمُوهَا فِي أَنْفُسِكُمْ؟ أَلَمْ آتِكُمْ ضُلَّالًا فَهَدَاكُمُ اللهُ بِي؟ وَعَالَةً فَأَغْنَاكُمُ اللهُ بِي؟ وَأَعْدَاءً فَأَلَّفَ اللهُ بَيْنَ قُلُوبِكُمْ؟»
> *"O company of the Anṣār! What is this talk that has reached me from you, and this resentment you felt within your souls? Did I not come to you when you were misguided, and Allāh guided you through me? And impoverished, and Allāh enriched you through me? And bitter enemies, and Allāh united your hearts through me?!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:403).

They replied in humility: *«Allāh and His Messenger are most bountiful and gracious!»* The Prophet ﷺ continued:
> «أَلَا تُجِيبُونَنِي يَا مَعْشَرَ الْأَنْصَارِ؟ أَمَا وَاللهِ لَوْ شِئْتُمْ لَقُلْتُمْ فَصَدَقْتُمْ وَصُدِّقْتُمْ: أَتَيْتَنَا مُكَذَّبًا فَصَدَّقْنَاكَ، وَمَخْذُولًا فَنَصَرْنَاكَ، وَطَرِيدًا فَآوَيْنَاكَ، وَعَائِلًا فَآسَيْنَاكَ! أَوَجَدْتُمْ فِي أَنْفُسِكُمْ يَا مَعْشَرَ الْأَنْصَارِ فِي لُعَاعَةٍ مِنَ الدُّنْيَا تَأَلَّفْتُ بِهَا قَوْمًا لِيُسْلِمُوا، وَوَكَلْتُكُمْ إِلَى إِسْلَامِكُمْ؟!  
> أَلَا تَرْضَوْنَ يَا مَعْشَرَ الْأَنْصَارِ أَنْ يَذْهَبَ النَّاسُ بِالشَّاءِ وَالْبَعِيرِ، وَتَرْجِعُونَ بِرَسُولِ اللهِ ﷺ إِلَى رِحَالِكُمْ؟!  
> فَوَالَّذِي نَفْسُ مُحَمَّدٍ بِيَدِهِ، لَوْلَا الْهِجْرَةُ لَكُنْتُ امْرَأً مِنَ الْأَنْصَارِ! وَلَوْ سَلَكَ النَّاسُ شِعْبًا وَسَلَكَتِ الْأَنْصَارُ شِعْبًا لَسَلَكْتُ شِعْبَ الْأَنْصَارِ!  
> اللَّهُمَّ ارْحَمِ الْأَنْصَارَ، وَأَبْنَاءَ الْأَنْصَارِ، وَأَبْنَاءَ أَبْنَاءِ الْأَنْصَارِ!»
> *"Will you not answer me, O assembly of the Anṣār? By Allāh, if you wished, you could have said—and you would have spoken the truth and been believed: 'You came to us rejected, and we believed in you! Forsaken, and we aided you! An exile, and we gave you refuge! Impoverished, and we shared our wealth with you!'  
> Did you find grief in your souls, O Anṣār, over the trivial dross of this fleeting world wherewith I reconciled the hearts of a people that they might embrace Islam, while I entrusted you to the depth of your own faith?!  
> Are you not well pleased, O company of the Anṣār, that the people depart with sheep and camels, while you return with the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ to your very dwellings?!  
> By Him in Whose Hand is the soul of Muḥammad, were it not for the Hijrah, I would have been a man of the Anṣār! If all mankind were to traverse one valley, and the Anṣār were to traverse another valley, I would surely walk the valley of the Anṣār!  
> O Allāh! Have mercy upon the Anṣār, and the children of the Anṣār, and the children's children of the Anṣār!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:403).

The Anṣār wept uncontrollably until their tears soaked their beards, and they cried out with one thunderous voice:
> «رَضِينَا بِرَسُولِ اللهِ قِسْمًا وَحَظًّا!»
> *"We are well pleased with the Messenger of Allāh as our portion and our reward!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:403).
