# ʿĀm al-Ḥuzn (عام الحزن)
*The Year of Sorrow — The Passing of Khadījah and Abū Ṭālib*

## 1. Overview
In the tenth year of Prophethood, shortly after the lifting of the three-year boycott, the Prophet [[Muḥammad b. ʻAbdullah|Muḥammad]] ﷺ was struck by two devastating personal losses in rapid succession:
1. **The Death of Abū Ṭālib**: His beloved uncle and steadfast tribal protector, leaving him exposed to uninhibited Meccan hostility.
2. **The Death of Khadījah (ra)**: His devoted companion for twenty-five years, mother of his children, and the first to believe in his mission.

The Prophet ﷺ named this period *ʿĀm al-Ḥuzn* (The Year of Sorrow). With tribal protection dismantled, street violence intensified; polytheists hurled filth and entrails upon the Prophet ﷺ while he was in prostration before the Kaʿbah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:402).

## 2. Subsequent Events
- **[[Riḥlat al-Ṭāʾif]]**: Seeking support and sanctuary among the tribe of Thaqīf (Shawwāl 10th Year).
- **[[al-Isrāʾ wal-Miʿrāj]]**: Divine solace and cosmic elevation by Allāh (late 10th or 11th Year).
