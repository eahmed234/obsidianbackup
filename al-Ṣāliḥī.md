# al-Ṣāliḥī (محمد بن يوسف الصالحي الشامي)
*Imām Shams al-Dīn Muḥammad b. Yūsuf al-Ṣāliḥī al-Shāmī — Author of the Master Encyclopedia of the Sīrah*

> [!NOTE] The Authority Behind This Wiki
> Every event, biography, and geographical node in this vault is verified against al-Ṣāliḥī's
> ***[[Subul al-Hudā war-Rashād]]***. He is the single scholarly voice standing behind the entire
> citation apparatus of the project.

## 1. Identity & Scholarly Formation
A Shāfiʿī ḥadīth master and historian, known by the *nisbah* **al-Ṣāliḥī** after the Ṣāliḥiyyah
quarter of Damascus, and **al-Shāmī** for his Syrian origin. He settled and died in Cairo in
**942 AH / 1536 CE**.

## 2. Method (*Manhaj*)
His distinction is *synthetic compilation* rather than fresh narration. He harmonizes more than
three hundred primary classical works into a single continuous account, naming his transmitters in
chains before each report — Ibn Isḥāq, [[Ibn Saʿd]], al-Wāqidī, Mūsā b. ʿUqbah, [[al-Bukhārī]],
[[Muslim]], al-Ṭabarī, [[Ibn Kathīr]], and the verdicts of [[Ibn Ḥajar al-ʿAsqalānī]] (cited
throughout simply as *al-Ḥāfiẓ*).

Where his authorities disagree, he characteristically lays out the competing reports and then
reconciles them (*ṭarīq al-jamʿ*) rather than silently choosing one.

## 3. Subul al-Hudā war-Rashād
Compiled over three decades, consulting libraries in Damascus, Jerusalem, Cairo, and Medina. The
printed Dār al-Kutub al-ʿIlmiyyah edition (Beirut, 1414/1993, ed. ʿĀdil Aḥmad ʿAbd al-Mawjūd and
ʿAlī Muḥammad Muʿawwaḍ) runs to **12 volumes**, and is the edition cited throughout this vault.
See [[Subul al-Hudā war-Rashād]] for the volume-by-volume structure.
