# Qatādah b. al-Nuʿmān (قتادة بن النعمان)
*The Badri Archer of Banū Ẓafar, Maternal Brother of Abū Saʿīd al-Khudrī, and He Whose Eye Was Restored by the Prophetic Palm*

## 1. Lineage & Early Islam
**Qatādah b. al-Nuʿmān** ﵁ belongs to the clan of Banū Ẓafar of the Aws tribe. His lineage traces through Qatādah b. al-Nuʿmān b. Zayd b. ʿĀmir b. Sawād b. Ẓafar al-Awsī al-Ẓafarī (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:317).

He was the maternal brother of the eminent Companion **[[Abū Saʿīd al-Khudrī]]** ﵁, their mother being Unaysah bint Qays al-Najjāriyyah (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:318).

According to Ibn Shāhīn citing Ibn Abī Dāwūd, Qatādah was the very first Companion to enter Medina bringing a chapter of the Qurʾān—namely **[[Sūrat Maryam]]** (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:318).

## 2. Key Sīrah Milestones

### The Veteran of Badr & The Miracle of the Restored Eye
Qatādah witnessed **[[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]]** alongside the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ and stood steadfast in the subsequent campaigns (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:318).

During battle (transmitted in narrations regarding Badr, and through other well-attested routes regarding **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]**), an enemy missile struck Qatādah in the eye, causing his eyeball to dislodge and spill down upon his cheek (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:318). When the Companions intended to sever the tendon, they halted to consult the Prophet ﷺ. The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ forbade cutting it, took the displaced eyeball in his blessed palm, placed it back into its socket, and gently pressed it into place (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:318).

The eye was miraculously restored to perfect health; it became the sharper and more beautiful of his two eyes, such that observers could not distinguish which eye had been injured (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:318).

### The Luminous Palm Branch
On a stormy, pitch-black night, the Prophet ﷺ stepped out for the ʿIshāʾ prayer when a flash of lightning illuminated the courtyard. Seeing Qatādah present, the Prophet ﷺ inquired why he ventured out in such darkness; Qatādah replied that he wished to attend the prayer because attendees were few (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:319). 

Upon completing the prayer, the Prophet ﷺ handed him a dry palm stalk (*ʿurjūn*), telling him: *"Take this, for it will illuminate ten spans before you and ten spans behind you"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:319).

### Later Campaigns & Passing
Qatādah accompanied the Prophet ﷺ at **[[Ghazwat Ḥunayn]]** as recorded by al-Wāqidī (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:319).

He passed away during the Caliphate of **[[ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb]]** ﵁. ʿUmar led the funeral prayer over him and personally descended into his grave to lay him to rest (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:319).
