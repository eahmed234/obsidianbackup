## 1. Overview
One of the very last complete surahs revealed. Because it abrogated all treaties with hostile paganism, it opens without the *Basmalah*. Classically termed *al-Fāḍiḥah* (The Exposer) by [[ʿAbdullāh b. ʿAbbās]] because it systematically dissected the psychological motivations and secrets of the hypocrites (*al-Munāfiqūn*) during **[[Ghazwat Tabūk]]**.

## 2. Key Asbāb al-Nuzūl
- **9:40**: The immortal testimony of **[[al-Hijrah ilā al-Madīnah]]** and **[[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]]** in the Cave: *«إِذْ هُمَا فِي الْغَارِ إِذْ يَقُولُ لِصَاحِبِهِ لَا تَحْزَنْ إِنَّ اللَّهَ مَعَنَا»*.
- **9:60**: The comprehensive legal codification of the eight categories of Zakat.
- **9:79**: Slander against the massive donations of **[[ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. ʿAwf]]** and the modest dates of Abū ʿAqīl.
- **9:84**: Absolute prohibition against praying the funeral prayer over hypocrites, revealed upon the demise of **[[ʿAbdullāh b. Ubayy b. Salūl]]**.
- **9:107–108**: Unmasking the subversive conspiratorial center: **Masjid al-Ḍirār**, ordered demolished by the Prophet ﷺ.
- **9:118**: Divine repentance granted to the three truthful Companions who lagged behind Tabūk without excuse: **[[Kaʿb b. Mālik]]**, Murārah b. al-Rabīʿ, and Hilāl b. Umayyah.
