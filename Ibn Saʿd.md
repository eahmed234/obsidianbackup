# Ibn Saʿd (محمد بن سعد بن منيع)
*Muḥammad b. Saʿd — Compiler of al-Ṭabaqāt al-Kubrā*

## 1. Identity
**Muḥammad b. Saʿd b. Manīʿ al-Baghdādī** (168–230 AH), secretary and student of **al-Wāqidī**, and
author of ***al-Ṭabaqāt al-Kubrā*** — the great prosopography arranging the Companions and the
generations after them into classes (*ṭabaqāt*).

## 2. Relevance to the Sīrah
He is among the authorities [[al-Ṣāliḥī]] cites most heavily, and is typically the source for
*biographical* rather than narrative detail — a Companion's clan, precedence in Islam, date of death,
and standing. Where this vault records that kind of particular, Ibn Saʿd is often behind it: the
account of **[[Asʿad b. Zurārah]]** ﵁ arriving from Shām as a merchant and his foretelling dream is
transmitted on his authority (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:133).

Notes in this vault citing *al-Ṭabaqāt* directly include **[[Saʿd b. al-Rabīʿ]]**
(*al-Ṭabaqāt al-Kubrā* 3:522–525) and **[[al-Ḥubāb b. al-Mundhir]]**
(*al-Ṭabaqāt al-Kubrā* 3:566–567) — note these are al-Ṭabaqāt's own volume and pages, not
Subul al-Hudā's.

> [!NOTE] A Caution on Grading
> Ibn Saʿd transmits abundantly and does not restrict himself to authenticated material the way the
> compilers of the two *Ṣaḥīḥs* do. Al-Ṣāliḥī accordingly reports him but defers to
> [[Ibn Ḥajar al-ʿAsqalānī]] and the *Ṣaḥīḥ* compilers where they conflict.
