# Jabal Salʿ (جبل سلع)
*Mount Salʿ — The Strategic Bastion and Command Post of the Trench*

## 1. Topography & Significance
A rugged basaltic hill located on the northwestern flank of **[[al-Madīnah al-Munawwarah]]**.

## 2. Role at Ghazwat al-Khandaq
When the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ surveyed Medina to prepare for the Confederate siege, he chose to place Mount Salʿ directly at the back of the Muslim army:
> *"He mounted his horse, accompanied by a party of the Muhājirūn and Anṣār, scouting an encampment; the most agreeable position to him was to anchor Mount Salʿ behind his back, and to dig the trench from al-Madhād to Dhubāb to Rātij"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:365).

The Muslim defensive line was established at the base of Mount Salʿ, with three thousand defenders fronting the trench while the heights protected their rear (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:371). On the slopes of Mount Salʿ, the Prophet's command pavilion (*al-qubbah*) was pitched, and it was upon its heights that he stood supplicating for three days until divine victory descended (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:383).
