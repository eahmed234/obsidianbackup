# Farwah b. ʿAmr al-Judhāmī (فروة بن عمرو الجذامي)
*Byzantine Governor of Maʿān, Pioneer Believer of Greater Syria, and Crucified Martyr of the Northern Frontier*

> [!NOTE] Primary Sourcing & Biographical Provenance
> Sourced directly from al-Ḥāfiẓ Ibn Ḥajar al-ʿAsqalānī's ***[[al-Iṣābah fī Tamyīz al-Ṣaḥābah]]***, vol. 5, p. 295 (entry 7035), and Imām Muḥammad b. Yūsuf al-Ṣāliḥī's ***[[Subul al-Hudā war-Rashād]]***, vol. 6, p. 391; vol. 7, p. 403.

---

## 1. Lineage & Administrative Station
Farwah b. ʿAmr belonged to the prominent southern Arab tribe of **Judhām** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:391; *al-Iṣābah*, 5:295).
- **Full Genealogy**: Farwah b. ʿAmr (and in some transmissions b. ʿĀmir) b. al-Nāqidah al-Bunnānī al-Judhāmī (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:295).
- **Imperial Governor of Maʿān**: Prior to his conversion, he served as the appointed Roman/Byzantine prefect (*ʿāmil li-Qayṣar*) presiding over the Arab border tribes in the strategic southern Syrian outpost of **Maʿān** and the adjacent marches of al-Balqāʾ and Palestine (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:391).

---

## 2. Conversion to Islam & Gift to the Prophet ﷺ
When the call of the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ reached Greater Syria (*al-Shām*), Farwah embraced Islam in his heart, recognizing the truth of the Prophetic mission (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:391).
- **The Emissary & The White Mule**: Ibn Isḥāq transmitted that Farwah dispatched a personal messenger to the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ formally conveying his conversion to Islam (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:391; *al-Iṣābah*, 5:295). Along with his letter of submission, he presented the Prophet ﷺ with a prized white mule (*baghlah bayḍāʾ*), identified as **al-Fiḍḍah** (and by some authorities linked to the steed al-Luḥayf) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:391; 7:403).
- **Acceptance by the Prophet ﷺ**: The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ welcomed his emissary, accepted his conversion and gifts, and prayed for him (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:391; *al-Iṣābah*, 6:79).

---

## 3. Arrest, Incarceration, and Poetic Defiance
When the Byzantine authorities discovered that their high-ranking Arab governor had abandoned Christianity and embraced the faith of Islam, they immediately arrested and imprisoned him (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:391; *al-Iṣābah*, 5:295).
- **The Prison Verses**: While languishing in chains under Roman guard, Farwah composed defiant verses rhyming in *nūn* declaring his unwavering faith:
  > طَرَقَتْ سُلَيْمَى مَوْهِنًا أَصْحَابِي ... وَالرُّومُ بَيْنَ الْبَابِ وَالْقَرَوَانِ  
  > صَدَّ الْخَيَالُ وَسَاءَهُ مَا قَدْ رَأَى ... وَهَمَمْتُ أَنْ أُغْفِيَ وَقَدْ أَبْكَانِي  
  > وَلَقَدْ عَلِمْتُ أَبَا كُبَيْشَةَ أَنَّنِي ... وَسَطَ الأَعِزَّةِ لَا يُحَصُّ لِسَانِي  
  > فَلَئِنْ هَلَكْتُ لَتَفْقِدُنَّ أَخَاكُمُ ... وَلَئِنْ بَقِيتُ لَتَعْرِفُنَّ مَكَانِي  
  > وَلَقَدْ جَمَعْتُ أَجَلَّ مَا جَمَعَ الْفَتَى ... مِنْ جَوْدَةٍ وَشَجَاعَةٍ وَبَيَانِ
  > *"Sulaymā visited my companions late in the night, while the Romans stood guard between the gate and the courtyard; the phantom turned away, grieved by the plight it witnessed, as I sought slumber through flowing tears; and indeed I knew, O Abū Kubayshah [the Prophet ﷺ], that amidst the noble and honorable my tongue would never falter; if I perish, you shall surely lose your brother, and if I survive, you shall know my station; for I have united the finest qualities a youth can assemble: generosity, valor, and eloquence!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:391).

---

## 4. Martyrdom & Crucifixion at ʿAfrāʾ
The Byzantine imperial court offered Farwah release and restoration of his high office on condition of renouncing Islam. When he steadfastly refused, they resolved to execute and crucify him publicly as a deterrent to other Arab tribes (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:391).
- **The Springs of ʿAfrāʾ**: The Romans marched him to a watering place in Palestine known as **ʿAfrāʾ** (*Māʾ ʿAfrāʾ*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:391).
- **The Execution & Final Stanza**: As the executioners brought him forward to be beheaded and suspended upon the cross, he declared his sublime final testament, recorded by Ibn Shihāb al-Zuhrī:
  > أَبْلِغْ سَرَاةَ الْمُسْلِمِينَ بِأَنَّنِي ... سَلَمٌ لِرَبِّي أَعْظُمِي وَمَقَامِي
  > *"Convey unto the noble leaders of the Muslims that I have surrendered my bones and my station entirely unto my Lord!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:391; *al-Iṣābah*, 5:295).
- They struck his neck and crucified his body at ʿAfrāʾ, sealing his destiny as one of the very first martyrs for Islam on Syrian soil (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:391; *al-Iṣābah*, 5:295, 327).
