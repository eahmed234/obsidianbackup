# ʿUkkāshah b. Miḥṣan (عكاشة بن محصن) ﵁
*The Promised Inheritor of Paradise Without Reckoning — Hero of the Divine Blade al-ʿAwn and Badri Martyr*

## 1. Lineage & Early Conversion
- **Lineage**: ʿUkkāshah b. Miḥṣan b. Ḥurthān b. Qays b. Murrah b. Bukayr b. Ghanm b. Dūdān b. Asad b. Khuzaymah al-Asadī (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:439–440, no. 5648).
- **Confederacy**: Confederate of Banū ʿAbd Shams b. ʿAbd Manāf in Makkah (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:440).
- **Brother**: Younger brother of **[[Abū Sinān b. Miḥṣan]]** ﵁, the Badri veteran who passed away during the siege of Banū Qurayẓah (*al-Iṣābah*, 7:163).
- **Early Precedence**: Counted among the foremost early converts (*al-Sābiqūn al-Awwalūn*) who embraced Islam in Makkah and performed the Hijrah to Medina (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:440).

## 2. Ghazwat Badr & The Miraculous Sword al-ʿAwn
- Marched alongside the Prophet ﷺ to **[[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]]** (**[[Ahl Badr]]**) (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:440).
- During the fierce fighting, ʿUkkāshah fought with such ferocity that his sword snapped in his hand, leaving him unarmed.
- He came to the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ seeking a weapon. The Prophet ﷺ handed him a dry branch of wood (*jidhlan min ḥaṭab*) and commanded: *«Fight with this, O ʿUkkāshah!»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:52–53).
- When ʿUkkāshah took the branch and brandished it, it miraculously transformed in his grasp into a formidable, glistening sword with a long blade, dense spine, and pure white steel:
  > «فَعَادَ سَيْفًا فِي يَدِهِ طَوِيلَ الْقَامَةِ، شَدِيدَ الْمَتْنِ، أَبْيَضَ الْحَدِيدَةِ، فَقَاتَلَ بِهِ حَتَّى فَتَحَ اللهُ عَلَى الْمُسْلِمِينَ»
  > *"It turned into a sword in his hand—long, strong-spined, and of white steel—and he fought with it until Allāh granted victory to the Muslims!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:53).
- The sword was named **al-ʿAwn** ("The Divine Aid"). ʿUkkāshah preserved it reverently and fought with it in all subsequent military expeditions alongside the Prophet ﷺ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:53).

## 3. The Seventy Thousand Promised Paradise Without Reckoning
- In the authentic hadīth recorded in *Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī* and *Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim*, when the Prophet ﷺ described the seventy thousand of his Ummah who shall enter Paradise without reckoning or punishment, ʿUkkāshah immediately stood up and pleaded: *«Supplicate Allāh to make me among them!»*
- The Prophet ﷺ replied: *«You are among them!»*
- When another man arose requesting the same, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ said:
  > «سَبَقَكَ بِهَا عُكَّاشَةُ»
  > *"ʿUkkāshah has preceded you to it!"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:440).
- This immortal prophetic proclamation became an enduring Arabic proverb applied to anyone who swiftly seizes a noble opportunity (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:440).

## 4. Martyrdom in the Wars of Apostasy
- Following the death of the Prophet ﷺ, ʿUkkāshah fought steadfastly under the banner of **[[Khālid b. al-Walīd]]** in the Wars of Apostasy (**[[Ḥurūb al-Riddah]]**).
- Attained martyrdom at the Battle of al-Buzākhah, slain by Ṭulayḥah b. Khuwaylid al-Asadī (who subsequently returned to Islam) (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:440; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:53).
