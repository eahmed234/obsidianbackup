# ʿUkkāshah b. Miḥṣan (عكاشة بن محصن)
*The First to Enter Paradise Without Reckoning, Bearer of the Miraculous Sword al-ʿAwn, and Martyr of Buzākhah*

## 1. Lineage and Early Life

ʿUkkāshah b. Miḥṣan b. Ḥurthān b. Qays b. Murrah b. Bukayr b. Ghanm b. Dūdān b. Asad b. Khuzaymah al-Asadī, an ally (*ḥalīf*) of Banū ʿAbd Shams of Quraysh (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:440). 

He was among the foremost pioneers (*al-Sābiqūn al-Awwalūn*) who accepted Islam early in Makkah, and was renowned among the Companions for his remarkable handsomeness, martial valor, and piety (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:440). His sister was the early emigrant Companion Umm Qays bint Miḥṣan ﵂ (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:440).

## 2. "ʿUkkāshah Has Preceded You!"

In *Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī* and *Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim*, the Messenger of Allah ﷺ described the seventy thousand of his Ummah who will enter Paradise without reckoning or punishment:
> «هم الذين لا يسترقون ولا يتطيرون ولا يكتوون وعلى ربهم يتوكلون»
> *"They are those who do not seek incantations, nor do they believe in evil omens, nor do they cauterize, and upon their Lord they place their complete reliance."*

Hearing this, ʿUkkāshah leaped to his feet and pleaded:
> «ادع الله أن يجعلني منهم»
> *"Supplicate to Allah that He make me one of them!"*

The Prophet ﷺ replied immediately:
> «أنت منهم»
> *"You are among them."*

Another man stood up and asked: *"Supplicate to Allah that He make me one of them as well!"* The Prophet ﷺ remarked:
> «سبقك بها عكاشة»
> *"ʿUkkāshah has preceded you to it!"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:440).

This statement became an enduring Arabic proverb applied to anyone who swiftly seizes a noble opportunity before anyone else (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:440).

## 3. The Miracle of the Sword *al-ʿAwn* at Badr (2 AH)

ʿUkkāshah fought heroically at [[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā|Badr]]. During the height of the combat, his sword shattered at the hilt, leaving him unarmed against the enemy ranks (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:53).

He hurried to the Messenger of Allah ﷺ in the command shelter (*al-ʿArīsh*). The Prophet ﷺ handed him a dry wooden stick of firewood (*jadhlan min ḥaṭab*) and commanded:
> «قاتل بهذا يا عكاشة»
> *"Fight with this, O ʿUkkāshah!"*

As soon as ʿUkkāshah took the stick and shook it in his hand, it miraculously transformed into a long, sturdy, gleaming white steel sword (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:53). ʿUkkāshah charged back into the battlefield with this miraculous weapon, fighting until Allah granted victory to the Muslims (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:53). 

That sword was named **al-ʿAwn** ("The Divine Aid"). ʿUkkāshah retained it as his personal weapon across all subsequent campaigns with the Prophet ﷺ until he was martyred holding it (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:53).

## 4. Commanded Expeditions

The Messenger of Allah ﷺ appointed ʿUkkāshah b. Miḥṣan to command multiple military detachments:
- In Rabīʿ al-Awwal of 6 AH, he was dispatched in command of forty men to Ghamr Marzūq, a watering place of Banū Asad, successfully dispersing the enemy force (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:77).
- In Rabīʿ al-Ākhar of 9 AH, the Prophet ﷺ dispatched him at the head of a detachment to al-Jibāb in the territory of ʿUdhrah and Balī (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:220).

## 5. Martyrdom at Buzākhah

During the [[Ḥurūb al-Riddah|Riddah Wars]], when Ṭulayḥah b. Khuwaylid al-Asadī falsely claimed prophethood and rallied tribes at al-Buzākhah, [[Khālid b. al-Walīd]] ﵁ dispatched ʿUkkāshah b. Miḥṣan alongside Thābit b. Aqram al-Anṣārī as an advance scouting party (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:440).

The two scouts encountered Ṭulayḥah and his brother Salamah. After a fierce duel, ʿUkkāshah was struck down and achieved martyrdom (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:440; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:53). Ṭulayḥah later repented, embraced Islam sincerely, and praised the valor of the two martyrs he had slain.
