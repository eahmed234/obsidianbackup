# Ḥanẓalah b. Abī ʿĀmir (حنظلة بن أبي عامر)
*Ghasīl al-Malāʾikah — The Martyr Washed by the Angels between Heaven and Earth*

## 1. Identity & Lineage
An Anṣārī nobleman of **[[al-Aws]]** (Banū ʿAmr b. ʿAwf). His father was Abū ʿĀmir ʿAmr b. Ṣayfī, known as *al-Rāhib* in the Jahiliyyah, whom the Prophet ﷺ designated *al-Fāsiq* (the corrupt transgressor) because he departed to Makkah with fifty men to incite Quraysh against Islam and dug trap-pits for the Muslims at Uḥud (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:183, 199, 213). Despite his father's enmity, Ḥanẓalah was among the sincere foremost believers.

## 2. The Nuptial Night and the Call to Arms
On the eve of **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]** (the night of Friday preceding the battle), Ḥanẓalah wed Jamīlah bint ʿAbdillāh b. Ubayy b. Salūl (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:213). He had requested permission from the Prophet ﷺ to remain that night in Medina with his bride, and permission had been granted (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:213).

At dawn on Saturday, after praying the morning prayer, he prepared to join the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ. Jamīlah clung to him, and he remained with her momentarily, entering a state of major ritual impurity (*janābah*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:213). Hearing the urgent battle-shout (*al-hātifah*) summoning the believers to Uḥud, Ḥanẓalah did not delay even to perform ritual ghusl; he seized his arms and rushed directly to the battlefield (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:213).

## 3. Battlefield Valor and Martyrdom
At Uḥud, Ḥanẓalah plunged into the ranks of Quraysh until he reached the supreme Meccan commander, **[[Abū Sufyān b. Ḥarb]]** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:213). Ḥanẓalah struck Abū Sufyān's horse, causing it to fall to the ground. As Abū Sufyān cried out in terror and Ḥanẓalah moved to dispatch him, Shaddād b. al-Aswad (also called Ibn Shuʿūb) charged Ḥanẓalah from behind, piercing him with a spear (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:213). Even as the spear pinned him, Ḥanẓalah advanced toward his assailant along the spear shaft until Shaddād struck him a second fatal blow (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:213).

## 4. The Washing by the Angels
Following his martyrdom, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ announced to the Companions:
> «إِنِّي رَأَيْتُ الْمَلَائِكَةَ تُغَسِّلُهُ بَيْنَ السَّمَاءِ وَالْأَرْضِ بِمَاءِ الْمُزْنِ فِي صِحَافِ الْفِضَّةِ!»
> *"Indeed, I saw the angels washing him between the heavens and the earth with rainwater in silver basins!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:213).

Abū Usayd al-Sāʿidī ﵁ related: *«We went to him, and water was dripping from his head!»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:213). The Prophet ﷺ instructed: *«Inquire of his family what his state was.»* They asked Jamīlah, who informed them: *«He went forth in a state of ritual impurity when he heard the war-cry.»* The Prophet ﷺ remarked: *«It was for that reason that the angels washed him»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:213).

From that day, the clan of the Aws took immense pride in him, exclaiming: *«Among us is the one washed by the angels, Ḥanẓalah b. Abī ʿĀmir!»*
