# Thābit b. Qays (ثابت بن قيس) ﵁
*Abū Muḥammad al-Khazrajī al-Anṣārī — Khaṭīb Rasūlillāh (The Orator of the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ), Promised Paradise by the Prophet ﷺ, Martyr of al-Yamāmah*

## 1. Lineage, Ancestry & Welcoming the Prophet ﷺ
### Patrilineal Descent & Clan
He is **Thābit b. Qays b. Shammās b. Zuhayr b. Mālik b. Imriʾ al-Qays b. Mālik b. Thaʿlabah b. Kaʿb b. al-Khazraj al-Anṣārī al-Khazrajī** (kunya: **Abū Muḥammad**) (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:511). 

- He belonged to the noble lineage of **[[al-Khazraj]]** in Yathrib and was endowed with an exceptionally resonant, powerful, and majestic speaking voice, celebrated as the supreme orator of the Anṣār (*Khaṭīb al-Anṣār*) (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:511).

### The Historic Welcome in Medina (1 AH)
Upon the arrival of the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ during **[[al-Hijrah ilā al-Madīnah]]**, Thābit b. Qays stepped forward to deliver the official address on behalf of the Anṣār, declaring:
> *“We pledge to protect you against that from which we protect our own lives and children! What then shall be our reward?”*
The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ smiled and replied with a single word:
> «الْجَنَّةُ!»
> *"Paradise!"*
Thābit and the Anṣār joyfully responded: *“We are well-pleased!”* (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:511).

## 2. Orator of Islam & Prophetic Glad Tidings
Whenever foreign delegations arrived to challenge the Muslims in oratorical rhetoric, the Prophet ﷺ appointed Thābit b. Qays to speak on behalf of Islam, while appointing Ḥassān b. Thābit for poetry (*Subul al-Hudā*, 10:58).

### The Encounter with Banū Tamīm (9 AH)
During **[[Wafd Banī Tamīm]]** (**[[ʿĀm al-Wufūd]]**), when the orator of Tamīm (ʿUṭārid b. Ḥājib) delivered a proud boast of his tribe's power, the Prophet ﷺ turned to Thābit b. Qays, commanding: *«Rise and answer his speech!»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:332). 

Thābit delivered a thunderous oration recounting Allāh's blessings, the election of the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ, the sacrifice of the Muhājirūn, and the loyalty of the Anṣār with such breathtaking eloquence that the delegation was left utterly speechless, leading to their collective acceptance of Islam (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:333).

### Sūrat al-Ḥujurāt & The Glad Tidings of Paradise
When the verse was revealed in Sūrat al-Ḥujurāt:
> «يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا لَا تَرْفَعُوا أَصْوَاتَكُمْ فَوْقَ صَوْتِ النَّبِيِّ ... أَن تَحْبَطَ أَعْمَالُكُمْ وَأَنتُمْ لَا تَشْعُرُونَ»
> *«O you who believe! Raise not your voices above the voice of the Prophet... lest your deeds be rendered fruitless while you perceive not»* (al-Ḥujurāt 49:2) (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:511; *Subul al-Hudā*, 10:59),
Thābit locked himself inside his house, weeping inconsolably and declaring: *“I am among the people of the Fire! For I am a man of loud voice, and my deeds have surely been rendered fruitless!”*

Missing his presence, the Prophet ﷺ inquired about him. **[[Saʿd b. Muʿādh]]** ﵁ visited Thābit and found him weeping in remorse. When Saʿd reported his plight to the Prophet ﷺ, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ declared with divine certainty:
> «كَلَّا! إِنَّهُ لَيْسَ مِنْ أَهْلِ النَّارِ، بَلْ هُوَ مِنْ أَهْلِ الْجَنَّةِ! يَعِيشُ حَمِيدًا، وَيُقْتَلُ شَهِيدًا، وَيَدْخُلُ الْجَنَّةَ!»
> *"No! He is not among the people of the Fire, but rather he is of the people of Paradise! He shall live praised, be slain as a martyr, and enter Paradise!"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:511; *Subul al-Hudā*, 10:60; *Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī*; *Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim*).

## 3. Martyrdom at al-Yamāmah & The Miraculous Testament (12 AH)
During the Caliphate of **[[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]]** ﵁, Thābit marched in the vanguard under **[[Khālid b. al-Walīd]]** ﵁ to combat the arch-apostate Musaylimah al-Kadhdhāb in **[[Ḥurūb al-Riddah]]**.

### The Epic at the Garden of Death
When the Muslim lines wavered under the ferocious onslaught of Banū Ḥanīfah, Thābit b. Qays and Sālim Mawlā Abī Ḥudhayfah dug trenches up to their mid-shins, put on white burial shrouds (*ḥunūṭ*), and cried out: *“Wretched is that to which you have accustomed your enemies!”* 

They stood like unyielding pillars until Thābit was struck down as a martyr, fulfilling the prophecy of the Prophet ﷺ (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:511).

### The Miraculous Testament Executed by Abū Bakr
Following the battle, a Muslim soldier had a dream in which Thābit b. Qays appeared to him, saying:
> *“Know that after I was slain, a Muslim took my armor and hid it under a pot near the edge of the camp, placing a horse saddle upon it. Go to Khālid and tell him to retrieve my armor. And when you reach Medina, tell the Caliph of the Messenger of Allāh, Abū Bakr: 'I owe such-and-such debts, so pay them from its value, and emancipate my slave so-and-so!' Beware that you do not dismiss this as a mere dream!”* (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:511–512).

The soldier reported the dream to Khālid b. al-Walīd, who went directly to the spot and found the valuable armor precisely as described. Upon reaching Medina, he narrated the vision to Caliph Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq ﵁, who fully executed the debts and emancipated the slave (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:512). Scholars of the Sunnah noted that no person in human history had their post-mortem testament legally executed through a dream except Thābit b. Qays ﵁.
