# ʿAbdullāh b. ʿAbdillāh b. Ubayy (عبد الله بن عبد الله بن أبي)
*The Paragon of Pure Monotheism — The Badri Knight Who Stood against Hypocrisy*

## 1. Lineage & Embracing of Islam
ʿAbdullāh b. ʿAbdillāh b. Ubayy b. Salūl al-Anṣārī al-Khazrajī was the son of the infamous arch-hypocrite of Medina, **[[ʿAbdullāh b. Ubayy b. Salūl]]** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:350). His original pre-Islamic name was **al-Ḥubāb**, but the Prophet ﷺ renamed him **ʿAbdullāh**, stating that al-Ḥubāb was a name of the devil (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:350).

In stark contrast to his father, ʿAbdullāh was renowned for unwavering faith, profound piety, and sincere devotion to the Prophet ﷺ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:350).

## 2. Key Sīrah Milestones
- **[[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]] (2 AH)**: Fought valiantly in the frontline ranks of the Anṣār (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:45).
- **Ghazwat Banī al-Muṣṭaliq (5 AH)**: Following the battle, when his father made the seditious declaration: *"If we return to Medina, the more honorable will surely expel therefrom the more base!"* (referring to the Prophet ﷺ), ʿAbdullāh drew his sword, halted his father at the gates of Medina, and forced him to testify that the Prophet ﷺ was the honorable and he was the base (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:350). He then petitioned the Prophet ﷺ:
  > *"O Messenger of Allāh! If you intend to execute my father for his treason, command me to do so and I shall bring you his head, lest another Muslim slay him and my soul be tempted by tribal blood!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:350).
  The Prophet ﷺ gently restrained him, saying: «بَلْ نَتَرَفَّقُ بِهِ، وَنُحْسِنُ صُحْبَتَهُ مَا بَقِيَ مَعَنَا» (*"Nay, rather we shall be gentle with him and treat him with good companionship as long as he remains among us"*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:350).
- **Filial Piety at his Father's Demise (9 AH)**: When his father died, ʿAbdullāh requested the Prophet's tunic to shroud his father's body and petitioned the Prophet ﷺ to offer the funeral prayer, demonstrating sublime filial compassion (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:192).

## 3. Martyrdom at al-Yamāmah (12 AH)
ʿAbdullāh fought through all the campaigns of the Prophet ﷺ and achieved martyrdom fighting the apostates at the Battle of al-Yamāmah (12 AH) during the caliphate of **[[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]]** ﵁ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:333).
