# Sariyyat Baṭn Iḍam (سرية أبي قتادة إلى بطن إضم)
*The eight-man strategic diversionary detachment commanded by Abū Qatādah al-Anṣārī prior to Fatḥ Makkah and the revelation of Qurʾān 4:94*

## 1. Strategic Diversion for the Conquest of Makkah

In the beginning of Ramaḍān 8 AH, when the Prophet ﷺ resolved to march secretly upon Makkah for **[[Fatḥ Makkah]]**, he maintained total operational security (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:190). To conceal the actual axis of advance and deceive enemy informants into believing that the Muslim army was targeting the northern desert tribes, the Prophet ﷺ dispatched a mobile detachment of **eight men** northward toward **Baṭn Iḍam** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:190). 

Baṭn Iḍam was a prominent valley located three postal stages (*thalāthati burud*) north of Medina (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:190, 191). The Prophet ﷺ placed the detachment under the trusted cavalry commander **[[Abū Qatādah al-Anṣārī]]** ﵁ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:190). Among the eight men in the unit were:
- **[[ʿAbdullāh b. Abī Ḥadrad al-Aslamī]]** ﵁
- **Muḥallim b. Jaththāmah al-Laythī**

## 2. The Encounter at Baṭn Iḍam

While the patrol traversed the valley bed of Baṭn Iḍam, a traveler named **ʿĀmir b. al-Aḍbaṭ al-Ashjaʿī** rode past them mounted upon a young camel (*qaʿūd*), carrying provisions and a leather vessel of milk (*waṭb min laban*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:190). 

Upon seeing the armed Muslim detachment, ʿĀmir greeted them with the Islamic greeting of peace (*sallama ʿalaynā bi-taḥiyyat al-Islām*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:190). Recognizing the greeting, Abū Qatādah and the rest of the company withheld their weapons (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:190). 

However, Muḥallim b. Jaththāmah harbored an old blood feud from the pre-Islamic Jahiliyyah against ʿĀmir (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:190). Overriding the greeting of peace, Muḥallim charged ʿĀmir, killed him, and seized his mount and provisions (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:190).

## 3. Revelation of Qurʾān 4:94 (Āyat al-Tabayyun)

Finding no enemy mobilization at Iḍam, the detachment turned back toward Medina (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:190). Upon reaching Dhū Khashab, they learned that the Prophet ﷺ had already mobilized the grand army of ten thousand and marched south toward Makkah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:190). Accelerating their pace along the route of Yubayyin, they overtook the Prophet ﷺ at **al-Suqyā** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:190).

When they reported the tragic slaying of ʿĀmir b. al-Aḍbaṭ, Allāh revealed the momentous divine decree in **Sūrat al-Nisāʾ (4:94)** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:190):
> ﴿يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا إِذَا ضَرَبْتُمْ فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ فَتَبَيَّنُوا وَلَا تَقُولُوا لِمَنْ أَلْقَىٰ إِلَيْكُمُ السَّلَامَ لَسْتَ مُؤْمِنًا تَبْتَغُونَ عَرَضَ الْحَيَاةِ الدُّنْيَا فَعِندَ اللَّهِ مَغَانِمُ كَثِيرَةٌ﴾
> *«O you who have believed, when you go forth in the cause of Allāh, investigate; and do not say to one who offers you peace: 'You are not a believer,' aspiring for the goods of worldly life; for with Allāh are abundant gains.»*

## 4. The Stern Rebuke of Muḥallim & Prophetic Lesson

When Muḥallim came before the Prophet ﷺ seeking forgiveness, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ questioned him with profound gravity:
> «أقتلته بعد ما قال إني مسلم؟ أفلا شققت عن قلبه لتعلم أصادق هو أم كاذب؟... لا ما في قلبه تعلم ولا لسانه صدّقت»
> *"Did you kill him after he declared: 'I am a Muslim'? Did you tear open his heart to know whether he was truthful or lying?... You neither knew what was in his heart nor did you believe what his tongue proclaimed!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:190).

When Muḥallim wept and pleaded: *"Ask forgiveness for me, O Messenger of Allāh!"*, the Prophet ﷺ responded with severe prophetic deterrence: *«Lā ghafara Allāhu laka»* (*"May Allāh not forgive you!"*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:190). Muḥallim departed weeping bitterly, and within seven days he passed away (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:190). 

When his companions attempted to bury him, the earth repeatedly cast his corpse out of the grave (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:191). The Prophet ﷺ declared:
> «إن الأرض تقبل من هو شرّ من صاحبكم ولكن اللَّه تعالى يريد أن يعظكم»
> *"Verily, the earth accepts those who are far worse than your companion, but Allāh the Exalted desires to admonish you!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:191).

They subsequently placed him in a mountain ravine and covered him with stones (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:191). The Prophet ﷺ subsequently mediated the payment of full blood-money (*diyah*) to the tribe of Ashjaʿ between ʿUyaynah b. Ḥiṣn and al-Aqraʿ b. Ḥābis (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:191).

## 5. Strategic Significance

1. **Mastery of Operational Deception**: The dispatch of Abū Qatādah's detachment to northern Baṭn Iḍam entirely misled Quraysh's intelligence network, ensuring that the 10,000-strong army arrived undetected at Marr al-Ẓahrān outside Makkah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:190).
2. **The Inviolability of the Islamic Salām**: The revelation of Qurʾān 4:94 irrevocably enshrined the legal principle that anyone who extends the greeting of peace (*al-Salām*) is entitled to absolute sanctity of life and property, forbidding any soldier from judging inner intent (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:190).
3. **Repudiation of Jahiliyyah Vengeance**: The severe prophetic reaction extinguished tribal blood-feuds from surviving under the guise of military operations (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:190–191).
