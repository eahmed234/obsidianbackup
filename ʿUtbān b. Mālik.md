# ʿUtbān b. Mālik (عتبان بن مالك)
*The Badri Companion of the Prayer Sanctuary — Brother-in-Faith to al-Fārūq*

## 1. Lineage & Early Islam
ʿUtbān b. Mālik b. ʿAmr al-Anṣārī al-Khazrajī was a respected noble of the Banū Sālim b. ʿAwf branch of **[[al-Khazraj]]** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:360). He embraced Islam early in Medina, and upon the Prophet's arrival, was paired in the covenant of brotherhood (**[[al-Muʾākhāh]]**) with **[[ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb]]** ﵁ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:360). Later, ʿUmar and an Anṣārī neighbor (often identified as ʿUtbān or his kinsman) took turns visiting the Prophet ﷺ on alternate days to report the latest revelations and guidance (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:365).

## 2. Key Sīrah Milestones
- **[[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]] (2 AH)**: Fought as a Badri combatant, subsequently witnessing **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]** and **[[Ghazwat al-Khandaq]]** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:45, 365).
- **The Sanctuary in his Home**: In his later years, ʿUtbān suffered failing eyesight, and seasonal torrential floods in the valley of his clan (*Wādī Baṭḥān*) frequently separated his home from the congregational mosque. He petitioned the Prophet ﷺ: *"O Messenger of Allāh! I desire that you visit my house and pray in a spot which I may take as a permanent prayer place [Muṣallā]"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 7:195).
The Prophet ﷺ arrived accompanied by **[[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]]** ﵁, asked: «أَيْنَ تُحِبُّ أَنْ أُصَلِّيَ لَكَ مِنْ بَيْتِكَ؟» (*"Where in your home would you like me to pray?"*), lined up the companions behind him, and offered two rakʿahs, sanctifying that precinct as a place of prayer (*Subul al-Hudā*, 7:195–197).

## 3. Demise & Legacy
ʿUtbān lived in Medina through the caliphates of the Rightly Guided Caliphs and died during the caliphate of Muʿāwiyah b. Abī Sufyān (*Subul al-Hudā*, 7:197).
