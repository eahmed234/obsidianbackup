# Abū Usayd al-Sāʿidī (أبو أسيد الساعدي)
*Mālik b. Rabīʿah — Archer of Badr, Standard-Bearer of Banū Sāʿidah, and Venerated Last Badri Veteran*

> [!NOTE] Canonical Sourcing
> Profile documented in *al-Iṣābah fī Tamyīz al-Ṣaḥābah* (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:535–536, entry 7644) and *Subul al-Hudā war-Rashād* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:116, 213, 353).

---

## 1. Lineage & Identity

**Mālik b. Rabīʿah** b. al-Badan b. ʿĀmir b. ʿAwf b. Ḥārithah b. ʿAmr b. al-Khazraj b. Sāʿidah b. Kaʿb b. al-Khazraj al-Anṣārī al-Sāʿidī (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:535).

- **Kunya**: Universally renowned by his kunya **Abū Usayd** (with a ḍammah on the hamzah in diminutive form: أُسَيْد) (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:535; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:116).
- **Physical Appearance**: Described as short in stature, with a thick beard and hair that turned pure white in his advanced age (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:536).

---

## 2. Key Sīrah Milestones & Martial Valor

- **Veteran of Badr**: He fought at [[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]], standing firmly in the ranks of the Anṣārī archers (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:535; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:116).
- **Witness to the Washing of Ḥanẓalah**: At [[Ghazwat Uḥud]], following the martyrdom of [[Ḥanẓalah b. Abī ʿĀmir]], Abū Usayd was among those dispatched to inspect the martyr's body. He testified before the Prophet ﷺ: *"We went to him, and water was dripping from his head,"* affirming the angelic washing (*ghasīl al-malāʾikah*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:213).
- **The Prophetic Horse Race**: When the Prophet ﷺ held horse races at al-Nuqayʿ, Abū Usayd rode the Prophet's swift horse **al-Ẓarb** and won the race (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:353).
- **Standard-Bearer at the Conquest**: On the day of [[Fatḥ Makkah]], Abū Usayd bore the supreme military banner (*rāyah*) of the clan of [[Banū Sāʿidah]] (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:535).

---

## 3. Transmission, Longevity & Demise

- **Hadīth Transmission**: Abū Usayd narrated numerous canonical traditions from the Prophet ﷺ, transmitted by his sons Ḥumayd, al-Zubayr, and al-Mubadhdhir, as well as Companions including [[Anas b. Mālik]] and [[Sahl b. Saʿd al-Sāʿidī]] (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:535).
- **Last Surviving Veteran of Badr**: Having lost his sight in old age, he outlived his fellow veterans and was recognized by classical biographical authorities as the final surviving warrior of Badr (*ākhir al-badriyyīn mawtā*) (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:536).
- **Demise**: He passed away in Madīnah around 60 AH at the age of approximately seventy-five to eighty years (*al-Iṣābah*, 5:536).
