# Ghazwat Banī Sulaym (غزوة بني سليم بالكدر)
*The Expedition of Qarqarat al-Kudr — The Dispersal of Sulaym and Ghaṭafān, The 500 Camels, and the Emancipation of the Praying Youth Yasār*

## 1. Strategic Context & Provocation
Merely seven nights following the Prophet's ﷺ victorious return to Medina from **[[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]]** in Shawwāl 2 AH, urgent military intelligence reached the city (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:172). 

A predatory coalition of **Banū Sulaym** (Banū Sulaym b. Manṣūr) and **[[Ghaṭafān]]** had assembled at **Qarqarat al-Kudr**—a smooth watering basin frequented by migrating dun-colored grouse birds (*al-kudr*)—preparing an assault against Medina to capitalize on any perceived exhaustion following Badr (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:172–173).

Refusing to yield the strategic initiative, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ ordered immediate counter-mobilization (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:172). He appointed **Sibāʿ b. ʿUrfuṭah al-Ghifārī** ﵁ (or **[[Abdullāh b. Umm Maktūm|ʿAbdullāh b. Umm Maktūm]]** ﵁) as deputy over Medina (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:172).

## 2. Rapid Advance to al-Kudr
The Prophet ﷺ entrusted the supreme white battle standard to **[[ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib]]** ﵁ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:172). 

The Muslim army marched with great speed, advancing directly across the desert to al-Kudr (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:172). Finding the encampments evacuated, the Prophet ﷺ deployed a pincer maneuver: he sent a detachment along the crest of the valley while he advanced through the valley floor (*baṭn al-wādī*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:172). 

They surprised the enemy's pastoral herdsmen, who scattered into the desert wilderness, leaving behind five hundred (500) prime camels (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:172).

## 3. The Emancipation of the Youth Yasār
Among the herdsmen captured was a young slave boy named **Yasār** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:172). 

When the spoils were inventoried, the Prophet ﷺ set aside the state fifth (*al-khums*) and distributed the remaining four-fifths among the warriors (each warrior receiving two camels) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:172). Yasār fell into the share of the Prophet's ﷺ fifth (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:172).

Observing Yasār in camp, the Prophet ﷺ noticed that he was performing the ritual prayer with deep reverence and sincerity (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:172). The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ immediately declared his full emancipation:
> «رَآهُ يُصَلِّي، فَقَالَ: «إِنِّي نُهِيتُ عَنْ قَتْلِ الْمُصَلِّينَ»، وَأَعْتَقَهُ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ لِصَلَاتِهِ!»
> *"He saw him praying, and declared: 'I have been forbidden from harming those who pray!' And the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ emancipated him solely on account of his prayer!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:172–173).

## 4. Strategic Impact
- **Immediate Post-Badr Deterrence**: Coming immediately after Badr, the swift dispersal of Banū Sulaym demonstrated to the warlike tribes of central Arabia that Medina was prepared to defend its territory on multiple fronts simultaneously.
- **Duration**: The Prophet ﷺ returned to Medina after an absence of only three (3) nights (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:172).
