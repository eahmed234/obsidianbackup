# Khuzaymah b. Mudrikah (خزيمة بن مدركة)
*The Prophet's ﷺ Ancestor of the Disputed Etymology*

## 1. Name
Al-Ṣāliḥī gives an unusually extended lexical treatment of *Khuzaymah*'s derivation, weighing several
competing roots: from the diminutive of *khuzmah* (a term for a kind of palm-like tree, per Abū Ḥanīfah
al-Dīnawarī), or from *khuzāmah* — the ring or cord placed through a camel's nose to lead it. Al-Ṣāliḥī
notes candidly that he has not seen anyone address the reasoning behind the name's transfer, adding
that such reasoning is not always required in personal names as it is in epithets
(*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:287).

## 2. Kunyah
His kunyah is given as **Abū Asad**.
