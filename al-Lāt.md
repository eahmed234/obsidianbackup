# al-Lāt (اللات)
*The Ancient Idol Sanctuary of Thaqīf at al-Ṭāʾif, Designated al-Rabbah and al-Ṭāghiyah, Demolished by al-Mughīrah b. Shuʿbah and Abū Sufyān*

> [!NOTE] Primary Sourcing & Geographical Provenance
> Sourced directly from *Subul al-Hudā war-Rashād*, vol. 6, pp. 226–227 (*al-Bāb al-Sābiʿ wal-Sittūn fī Baʿthatihi ﷺ Abā Sufyān b. Ḥarb wal-Mughīrah b. Shuʿbah li-Hadm al-Ṭāghiyah*); vol. 12, p. 48.

---

## 1. Description & Jāhiliyyah Veneration
**al-Lāt** (referred to by her devotees as *al-Rabbah* and termed by the Prophet ﷺ and the believers *al-Ṭāghiyah*) was the premier idol and patron deity of the tribe of **Thaqīf** at **[[al-Ṭāʾif]]** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:226).

It consisted of a massive white carved cubic granite rock (*ḥajar wa-madar*), housed within an ornate temple edifice surrounded by an enclosed sacred precinct (*ḥimā*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:226–227). The sanctuary possessed:
- A hereditary keeper (*sādin*).
- An inner treasure chamber filled with gold, silver, jewels, rich vestments (*kiswah*), and expensive perfumes and unguents offered by Arab pilgrims (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:227).
- Mention in the Qurʾān alongside al-ʿUzzā and Manāt in Sūrat al-Najm: *«Have you then considered al-Lāt and al-ʿUzzā?»* (Qurʾān 53:19).

---

## 2. Diplomatic Negotation with Wafd Thaqīf (9 AH)
In Ramaḍān 9 AH, when the delegation of Thaqīf (**[[Wafd Thaqīf]]**) arrived in Medina to negotiate their submission and entry into Islam, their leaders (including ʿAbd Yālīl b. ʿAmr) pleaded with the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ regarding al-Lāt:
> *"What shall we do with al-Rabbah?"* 
The Prophet ﷺ replied:
> «اهْدِمُوهَا»
> *"Demolish her!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:226).

They protested that the common folk, women, and youths would be terrified that the idol would slay them. They pleaded:
1. To leave her standing untouched for three years; the Prophet ﷺ refused (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:226).
2. For two years, then one year, then for a single month; the Prophet ﷺ adamantly refused any reprieve (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:226).
3. They requested to be exempted from demolishing it with their own hands: *"Relieve us of destroying it, for we shall never demolish it ourselves!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:226).

The Prophet ﷺ accepted their exemption from manual demolition and declared:
> «أَنَا أَبْعَثُ أَبَا سُفْيَانَ بْنَ حَرْبٍ وَالْمُغِيرَةَ بْنَ شُعْبَةَ يَهْدِمَانِهَا»
> *"I shall dispatch Abū Sufyān b. Ḥarb and [[al-Mughīrah b. Shuʿbah]] to demolish her!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:226).

---

## 3. Demolition by al-Mughīrah b. Shuʿbah & Abū Sufyān
Following the conclusion of the treaty, **[[al-Mughīrah b. Shuʿbah]]** ﵁ (himself a native son of Thaqīf) and **[[Abū Sufyān b. Ḥarb]]** ﵁ departed for al-Ṭāʾif with a detachment of companions (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:226).

### The Dramatic Ruse
When they arrived at the sanctuary, the entire population of Thaqīf—men, women, and secluded maidens (*al-ʿawātiq*)—assembled in grief and dread, convinced that al-Lāt would strike down anyone who dared harm her (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:226).

Al-Mughīrah announced to his companions: *"Today I shall make you laugh at Thaqīf!"* 
He took the pickaxe (*al-miʿwal*), climbed upon the structure, and struck a blow; then he intentionally collapsed to the ground, kicking his legs as if struck dead (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:226–227). 

A massive roar erupted from the polytheists: *"May Allāh destroy al-Mughīrah! The Rabbah has slain him! None can ever destroy her!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:227). 

Al-Mughīrah sprang immediately to his feet, mocking their superstition:
> «قَبَّحَكُمُ اللَّهُ يَا مَعْشَرَ ثَقِيفٍ! إِنَّمَا هِيَ لَكَاعٌ، حِجَارَةٌ وَمَدَرٌ، فَاقْبَلُوا عَافِيَةَ اللَّهِ تَعَالَى وَلَا تَعْبُدُوهَا!»
> *"May Allāh confound you, O people of Thaqīf! It is nothing but worthless stone and clay; accept the grace of Allāh the Exalted and worship her not!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:227).

### Obliteration and Distribution of Spoils
Al-Mughīrah and his detachment smashed the gates, climbed the walls, and dismantled the idol stone by stone until it was leveled with the ground (*sawwūhā bil-arḍ*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:227). 
When the idol keeper warned that the foundation would cause the earth to swallow them, al-Mughīrah personally excavated the very foundations (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:227).

They retrieved the extensive treasures, jewelry, gold, silver, and ornamental robes from the vault, loading them upon camels and delivering them directly to the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ in Medina (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:227). The Prophet ﷺ praised Allāh for the triumph of Islam, and utilized the recovered wealth to settle outstanding debts, including the debts of ʿUrwah b. Masʿūd al-Thaqafī (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:227).
