# ʿAbdullāh b. Masʿūd (عبد الله بن مسعود الهذلي) ﵁
*Abū ʿAbd al-Raḥmān al-Hudhalī — Ibn Umm ʿAbd, Custodian of Prophetic Secrets, Foremost Scholar of the Qurʾān*

## 1. Lineage & Early Conversion
He is ʿAbdullāh b. Masʿūd b. Ghāfil b. Ḥabīb al-Hudhalī (kunya: Abū ʿAbd al-Raḥmān; titled affectionately after his mother: *Ibn Umm ʿAbd*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:310; 11:330). In youth, he was a shepherd tending the flocks of ʿUqbah b. Abī Muʿayṭ in Mecca.

He met the Prophet ﷺ and Abū Bakr while they were walking in the hills of Mecca escaping persecution (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:310). When the Prophet ﷺ asked for milk, Ibn Masʿūd replied honestly: *“I am entrusted with them and cannot give you.”* The Prophet ﷺ asked for a virgin ewe that had never mated; when brought, the Prophet ﷺ stroked its udder and mentioned Allāh’s name, and milk flowed abundantly. Amazed, Ibn Masʿūd asked: *“Teach me of these words!”* The Prophet ﷺ stroked his head and said: *“You are an educated young boy!”* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:310). He embraced Islam as the sixth person on earth (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:310).

## 2. Key Sīrah Milestones
- **The First Public Recitation at the Kaʿbah**: The first companion to recite the Qurʾān openly before the assembly of Quraysh at the Kaʿbah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:310). Standing at Maqām Ibrāhīm, he raised his voice with Sūrat al-Raḥmān. Polytheists rushed upon him, striking his face and eyes until he was bruised, but he declared: *“By Allāh, the enemies of Allāh were never more insignificant to me than they are now!”* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:310).
- **Custodian of the Prophetic Intimacy**: Known as *Ṣāḥib al-Siwāk wal-Wisādah wal-Naʿlayn* (Custodian of the Prophet's tooth-stick, cushion, and sandals) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:330). He possessed unique permission to enter the private quarters of the Prophet ﷺ and travel with him, acquiring the Prophet's exact manner of dress, recitation, and legal contemplation.
- **Retribution over Abū Jahl at Badr (2 AH)**: Fought at **[[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]]** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:92). Toward the battle's close, the Prophet ﷺ dispatched him to search for the arch-enemy of Islam, **Abū Jahl (ʿAmr b. Hishām)** ☠. Finding him lying fatally wounded after being struck by the two sons of ʿAfrāʾ, Ibn Masʿūd placed his foot upon Abū Jahl’s neck (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:71). Abū Jahl hissed: *“You have climbed a high perch, O miserable shepherd of sheep!”* Ibn Masʿūd severed his head and dragged it before the Prophet ﷺ, who praised Allāh thrice: *“Allāhu Akbar! Praise be to Allāh Who fulfilled His promise and aided His servant! This was the Pharaoh of this Ummah!”* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:71–72).
- **The Scales of Mount Uḥud**: Slender and frail in stature, he once climbed an arak tree to gather tooth-sticks. A desert wind blew, revealing his exceedingly thin shins, causing some companions to chuckle. The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ said:
  > «مِمَّ تَضْحَكُونَ؟ لَرِجْلُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ أَثْقَلُ فِي الْمِيزَانِ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ مِنْ أُحُدٍ!»
  > *"What are you laughing at?! By Him in Whose hand is my soul, the leg of ʿAbdullāh is heavier in the Scales on the Day of Resurrection than Mount Uḥud!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 10:208; *Musnad Aḥmad*, no. 3991).
- **Master of the Qurʾān**: The Prophet ﷺ urged the community:
  > «مَنْ أَحَبَّ أَنْ يَقْرَأَ الْقُرْآنَ غَضًّا كَمَا أُنْزِلَ، فَلْيَقْرَأْهُ عَلَى قِرَاءَةِ ابْنِ أُمِّ عَبْدٍ!»
  > *"Whoever loves to recite the Qurʾān fresh and pristine as it was revealed, let him recite it according to the recitation of Ibn Umm ʿAbd!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:330).

## 3. Demise & Legacy
Under the Caliphate of ʿUmar, he was dispatched to Kūfah to teach the Qurʾān and administer the public treasury. He became the foundational authority of the Kūfan legal school, directly shaping Islamic jurisprudence.

Returning to Medina in his later years, he passed away in 32 AH at over sixty years of age, and was laid to rest at night in **[[Jannat al-Baqīʿ]]**, with ʿUthmān b. ʿAffān ﵁ leading his funeral prayer (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:330).
