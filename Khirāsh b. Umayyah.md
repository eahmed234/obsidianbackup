# Khirāsh b. Umayyah (خراش بن أمية الخزاعي)
*The Envoy to Makkah — Bearer of the Prophetic Message and Shaver of the Sacred Locks*

## 1. Lineage & Confederacies
His full genealogy is **Khirāsh b. Umayyah b. Rabīʿah b. al-Faḍl b. Munqidh b. ʿAfīf b. Kulayb b. Ḥubshiyyah b. Salūl al-Khuzāʿī al-Kulaybī** (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:231). His *kunyah* was **Abū Naḍlah** (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:231).

He belonged to the prominent southern tribe of **[[Banū Khuzāʿah]]**, specifically the sub-clan of Banū Kulayb b. Ḥubshiyyah. In pre-Islamic Makkah, he lived as a sworn confederate ally (*ḥalīf*) of the aristocratic Qurashī clan of **Banū Makhzūm** (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:231). Ibn al-Kalbī notes that he practiced the trade of cupping and shaving (*ḥajjām*) (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:231).

## 2. Key Sīrah Milestones

### Ghazwat Banī al-Muṣṭaliq (al-Muraysīʿ)
Khirāsh participated in the expedition of al-Muraysīʿ against Banū al-Muṣṭaliq (a branch of Khuzāʿah) in Shaʿbān 5 or 6 AH (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:231). During the chaos following the clash, when the warriors were scouring the field, Khirāsh threw his own body over **ʿĀmir b. Abī Ḍirār al-Khuzāʿī** (the brother of the chieftain al-Ḥārith b. Abī Ḍirār and uncle of Umm al-Muʾminīn Juwayriyah) to protect him from being mistakenly slain by an Anṣārī warrior (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:231).

### The Perilous Diplomatic Mission at al-Ḥudaybiyah (6 AH)
During **[[Ṣulḥ al-Ḥudaybiyah]]** in Dhū al-Qaʿdah 6 AH, when the Muslims were halted at the borders of the Sanctuary, the Prophet ﷺ selected Khirāsh as his very first official diplomatic ambassador dispatched directly into the heart of Makkah (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:231; *Subul al-Hudā*, 5:45). 

The mission was to deliver a direct message to the Qurashī assembly: that the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ had not marched out for war, but solely as peaceful pilgrims honoring the Sacred House and driving sacrificial camels (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:231; *Subul al-Hudā*, 5:45). To dignify the embassy, the Prophet ﷺ mounted Khirāsh upon his own personal riding camel, named **al-Thaʿlab** (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:231; *Subul al-Hudā*, 5:45).

When Khirāsh entered Makkah, Quraysh flagrantly violated ancient Arabian diplomatic immunity:
1. They hamstrung and slaughtered the Prophet's camel *al-Thaʿlab* (*ʿaqarū jamalahu*) (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:231; *Subul al-Hudā*, 5:45).
2. They drew weapons and surrounded Khirāsh intending to execute him (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:231; *Subul al-Hudā*, 5:45).

At this critical juncture, the tribal leadership of **al-Aḥābīsh** (the allied confederates of Quraysh) intervened forcefully, shielding Khirāsh from harm, rebuking Quraysh for violating diplomatic sanctuary, and safely escorting him out of Makkah (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:231; *Subul al-Hudā*, 5:45). 

Following Khirāsh's narrow escape and return to the Muslim camp, the Prophet ﷺ recognized that any future envoy must have powerful clan protection inside Makkah, leading directly to the dispatch of **[[ʿUthmān b. ʿAffān]]** ﵁ under the aegis of Banū Umayyah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:46).

### Shaving of the Prophet ﷺ
Following the signing of the treaty and the sacrifice of the camels, Khirāsh b. Umayyah had the exalted privilege of serving as the barber who shaved the blessed head of the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ upon his desacralization at al-Ḥudaybiyah (and similarly at the climax of *ʿUmrat al-Qaḍāʾ* at al-Marwah in 7 AH) (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:231; *Subul al-Hudā*, 5:58).

### Subsequent Campaigns
Ibn ʿAbd al-Barr and Ibn Ḥajar record that Khirāsh participated in **[[Ghazwat Khaybar]]** (7 AH) and all major campaigns thereafter alongside the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:231).
