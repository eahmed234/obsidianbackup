# al-Dhahabī (محمد بن أحمد الذهبي)
*Shams al-Dīn al-Dhahabī — Biographer and Appraiser of the Generations*

## 1. Identity
**Muḥammad b. Aḥmad b. ʿUthmān al-Dhahabī** (673–748 AH) of Damascus, the great biographer and
ḥadīth appraiser of the Mamlūk period, teacher of **[[Ibn Kathīr]]** and a decisive influence on
**[[Ibn Ḥajar al-ʿAsqalānī]]**.

## 2. Relevance to the Sīrah
Two works bear on this vault:
- ***Siyar Aʿlām al-Nubalāʾ***, his biographical encyclopedia, which supplies the appraisals and
  death-dates behind the Companion entries here — cited in **[[ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. ʿAwf]]** and
  **[[al-Miqdād b. ʿAmr]]**.
- ***Tārīkh al-Islām***, his chronological history, whose early volumes cover the Sīrah period.

His characteristic contribution is *evaluative* rather than narrative: where earlier compilers
transmit, al-Dhahabī weighs — grading a transmitter, dating a death, adjudicating between competing
reports. That is the function he serves in the citation apparatus of this vault, alongside
[[Ibn Ḥajar al-ʿAsqalānī]].
