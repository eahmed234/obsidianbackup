# Sūrat Yā-Sīn (سورة يس)
*The Heart of the Qurʾān — The Celestial Shield on the Night of the Hijrah*

## 1. Overview and Significance
**Sūrat Yā-Sīn** is the thirty-sixth chapter of the Qurʾān, revealed during the Makkan era to affirm the divine truth of the Messengership of Muḥammad ﷺ, establish the certainty of bodily resurrection (*al-Baʿth*), and celebrate the signs of Allāh across the cosmos (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:232–234).

In the prophetic chronicles, its opening passage holds an iconic role as the miraculous divine barrier recited by the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ to blind the sensory perception of the polytheist assassins besieging his dwelling on the historic Night of the Hijrah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:232).

## 2. Occasion of Revelation: The Siege of Dār al-Nadwah
When the leaders of Quraysh gathered in **Dār al-Nadwah** alongside Iblīs (who appeared in the guise of an elder of Najd) to finalize their conspiracy against the Prophet ﷺ, **[[Abū Jahl (ʿAmr b. Hishām)]]** proposed selecting a strong, noble youth from every tribe to strike the Prophet ﷺ simultaneously with their swords so that his blood would be dispersed among all clans (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:232).

Jibrīl ﵊ descended to the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ and warned him: *«Do not sleep this night upon the bed on which you customarily sleep,»* informing him of the plot and conveying Allāh's permission to make the Hijrah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:232).

The Prophet ﷺ directed **[[ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib]]** ﵁ to wrap himself in his green Ḥaḍramī mantle and sleep upon his couch, assuring him that no harm would reach him from them (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:232).

## 3. The Miraculous Recitation & Scattering of Dust
As the armed youths encircled his door in the early night awaiting his emergence, Abū Jahl mocked the Prophetic promise of eternal Paradise and warnings of Hellfire (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:232).

The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ stepped out directly into their midst, took a handful of dust in his noble hand, and proclaimed:
> «نَعَمْ، أَنَا أَقُولُ ذَلِكَ، وَأَنْتَ أَحَدُهُمْ!»
> *"Yes, I say that, and you are one of them!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:232).

Allāh seized their vision so they could not see him. The Prophet ﷺ began scattering the dust upon the heads of each conspirator while reciting the opening verses of **Sūrat Yā-Sīn**:
> «يس ۝ وَالْقُرْآنِ الْحَكِيمِ ۝ إِنَّكَ لَمِنَ الْمُرْسَلِينَ ۝ عَلَىٰ صِرَاطٍ مُسْتَقِيمٍ ۝ تَنْزِيلَ الْعَزِيزِ الرَّحِيمِ ۝ لِتُنْذِرَ قَوْمًا مَا أُنْذِرَ آبَاؤُهُمْ فَهُمْ غَافِلُونَ ۝ لَقَدْ حَقَّ الْقَوْلُ عَلَىٰ أَكْثَرِهِمْ فَهُمْ لَا يُؤْمِنُونَ ۝ إِنَّا جَعَلْنَا فِي أَعْنَاقِهِمْ أَغْلَالًا فَهِيَ إِلَى الْأَذْقَانِ فَهُمْ مُقْمَحُونَ ۝ وَجَعَلْنَا مِنْ بَيْنِ أَيْدِيهِمْ سَدًّا وَمِنْ خَلْفِهِمْ سَدًّا فَأَغْشَيْنَاهُمْ فَهُمْ لَا يُبْصِرُونَ»
> *"Yā-Sīn. By the wise Qurʾān. Indeed you are of the messengers, on a straight path. A revelation of the Exalted in Might, the Merciful, that you may warn a people whose forefathers were not warned, so they are unaware. Already the word has come into effect upon most of them, so they do not believe. Indeed, We have put shackles on their necks, and they are up to their chins, so their heads are forced upward. And We have put before them a barrier and behind them a barrier and covered them, so they do not see."* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:232).

Not a single man among the besiegers remained except that the Prophet ﷺ placed dust upon his head, before walking past them unhindered to begin his journey with **[[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]]** ﵁ toward Cave Thawr (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:232–233).

## 4. Aftermath and Scholarly Rulings
When a traveler passed by the house and asked the bewildered sentries whom they were awaiting, they replied: *"Muḥammad!"* He retorted: *"May Allāh confound you! Muḥammad has already departed and left not a single man among you without placing dust upon his head!"* When they felt their heads, each discovered the dust upon his crown (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:233).

Classical jurists and scholars of Sīrah note:
- The spiritual protection of reciting Sūrat Yā-Sīn in moments of peril: As Imām al-Ṣāliḥī records, the Prophetic recitation provides a established Sunnah and shield for the fearful (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:234).
- The symbolic significance of placing dust upon their heads: An omen and physical sign that these proud pagan chieftains were humbled and destined for destruction, as later fulfilled at Badr (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:234).
