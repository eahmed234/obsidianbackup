# Ḥudhayfah b. al-Yamān (حذيفة بن اليمان) ﵁
*Ṣāḥib Sirr Rasūlillāh ﷺ (Keeper of the Prophet's Secret) — Hero of the Trench Reconnaissance, Sentinel of Tabūk, and Governor of al-Madāʾin*

> [!NOTE]
> **Canonical Sourcing**: This biographical profile is established upon *al-Iṣābah fī Tamyīz al-Ṣaḥābah* by al-Ḥāfiẓ Ibn Ḥajar al-ʿAsqalānī (vol. 2, pp. 39–40, entry 1652; vol. 2, p. 66, ) and *[[Subul al-Hudā war-Rashād]]* by Imām Muḥammad b. Yūsuf al-Ṣāliḥī (vol. 4, pp. 387–390; vol. 5, pp. 466–468).

---

## 1. Lineage, Confederacy & Dual Affiliation
- **Full Lineage**: Ḥudhayfah b. Ḥusīl (or Ḥasl, known as al-Yamān) b. Jābir b. Rabīʿah b. Farwah b. al-Ḥārith b. Māzin b. Qaṭīʿah b. ʿAbs al-ʿAbsī (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:39, no. 1652; 2:66, ).
- **Paternal Confederacy (*Ḥilf*)**: His father Ḥusīl committed a blood transgression (*dam*) among his people in Najd and sought refuge in Medina, entering into a formal pact of confederacy (*ḥilf*) with the clan of Banū ʿAbd al-Ashhal of the Aws tribe (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:39). Because Banū ʿAbd al-Ashhal belonged to the southern Yemenite lineage of Aws, the northern kinsmen of Ḥusīl nicknamed him *al-Yamān* (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:39).
- **Maternal Roots**: His mother was from the Anṣār, and Ḥudhayfah was born and raised in Medina (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:39).
- **The Choice Between Hijrah and Nuṣrah**: When Ḥudhayfah accepted Islam, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ offered him the choice of status:
  > «خَيَّرَنِي رَسُولُ اللهِ ﷺ بَيْنَ الْهِجْرَةِ وَالنُّصْرَةِ، فَاخْتَرْتُ النُّصْرَةَ»
  > *"The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ gave me the choice between the status of the Muhājirūn and that of the Anṣār, so I chose the Nuṣrah (to be numbered among the Anṣār)."* (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:39).

## 2. Early Sīrah Milestones: Badr & Uḥud
- **The Covenant Before Badr (2 AH)**: Prior to **[[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]]**, Ḥudhayfah and his father **[[Ḥusīl b. Jābir (al-Yamān)]]** set out from Medina and were captured by Quraysh pagan riders. The polytheists interrogated them: *«You intend to join Muḥammad!»* They answered that they only desired to go to Medina, giving an explicit oath not to fight alongside the Prophet ﷺ at Badr (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:39, 66; *Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim*). When they informed the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ, he strictly commanded them:
  > «نَسْتَعِينُ اللهَ عَلَيْهِمْ، وَنَفِي لَهُمْ بِعَهْدِهِمْ»
  > *"We seek Allāh's aid against them, and we fulfill our covenant to them."*
  Because of this solemn covenant, both Ḥudhayfah and his father were held back from fighting at Badr (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:39, 66).
- **The Tragedy of Uḥud (3 AH)**: At **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]**, Ḥudhayfah fought in the front ranks. His elderly father Ḥusīl and Thābit b. Waqsh took their swords from the fortresses to join the engagement. In the confusion following the flank retreat, Muslim swords fell mistakenly upon his aged father. Ḥudhayfah cried out desperately: *«O servants of Allāh! My father! My father!»* (*Abī! Abī!*), but he was slain before they recognized him (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:39; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:211). Ḥudhayfah exhibited sublime forbearance and pardon, declaring:
  > «يَغْفِرُ اللهُ لَكُمْ وَهُوَ أَرْحَمُ الرَّاحِمِينَ»
  > *"May Allāh forgive you, and He is the Most Merciful of the merciful."*
  When the Prophet ﷺ ordered full blood-money (*diyah*) paid from the public treasury, Ḥudhayfah waived it and gave it as charity (*ṣadaqah*) to the Muslim community (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:39, 66).

## 3. The Reconnaissance on the Bitter Night of the Trench (5 AH)
During **[[Ghazwat al-Khandaq]]**, the Confederate siege pushed the Muslims to immense distress under freezing cold, biting winds, and pitch-black darkness (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:387–388).
- **The Prophetic Selection**: The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ stood among approximately three hundred Companions and called out three times: *«Is there not a man who will bring me news of the enemy, and be with me on the Day of Resurrection?»* Due to extreme exhaustion, hunger, and cold, no one rose (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:387).
- The Prophet ﷺ approached Ḥudhayfah, who was curled upon the earth wrapped only in a small woolen cloak belonging to his wife that barely reached his knees. The Prophet ﷺ called: *«Ḥudhayfah!»* Ḥudhayfah responded: *«At your service, O Messenger of Allāh»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:388).
- **The Divine Armor & Warmth**: The Prophet ﷺ said: *«Go into the midst of the enemy and bring me their news, but do not provoke anything against them until you return to me.»* He supplicated for him:
  > «اللَّهُمَّ احْفَظْهُ مِنْ بَيْنِ يَدَيْهِ وَمِنْ خَلْفِهِ، وَعَنْ يَمِينِهِ وَعَنْ شِمَالِهِ، وَمِنْ فَوْقِهِ وَمِنْ تَحْتِهِ»
  > *"O Allāh, protect him from before him and from behind him, on his right and on his left, and from above him and beneath him."* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:388).
  Ḥudhayfah related that all cold and fear instantly vanished from his body, and he felt as though he was walking through a warm bath (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:388).
- **Infiltrating the Camp of Abū Sufyān**:
  - Ḥudhayfah penetrated deep into the confederate camp and sat beside the fire warming the enemy chieftain **[[Abū Sufyān b. Ḥarb]]**.
  - Ḥudhayfah drew an arrow and placed it in the notch of his bow, intending to slay Abū Sufyān while he was warming his hands over the fire. But he remembered the strict injunction of the Prophet ﷺ: *«Do not provoke anything until you return to me»*, and he returned the arrow to his quiver (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:388).
  - Suspecting an infiltrator in the gloom, Abū Sufyān commanded: *«Let every man take the hand of his seatmate and ask him who he is!»* Ḥudhayfah acted with lightning cunning: he immediately seized the hand of the man to his right and demanded: *«Who are you?»* The man replied: *«Muʿāwiyah b. Abī Sufyān.»* He then grabbed the hand of the man to his left and asked: *«Who are you?»* The man replied: *«ʿAmr b. al-ʿĀṣ»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:388).
  - Ḥudhayfah sowed psychological discord among Banū Kinānah and Qays according to the Prophet's instructions, observed the raging howling wind overturning the polytheists' tents and caldrons, and witnessed their disordered retreat before slipping back safely to Medina (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:388–389).

## 4. Keeper of the Prophet's Secrets (*Ṣāḥib al-Sirr*)
Ḥudhayfah was uniquely endowed with the sole custody of the names of the hypocrites (*al-munāfiqūn*) and the portents of future tribulations (*al-fitan*) (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:39–40).
- **The Conspiracy of the Mountain Pass (Tabūk, 9 AH)**:
  - On the return march from **[[Ghazwat Tabūk]]**, a band of twelve masked conspirators plotted to assassinate the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ by stampeding his she-camel over the cliff edge of a narrow mountain pass (*al-ʿAqabah*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:466–468).
  - While **[[ʿAmmār b. Yāsir]]** ﵁ led the Prophet's mount by the halter, Ḥudhayfah drove it from behind. When the plotters approached in the dark, the Prophet ﷺ ordered Ḥudhayfah to drive them off. Ḥudhayfah charged at them with a curved staff (*miḥjan*), striking the faces of their mounts until they fled down the ravine into the mass of the army (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:466).
  - The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ revealed to Ḥudhayfah the explicit names of those conspirators and forbade him from disclosing them, swearing him to secrecy (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:466–468).
- **The Benchmark of ʿUmar**:
  - In the era of the caliphate, **[[ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb]]** ﵁ would never pray the funeral prayer (*janāzah*) over any deceased individual unless Ḥudhayfah attended the prayer (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:40).
  - Whenever someone died whose character was doubtful, ʿUmar looked to see if Ḥudhayfah prayed; if Ḥudhayfah joined, ʿUmar prayed, and if Ḥudhayfah refrained, ʿUmar refrained (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:40).
  - ʿUmar once asked him in private anxiety: *«I adjure you by Allāh, did the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ name me among the hypocrites?»* Ḥudhayfah answered: *«No, and I will not absolve anyone after you»* (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:40).
- **Comprehensive Knowledge of Tribulations**:
  - Ḥudhayfah transmitted:
    > «لَقَدْ حَدَّثَنِي رَسُولُ اللهِ ﷺ مَا كَانَ وَمَا يَكُونُ حَتَّى تَقُومَ السَّاعَةُ»
    > *"The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ narrated to me everything that had occurred and everything that shall occur until the Day of Resurrection."* (*Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim*; *al-Iṣābah*, 2:40).
  - In *al-Ṣaḥīḥayn*, Abū al-Dardāʾ ﵁ remarked to ʿAlqamah in Iraq: *«Is there not among you the Keeper of the Secret whom none knows but he?»* referring to Ḥudhayfah (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:40).

## 5. Later Role, Governance & Passing (36 AH)
- **Campaigns in Iraq & Persia**: Participated extensively in the Islamic conquests of Iraq and Western Persia, displaying exemplary command at Nihāwand and the conquest of Dinawar and Hamadhān (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:40).
- **Governance of al-Madāʾin**: Caliph ʿUmar appointed Ḥudhayfah governor over the historic Sasanian capital of al-Madāʾin (Ctesiphon). He ruled with extreme humility, riding a donkey with a sack of grain and eating simple barley bread (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:39).
- **Passing**: He remained resident in al-Madāʾin until his death in the year 36 AH, forty days after the murder of Caliph ʿUthmān b. ʿAffān and the accession of ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:39).
