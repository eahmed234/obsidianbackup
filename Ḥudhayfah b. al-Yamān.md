# Ḥudhayfah b. al-Yamān (حذيفة بن اليمان) ﵁
*Abū ʿAbdillāh al-ʿAbsī — Ṣāḥib Sirr Rasūlillāh (Keeper of the Prophet's Secrets), Reconnaissance Hero of Khandaq, Catalyst of the ʿUthmānic Qurʾānic Standardization*

## 1. Lineage, Ancestry & Precedence
### Patrilineal Descent & Confederacies
He is **Ḥudhayfah b. Ḥusl (known as al-Yamān) b. Jābir b. ʿAmr b. Rabīʿah b. Jarwah b. al-Ḥārith b. Qatīʿah b. ʿAbs b. Baghīḍ al-ʿAbsī** (kunya: **Abū ʿAbdillāh**) (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:39). 

- **Father**: **Ḥusl b. Jābir (al-Yamān)**, of Banū ʿAbs, who had slain someone in Jāhiliyyah and fled to Medina, forming a confederacy (*ḥilf*) with Banū ʿAbd al-Ashhal of the Aws and marrying into them (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:39).
- **Mother**: An Anṣārī woman of Banū ʿAbd al-Ashhal.
- **Muhājir or Anṣārī?**: When Ḥudhayfah embraced Islam, he asked the Prophet ﷺ: *“Am I a Muhājir or an Anṣārī?”* The Prophet ﷺ replied: *«If you wish, you are among the Muhājirūn, and if you wish, you are among the Anṣār; choose whichever you desire!»* Ḥudhayfah replied: *“Rather, I am an Anṣārī!”* (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:39).

## 2. Key Sīrah Milestones
### The Martyrdom of His Father at Uḥud (3 AH)
At **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]**, Ḥudhayfah's elderly father al-Yamān and Thābit b. Waqsh took up swords to defend the rear lines. In the swirling chaos of the melee, Muslim soldiers mistook al-Yamān for an enemy. Ḥudhayfah saw them and cried out desperately: *“My father! My father!”* but before they understood, he was struck down (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:209). 

Seeing the tragedy, Ḥudhayfah displayed sublime Islamic forbearance, saying:
> «يَغْفِرُ اللَّهُ لَكُمْ، وَهُوَ أَرْحَمُ الرَّاحِمِينَ!»
> *"May Allāh forgive you, and He is the Most Merciful of those who show mercy!"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:39; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:209).
When the Prophet ﷺ ordered that blood-money (*diyah*) be paid to Ḥudhayfah from the treasury, Ḥudhayfah refused it, giving it as voluntary charity to the believers—an act that elevated his esteem enormously in the eyes of the Prophet ﷺ (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:39).

### The Infiltration Mission at Khandaq (5 AH)
During the freezing, tempestuous night of **[[Ghazwat al-Khandaq]]**, when confederate siege lines encircled Medina, the Prophet ﷺ sought a scout:
> *“Who will go and bring me news of the enemy, and he shall be my companion in Paradise?”*
Due to intense cold and hunger, none moved until the Prophet ﷺ named Ḥudhayfah directly: *«Stand, O Ḥudhayfah, and bring us news of the folk, and do not provoke them against me!»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:381–383).

Ḥudhayfah slipped across the trench through pitch darkness, infiltrating the enemy camp where he sat beside **[[Abū Sufyān b. Ḥarb]]** at his campfire as fierce winds overturned their kettles. Having an arrow nocked, Ḥudhayfah could have assassinated Abū Sufyān, but remembered the Prophet's instruction: *«Do not provoke them against me!»* He returned safely to the Prophet ﷺ, who wrapped Ḥudhayfah in his blessed cloak to warm him (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:383).

## 3. Keeper of the Prophet's Secrets (Ṣāḥib al-Sirr)
The Prophet ﷺ entrusted Ḥudhayfah with exclusive knowledge possessed by no other living human:
- The exact personal names of the hypocrites (*al-munāfiqūn*) who conspired against Islam, including those who attempted to assassinate the Prophet ﷺ at the ravine of ʿAqabah upon the return from Tabūk (*Subul al-Hudā*, 10:132).
- Detailed knowledge of future tribulations (*al-fitan*) and historical upheavals until the Day of Resurrection (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:40).

Amīr al-Muʾminīn **[[ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb]]** ﵁ held Ḥudhayfah in immense awe: whenever a prominent figure died in Medina, ʿUmar looked to see if Ḥudhayfah attended the funeral prayer; if Ḥudhayfah prayed, ʿUmar prayed, and if Ḥudhayfah abstained, ʿUmar abstained (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:40). ʿUmar once pulled Ḥudhayfah aside, asking with trembling humility: *“I adjure you by Allāh, did the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ name me among the hypocrites?!”* Ḥudhayfah replied: *“No! And I shall not exonerate anyone after you!”* (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:40).

---

## 4. Conquests & The Standardization of the Holy Qurʾān
- **Conquests of Persia**: Ḥudhayfah served as supreme commander following the martyrdom of al-Nuʿmān b. Muqarrin at the epic Battle of Nahāwand (21 AH), leading the Muslim armies to total victory and opening Hamadhān, al-Rayy, and Dīnawar (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:40).
- **Governor of al-Madāʾin**: Appointed governor of al-Madāʾin by ʿUmar, he lived with extreme asceticism, arriving in the capital riding an unadorned donkey with coarse provisions.
- **Catalyst of the ʿUthmānic Codex (25 AH)**: During the campaigns in Armenia and Azerbaijan, Ḥudhayfah witnessed severe disputes over Qurʾānic readings among troops from Iraq and Syria. Horrified, he hurried directly to Caliph **[[ʿUthmān b. ʿAffān]]** ﵁, crying: *“O Amīr al-Muʾminīn! Save this Ummah before they differ over the Scripture as the Jews and Christians differed!”* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:285). This historic intervention directly catalyzed the unified standardisation of **[[Jamʿ al-Qurʾān]]**.

---

## 5. Death
Ḥudhayfah passed away in al-Madāʾin in the year 36 AH, only forty days after receiving news of the martyrdom of Caliph **[[ʿUthmān b. ʿAffān]]** ﵁ (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:40). He was buried in al-Madāʾin. Over one hundred authentic traditions are transmitted from him.
