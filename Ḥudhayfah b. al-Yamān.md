# Ḥudhayfah b. al-Yamān (حذيفة بن اليمان العبسي)
*Ṣāḥib Sirr Rasūlillāh ﷺ (The Keeper of the Prophet's Secret) & Valiant Infiltrator of the Trench*

> [!NOTE] Primary Sourcing & Biographical Provenance
> Sourced directly from *al-Iṣābah fī Tamyīz al-Ṣaḥābah*, 2:39–40 (entry 1652), and *Subul al-Hudā war-Rashād*, 4:387–389, 5:466–467.

---

## 1. Lineage, Tribal Confederation & Status

Ḥudhayfah was an illustrious Companion of northern Arabian origin who grew up in Medina as an ally of the Anṣār (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:39).

- **Full Paternal Lineage**: Ḥudhayfah b. Ḥusīl (known as al-Yamān) b. Jābir b. Rabīʿah b. Farwah b. al-Ḥārith b. Māzin b. Qaṭīʿah b. ʿAbs al-ʿAbsī (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:39, 66).
- **Origin of the Epithet 'al-Yamān'**: His father Ḥusīl had shed blood in pre-Islamic times and fled to Yathrib, entering into a confederacy (*ḥilf*) with Banū ʿAbd al-Ashhal of the Aws; because he allied with the southern Yemenite stock (the Anṣār), his northern kin termed him *al-Yamān* (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:39).
- **The Choice between Hijrah and Nuṣrah**: The Prophet ﷺ gave Ḥudhayfah the choice between being reckoned among the Muhājirūn or the Anṣār; Ḥudhayfah chose the fellowship of the Anṣār (*al-Nuṣrah*) (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:39).

## 2. Early Sīrah Milestones: From Badr to Uḥud

### The Pledge before Badr
Ḥudhayfah and his father set out to join the Prophet ﷺ at **[[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]]**, but were intercepted by the polytheists of Quraysh. The Qurashīs extracted an oath that they would proceed solely to Medina and not fight against them. Upon reaching the Prophet ﷺ and relating what had occurred, the Prophet ﷺ commanded them:
> «انْصَرِفَا، نَفِي لَهُمْ بِعَهْدِهِمْ، وَنَسْتَعِينُ اللَّهَ عَلَيْهِمْ»
> *"Depart! We shall fulfill their covenant to them, and we seek the assistance of Allāh against them!"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:39, 66).

### Martyrdom of His Father at Uḥud (3 AH)
Both Ḥudhayfah and his father participated in **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]**. Amidst the turmoil of the battlefield, Muslim warriors mistakenly fell upon the aged Ḥusīl (*al-Yamān*), failing to recognize him. Ḥudhayfah cried out desperately: *"O servants of Allāh, my father! My father!"* but he was slain before they ceased (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:39, 66). 
- Demonstrating supreme faith, Ḥudhayfah said: *«May Allāh forgive you, and He is the Most Merciful of those who show mercy»* (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:66).
- When the Prophet ﷺ sought to pay the blood-money (*diyah*) from his own funds, Ḥudhayfah gave it away as charity to the Muslims (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:66).

## 3. Night Infiltration of the Enemy Camp at the Trench (5 AH)

During the critical climax of **[[Ghazwat al-Khandaq]]**, when the confederate army was besieged by biting freezing wind and terror, the Prophet ﷺ sought a volunteer to infiltrate the enemy lines (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:388).

- **The Call in the Piercing Cold**: In the pitch-black freezing night, the Prophet ﷺ approached Ḥudhayfah and commanded:
  > «قُمْ يَا حُذَيْفَةُ، فَأْتِنِي بِخَبَرِ الْقَوْمِ، وَلَا تَذْعَرْهُمْ عَلَيَّ»
  > *"Rise, O Ḥudhayfah! Bring me news of the enemy, and do not alarm them against me!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:388).
- The Prophet ﷺ prayed for him: *«There shall be no harm upon you from cold or heat until you return to me»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:388).
- **Within Abu Sufyan's Lines**: Ḥudhayfah crept into the confederate camp and witnessed [[Abū Sufyān b. Ḥarb]] warming himself at a fire. Ḥudhayfah drew an arrow and was about to shoot him, but remembered the Prophet's command not to alarm the enemy. He observed the devastating howling wind of divine retribution overturning the enemy cauldrons and tearing down their tents, returning safely to deliver the decisive intelligence of the confederates' flight (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:388–389).

## 4. Custodian of the Secrets (*Ṣāḥib al-Sirr*)

During the return from **[[Ghazwat Tabūk]]** in nine AH, a band of hypocrites attempted an assassination of the Prophet ﷺ along the mountain pass of al-ʿAqabah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:466).
- The Prophet ﷺ disclosed the identities of the hypocrites privately to Ḥudhayfah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:466–467; *al-Iṣābah*, 2:40).
- Because of this unique exclusive trust, Abū al-Dardāʾ proclaimed:
  > «أَلَيْسَ فِيكُمْ صَاحِبُ السِّرِّ الَّذِي لَا يَعْلَمُهُ غَيْرُهُ؟»
  > *"Is there not among you the keeper of the secret which none other knows?"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:40).
- Caliph **[[ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb]]** ﵁ would never participate in a funeral prayer unless Ḥudhayfah was present and prayed over the deceased, consulting him on the inner conditions of men (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:40).

---

## 5. Later Life & Passing

- **Governance**: ʿUmar appointed Ḥudhayfah as governor over al-Madāʾin in Iraq, where he remained renowned for his absolute asceticism and justice (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:39).
- **Passing**: Ḥudhayfah passed away in al-Madāʾin in the year thirty-six AH, forty days after the murder of ʿUthmān and the pledge to ʿAlī (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:39).
