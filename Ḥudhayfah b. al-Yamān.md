# Ḥudhayfah b. al-Yamān (حذيفة بن اليمان)
*Ṣāḥib Sirr Rasūlillāh ﷺ — The Keeper of the Prophet's Secrets, Scout of the Trench, and Governor of al-Madāʾin*

## 1. Lineage and Family

Ḥudhayfah b. Ḥusayl (or Ḥasl) b. Jābir b. Rabīʿah b. Farwah b. al-Ḥārith b. Māzin b. Qaṭīʿah b. ʿAbs al-ʿAbsī (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:39, 66). His father, Ḥusayl, had slain a man among his people in pre-Islamic times and fled to [[al-Madīnah al-Munawwarah|Medina]], entering into a mutual alliance (*ḥilf*) with Banū ʿAbd al-Ashhal of the Aws; because the Aws were of Yemeni extraction, his people nicknamed him *al-Yamān* (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:39). 

His mother was al-Rabāb bint Kaʿb b. ʿAdī b. ʿAbd al-Ashhal al-Anṣāriyyah, an early convert who pledged allegiance to the Messenger of Allah ﷺ (*al-Iṣābah*, 8:131). Ḥudhayfah was born in Medina as an ally of the Anṣār, and when he embraced Islam, the Prophet ﷺ gave him the choice between identifying as a Muhājir or an Anṣārī, and Ḥudhayfah chose the Anṣār (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:39).

## 2. Missed Badr and Trial at Uḥud

Ḥudhayfah and his father al-Yamān set out intending to join the Prophet ﷺ at [[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā|Badr]], but they were intercepted by the polytheists of [[Quraysh]], who demanded their solemn covenant not to fight alongside Muḥammad ﷺ. When they reached the Messenger of Allah ﷺ and informed him, he commanded them to honor their covenant, saying:
> «انصرفا، نفي لهم بعهدهم، ونستعين الله عليهم»
> *"Depart; we fulfill their covenant to them, and we seek Allah's assistance against them."* (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:39, 66).

Both Ḥudhayfah and his father took part in [[Ghazwat Uḥud]]. During the chaos following the turning of the battle, the Muslim ranks became intermingled and confused. A group of Muslims surrounded al-Yamān, mistaking him for one of the idolaters. Ḥudhayfah cried out desperately:
> «أي عباد الله، أبي! أبي!»
> *"O servants of Allah! My father! My father!"*

They struck him down before realizing his identity. Rather than expressing bitterness, Ḥudhayfah immediately pardoned them, saying:
> «يغفر الله لكم وهو أرحم الراحمين»
> *"May Allah forgive you, and He is the Most Merciful of the merciful!"*

When the Prophet ﷺ offered blood money (*diyah*) for his father from his own means, Ḥudhayfah waived it and donated it as charity to the Muslims (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:39, 66).

## 3. The Reconnaissance at the Trench (5 AH)

During [[Ghazwat al-Khandaq]], on a night of piercing cold, howling wind, and pervasive fear, the Messenger of Allah ﷺ called upon Ḥudhayfah to infiltrate the camp of the confederate army and bring back intelligence (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:387–388).

The Prophet ﷺ supplicated for him:
> «لا بأس عليك من حر ولا برد حتى ترجع إلي»
> *"No harm from heat or cold shall touch you until you return to me."*

Ḥudhayfah slipped undetected into the enemy lines, witnessing Abū Sufyān warming himself and ordering the withdrawal. Ḥudhayfah could have assassinated Abū Sufyān with an arrow, but refrained in strict obedience to the Prophet's command not to provoke anything until his return (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:388). He returned safely and conveyed the decisive intelligence that the confederates were breaking camp (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:39; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:388).

## 4. The Keeper of the Secret (*Ṣāḥib al-Sirr*)

Following the expedition of Tabūk, when twelve hypocrites conspired to assassinate the Prophet ﷺ along the mountain pass of al-ʿAqabah, Allah protected His Messenger and revealed their specific names to him (*Subul al-Hudā*, 10:262–263). The Prophet ﷺ disclosed this confidential register of hypocrites exclusively to Ḥudhayfah, who alone held the identity of the hypocrites (*Ṣāḥib Sirr Rasūlillāh ﷺ*).

Because of this unique trust:
- [[Abū al-Dardāʾ]] ﵁ asked ʿAlqamah: *"Is not the Keeper of the Secret among you, whom none else knows?"* referring to Ḥudhayfah (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:40).
- [[ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb]] ﵁ would monitor Ḥudhayfah whenever a prominent figure died; if Ḥudhayfah refrained from attending the funeral prayer, ʿUmar would also refrain (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:40).
- ʿUmar repeatedly questioned Ḥudhayfah in private: *"Did the Messenger of Allah ﷺ mention my name among them?"* and Ḥudhayfah replied: *"No, and I shall not exonerate anyone after you."*

## 5. Standardizing the Qurʾānic Text

During the campaigns in Armenia and Azerbaijan, Ḥudhayfah observed alarming disputes regarding variant recitations of the Qurʾān among soldiers from Syria and Iraq, where some accused others of disbelief (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:286). 

Deeply alarmed, Ḥudhayfah hurried directly to Medina and urged Caliph [[ʿUthmān b. ʿAffān]] ﵁:
> «يا أمير المؤمنين، أدرك هذه الأمة قبل أن تختلف كاختلاف اليهود والنصارى»
> *"O Commander of the Faithful! Rescue this Ummah before they differ concerning the Scripture as the Jews and Christians differed!"*

This warning was the direct catalyst for ʿUthmān ordering the compilation and distribution of the standard *Muṣḥaf al-Imām* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:286).

## 6. Governance and Passing

Ḥudhayfah participated in the major conquests of Iraq and was appointed governor of al-Madāʾin by [[ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb]] ﵁, a post he maintained with legendary asceticism and justice (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:39). 

He passed away in al-Madāʾin in 36 AH, forty days after the assassination of [[ʿUthmān b. ʿAffān]] ﵁ and the pledge of allegiance to [[ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib]] ﵁ (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:39).
