# al-Nahdiyyah (النهدية) and Her Daughter
*Freed by Abū Bakr After Refusing to Grind Grain for Her Mistress*

## 1. Identity
Al-Ṣāliḥī identifies her as a freedwoman (*muwalladah*) originally of **Banū Nahd b. Zayd**, who came
into the possession of a woman of **Banū ʿAbd al-Dār** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:361). She is named among the
**seven** whom Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq ﵁ purchased and freed from torture, alongside her own **unnamed
daughter**, **[[Bilāl b. Rabāḥ]]**, **[[ʿĀmir b. Fuhayrah]]**, **[[Zunnīrah al-Rūmiyyah|Zunnīrah]]**,
**[[Lubaybah]]**, and **[[Umm ʿUnays]]** (*al-Iṣābah*, 8:434, no. 12163).

> [!NOTE] A Possible Further Notice
> Al-Iṣābah separately records an **Umm ʿAfīf al-Nahdiyyah** who took the Prophet's ﷺ pledge among the
> women, on condition they not converse privately with men outside the bounds of maḥram relation, and
> that they recite the Opening of the Book (al-Fātiḥah) over their dead (*al-Iṣābah*, 8:438, no.
> 12174). Whether this is the same woman as the mawlāt of Banū Nahd tortured in Makkah is not stated by
> either source, and is left here unresolved rather than assumed.

## 2. Her Mistress's Cruelty
Her mistress used to torment both mother and daughter, telling them, *"By Allāh, I will not relent
toward you both until whoever has apostatized on your account frees you."* **Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq** ﵁
came upon them one day as their mistress had sent them out to grind flour for her, still declaring, *"By
Allāh, I will never free you."* Abū Bakr said, *"Enough, mother of so-and-so."* She retorted, *"You are
the one who has ruined them — you free them, then!"* He asked her price; she named it, and he bought
them on the spot — **already freeing them before the transaction was even settled**. The two women
asked whether they should first finish the grinding they had been sent to do and return it to their
former mistress; Abū Bakr told them, *"Do so, if you both wish"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:361).
