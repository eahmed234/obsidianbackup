# Salmān al-Fārisī (سلمان الفارسي)
*Abū ʿAbdillāh — The Seeker of Truth, From the Household of the Prophet ﷺ*

## 1. Journey to Truth
Born into Zoroastrian nobility in Persia, he fled to Syria and Iraq studying under Christian monks seeking the prophesied Final Messenger. Enslaved by an Arab caravan, he was sold into Medina. Upon identifying the three physical marks of prophethood, he embraced Islam (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:340).

## 2. The Trench Innovation
Architect of the trench at **[[Ghazwat al-Khandaq]]** (5 AH), which neutralized 10,000 confederate besiegers. When Muhājirūn and Anṣār disputed over him, the Prophet ﷺ honored him with the highest lineage:
> «سَلْمَانُ مِنَّا أَهْلَ الْبَيْتِ!»
> *"Salmān is of us, the Ahl al-Bayt!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:372).
