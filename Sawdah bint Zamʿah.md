# Sawdah bint Zamʿah (سودة بنت زمعة) ﵂
*Umm al-Muʾminīn (Mother of the Believers) — First to Wed the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ After Khadījah, The Lady of Generous Devotion and Noble Humility*

## 1. Lineage, Ancestry & Sacred Kinship
### Patrilineal Descent & Clan
She is **Sawdah bint Zamʿah b. Qays b. ʿAbd Shams b. ʿAbd Wudd b. Naṣr b. Mālik b. Ḥisl b. ʿĀmir b. Luʾayy al-Qurashiyyah al-ʿĀmiriyyah** (*al-Iṣābah*, 8:118; *Subul al-Hudā*, 11:198). 

- **Father**: **Zamʿah b. Qays**, nobleman of Banū ʿĀmir b. Luʾayy.
- **Mother**: **al-Shumūs bint Qays b. ʿAmr b. Zayd b. Labīd b. Khidāsh b. ʿĀmir b. Ghanm b. ʿAdī b. al-Najjār**, noblewoman of the maternal relatives of the Prophet's grandfather ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib in Yathrib (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:198).
- **Brother**: ʿAbdullāh b. Zamʿah ﵁.

### First Marriage & Migration to Abyssinia
Embracing Islam in the earliest days of the Makkan mission, Sawdah was married to her paternal cousin **al-Sakrān b. ʿAmr b. ʿAbd Shams al-ʿĀmirī** (brother of Suhayl b. ʿAmr) (*al-Iṣābah*, 8:118; *Subul al-Hudā*, 11:198). 

The couple endured severe persecution from Quraysh and embarked upon the second migration across the Red Sea to **[[Abyssinia]]** (**[[al-Hijrah ilā al-Ḥabashah]]**) to safeguard their religion (*al-Iṣābah*, 8:118). Returning later to Makkah, al-Sakrān fell ill and passed away, leaving Sawdah a solitary Muslim widow surrounded by polytheist relatives (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:198).

---

## 2. Marriage to the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ (10 BH / 620 CE)
Following the passing of **[[Khadījah bint Khuwaylid]]** ﵂ during the Year of Sorrow (*ʿĀm al-Ḥuzn*), the Prophet ﷺ was left alone with his young daughters in Makkah. 

**Khawlah bint Ḥakīm al-Sulamiyyah** ﵂ approached the Prophet ﷺ, asking: *“O Messenger of Allāh, will you not marry?”* When he asked who, she replied: *“If you desire a maiden, then ʿĀʾishah bint Abī Bakr; and if you desire a mature widow (*thayyib*), then Sawdah bint Zamʿah, who has believed in you and followed your path!”* (*al-Iṣābah*, 8:118; *Subul al-Hudā*, 11:166, 198).

The Prophet ﷺ dispatched Khawlah to propose to Sawdah on his behalf. Sawdah accepted with deep gratitude, and her father Zamʿah b. Qays contracted the marriage in Makkah for a dowry of four hundred dirhams (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:198–199).

For roughly three years in Makkah until the migration to Medina and the consummation of the Prophet's marriage to **[[ʿĀʾishah bint Abī Bakr]]** ﵂, Sawdah was the sole wife of the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ, rearing his young daughters **[[Fāṭimah al-Zahrāʾ]]** and Umm Kulthūm with consummate maternal tenderness and loyalty (*al-Iṣābah*, 8:118).

---

## 3. The Gift of Her Night & Qurʾānic Revelation
As Sawdah advanced in years, she feared that the Prophet ﷺ might divorce her out of concern that he could not fulfill her marital desires. Desiring solely to remain his wife in this world and be resurrected among his wives in the Hereafter, she approached the Prophet ﷺ and said:
> *“O Messenger of Allāh! I have reached old age and have no desire for intimacy; my only wish is to be resurrected among your wives on the Day of Judgment! I freely grant my day and night to ʿĀʾishah, seeking thereby the pleasure of Allāh and His Messenger!”* (*al-Iṣābah*, 8:118; *Subul al-Hudā*, 11:199).

The Prophet ﷺ accepted her noble sacrifice with deep affection and maintained her status as Umm al-Muʾminīn. Concerning this noble conciliation, Allāh Almighty revealed:
> «وَإِنِ امْرَأَةٌ خَافَتْ مِن بَعْلِهَا نُشُوزًا أَوْ إِعْرَاضًا فَلَا جُنَاحَ عَلَيْهِمَا أَن يُصْلِحَا بَيْنَهُمَا صُلْحًا ۚ وَالصُّلْحُ خَيْرٌ»
> *«And if a woman fears from her husband indifference or evasion, there is no sin upon them if they make terms of settlement between them; and settlement is best»* (al-Nisāʾ 4:128) (*al-Iṣābah*, 8:118; *Subul al-Hudā*, 11:199).

ʿĀʾishah ﵂ held Sawdah in supreme reverence, declaring:
> «مَا مِنْ امْرَأَةٍ أَحَبَّ إِلَيَّ أَنْ أَكُونَ فِي مِسْلَاخِهَا [أَيْ شَبَهِ هَدْيِهَا وَسِيرَتِهَا] مِنْ سَوْدَةَ بِنْتِ زَمْعَةَ!»
> *"There is no woman whose disposition and righteous path I would love to embody more than Sawdah bint Zamʿah!"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 8:118).

## 4. Unwavering Obedience & Demise
Sawdah was tall and stout, endowed with a generous heart and humor. During **[[Ḥajjat al-Wadāʿ]]** (10 AH), she requested permission from the Prophet ﷺ to depart Muzdalifah before the dawn crowd due to her heavy gait, which the Prophet ﷺ granted (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:200).

When the Prophet ﷺ instructed his wives during the Farewell Pilgrimage: *«This is your final journey, thereafter remain upon the mats of your homes»*, Sawdah and Zaynab bint Jaḥsh took his words with literal, absolute devotion, saying: *“By Allāh, no beast of burden shall ever carry us again!”*—never leaving their residences in Medina even for voluntary pilgrimage until their passing (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:200).

### Passing and Burial
Sawdah passed away in Medina toward the end of the Caliphate of **[[ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb]]** ﵁ (~23 AH), or according to other transmissions in 54 AH (*al-Iṣābah*, 8:119; *Subul al-Hudā*, 11:200). She was laid to rest in **[[Jannat al-Baqīʿ]]**. Five authentic traditions are transmitted from her from the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:200).
