# Sawdah bint Zamʿah (سودة بنت زمعة) ﵂
*Umm al-Muʾminīn — Mother of the Believers, First Spouse Following Khadījah, Altruist of the Sacred Household*

## 1. Lineage & Maternal Roots
She is **Sawdah bint Zamʿah b. Qays b. ʿAbd Shams b. ʿAbd Wudd b. Naṣr b. Mālik b. Ḥisl b. ʿĀmir b. Luʾayy al-Qurashiyyah al-ʿĀmiriyyah** (*al-Iṣābah*, 8:196, no. 11363; *Subul al-Hudā*, 11:144, 198). 

Her mother was **al-Shumūs bint Qays b. ʿAmr b. Zayd b. Labīd b. Khidāsh b. ʿĀmir b. Ghanam b. ʿAdī b. al-Najjār** of the Anṣār, niece of Salmā bint ʿAmr (the mother of ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib), thus creating an early maternal kinship link with the Prophet's ancestral house (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:198; *al-Iṣābah*, 8:196).

## 2. Early Islam, Abyssinian Migration & Marriage to the Prophet ﷺ
Sawdah embraced Islam at its very dawn in Makkah alongside her paternal cousin and first husband, **al-Sakrān b. ʿAmr b. ʿAbd Shams** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:198; *al-Iṣābah*, 8:196). Together they endured the Makkan boycott and persecution, taking part in the Second Migration to Abyssinia (**[[al-Hijrah ilā al-Ḥabashah]]**) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:198).

Following their return to Makkah, her husband passed away (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:198–199; *al-Iṣābah*, 8:196). After the passing of the Mother of the Believers **[[Khadījah bint Khuwaylid]]** ﵂, **Khawlah bint Ḥakīm** ﵂ suggested to the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ: *«Shall I not propose a marriage for you?»* The Prophet ﷺ consented, and Khawlah approached Sawdah and her elderly father Zamʿah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:166, 198–199; *al-Iṣābah*, 8:196). 

The marriage was concluded in Makkah in the tenth year of prophethood, and she was the very first woman the Prophet ﷺ wed after Khadījah (*al-Iṣābah*, 8:196; *Subul al-Hudā*, 11:198). She assumed the care of the Prophet's young household and daughters in Makkah during the most challenging days of the daʿwah.

## 3. High Character, Humor & Altruism
Sawdah was renowned for her generous disposition, cheerfulness, and towering generosity:
- **Affection of ʿĀʾishah**: The Mother of the Believers **[[ʿĀʾishah bint Abī Bakr]]** ﵂ held her in the highest esteem, stating:
  > «مَا مِنَ النَّاسِ أَحَدٌ أَحَبَّ إِلَيَّ أَنْ أَكُونَ فِي مِسْلَاخِهِ مِنْ سَوْدَةَ بِنْتِ زَمْعَةَ!»
  > *"There is no person on earth whom I would more love to be in her skin (emulate her exemplary manner) than Sawdah bint Zamʿah!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:199; *al-Iṣābah*, 8:196).
- **Gifting Her Day to ʿĀʾishah (Revelation of Qurʾān 4:128)**: In her advanced years, wishing solely to remain forever among the Prophet's wives in the Hereafter and to please the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ, Sawdah magnanimously gifted her allocated day to ʿĀʾishah, declaring: *«I have no longing for what women desire; I desire only that Allāh resurrect me on the Day of Judgment as your wife!»* Concerning this reconciliation of hearts, Allāh revealed Sūrat al-Nisāʾ: 128 (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:170, 199; *al-Iṣābah*, 8:196).
- **Early Departure at Muzdalifah**: During the Farewell Pilgrimage, because of her heavy, dignified stature (*imraʾatan thabiṭah*), the Prophet ﷺ granted her special permission to depart Muzdalifah before the dawn crowd (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:199–200; *al-Iṣābah*, 8:196).
- **Total Detachment from Wealth**: When Caliph **[[ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb]]** ﵁ dispatched to her a large sack of dirhams, she immediately distributed every coin to the poor before rising from her seat (*al-Iṣābah*, 8:197).

## 4. Strict Adherence to the Prophetic Injunction & Demise
During the Farewell Pilgrimage, the Prophet ﷺ advised his wives: *«This is your pilgrimage, after which adhere to the home carpets (*hadhihi ẓuhūr al-ḥuṣur*)»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:200). Out of devotion to that injunction, Sawdah resolved never to mount a riding beast again for voluntary travel, remaining devoted to prayer in Medina (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:200).

She passed away in Medina; it is recorded that she died in fifty-four AH during the caliphate of Muʿāwiyah, and it is also related that her passing occurred toward the close of the caliphate of ʿUmar (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:200; *al-Iṣābah*, 8:197).
