# Subul al-Hudā war-Rashād (سبل الهدى والرشاد في سيرة خير العباد)
*The Magnum Opus Encyclopedia of Prophetic Biography and Characteristics*

> [!NOTE] Primary Anchor of this Wiki
> This encyclopedia is the master backbone for our entire Seerah Wiki. Every event, biography, and geographical node is verified against its 12 volumes, which harmonize over three hundred primary classical Arabic sources.

## 1. Author & Scholarly Profile
Written by the 10th-century Shāfiʿī hadith master **Imām Muḥammad b. Yūsuf al-Ṣāliḥī al-Shāmī** (d. 942 AH in Cairo). He spent over three decades compiling the work, consulting libraries across Damascus, Jerusalem, Cairo, and Medina.

## 2. Structure of the 12 Volumes
- **Volume 1 & 2**: Lineage, Ancestors, The Year of the Elephant, [[Mawlid al-Nabī]], Nursing, Youth, [[Tajdīd al-Kaʿbah]], [[Mabʿath al-Nabī]], and the Makkan Daʿwah.
- **Volume 3**: [[al-Hijrah ilā al-Ḥabashah]], [[al-Isrāʾ wal-Miʿrāj]], the Pledges of al-ʿAqabah, [[al-Hijrah ilā al-Madīnah]], and early Madinan institutions.
- **Volume 4**: The Great Expeditions (Part 1): [[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]], Banū Qaynuqāʿ, [[Ghazwat Uḥud]], and [[Ghazwat al-Khandaq]].
- **Volume 5**: The Great Expeditions (Part 2): Banū Qurayẓah, [[Ṣulḥ al-Ḥudaybiyah]], [[Ghazwat Khaybar]], [[Ghazwat Muʾtah]], [[Fatḥ Makkah]], Ḥunayn, and [[Ghazwat Tabūk]].
- **Volume 6 & 7**: The Minor Expeditions and Cavalry Detachments (*al-Sarāyā wal-Buʿūth*), including [[Sariyyat Dūmat al-Jandal]].
- **Volume 8 & 9**: Prophetic Character (*al-Shamāʾil*), Worship, Living Quarters, Miracles (*Dalāʾil al-Nubuwwah*), and [[Ḥajjat al-Wadāʿ]].
- **Volume 10**: Prophetic Medicine, Jurisprudence, and Everyday Sunnah.
- **Volume 11**: Biographies and Virtues of the Household (*Ahl al-Bayt*), the Mothers of the Believers, the Ten Promised Paradise (including [[ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. ʿAwf]]), and the foremost Companions.
- **Volume 12**: Passing of the Prophet ﷺ (**[[Wafāt al-Nabī]]**), [[Saqīfat Banī Sāʿidah]], and Comprehensive Indexes.
