# ʿAbdullāh b. Jaʿfar (عبد الله بن جعفر)
*The Firstborn of Abyssinia, Nephew of the Prophet ﷺ, and Ocean of Munificence*

> [!NOTE] Canonical Sourcing
> Biographical record extracted from *al-Iṣābah fī Tamyīz al-Ṣaḥābah* (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:36–38, entry 4608) and *Subul al-Hudā war-Rashād* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:79).

---

## 1. Lineage & Abyssinian Birth

**ʿAbdullāh b. Jaʿfar** b. Abī Ṭālib b. ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib b. Hāshim al-Qurashī al-Hāshimī, bearing the patronymics Abū Jaʿfar and Abū Hāshim (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:36).

- **Parents**: Son of the heroic martyr of Muʾtah [[Jaʿfar b. Abī Ṭālib]] (Dhū al-Janāḥayn) and the noble companion [[Asmāʾ bint ʿUmays]] (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:36).
- **First Muslim Born in Exile**: He was the very first Muslim child born on the soil of Abyssinia during the Second Migration to the Negus (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:36). When the Negus had a newborn son, he named him ʿAbdullāh in honor of the Muslims, and Asmāʾ nursed him (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:36).
- **Return to Madīnah**: He accompanied his parents aboard the ship arriving in Madīnah concurrently with the victory of [[Ghazwat Khaybar]] in 7 AH (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:36).

---

## 2. Prophetic Upbringing, Blessings & Physical Resemblance

Following the martyrdom of his father Jaʿfar at [[Ghazwat Muʾtah]] in 8 AH, the Prophet ﷺ took direct personal custody of Jaʿfar's children (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:36–37).

- **Supplication for Commerce**: The Prophet ﷺ stroked young ʿAbdullāh's head, took him by the hand, and made the famous triple supplication:
  > «اللَّهمّ اخلف جعفرا في أهله، وبارك لعبد اللَّه في صفقة يمينه... وأنا وليّهم في الدّنيا والآخرة»
  > *"O Allah, succeed Jaʿfar in his family, and bless ʿAbdullāh in the transaction of his right hand! I am their guardian in this world and the Hereafter."* (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:37).
- **Physical Resemblance**: The Prophet ﷺ praised him saying:
  > «وأمّا عبد اللَّه فيشبه خلقي وخلقي»
  > *"As for ʿAbdullāh, he resembles my physical form and my moral character."* (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:37).
- **Riding Behind the Prophet ﷺ**: The Prophet ﷺ frequently mounted ʿAbdullāh behind him on his mount (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:37).

---

## 3. The Exemplar of Munificence & Legacy

Renowned across Islamic history as *Baḥr al-Jūd* (The Ocean of Generosity) and *Quṭb al-Sakhāʾ* (The Pivot of Munificence), ʿAbdullāh never permitted wealth to abide overnight in his possession (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:37–38).

- When presented with massive wealth from rulers and nobles, he distributed every dinar to the poor of Madīnah before setting foot in his residence (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:38). When an official offered him forty thousand dirhams for interceding with Caliph ʿAlī on his behalf, ʿAbdullāh refused, stating: *«إنا لا نبيع معروفا»* ("We do not sell a deed of kindness") (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:38).
- **Military Service**: During the Caliphate of his uncle [[ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib]], he served prominently as a trusted advisor and senior commander (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:37).
- **Demise**: He passed away in Madīnah in 80 AH (during the year of the flood known as *ʿĀm al-Juḥāf*). His funeral prayer was led by the governor of Madīnah, Abān b. ʿUthmān b. ʿAffān, and he was laid to rest in [[Jannat al-Baqīʿ]] (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:37).
