# Ṭalḥah b. ʿUbaydillāh (طلحة بن عبيد الله التيمي) ﵁
*Abū Muḥammad al-Taymī al-Qurashī — The Living Martyr, Ṭalḥat al-Khayr, Unshakeable Shield of Uḥud*

## 1. Lineage, Ancestry & Physical Description
### Patrilineal Descent & Clan Kinship
He is **Ṭalḥah b. ʿUbaydillāh b. ʿUthmān b. ʿAmr b. Kaʿb b. Saʿd b. Taym b. Murrah b. Kaʿb b. Luʾayy b. Ghālib al-Qurashī al-Taymī** (kunya: **Abū Muḥammad**; honorifics: *Ṭalḥat al-Khayr*, *Ṭalḥat al-Jūd*, *Ṭalḥat al-Fayyāḍ*) (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:430; *Subul al-Hudā*, 11:237, 308).

His lineage merges directly with that of the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ and **[[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]]** ﵁ at their shared forefather **Murrah b. Kaʿb** in the sixth generation (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:430). Belonging to the clan of **[[Banū Taym]]**, Ṭalḥah was one of the early aristocratic merchant leaders of Quraysh in pre-Islamic Makkah, conducting high-value mercantile expeditions across the caravan routes of Syria.

### Maternal Heritage
- **Mother**: **al-Ṣaʿbah bint al-Ḥaḍramī**, a noblewoman of Yemeni descent, daughter of ʿAbdullāh b. ʿAbbād b. Rabīʿah (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:430).
- **Maternal Uncle**: She was the sister of the celebrated Companion, commander, and governor of Baḥrayn, **al-ʿAlāʾ b. al-Ḥaḍramī** ﵁ (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:430). Through this connection, Ṭalḥah was tied to the prominent Banū al-Ḥaḍramī confederates of Banū Umayyah.

### Early Brotherhoods (al-Muʾākhāh)
- **Makkan Brotherhood**: Prior to the Hijrah in Makkah, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ paired Ṭalḥah in fraternal brotherhood with his close companion **[[al-Zubayr b. al-ʿAwwām]]** ﵁ (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:431).
- **Medinan Brotherhood**: Following the migration to Medina, the Prophet ﷺ paired him in brotherhood with the prominent Anṣārī host **Abū Ayyūb al-Anṣārī** ﵁ (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:431).

### Physical Appearance & Bearing
- **Build**: Medium stature tending toward shorter, exceptionally broad-chested (*raḥb al-ṣadr*), wide between the shoulders (*baʿīd mā bayn al-mankibayn*), with large, sturdy feet (*ḍakhm al-qadamayn*) (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:430).
- **Countenance & Hair**: Fair complexion tinged with reddish undertones (*abyaḍ yaḍribu ilā al-ḥumrah*) or olive-toned (*ādam*), remarkably handsome face (*ḥasan al-wajh*), slender aquiline nose (*daqīq al-ʿirnīn*), and abundant wavy hair that he left undyed in his elder years (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:430–431).
- **Gait & Demeanor**: When he turned, he turned his entire body gracefully (*idhā iltafata iltafata jamīʿan*), and he walked with a rapid, energetic pace (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:430–431).

## 2. Marriages, Kinship Networks & Progeny
Ṭalḥah contracted a remarkable series of marriages that created an extraordinary marital network across Quraysh, including the unique distinction noted by Ibn al-Sakan: marrying four women who were each sisters of one of the Mothers of the Believers (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:432):

1. **Umm Kulthūm bint Abī Bakr al-Ṣiddīq**:
   - Sister of the Mother of the Believers **[[ʿĀʾishah bint Abī Bakr]]** ﵂; bore him **Zakariyyā**, **Yūsuf** (died in infancy), and **ʿĀʾishah bint Ṭalḥah** (renowned for her beauty, intellect, and narration of hadith) (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:432).
2. **Ḥamnah bint Jaḥsh al-Asadiyyah** ﵂:
   - Sister of the Mother of the Believers **Zaynab bint Jaḥsh** ﵂ and the martyr ʿAbdullāh b. Jaḥsh ﵁; bore him **Muḥammad b. Ṭalḥah** (known as *al-Sajjād* due to his immense devotion, martyred at the Camel) and **ʿImrān b. Ṭalḥah** (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:432).
3. **al-Fāriʿah bint Abī Sufyān b. Ḥarb**:
   - Sister of the Mother of the Believers **Umm Ḥabībah Ramlah bint Abī Sufyān** ﵂ and Muʿāwiyah b. Abī Sufyān (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:432).
4. **Ruqayyah bint Abī Umayyah al-Makhzūmiyyah**:
   - Sister of the Mother of the Believers **[[Umm Salamah]]** ﵂ (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:432).
5. **Suʿdā bint ʿAwf al-Murriyyah**:
   - From Banū Murrah; bore him **ʿĪsā** and **Yaḥyā** (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:430).
6. **Khawlah bint al-Qaʿqāʿ b. Maʿbad al-Tamīmiyyah**:
   - Bore him **Mūsā b. Ṭalḥah** (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:430).
7. **Umm Abān bint ʿUtbah b. Rabīʿah**:
   - Sister of Hind bint ʿUtbah; bore him **Isḥāq** and **Yaʿqūb**.

## 3. Key Sīrah Milestones
### Conversion in Buṣrā & Early Trials
While trading at the market of Buṣrā in the Levant, a Christian monk addressed the merchant crowd asking if anyone hailed from the sacred precinct of Mecca, prophesying that the final Prophet of mankind had emerged whose migration would be to a palm-laden, salty terrain (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:430–431). Ṭalḥah rushed back to Makkah, where **[[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]]** ﵁ informed him of the mission of **[[Muḥammad b. ʻAbdullah|Muḥammad b. ʿAbdullāh]] ﷺ**. He accepted Islam immediately, becoming one of the first eight believers (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:430). 

During early persecutions, Nawfal b. Khuwaylid b. Asad seized Ṭalḥah and Abū Bakr and bound them together with a single rope to prevent them from praying; neither resisted nor renounced faith, earning them the title **al-Qarīnān** ("The Two Inseparable Bound Companions") (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:308).

### Strategic Reconnaissance at Badr (2 AH)
Prior to **[[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]]**, the Prophet ﷺ dispatched Ṭalḥah alongside **[[Saʿīd b. Zayd]]** ﵁ to intercept and scout the northern Syrian trade caravan led by Abū Sufyān (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:430; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:24; 11:309). They reached the northern waystation of Hawrāʾ, returning to Medina just as the Muslim forces returned victorious from Badr. The Prophet ﷺ allocated them their full share of the battle spoils and declared them entitled to the full heavenly reward of **[[Ahl Badr]]** (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:430; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:24; 11:309).

### The Living Martyr & Savior of the Prophet at Uḥud (3 AH)
At **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]**, when the polytheist cavalry counter-attack threw the Muslim army into disarray, Ṭalḥah placed his own body between the polytheist archers and the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:430; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:205; 11:309):
- **Paralyzed Hand**: Deflected volleys of lethal incoming arrows barehanded, severing his tendons until his fingers became permanently paralyzed (*shallat yaduhu*) (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:430, 432).
- **Endurance of Catastrophic Wounds**: Sustained between thirty and over seventy distinct sword, spear, and arrow wounds across his body without yielding ground (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:205; 11:309).
- **Hoisting the Prophet ﷺ**: When the Prophet ﷺ attempted to mount a tall rock face in the mountain ravine while weighed down by two heavy coats of mail and weakened by facial wounds, he was unable to ascend. Ṭalḥah knelt beneath him, hoisting the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ upon his back until the Prophet stood firmly atop the boulder (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:431; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:225; 11:309). Thereupon the Prophet ﷺ declared:
  > «أَوْجَبَ طَلْحَةُ!»
  > *"Ṭalḥah has made Paradise obligatory for himself!"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:431; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:225; 11:309).
- **The Living Martyr**: The Prophet ﷺ repeatedly proclaimed to the Companions:
  > «مَنْ سَرَّهُ أَنْ يَنْظُرَ إِلَى شَهِيدٍ يَمْشِي عَلَى وَجْهِ الْأَرْضِ، فَلْيَنْظُرْ إِلَى طَلْحَةَ بْنِ عُبَيْدِ اللَّهِ!»
  > *"Whoever takes pleasure in gazing upon a living martyr walking upon the earth, let him look upon Ṭalḥah b. ʿUbaydillāh!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:205; 11:309).

### Titles of Magnanimity & Later Ghazawāt
The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ conferred three distinct titles of generosity upon Ṭalḥah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:308):
- **Ṭalḥat al-Khayr** ("Ṭalḥah of Goodness") on the day of Uḥud.
- **Ṭalḥat al-Fayyāḍ** ("Ṭalḥah the Overflowing") during the expedition of Dhū Qarad, when he purchased the brackish well of Bīsān, which the Prophet renamed Nuʿmān, and endowed it as fresh water for the believers (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:430; *Subul al-Hudā*, 11:308).
- **Ṭalḥat al-Jūd** ("Ṭalḥah of Bounty") on the day of Ḥunayn.
He fought at **[[Ghazwat al-Khandaq]]**, swore **[[Bayʿat al-Riḍwān]]** at Ḥudaybiyah, and took part in **[[Ghazwat Khaybar]]**, **[[Fatḥ Makkah]]**, and **[[Ghazwat Ḥunayn]]** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:370; 5:48, 124, 312).

## 4. Caliphal Shūrā, Martyrdom & Burial
Appointed by Caliph ʿUmar as one of the six elite electors of the Shūrā council in 23 AH, Ṭalḥah was renowned for staggering charity, once dispensing an entire payment of 100,000 dirhams in a single sitting without keeping a dirham for his household (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:310).

During the tragic conflict at the Battle of the Camel on Thursday, 10 Jumādā al-Ūlā 36 AH, Ṭalḥah withdrew from the battlefield after being reminded by ʿAlī of the Prophet's words. As he retired, he was struck in the knee by an arrow fired by Marwān b. al-Ḥakam, which severed his artery (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:432). He bled to death at the age of sixty-four and was buried near the battlefield outside Baṣrah (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:432; *Subul al-Hudā*, 11:312). When ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib ﵁ passed over his fallen body, he wept, wiped the dust from Ṭalḥah's face, and exclaimed: *"May Allāh have mercy on you, O Abū Muḥammad! It is agonizing for me to see you cast down beneath the stars of the sky!"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:432).
