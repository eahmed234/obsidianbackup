# Bayʿat al-Riḍwān (بيعة الرضوان)
*The Pledge of Divine Pleasure — The Historic Covenant of Steadfastness Beneath the Tree of al-Ḥudaybiyah*

## 1. Strategic Context & The Provocation
In Dhū al-Qaʿdah 6 AH, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ marched out from Medina with **fourteen hundred** Companions (*arbaʿata ʿashara miʾah*) intending only to perform the minor pilgrimage (*ʿUmrah*), driving sacrificial animals with no offensive weaponry (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:41, 5:48). 

Halted at **[[al-Ḥudaybiyah]]** by the armed mobilization of Quraysh, the Prophet ﷺ dispatched **[[ʿUthmān b. ʿAffān]]** ﵁ into Makkah as his diplomatic emissary to assure the patricians that the Muslims came solely as peaceful pilgrims (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:46). Ten other Muslims entered Makkah under ʿUthmān's protection or secretly to visit family (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:48).

When ʿUthmān's diplomatic return was severely delayed and Quraysh detained the party, an urgent report reached the encampment that ʿUthmān and his companions had been murdered within the sanctuary (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:48).

## 2. The Summons Under the Tree
Upon receiving the news of ʿUthmān's martyrdom, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ resolved upon armed confrontation, declaring:
> «لَا نَبْرَحُ حَتَّى نُنَاجِزَ الْقَوْمَ»
> *"We shall not depart until we engage the people in battle to the finish!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:48).

The Prophet ﷺ took his seat in the quarters of Banū Māzin b. al-Najjār beneath the shade of a lush Egyptian thorn tree—an acacia (*samurah*)—and announced: *«إِنَّ اللَّهَ تَعَالَى أَمَرَنِي بِالْبَيْعَةِ»* (*"Indeed, Allāh the Exalted has commanded me to take the pledge of allegiance!"*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:48, 5:49).

The prophetic herald cried out across the valley:
> «أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ: الْبَيْعَةَ الْبَيْعَةَ! نَزَلَ رُوحُ الْقُدُسِ فَاخْرُجُوا عَلَى اسْمِ اللَّهِ!»
> *"O people! To the pledge! To the pledge! The Holy Spirit [Jibrīl ﵊] has descended, so come forth in the name of Allāh!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:48).

The Companions surged forward in overwhelming devotion, donning their arms, while Umm ʿUmārah (**[[Nusaybah bint Kaʿb]]** ﵂) grasped a wooden tent pole and tied a dagger to her waist ready for combat (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:48).

## 3. The Terms & Ceremonial Details of the Pledge
The Companions pressed forward to clasp the Prophet's noble hand:
- **Holding the Branches**: **[[ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb]]** ﵁ grasped the Prophet's hand, while **al-Nuʿmān b. Muqarrin al-Muzanī** (and in other narrations, **[[Maʿqil b. Yasār]]** or **[[Jābir b. ʿAbdillāh]]**) held up the thorny acacia branches above the Prophet's head so they would not brush against him (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:49–50).
- **The First to Pledge**: The first man to step forward was **Abū Sinān Wahb b. Miḥṣan al-Asadī** ﵁ (or his son **[[Sinān b. Abī Sinān al-Asadī]]** ﵁), saying: *"Stretch forth your hand that I may pledge!"* The Prophet asked: *«عَلَامَ تُبَايِعُنِي؟»* (*"Upon what do you pledge allegiance to me?"*). Abū Sinān answered:
  > «عَلَى مَا فِي نَفْسِكَ: أَضْرِبُ بِسَيْفِي بَيْنَ يَدَيْكَ حَتَّى يُظْهِرَكَ اللَّهُ أَوْ أُقْتَلَ!»
  > *"Upon whatever is in your soul: that I shall strike with my sword before you until Allāh grants victory or I am slain!"*
  The Prophet accepted his oath, and the entire army followed, swearing allegiance upon the pledge of Abū Sinān (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:50).

### What Did They Pledge Upon?
Hadith masters record complementary facets of the covenant:
- **Steadfastness Without Retreat**: Jābir b. ʿAbdillāh ﵄ testified in *Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim*: *«بَايَعْنَاهُ عَلَى أَلَّا نَفِرَّ، وَلَمْ نُبَايِعْهُ عَلَى الْمَوْتِ»* (*"We pledged to him that we would never flee; we did not pledge to him upon death itself"*), (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:50).
- **Fighting to the Death**: **[[Salamah b. al-Akwaʿ]]** ﵁ testified in *Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī*: *«بَايَعْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ تَحْتَ الشَّجَرَةِ عَلَى الْمَوْتِ»* (*"I pledged allegiance to the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ beneath the tree upon death"*), (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:49).
- **Harmonization**: Classical scholars explain there is no contradiction: pledging "never to flee" even in the face of annihilation is functionally identical to fighting to the death in perseverance.

### The Triple Pledge of Salamah b. al-Akwaʿ
Salamah b. al-Akwaʿ ﵁ pledged allegiance among the very first cohort. As the line continued, the Prophet ﷺ noticed him unarmed in the middle of the people and called: *«بَايِعْ يَا سَلَمَةُ!»* (*"Pledge, O Salamah!"*). When Salamah noted he had already pledged, the Prophet ﷺ replied: *«وَأَيْضًا»* (*"Again!"*), and handed him a leather shield (*ḥajafah*). At the very end of the line, the Prophet ﷺ called him a third time: *«أَلَا تُبَايِعُنِي يَا سَلَمَةُ؟»*, taking his pledge for the third time (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:49).

### The Hand of ʿUthmān
When the entire army had pledged, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ raised his right hand and declared:
> «اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّ عُثْمَانَ فِي حَاجَتِكَ وَحَاجَةِ رَسُولِكَ!»
> *"O Allāh! Indeed ʿUthmān is away upon Your mission and the mission of Your Messenger!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:50).
He then struck his right hand upon his left hand, clasping the pledge on behalf of **[[ʿUthmān b. ʿAffān]]** ﵁. The Companions observed: *«فَكَانَتْ يَدُ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ ﷺ لِعُثْمَانَ خَيْرًا مِنْ أَيْدِيهِمْ لِأَنْفُسِهِمْ»* (*"And the hand of the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ for ʿUthmān was better than their own hands for themselves!"*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:50).

### The Lone Defector
Every single soul of the fourteen hundred present took the sacred pledge, save for one notorious hypocrite: **al-Jadd b. Qays**, who crouched beneath the flank of his red she-camel, hiding to avoid taking the oath (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:49–50).

## 4. Qurʾānic Inscription & Celestial Virtues
The supreme theological distinction of those who swore allegiance beneath the tree was revealed in **[[Sūrat al-Fatḥ]]**:
> «لَّقَدْ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنِ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ إِذْ يُبَايِعُونَكَ تَحْتَ الشَّجَرَةِ فَعَلِمَ مَا فِي قُلُوبِهِمْ فَأَنزَلَ السَّكِينَةَ عَلَيْهِمْ وَأَثَابَهُمْ فَتْحًا قَرِيبًا»
> *"Certainly has Allāh been pleased with the believers when they pledged allegiance to you beneath the tree, and He knew what was in their hearts, so He sent down tranquility upon them and rewarded them with an imminent conquest..."* (Qurʾān 48:18; *Subul al-Hudā*, 5:50).

From this divine phrase—*raḍiya Allāhu ʿan al-muʾminīn*—the event took its eternal title: **Bayʿat al-Riḍwān** (The Pledge of Divine Pleasure).

In *Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim*, the Prophet ﷺ declared:
> «لَا يَدْخُلُ النَّارَ أَحَدٌ بَايَعَ تَحْتَ الشَّجَرَةِ»
> *"None shall enter the Fire of Hell who swore allegiance beneath the Tree."* (*Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim*, no. 2496).

## 5. The Aftermath & The Fate of the Tree
The news of the unified, unwavering death-pledge terrified Quraysh, who quickly released ʿUthmān and his companions and dispatched [[Suhayl b. ʿAmr]] to urgently sue for peace, directly culminating in **[[Ṣulḥ al-Ḥudaybiyah]]** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:48, 5:51).

### The Erasure of the Tree
When the Companions returned to al-Ḥudaybiyah the following year for *ʿUmrat al-Qaḍāʾ*, they searched for the specific acacia tree beneath which they had pledged, but by divine wisdom, its exact identity had been obscured from their memory (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:50). Saʿīd b. al-Musayyib related from his father: *"We returned the following year, and we could not recognize it; it was a mercy from Allāh"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:50).

During the Caliphate of **[[ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb]]** ﵁, word reached him that certain travelers were seeking out a tree at al-Ḥudaybiyah to pray beside it. Fearing the subtle emergence of idolatrous veneration and polytheistic relics (*fitnah*), ʿUmar sternly warned them and ordered the tree chopped down (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:50).
