# al-Ḥubāb b. al-Mundhir (الحباب بن المنذر)
*Abū ʿAmr al-Khazrajī — Dhū al-Raʾy (The Possessor of Sound Counsel)*

> [!NOTE] Biographical Summary
> **al-Ḥubāb b. al-Mundhir b. al-Jumūḥ al-Anṣārī al-Khazrajī** (raḍiya Allāhu ʿanh) was a prominent chieftain of the [[Banū Salimah]] clan of the [[al-Khazraj]]. Known among the Arabs for his sharp military acumen, tactical foresight, and poetic eloquence, he was honored with the title ***Dhū al-Raʾy*** (*The Man of Sound Counsel*). He was thirty-three years old during [[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]].

---

## 1. Decisive Role in Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā

Al-Ḥubāb’s most famous historical achievement occurred when the Muslim army encamped before the clash at [[Badr]]. Observing the water geography of the valley, he approached the Prophet [[Muḥammad b. ʻAbdullah|Muḥammad]] ﷺ with the utmost reverence and inquired:

> «يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ: أَرَأَيْتَ هَذَا الْمَنْزِلَ، أَمَنْزِلاً أَنْزَلَكَهُ اللَّهُ لَيْسَ لَنَا أَنْ نَتَقَدَّمَهُ وَلَا نَتَأَخَّرَ عَنْهُ، أَمْ هُوَ الرَّأْيُ وَالْحَرْبُ وَالْمَكِيدَةُ؟»
> *"O Messenger of Allāh: Have you viewed this encampment as a station that Allāh has revealed to you, such that we may neither advance beyond it nor retreat from it? Or is it a matter of tactical opinion, warfare, and military strategy?"*

The Prophet ﷺ answered:
> «بَلْ هُوَ الرَّأْيُ وَالْحَرْبُ وَالْمَكِيدَةُ»
> *"Rather, it is a matter of tactical opinion, warfare, and military strategy."*

Al-Ḥubāb responded:
> *"O Messenger of Allāh, this is not the station. Advance the men until we come to the water well nearest to the enemy. We shall encamp there, seal up and bury all the other wells behind us, construct a reservoir over our well, and fill it with water. Thus, we shall engage them while we have water to drink and they have none!"*

The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ accepted his counsel immediately:
> «لَقَدْ أَشَرْتَ بِالرَّأْيِ»
> *"You have indeed indicated the sound counsel!"* (*[[Subul al-Hudā war-Rashād]]*, 4:27–28; *al-Sīrah al-Nabawiyyah* by [[Ibn Hishām]], 1:620).

---

## 2. Juristic Significance: Revelation vs. Worldly Strategy

In Sunni jurisprudence (*uṣūl al-fiqh*), al-Ḥubāb’s inquiry established a foundational legal and theological demarcation:
1. **Tawqīfī (Divine Injunction)**: Matters of creed (*ʿaqīdah*), ritual acts of worship (*ʿibādāt*), and revealed law cannot be modified by human reason or worldly convenience.
2. **Ijtihādī / Dunyawī (Tactical Discretion)**: Matters of logistical strategy, agricultural engineering, military placement, and medical administration are open to human deduction, expert consultation, and empirical trial.

This precedent directly parallels the famous hadith in *Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim*:
> «أَنْتُمْ أَعْلَمُ بِأَمْرِ دُنْيَاكُمْ»
> *"You know better regarding the affairs of your world."*

---

## 3. Other Events in the Sīrah

Al-Ḥubāb witnessed all major campaigns alongside the Prophet ﷺ:
- **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]] (3 AH)**: When panic spread following the archers' descent, al-Ḥubāb stood resolute amongst the steadfast cadre guarding the Prophet ﷺ.
- **[[Ghazwat Khaybar]] (7 AH)**: Al-Ḥubāb advised the Prophet ﷺ regarding the tactical vulnerability of encamping near the fort of Naṭāh due to malaria-ridden dampness and enemy missile range. The Prophet ﷺ praised his assessment and relocated the camp (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:124).
- **[[Saqīfat Banī Sāʿidah]] (11 AH)**: Following the demise of the Prophet ﷺ, al-Ḥubāb was the chief spokesman for the Anṣār advocating a dual leadership arrangement (*"minnā amīr wa-minkum amīr"*), before **[[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]]** and **[[ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb]]** established the consensus (*ijmāʿ*) upon the Caliphate of Abū Bakr. Al-Ḥubāb subsequently gave his pledge of allegiance (*bayʿah*) and remained loyal to the unified state.

---

## 4. Ḥadīth Transmission

- Al-Ḥubāb transmitted reports regarding the military dispositions and logistics of [[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]], documented through [[ʿUrwah b. al-Zubayr]] and [[Mūsā b. ʿUqbah]].
