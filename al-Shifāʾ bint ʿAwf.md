# al-Shifāʾ bint ʿAwf (الشفاء بنت عوف)
*The Midwife of the Mawlid, and Mother of ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. ʿAwf ﵁*

## 1. Two Distinctions
Of **[[Banū Zuhrah]]** — the Prophet's ﷺ maternal clan — al-Shifāʾ ﵂ holds two places in the Sīrah
that are rarely joined in one person:

1. She was the **midwife (*qābilah*) who attended the birth of the Prophet ﷺ**. Al-Ṣāliḥī names her
   in exactly this capacity when recording the signs witnessed at the birth — *wa-mā raʾathu
   qābilatuhu al-Shifāʾ umm ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. ʿAwf min al-āyāt*, "and what his midwife al-Shifāʾ, the
   mother of ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. ʿAwf, saw of the signs" (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:341). Among what she
   reported was seeing the newborn descend in prostration, supporting himself on his hands.
2. She was the **mother of [[ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. ʿAwf]]** ﵁, of the Ten Promised Paradise.

## 2. Significance
Her testimony belongs to the *dalāʾil al-nubuwwah* material of Volume 1, and she is one of the small
number of eyewitnesses to **[[Mawlid al-Nabī]]** itself whose account is transmitted by name. That
she was of Banū Zuhrah reflects how closely the Prophet's ﷺ birth was attended by his mother's clan —
see [[Āminah bint Wahb]].
