# Muzdalifah (مزدلفة)
*Al-Mashʿar al-Ḥarām, Manzil al-Jamʿ bayna al-Ṣalātayn, wa-Mawqif al-Hudā fī Ḥajjat al-Wadāʿ*

## 1. Topography, Nomenclature & Sanctuary Bounds

**Muzdalifah** (مزدلفة) is a sacred valley basin in the Hijaz situated between the plains of [[ʿArafāt]] and the encampments of [[Minā]]:
- **Boundaries**: It is demarcated geographically by the mountain defile of al-Maʾzimayn (through which pilgrims enter from ʿArafāt) and the valley of Wādī Muḥassir (which separates Muzdalifah from Minā) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:472–473, 493).
- **Sanctuary Status**: Muzdalifah lies entirely within the Sacred Precinct (*al-Ḥaram*), marking the point where pilgrims re-enter the consecrated territory after completing the pillar of standing at ʿArafāt outside the Ḥaram (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:178; 8:473).
- **Names**:
  - *Muzdalifah*: Derived from *al-izdilāf* (approach or gathering), because pilgrims draw near to the Sanctuary and congregate therein (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:472).
  - *Jamʿ* (The Assembly): So designated because the multitude gathers there, and because the two prayers of Maghrib and ʿIshāʾ are joined (*jamʿ*) during the overnight stay (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:235, 472).
  - *al-Mashʿar al-Ḥarām* (The Sacred Monument): The specific elevated sanctuary located upon the hill of Quzaḥ within Muzdalifah, explicitly highlighted in the Qurʾān:
    > ﴿فَإِذَا أَفَضْتُمْ مِنْ عَرَفَاتٍ فَاذْكُرُوا اللَّهَ عِنْدَ الْمَشْعَرِ الْحَرَامِ﴾
    > *"Then when you depart from ʿArafāt, remember Allāh at the Sacred Monument (al-Mashʿar al-Ḥarām)."* (Qurʾān 2:198; *Subul al-Hudā*, 8:473).
- **Prophetic Boundary Injunction**: The Prophet ﷺ established the whole valley as permissible for the overnight stay, announcing:
  > «كل المزدلفة موقفنا إلا بطن محسّر»
  > *"All of Muzdalifah is a standing-place, except the interior of Wādī Muḥassir."* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:473).

## 2. Pre-Islamic History & the Innovation of the Ḥums

In the pre-Islamic Jahiliyyah era, Muzdalifah occupied a prominent place in tribal pilgrimage customs:
- **Quṣayy b. Kilāb's Fire**: **[[Quṣayy b. Kilāb]]** instituted the custom of kindling a beacon fire atop the hill of Quzaḥ in Muzdalifah so that pilgrims descending through the dark passes from ʿArafāt could easily orient themselves (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:276; 8:472).
- **The Elitism of al-Ḥums**: The Quraysh and their allied clans styled themselves *al-Ḥums* (the strict custodians of the Sanctuary). Driven by aristocratic pride, they refused to leave the boundaries of the Ḥaram to stand with other Arab tribes at ʿArafāt; instead, they halted only at Muzdalifah, considering themselves too noble to exit the Sanctuary (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:178; 8:473).
- **Sunrise Departure Chant**: In Jahiliyyah, the pilgrims refused to depart from Muzdalifah until the sun had crested the peaks of Mount Thabīr, chanting aloud:
  > «أشرق ثبير كيما نغير»
  > *"Shine, O Thabīr, so that we may hasten onward!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:473).

## 3. Prophetic Rites during the Farewell Pilgrimage (Ḥajjat al-Wadāʿ)

During the **[[Ḥajjat al-Wadāʿ|Farewell Pilgrimage]]** (10 AH), the Prophet ﷺ comprehensively reformed and sanctified the rites of Muzdalifah:
- **Abolition of Tribal Elitism**: The Prophet ﷺ stood at ʿArafāt alongside all pilgrims, overturning the discriminatory custom of the Ḥums and restoring the authentic Abrahamic pilgrimage (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:178; 8:472–473).
- **Descent in Tranquility (*al-Sakīnah*)**: Departing ʿArafāt after sunset with Usāmah b. Zayd riding behind him, the Prophet ﷺ restrained the reins of his camel al-Qaṣwāʾ and called out with his right hand:
  > «أيها الناس عليكم السكينة، فإن البرّ ليس بالإيضاع»
  > *"O people, observe tranquility! For righteousness does not consist in rushing mounts!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:472; 11:95).
- **Combining the Prayers**: Upon reaching Muzdalifah near Quzaḥ, he performed ablution and prayed Maghrib immediately before unpacking mounts, followed by ʿIshāʾ with a separate iqāmah, combining both prayers without any voluntary prayers in between (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:235, 472).
- **Overnight Rest & Concession for the Weak**: The Prophet ﷺ slept through the night until dawn (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:472). In the late hours of the night (*saḥar*), he granted permission (*rukhṣah*) to the weak, elderly, women (including **[[Sawdah bint Zamʿah]]** and **[[Umm Ḥabībah]]** ﵅), and children to proceed ahead to Minā before the morning rush (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:472–473; *al-Iṣābah*, 8:196).
- **Standing at al-Mashʿar al-Ḥarām**: After praying Fajr at the onset of dawn on the Day of Immolation, he mounted al-Qaṣwāʾ and stood at al-Mashʿar al-Ḥarām upon Quzaḥ, facing the Qiblah in supplication, glorification, and remembrance until the morning light was radiant (*asfara jiddan*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:473).
- **Defiance of Jahili Custom**: He departed before sunrise, explicitly repudiating the Jahili chant (*«Ashriq Thabīr»*) and affirming the revival of the pure prophetic way of Ibrāhīm ﵊ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:473).
- **Gathering the Pebbles**: Departing with **[[al-Faḍl b. al-ʿAbbās]]** ﵁ riding pillion, the Prophet ﷺ instructed him to collect seven small pebbles (*ḥaṣā al-khadhf*), cautioning the believers:
  > «أمثال هؤلاء، فارموا، وإياكم والغلوّ في الدّين، فإنما أهلك من كان قبلكم الغلوّ في الدّين»
  > *"Pelt with the likes of these! And beware of extremism in religion, for those before you were destroyed only by extremism in religion!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:473).
- **Comprehensive Ruling for Pilgrims**: At Muzdalifah, ʿUrwah b. Muḍarris al-Ṭāʾī ﵁ arrived after an arduous journey, having traversed every mountain, and asked if his pilgrimage was valid. The Prophet ﷺ gave the definitive ruling:
  > «من شهد صلاتنا هذه، فوقف معنا حتى ندفع وقد وقف بعرفة قبل ذلك ليلًا أو نهارًا، فقد أتم حجّه وقضى تفثه»
  > *"Whoever witnesses this prayer of ours, and stands with us until we depart, having stood at ʿArafāt before that by night or by day, has completed his Hajj and fulfilled his rites."* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:473; *al-Iṣābah*, 4:408).
