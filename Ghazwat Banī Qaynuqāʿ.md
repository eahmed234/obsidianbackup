# Ghazwat Banī Qaynuqāʿ (غزوة بني قينقاع)
*The Siege and Expulsion of the Fortress Clan of Banū Qaynuqāʿ*

## 1. Overview
Following the triumph of [[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]], tensions surged in Medina. The Jewish goldsmith and warrior clan of **[[Banū Qaynuqāʿ]]** breached their treaty obligations (*Dustūr al-Madīnah*), boasting: *"Do not be deceived by your victory over Quraysh; they know not how to fight! If you fight us, you will learn that we are real men!"*

The flashpoint occurred when a Muslim woman in their marketplace was humiliated by a goldsmith pinning her skirt to her back. When an observant Muslim defended her honor, the market mob murdered him.

## 2. Siege & Expulsion
The Prophet ﷺ besieged their fortresses for fifteen days until they surrendered unconditionally. The arch-hypocrite **[[ʿAbdullāh b. Ubayy b. Salūl]]** pleaded aggressively for his former allies. The Prophet ﷺ granted clemency from execution but ordered their permanent exile to Syria (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:180).
