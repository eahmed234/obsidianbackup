# Ḥunayn (حنين)
*The Dramatic Valley of Ambush and Ultimate Victory Near Dhū al-Majāz*

> [!NOTE] Canonical Sourcing
> Topographical and historical details derived from *Subul al-Hudā war-Rashād* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:310, 351; 6:302).

---

## 1. Topography & Geographic Orientation

- **Nature & Name**: A wide valley (*wādī*) situated in the Ḥijāz, traditionally vocalized with a ḍammah on the ḥāʾ in diminutive form (*Ḥunayn*). According to Abū ʿUbayd al-Bakrī, it was named after Ḥunayn b. Qāniyah b. Mahlāʾīl (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:351).
- **Location**: Situated adjacent to the ancient Arabian market of **Dhū al-Majāz**, roughly ten to fifteen miles from Makkah on the approach toward [[al-Ṭāʾif]], prior to reaching Wādī Wajj (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:351, 6:302).
- **Distinction from Awṭās**: Imām [[al-Ṣāliḥī]] affirms that Wādī Ḥunayn is geographically distinct from [[Awṭās]], which lies further within the territory of Hawāzin (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:352).

---

## 2. Primary Sīrah Significance: Ghazwat Ḥunayn

Ḥunayn serves as the titular battleground for [[Ghazwat Ḥunayn]] in Shawwāl 8 AH (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:310).

- **The Ambush**: The confederacy of [[Hawāzin]] and [[Thaqīf]], commanded by Mālik b. ʿAwf al-Naṣrī, concealed their archers in the narrow gorges and ravines (*shiʿāb*) of Wādī Ḥunayn (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:317–318).
- **Reversal and Divine Aid**: As the vanguard of the Muslim army of twelve thousand advanced into the valley at dawn, they were showered with arrows, causing a sudden panic and initial retreat (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:318).
- **The Prophetic Stand**: Standing unyielding upon his white mule Duldul in the center of the valley, the Prophet [[Muḥammad b. ʻAbdullah|Muḥammad]] ﷺ rallied the believers, declaring:
  > «أنا النبيّ لا كذب ... أنا ابن عبد المطّلب»
  > *"I am the Prophet, there is no falsehood! I am the son of ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:324).
- **Scriptural Node**: The battle in this valley is explicitly immortalized in the Qurʾān:
  > ﴿لَقَدْ نَصَرَكُمُ اللَّهُ فِي مَواطِنَ كَثِيرَةٍ وَيَوْمَ حُنَيْنٍ إِذْ أَعْجَبَتْكُمْ كَثْرَتُكُمْ فَلَمْ تُغْنِ عَنْكُمْ شَيْئًا﴾
  > *"Allah has already given you victory in many regions and on the day of Ḥunayn, when your great numbers pleased you, but they did not avail you at all..."* (Sūrat al-Tawbah: 25; *Subul al-Hudā*, 5:351).
