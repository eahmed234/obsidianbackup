# Shaqq al-Ṣadr (شق الصدر الشريف وغسل القلب)
*The Miraculous Cleansing of the Prophetic Heart, the Extirpation of Shayṭān's Clot, and the Inscribing of the Celestial Seal*

## 1. Historical Setting in the Encampment of Banū Saʿd
Following his weaning (*fiṣāl*) at two years of age, **[[Ḥalīmah al-Saʿdiyyah]]** ﵂ returned the infant Prophet ﷺ to his mother, **[[Āminah bint Wahb]]**, in Makkah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:388). However, due to the unprecedented celestial blessings (*barakah*) that had enriched Ḥalīmah's household and livestock, coupled with fears of an epidemic in Makkah, Ḥalīmah pleaded with Āminah to allow him to remain in the pure desert air of **[[Banū Saʿd]]** b. Bakr.

When he reached approximately **four years of age**, while out in the desert pastures accompanying his foster-brother grazing young lambs (*buhm*) behind the tents, the landmark event of *Shaqq al-Ṣadr* occurred (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:388).

## 2. The Descents & The Cleansing Ritual
The foster-brother came sprinting in sheer terror back to the encampment, crying to Ḥalīmah and her husband al-Ḥārith:
> «يَا أَبَةِ وَيَا أُمَّاهُ! إِلْحَقَا أَخِي مُحَمَّدًا، فَقَدْ جَاءَهُ رَجُلٌ فَأَخَذَهُ فَعَلَا بِهِ ذِرْوَةَ الْجَبَلِ وَشَقَّ عَنْ بَطْنِهِ!»
> *"O father! O mother! Rush to my brother Muḥammad! For a man has seized him, taken him to the summit of the hill, and split open his belly!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:388).

Ḥalīmah and al-Ḥārith raced to the hilltop and found the young child seated alone upon the crest, gazing serenely upward into the heavens, though his face was pale (*muntaqaʿan lawnuhu*). Embracing him, Ḥalīmah asked in distress what had transpired. The child ﷺ comforted her and narrated the celestial intervention (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:388):

### The Canonical Transmission in Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim
As narrated by **[[Anas b. Mālik]]** ﵁:
> «أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ أَتَاهُ جِبْرِيلُ ﵊ وَهُوَ يَلْعَبُ مَعَ الْغِلْمَانِ، فَأَخَذَهُ فَصَرَعَهُ، فَشَقَّ عَنْ قَلْبِهِ، فَاسْتَخْرَجَ الْقَلْبَ، فَاسْتَخْرَجَ مِنْهُ عَلَقَةً، فَقَالَ: هَٰذَا حَظُّ الشَّيْطَانِ مِنْكَ! ثُمَّ غَسَلَهُ فِي طَسْتٍ مِنْ ذَهَبٍ بِمَاءِ زَمْزَمَ، ثُمَّ لَأَمَهُ ثُمَّ أَعَادَهُ فِي مَكَانِهِ»
> *"The Archangel Jibrīl ﵊ came to the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ while he was playing with the youths. He took him, laid him gently down, opened his chest, and drew out his heart. He then extracted from it a dark blood-clot (*ʿalaqah*) and declared: 'This is the portion of Shayṭān from you!' Then he washed it in a golden basin with Zamzam water, sealed it together, and restored it to its place."* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:389; *Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim*, no. 162).

### The Infilling with Sakīnah & The Celestial Seal
Other authentic transmissions (including those of Ibn ʿAbbās, Shaddād b. Aws, and ʿAbdullāh b. ʿUtbah) detail that two or three angelic figures descended in dazzling white robes, carrying a silver ewer and an emerald basin filled with pure celestial snow (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:388–389):
- They opened his chest without pain or sensation of wound (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:389).
- After washing the viscera and heart with snow water, they filled the cavity with divine tranquility (**sakīnah**), wisdom (*ḥikmah*), and faith (*īmān*).
- Jibrīl ﵊ stamped his chest/heart with the **Seal of Prophethood** (*Khātam al-Nubuwwah*) of luminous light, leaving an enduring physical mark. Anas b. Mālik ﵁ testified:
  > «وَقَدْ كُنْتُ أَرَى أَثَرَ ذَلِكَ الْمِخْيَطِ فِي صَدْرِهِ»
  > *"And indeed, I used to clearly see the trace of that stitching across his chest!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:389; *Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim*, no. 162).

## 3. The Symbolism of the Weighing (*al-Wazn*)
Directly following the cleansing, one angel instructed the other:
> «زِنْهُ بِعَشَرَةٍ مِنْ أُمَّتِهِ!»
> *"Weigh him against ten men of his nation!"*
He was placed on a celestial scale, and he outweighed all ten. The angel said: *"Weigh him against a hundred of his nation!"* He outweighed all one hundred. The angel said: *"Weigh him against a thousand of his nation!"* He outweighed all one thousand (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:389–390). The angel then exclaimed:
> «دَعْهُ عَنْكَ! فَوَاللَّهِ لَوْ وَزَنْتَهُ بِأُمَّتِهِ كُلِّهَا لَرَجَحَ بِهَا!»
> *"Leave him be! For by Allāh, were you to weigh him against his entire nation combined, he would surely outweigh them all!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:390, 1:393).

Al-Ṣāliḥī notes that this spiritual weighing represented the immense weight of moral excellence, spiritual virtue, and cosmic superiority granted to him over the entirety of humanity (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:393).

## 4. Multiplicity of the Openings of the Chest
Classical scholars, synthesizing the diverse authentic narrations, establish that the physical opening of the Prophet's chest occurred at multiple critical junctures in his life (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:390; 3:65):
1. **In Early Childhood (Banū Saʿd)**: At age four, to purge the subtle origin of satanic whisperings (*ḥaẓẓ al-shayṭān*) and fortify him with pristine purity from early youth.
2. **At the Dawn of Revelation (al-Mabʿath)**: At age forty, to prepare his heart to receive the crushing weight of the divine Word (*Qawlan Thaqīlan*).
3. **Prior to the Ascension ([[al-Isrāʾ wal-Miʿrāj]])**: In the chamber next to the Kaʿbah, where his chest was reopened and filled with faith and light to enable him to witness the celestial realms and converse in the Divine Presence (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:65).

## 5. Aftermath: The Return to Makkah
Terrified by this miraculous manifestation and dreading that unseen forces or hostile sorcerers might harm their precious foster-child, Ḥalīmah and al-Ḥārith resolved to return him immediately to his mother in Makkah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:390).

When Āminah asked why they had brought him back so precipitously after having previously insisted upon keeping him, Ḥalīmah confessed what had transpired. Āminah comforted her with serene conviction:
> «كَلَّا، وَاللَّهِ مَا لِلشَّيْطَانِ عَلَيْهِ مِنْ سَبِيلٍ! وَإِنَّ لِابْنِي هَذَا لَشَأْنًا عَظِيمًا!»
> *"Never! By Allāh, Shayṭān has no path of influence over him! Indeed, for this son of mine there is a magnificent destiny!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:390).
