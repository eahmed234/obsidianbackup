# Salamah b. al-Akwaʿ (سلمة بن الأكوع)
*The Champion Runner of Islam — Hero of the Three Pledges and Knight of Dhī Qarad*

## 1. Lineage & Physical Attributes
His full lineage is **Salamah b. ʿAmr b. al-Akwaʿ**; the title *al-Akwaʿ* was the agnomen of his father or grandfather, **Sinān b. ʿAbdillāh b. Qushayr b. Khuzaymah b. Mālik b. Salāmān b. Aslam al-Aslamī** (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:127). His *kunyah* was **Abū Muslim**, though some authorities note **Abū Iyās** (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:128). His full brother was the illustrious warrior and martyr of Khaybar, **ʿĀmir b. al-Akwaʿ** ﵁ (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:127).

Salamah was legendary among the Arabs for his supreme physical conditioning, peerless foot-speed, and deadly archery. Classical authorities record that he was so swift that he could literally outrun galloping Arabian warhorses on foot (*yasbiqu al-faras ʿadwan*) (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:127).

## 2. Key Sīrah Milestones

### The Three Pledges of al-Ḥudaybiyah (6 AH)
The first military campaign Salamah witnessed alongside the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ was **[[Ṣulḥ al-Ḥudaybiyah]]** in Dhū al-Qaʿdah 6 AH (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:127). 

When the call was sounded for **[[Bayʿat al-Riḍwān]]** beneath the tree, Salamah was among the very first group to step forward and swear fealty to the Prophet ﷺ (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:127; *Subul al-Hudā*, 5:48). As the pledge progressed and hundreds swore allegiance, the Prophet ﷺ noticed Salamah standing nearby unarmed and summoned him:
> «بَايِعْ يَا سَلَمَةُ!» فَقَالَ: «قَدْ بَايَعْتُ يَا رَسُولَ اللهِ فِي الأَوَّلِ!» قَالَ: «وَفِي الأَوْسَطِ!» فَبَايَعَ. ثُمَّ لَمَّا فَرَغَ النَّاسُ قَالَ: «أَلَا تُبَايِعُ يَا سَلَمَةُ؟» قَالَ: «قَدْ بَايَعْتُ فِي الأَوَّلِ وَالأَوْسَطِ!» قَالَ: «وَفِي الآخِرِ!»
> *"Pledge allegiance, O Salamah!" Salamah said: "I already pledged allegiance with the first group, O Messenger of Allāh!" The Prophet said: "And in the middle group as well!" So he pledged. Then, when the people had finished pledging, the Prophet said: "Will you not pledge, O Salamah?" He replied: "I have pledged in the first and the middle!" The Prophet ﷺ said: "And in the final group as well!"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:127; *Subul al-Hudā*, 5:48).

Salamah uniquely attained the distinction of offering the covenant beneath the tree three times.

### Heroism at Ghazwat Dhī Qarad (Ghazwat al-Ghābah)
Shortly before or after Ḥudaybiyah, the raiders of Ghaṭafān led by ʿUyaynah b. Ḥiṣn ambushed the milch-camels of the Prophet ﷺ at al-Ghābah, killing the shepherd and carrying off the herd (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:337; *Subul al-Hudā*, 5:95). 

Salamah b. al-Akwaʿ set out alone on foot, overtaking the raiding party through sheer sprint. Scaling elevated basalt outcrops, he harassed them ceaselessly with arrows and slingshot rocks, shouting his celebrated war anthem:
> خُذْهَا وَأَنَا ابْنُ الأَكْوَعِ ... وَاليَوْمُ يَوْمُ الرُّضَّعِ!
> *"Take this! For I am Ibn al-Akwaʿ! And today is the day the base shall perish!"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:127; *Subul al-Hudā*, 5:96).

He held off the entire host of raiders on foot until the Madinan cavalry arrived, reclaiming every stolen camel and mount. When the battle concluded, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ declared before the army:
> «خَيْرُ فُرْسَانِنَا اليَوْمَ أَبُو قَتَادَةَ، وَخَيْرُ رَجَّالَتِنَا سَلَمَةُ»
> *"The best of our cavalrymen today is [[Abū Qatādah]], and the best of our infantrymen is Salamah!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:99).
The Prophet awarded Salamah two shares of the spoils—a horseman's share and a foot-soldier's share—and seated him behind himself on his own camel *al-ʿAḍbāʾ* upon the triumphal return to Medina (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:100).

### Subsequent Campaigns
Salamah participated in seven major campaigns directly in the presence of the Prophet ﷺ (including **[[Ghazwat Khaybar]]**, **[[Fatḥ Makkah]]**, and **[[Ghazwat Ḥunayn]]**), and nine expeditions under designated field commanders (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:497; 3:128). At Khaybar, his brother ʿĀmir b. al-Akwaʿ fell as a martyr when his sword rebounded during a duel, and the Prophet ﷺ explicitly affirmed ʿĀmir's martyrdom and double reward (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:127).

## 3. Later Years & Passing
Following the assassination of Caliph [[ʿUthmān b. ʿAffān]] ﵁, Salamah abhorred political discord and civil bloodshed. He retired from urban Medina and took up residence in the desert outpost of **al-Rabadhah**, where he lived as an ascetic, married, and raised his family (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:128). 

In his advanced age in **74 AH** (693 CE), he felt a longing to return to the city of the Prophet ﷺ. He arrived in Medina and passed away just two nights later at the age of eighty (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:128). He was buried in **[[Jannat al-Baqīʿ]]**.
