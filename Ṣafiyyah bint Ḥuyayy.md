# Ṣafiyyah bint Ḥuyayy (صفية بنت حيي) ﵂
*Umm al-Muʾminīn — Daughter of Chieftains and Noble Descendant of the Prophet Aaron*

## 1. Lineage & Nobility
Daughter of Ḥuyayy b. Akhṭab, supreme chieftain of Banū al-Naḍīr, and descendant of the Prophet Hārūn (Aaron) ﵊ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:132). Prior to Islam, she was wed to Sallām b. Mishkam, then to Kinānah b. al-Rabīʿ b. Abī al-Ḥuqayq, the treasurer of Khaybar.

## 2. Marriage to the Prophet ﷺ at Khaybar
Following the fall of the fortresses of **[[Khaybar]]** (7 AH), Ṣafiyyah was among the captives. Recognizing her noble lineage as the mistress of her people (*sayyidat qawmihā*), the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ offered her Islam and freedom: he emancipated her and made her manumission her bride-price (*ṣadāqahā ʿitquhā*), wedding her outside Khaybar at Sadd al-Ṣahbāʾ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:132).

On their journey, the Prophet ﷺ noticed a green bruise upon her eye and inquired of its cause; she related:
> *"I saw in a dream, while still in Yathrib, as though the moon had detached itself from the heavens and fallen into my lap. When I recounted this to my husband Kinānah, he struck my face, crying: 'You desire none other than the King of Yathrib, Muḥammad!'"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:132).

## 3. High Prophetic Regard
The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ honored her greatly. When some women of the household made remarks concerning her Jewish heritage, the Prophet ﷺ instructed her:
> «أَلَا قُلْتِ: كَيْفَ تَكُونِينَ خَيْرًا مِنِّي وَأَبِي هَارُونُ، وَعَمِّي مُوسَى، وَزَوْجِي مُحَمَّدٌ؟!»
> *"Why did you not say: 'How can you be better than me, when my father is Hārūn, my uncle is Mūsā, and my husband is Muḥammad?!'"*

She remained devoted, pious, and generous throughout her life, passing away in Medina and being laid to rest in al-Baqīʿ.
