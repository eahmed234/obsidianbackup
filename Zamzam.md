# Zamzam (زمزم)
*The Primordial Well of the Sacred Sanctuary, the Drink of Ismāʿīl, and the Legacy of ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib*

## 1. Sanctified Names & Miraculous Origin
**Zamzam** is the primordial sacred well situated in Makkah within **[[al-Masjid al-Ḥarām]]**, immediately adjacent to the Kaʿbah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:185, 1:188).

It carries numerous honored titles in classical tradition, including:
- *Hazmat Jibrīl* (The Excavation of Gabriel)
- *Suqyā Ismāʿīl* (The Water Provided for Ishmael)
- *Ṭaʿāmu Ṭuʿm* (Food That Sustains)
- *Shifāʾu Saqam* (Cure for Affliction)
- *Shabbāʿat al-ʿIyāl* (Satisfier of Households)
- *Barakah* and *Ṭāhirah* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:185).

The well first burst forth miraculously through the strike of the angel **[[Jibrīl]]** ﵊ beneath the infant Ismāʿīl ﵊ when his mother Hājar ran between **[[al-Ṣafā]]** and **[[al-Marwah]]** in search of water (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:187). Imām al-Suhaylī notes that Jibrīl excavated it with his heel rather than his hand to signify that it would remain an enduring inheritance for Ismāʿīl's progeny—the Prophet ﷺ and his community (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:187).

## 2. Concealment & Rediscovery by ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib
Following centuries of habitation by the tribe of Jurhum, iniquity and transgression spread, prompting the tribe to bury the well, conceal its location, and deposit the treasures of the Kaʿbah within it before their expulsion from Makkah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:187).

Its exact location remained forgotten until **[[ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib b. Hāshim]]**, the grandfather of the Prophet ﷺ, was chosen by divine guidance (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:187–188). In repeated nocturnal dreams beside the Kaʿbah, a celestial caller commanded him:
> «احْفِرْ زَمْزَمَ، لَا تَنْزِفُ وَلَا تُذَمُّ، تَسْقِي الْحَجِيجَ الْأَعْظَمَ!»
> *"Dig Zamzam! It never runs dry, nor is it blameworthy; it shall give drink to the greatest pilgrimage!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:188).

Directed by unmistakable omens—between dung and blood, at the pecking place of the raven with white wings, in the ant hill near the idols of Quraysh—ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib, accompanied by his eldest son al-Ḥārith, excavated the earth until the ancient masonry and water were revealed (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:188).

He instituted the sacred office of providing water to pilgrims (*Siqāyah*), an honor inherited by his lineage and maintained through **[[al-ʿAbbās b. ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib]]** ﵁ into the Islamic era (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:185, 1:188).
