# Daws (دوس)
*The High-Mountain Fortress Tribe of al-Azd, the Mission of Dhū al-Nūr, and the Vanguard of Khaybar*

## 1. Lineage & Highland Bastion
**Daws** are an illustrious branch of the Azdite tribal confederation (**[[al-Azd]]**) of southern Arabia (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:417, 6:336).

Their mountainous domain in the Sarāt range formed a formidable highland bastion, renowned for martial resilience, proud independence, and skilled horsemanship (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:417).

## 2. Key Sīrah Milestones

### The Conversion of al-Ṭufayl b. ʿAmr (Dhū al-Nūr)
During the Makkan period, the chieftain and nobleman of Daws, **[[al-Ṭufayl b. ʿAmr al-Dawsī]]** ﵁, arrived in Makkah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:417). 

The Quraysh surrounded him with intense propaganda, warning him that the Prophet's words were like sorcery separating father from son and husband from wife. Fearing influence, al-Ṭufayl stuffed his ears with raw cotton (*kursuf*) upon entering the Sacred Mosque (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:417). 

However, upon seeing the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ praying at the Kaʿbah, he resolved to listen with discernment. Hearing the divine speech, he recognized its unmatched truth, followed the Prophet ﷺ to his home, and embraced Islam (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:417). 

Requesting a sign to assist his preaching among his tribe, a divine radiance shone between his eyes, which was transferred to the tip of his whip like a suspended lantern, earning him the title *Dhū al-Nūr* (Possessor of the Light) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:417, 6:337).

### Prophetic Compassion: «اللَّهُمَّ اهْدِ دَوْسًا»
When al-Ṭufayl returned to his highlands, his father and wife accepted Islam, but the broader tribe resisted stubbornly (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:418). 

Returning to Makkah, al-Ṭufayl pleaded with the Prophet ﷺ to invoke divine punishment against Daws. Instead of cursing them, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ raised his hands to the heavens in compassionate intercession:
> «اللَّهُمَّ اهْدِ دَوْسًا وَائْتِ بِهِمْ، ارْجِعْ إِلَى قَوْمِكَ وَارْفُقْ بِهِمْ!»
> *"O Allāh, guide Daws and bring them! Return to your people and treat them with gentleness!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:418).

### The Migration to Khaybar (7 AH)
Al-Ṭufayl continued patient daʿwah throughout the years of the Hijrah, Badr, Uḥud, and the Trench (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:418).

In the year of **[[Ghazwat Khaybar]]** (7 AH), the prayer of the Prophet ﷺ bore historic fruit: al-Ṭufayl led an entire migration of seventy or eighty households of Daws into Medina and caught up with the Prophet ﷺ at the fortresses of Khaybar (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:418). 

Among this migrating party was the foremost preserver of Prophetic Hadīth, **[[Abū Hurayrah]]** ﵁ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:418). The Prophet ﷺ allotted them equal shares from the spoils of Khaybar alongside the participating army (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:418).

### Demolition of Dhū al-Kaffayn & Service in the Caliphate
Following **[[Fatḥ Makkah]]**, the Prophet ﷺ dispatched al-Ṭufayl b. ʿAmr with a detachment to demolish the pagan wooden idol of Daws, *Dhū al-Kaffayn*, eradicating polytheism from their homeland (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:210).

During the Caliphate of **[[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]]** ﵁, the warriors of Daws marched under the grand banner of Islam in **[[Ḥurūb al-Riddah]]**, where al-Ṭufayl achieved martyrdom at al-Yamāmah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:337).
