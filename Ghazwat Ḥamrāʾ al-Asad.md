# Ghazwat Ḥamrāʾ al-Asad (غزوة حمراء الأسد)
*The Pursuit of Moral Deterrence — The Resolute Stand after Uḥud*

## 1. Overview
At dawn on the morning following the trauma of [[Ghazwat Uḥud]], the Prophet ﷺ ordered an immediate pursuit of the retreating Meccan army to demonstrate psychological deterrence and prevent a counter-assault upon Medina. In a display of supreme discipline, he decreed that **only those wounded veterans who had fought at Uḥud the previous day** could participate.

Severely wounded companions—such as [[ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. ʿAwf]] with his paralyzed leg and [[Ṭalḥah b. ʿUbaydillāh]]—limped into formation (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:308).

## 2. Divine Praise
Encamping at Ḥamrāʾ al-Asad, the Muslims lit five hundred fires across the hills. Terror seized Abū Sufyān's army, causing them to accelerate their retreat to Makkah. Allāh commemorated this courage:
$$\text{الَّذِينَ اسْتَجَابُوا لِلَّهِ وَالرَّسُولِ مِن بَعْدِ مَا أَصَابَهُمُ الْقَرْحُ ۚ لِلَّذِينَ أَحْسَنُوا مِنْهُمْ وَاتَّقَوْا أَجْرٌ عَظِيمٌ}$$
> *"Those who responded to Allāh and the Messenger after injury had afflicted them—for those who did good among them and feared Allāh is a great reward."* (Qurʾān 3:172).
