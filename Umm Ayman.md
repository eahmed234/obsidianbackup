# Umm Ayman (أم أيمن)
*The Remnant of the Prophetic Household — The Blessed Nursemaid of the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ*

## 1. Heritage & Childhood Care of the Prophet ﷺ
Barakah bint Thaʿlabah al-Ḥabashiyyah, renowned by her kunya **Umm Ayman**, was an Abyssinian woman inherited by the Prophet ﷺ from his father **[[ʿAbdullāh b. ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib]]** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:378; 11:110). 

She nursed and tended the Prophet ﷺ throughout his infancy and childhood. Following the demise of his mother **[[Āminah bint Wahb]]** at al-Abwāʾ, it was Umm Ayman who brought the six-year-old orphaned child safely back to Makkah to his grandfather **[[ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib]]** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:378; 2:140). The Prophet ﷺ manumitted her upon his marriage to **[[Khadījah bint Khuwaylid]]** ﵂ and frequently honored her:
> «أُمُّ أَيْمَنَ أُمِّي بَعْدَ أُمِّي، وَهَذِهِ بَقِيَّةُ أَهْلِ بَيْتِي»
> *"Umm Ayman is my mother after my own mother, and she is the remnant of my household!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:110).

## 2. Key Sīrah Milestones
- **Marriages & Household**: First married ʿUbayd b. al-Ḥārith al-Khazrajī in Makkah, bearing him Ayman (martyred at Ḥunayn). Later, the Prophet ﷺ said: *"Whoever wishes to marry a woman from the people of Paradise, let him marry Umm Ayman"*; his adopted son **[[Zayd b. Ḥārithah]]** ﵁ married her, bearing the beloved commander **[[Usāmah b. Zayd]]** ﵁ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:110).
- **The Miraculous Thirst on the Hijrah**: Migrated on foot from Makkah to Medina during scorching midsummer heat without provisions; when she was on the verge of dying of dehydration at al-Munṣaraf, a white celestial bucket suspended from heaven descended before her face. She drank from it to repletion and never suffered from thirst again for the remainder of her life (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:245; 11:111).
- **Battlefield Service**: Attended **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]** and **[[Ghazwat Khaybar]]**, tending the wounded and carrying water to the warriors (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:205; 5:115).

## 3. Demise & Legacy
Following the death of the Prophet ﷺ, **[[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]]** ﵁ and **[[ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb]]** ﵁ visited her home regularly in filial devotion to honor her prophetic status (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:112). She died early in the caliphate of **[[ʿUthmān b. ʿAffān]]** ﵁ and was buried in **[[Jannat al-Baqīʿ]]** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:113).
