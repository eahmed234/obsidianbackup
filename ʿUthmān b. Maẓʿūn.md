# ʿUthmān b. Maẓʿūn (عثمان بن مظعون)
*The Thirteenth Convert — First of the Muhājirūn to Die in Madīnah*

## 1. Lineage and Early Islam
He is **ʿUthmān b. Maẓʿūn b. Ḥabīb b. Wahb b. Ḥudhāfah b. Jumaḥ al-Jumaḥī** (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:381, no.
5469). Ibn Isḥāq places him among the early converts, accepting Islam **after thirteen men** had
already done so (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:381). Subul al-Hudā's own register of the earliest converts names him
among this same company (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:305).

## 2. The First Hijrah to Abyssinia
He emigrated to Abyssinia in the **First Hijrah**, together with his son **al-Sāʾib**, among a company
of the earliest Muslims (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:381; *Subul al-Hudā*, 2:363). When word reached the emigrants
that Quraysh had accepted Islam, he returned to Makkah with the others.

## 3. Entering, Then Renouncing, al-Walīd's Protection
On his return, he entered under the protection (*jiwār*) of **al-Walīd b. al-Mughīrah** (*al-Iṣābah*,
4:381). Seeing his own companions suffer persecution while he himself moved freely under a polytheist's
protection, ʿUthmān judged this a diminishment of his own standing. He went to al-Walīd and said,
*"O Abū ʿAbd Shams, your pledge is fulfilled, and I return your protection to you."* Al-Walīd, puzzled,
asked whether one of his own people had wronged him; ʿUthmān answered, *"No — but I prefer the
protection of Allāh, Mighty and Glorious, and do not wish to seek protection from any other."* At his
request, they returned to the mosque so the release could be as public as the grant had been
(*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:367–368).

## 4. Struck for Defending the Qurʾān's Promise
Sitting afterward, unprotected, in a gathering where Quraysh's poet **Labīd b. Rabīʿah** was reciting
before his own conversion, ʿUthmān affirmed Labīd's line *"Indeed everything besides Allāh is
false,"* but when Labīd continued, *"and all bounty is inevitably fleeting,"* ʿUthmān corrected him:
*"You lie — the bounty of Paradise never fades."* A foolish man in the gathering rose and struck his
eye, bruising it green. Al-Walīd, witnessing this, remarked that ʿUthmān's eye had needed no such
protection while under his own; ʿUthmān answered that his other, still-sound eye was equally in need
of what had befallen its fellow, **for the sake of Allāh** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:367–368; *al-Iṣābah*,
4:381).

## 5. Refused Permission for Celibacy
Saʿd b. Abī Waqqāṣ ﵁ narrates that the Prophet ﷺ **refused ʿUthmān b. Maẓʿūn's request to embrace
lifelong celibacy (*al-tabattul*)** — Saʿd adding, *"had he permitted it, we would have taken to
castration [ourselves, to devote entirely to worship]."* A further report has ʿUthmān ask directly for
permission to be castrated, out of the hardship celibacy posed to him on campaigns; the Prophet ﷺ told
him instead, *"No — but fasting is upon you"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:381–382).

## 6. Badr and Death
He witnessed **Badr**. He died in **Madīnah in the second year of the Hijrah**, becoming **the first of
the Muhājirūn to die there**, and **the first of them to be buried at al-Baqīʿ**. **Umm al-ʿAlāʾ**
recalled that upon his death she said, *"My testimony is for you, O Abū al-Sāʾib — Allāh has surely
honoured you."* When the Prophet's ﷺ own son **Ibrāhīm** later died, he said: *"Join our righteous
predecessor, ʿUthmān b. Maẓʿūn."* ʿĀʾishah ﵂ narrates that the Prophet ﷺ kissed ʿUthmān as he lay dead,
weeping, his eyes streaming with tears (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:382).
