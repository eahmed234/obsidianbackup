# Sariyyat Saʿd b. Zayd al-Ashhalī ilā Manāt (سرية سعد بن زيد الأشهلي إلى مناة)
*The Expedition of Saʿd b. Zayd al-Ashhalī to Demolish Manāt — The Eradication of the Ancient Coastal Idol of al-Aws and al-Khazraj*

> [!NOTE] Scholarly Provenance & Primary Source
> This master entry is derived strictly from *Subul al-Hudā*, Volume 6, *al-Bāb al-Rābiʿ wal-Khamsūn fī Sariyyat Saʿd b. Zayd al-Ashhalī rḍy Allāh taʿālā ʿanhu ilā Manāt wa-huwa bil-Mushallal li-sitt baqīna min Ramaḍān sanata thamān fī Fatḥ Makkah* (p. 199), synthesized from [[Ibn Isḥāq]], [[al-Wāqidī]], and [[Ibn Saʿd]], cross-referenced with *al-Iṣābah*, 3:56.

---

## 1. Ancient Veneration of Manāt

Among the three chief false deities denounced by name in the Qurʾān (*Sūrat al-Najm* 53:19–20: «أَفَرَأَيْتُمُ اللَّاتَ وَالْعُزَّى * وَمَنَاةَ الثَّالِثَةَ الْأُخْرَى»), **Manāt** (*Manāt*) was historically the most ancient (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:199).

It stood at **al-Mushallal** (*al-Mushallal*), a prominent mountain ridge overlooking the Red Sea coast through which travelers descended toward Qudayd (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:199). Manāt was especially venerated as the patron deity of the Yemeni tribes of Yathrib—**[[al-Aws]]** and **[[al-Khazraj]]**—as well as the northern Arab kingdom of **Ghassān** (*Ghassān*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:199). In the pre-Islamic era, pilgrims from Yathrib would complete their pilgrimage rites at the Kaʿbah and then enter the sacral state (*iḥrām*) or perform their final deconsecration (*taḥallul*) only upon reaching the sanctuary of Manāt at al-Mushallal.

---

## 2. Dispatch of Saʿd b. Zayd al-Ashhalī ﵁

When the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ conquered Makkah (**[[Fatḥ Makkah]]**), he resolved to extirpate the roots of idolatry across the surrounding territories (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:199). With six nights remaining in the blessed month of Ramaḍān in the year eight AH, the Prophet ﷺ appointed the Anṣārī knight **[[Saʿd b. Zayd al-Ashhalī]]** ﵁ to lead twenty horsemen to al-Mushallal and demolish the sanctuary (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:199).

Upon reaching the shrine, Saʿd found the hereditary temple custodian (*sādin*) standing guard (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:199). 

The custodian asked:
> «مَا تُرِيدُ؟»
> *"What do you want?"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:199).

Saʿd replied firmly:
> «هَدْمَ مَنَاةَ!»
> *"The demolition of Manāt!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:199).

The custodian stepped aside, resigning the idol to its fate, saying:
> «أَنْتَ وَذَاكَ!»
> *"That is between you and it!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:199).

---

## 3. The Manifestation and Destruction

Saʿd b. Zayd ﵁ advanced on foot toward the idol (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:199). Suddenly, there rushed out toward him from the interior of the shrine a naked, jet-black woman with disheveled hair (*imraʾah ʿuryānah sawdāʾ thāʾirat al-raʾs*), screaming curses and lamentations of ruin (*tadʿū bil-wayl*) and furiously beating her chest (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:199).

Seeing this horrifying demonic manifestation, the custodian shouted:
> «مَنَاةُ! دُونَكِ بَعْضَ غَضَبَاتِكِ!»
> *"O Manāt! Unleash some of your furies upon him!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:199).

Undeterred by fear, Saʿd b. Zayd al-Ashhalī ﵁ struck the apparition down with his sword and slew her on the spot (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:199). 

Saʿd and his twenty companions then converged upon the stone idol of Manāt and smashed it to rubble (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:199). They inspected its treasury chamber (*khizānah*), found no wealth inside, and razed the structure to the ground (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:199). Saʿd and his men then rode back to Makkah and reported the complete extirpation of the shrine to the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:199).
