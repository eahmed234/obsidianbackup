# Zayd b. Thābit (زيد بن ثابت الأنصاري) ﵁
*The Chief Scribe of Revelation, Master of Inheritance, and Compiler of the Qurʾān*

## 1. Lineage & Early Life
He is Zayd b. Thābit b. al-Ḍaḥḥāk b. Zayd b. Lawdhān al-Najjārī al-Khazrajī, hailing from the maternal clan of the Prophet ﷺ, **[[Banū al-Najjār]]** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:27 n. 1 taḥqīq; 11:382). Orphaned when his father died at the pre-Islamic battle of Buʿāth, he was approximately eleven years old when the Prophet ﷺ arrived in Medina during **[[al-Hijrah ilā al-Madīnah]]**.

Possessing an extraordinary intellect and memorization capacity, he presented himself for military service at **[[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]]** and **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]**, but the Prophet ﷺ turned him back on account of his youth (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:185). He subsequently stood firm at **[[Ghazwat al-Khandaq]]** and all following expeditions.

## 2. Key Sīrah Milestones
- **Scribe of Divine Revelation (*Kātib al-Waḥy*)**: Serving as the primary scribe to whom the Prophet ﷺ dictated newly revealed Qurʾānic verses (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:382).
- **Mastery of Foreign Languages**: The Prophet ﷺ commanded him to master the writing of the Jews in Syriac and Hebrew to handle correspondence without breach of security; Zayd mastered it comprehensively within fifteen days (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:382).
- **Assessor of Spoils at Khaybar**: During **[[Ghazwat Khaybar]]** (7 AH), the Prophet ﷺ appointed Zayd alongside Jabbār b. Ṣakhr as the official census-taker and distributor (*ḥāsib qāsim*) of the spoils across the 1,800 shares (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:142–143).
- **Teacher and Muftī**: Counted among the handful of companions who had completely committed the Qurʾān to memory during the lifetime of the Prophet ﷺ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:330), and was among the authorized muftīs of Medina (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:328).

## 3. Demise & Legacy
Following the Prophet's ﷺ demise, **[[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]]** ﵁ and **[[ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb]]** ﵁ entrusted Zayd with the monumental task of compiling the Qurʾān into a single compendium following the battle of Yamāmah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:27 n. 1). Later under **[[ʿUthmān b. ʿAffān]]** ﵁, he headed the committee that transcribed the canonical standard codices (*Maṣāḥif*). He passed away in Medina around 45 AH (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:27 n. 1).
