# Zayd b. Thābit (زيد بن ثابت الأنصاري) ﵁
*Abū Saʿīd al-Khazrajī al-Najjārī — Chief Scribe of Divine Revelation, Master of Inheritance Law, and Compiler of the Holy Qurʾān*

## 1. Lineage, Family & Early Life
He is Zayd b. Thābit b. al-Ḍaḥḥāk b. Zayd b. Lawdhān b. ʿAmr b. ʿAwf b. Ghanam b. Mālik b. al-Najjār al-Anṣārī al-Khazrajī al-Najjārī (kunyas: Abū Saʿīd and Abū Thābit) (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:490). His mother was **al-Nawwār bint Mālik b. Muʿāwiyah b. ʿAdī** (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:491). Through Banū al-Najjār, he belonged to the maternal clan of the Prophet's grandfather **[[ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib]]**.

His father Thābit b. al-Ḍaḥḥāk was killed during the pre-Islamic battle of Buʿāth, five years prior to the Hijrah, leaving Zayd an orphan (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:491). When the Prophet ﷺ arrived in Medina during **[[al-Hijrah ilā al-Madīnah]]**, Zayd was brought to him as an exceptionally intelligent youth of approximately eleven years old (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:382).

Zayd presented himself for mobilization at **[[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]]** and **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]**, but the Prophet ﷺ turned him back due to his tender age (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:490; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:185). He subsequently participated in **[[Ghazwat al-Khandaq]]** and all following expeditions, carrying the military standard of Banū al-Najjār on the expedition to Tabūk (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:490).

## 2. Chief Scribe of Revelation & Linguistic Prodigy
- **Scribe of Divine Revelation (*Kātib al-Waḥy*)**: Zayd served as the primary scribe recording the verses of the Qurʾān as they descended upon the Prophet ﷺ in Medina (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:491; *Subul al-Hudā*, 11:382).
- **Mastery of Languages**: The Prophet ﷺ commanded Zayd to master the written script of the Jews to protect diplomatic correspondence from alteration or breach of trust; Zayd mastered Hebrew and Syriac in a mere fifteen days (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:491; *Subul al-Hudā*, 11:382).
- **Administrative Responsibilities**: During **[[Ghazwat Khaybar]]**, the Prophet ﷺ appointed Zayd alongside Jabbār b. Ṣakhr as official assessor and distributor of the spoils (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:142–143). Later during the Syrian conquests, Zayd oversaw the distribution of the spoils at the battle of Yarmūk (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:491).

## 3. Master of Inheritance & The Compilation of the Qurʾān
- **Master of Farāʾiḍ**: The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ praised his mastery of sacred law:
  > «أَفْرَضُكُمْ زَيْدٌ»
  > *"The most knowledgeable of you concerning inheritance laws is Zayd"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:492).
  He was counted among the six foremost muftīs of the Companions alongside ʿUmar, ʿAlī, Ibn Masʿūd, Ubayy, and Abū Mūsā (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:492; *Subul al-Hudā*, 11:328).
- **The First Compilation under Abū Bakr**: Following the martyrdom of scores of reciters at the battle of Yamāmah (12 AH), **[[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]]** ﵁ and **[[ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb]]** ﵁ commissioned Zayd to gather the scattered parchments and oral transmissions of the Qurʾān into a single bound codex (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:491). Abū Bakr said to him: *“You are an intelligent young man whom we do not suspect, and you used to record the revelation for the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ!”* (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:491).
- **The Standardized Codices under ʿUthmān**: During the Caliphate of **[[ʿUthmān b. ʿAffān]]** ﵁, Zayd presided over the supreme committee that transcribed the official standardized Qurʾānic codices (*Maṣāḥif*) distributed to the metropolitan centers of the Islamic world (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:491).

## 4. Statesmanship, Character & Demise
Whenever the Caliph **[[ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb]]** ﵁ departed Medina on journeys for Ḥajj or to Syria, he habitually appointed Zayd as his deputy governor over Medina, and frequently rewarded him with date-palm groves upon returning (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:492).

Thābit b. ʿUbayd remarked on his balanced temperament:
> *"I never saw a man more cheerful and pleasant within his household, nor more dignified and solemn in his public assembly, than Zayd b. Thābit!"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:492).

Zayd passed away in Medina around 45 AH (or 55 AH) (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:491). At his funeral, **[[ʿAbdullāh b. ʿAbbās]]** ﵄ took hold of the reins of Zayd's mount and proclaimed: *“Thus are we commanded to treat our scholars and our elders!”* (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:492).
