# Ghazwat Muʾtah (غزوة مؤتة)
*The Battle of Muʾtah — The Epic Clash against the Byzantine Empire*

## 1. The Provocation
Dispatched to avenge the murder of the Prophet's envoy al-Ḥārith b. ʿUmayr al-Azdī by the Ghassānid governor Shuraḥbīl b. ʿAmr, an army of 3,000 Muslims marched into southern Syria, where they encountered an imperial Byzantine army exceeding 100,000 troops.

## 2. Succession of Commanders
The Prophet ﷺ had established a strict command chain in Medina:
- **Zayd b. Ḥārithah** fought until pierced by Byzantine spears.
- **Jaʿfar b. Abī Ṭālib** seized the banner; when his right hand was severed, he gripped it with his left; when his left was severed, he clamped the standard to his chest with his upper arms until martyred with over ninety wounds. The Prophet ﷺ revealed in Medina: *"Allāh has replaced his two hands with two wings of emerald with which he flies through Paradise alongside the angels!"* ([`Dhū al-Janāḥayn`](file:///home/eahmed/Projects/obsidianbackup/Jaʿfar%20b.%20Abī%20Ṭālib.md)).
- **ʿAbdullāh b. Rawāḥah** took the standard and fought until martyred.

## 3. Khālid b. al-Walīd and Tactical Withdrawal
The soldiers unanimously elected **[[Khālid b. al-Walīd]]**. Displaying miraculous military maneuvering, Khālid broke nine swords in combat, reorganized the wings and vanguard overnight to simulate incoming massive reinforcements, panicked the Byzantines, and executed a masterclass tactical withdrawal without pursuit (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:230).
