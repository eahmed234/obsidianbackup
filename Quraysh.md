# Quraysh (قريش)
*The Sovereign Custodians of the Sacred Sanctuary and Noble Kin of the Prophet ﷺ*

## 1. Lineage & Origins
Quraysh is the elite ʿAdnānī Arab tribe descended from **al-Naḍr b. Kinānah** b. Khuzaymah b. Mudrikah b. Ilyās b. Muḍar (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:297). The term *Quraysh* stems either from *taqarrush* (gathering together in unity following dispersion, effected by their ancestor Quṣayy b. Kilāb) or from the word for investigating and trade (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:297–298).

The tribe was partitioned by Quṣayy b. Kilāb into two geographical branches:
1. **Quraysh al-Biṭāḥ**: Those dwelling in the hollow of the valley immediately encircling the Kaʿbah, comprising the aristocratic ruling clans: **[[Banū Hāshim]]**, **[[Banū al-Muṭṭalib]]**, Banū Umayyah, Banū Nawfal, Banū Asad b. ʿAbd al-ʿUzzā, Banū ʿAbd al-Dār, **[[Banū Zuhrah]]**, Banū Taym, Banū Makhzūm, Banū Jumaḥ, Banū Sahm, and Banū ʿAdī (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:298).
2. **Quraysh al-Ẓawāhir**: Those inhabiting the outer hills and perimeter of Mecca, including Banū Muḥārib and Banū al-Ḥārith b. Fihr (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:298).

## 2. Custodial Preeminence & Sacred Offices
Holding sacred hegemony over the Kaʿbah, Quraysh maintained the ancestral institutions of the pilgrimage:
- *al-Siqāyah* (provision of water from Zamzam) and *al-Rifādah* (feeding the pilgrims), held by Banū Hāshim (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:299).
- *al-Liwāʾ* (the banner of war) and *al-Ḥijābah* (custody of the keys of the Kaʿbah), held by Banū ʿAbd al-Dār (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:300; 4:183).
- *al-Nadwah* (the council house of leadership, **Dār al-Nadwah**) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:300).
- *al-Qiyādah* (command of the cavalry), held by Banū Umayyah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:300).

## 3. Role in the Sīrah
- **Persecution & Opposition**: Led the fierce decade-long resistance against the Prophet ﷺ in Mecca, subjecting the early Muslims to boycotts during **[[al-Ḥiṣār fī Shiʿb Abī Ṭālib]]** and plotting assassination prior to **[[al-Hijrah ilā al-Madīnah]]**.
- **Military Mobilizations**: Marched out in defense of their trade caravan at **[[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]]** (2 AH) suffering devastating loss of their patrician leaders; rallied 3,000 warriors at **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]** (3 AH); and forged the great 10,000-man coalition during **[[Ghazwat al-Khandaq]]** (5 AH) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:15, 182, 363).
- **The Reconciliation**: Concluded the ten-year truce at **[[Ṣulḥ al-Ḥudaybiyah]]** (6 AH). Following their breach of the pact, the Prophet ﷺ entered Mecca victoriously during **[[Fatḥ Makkah]]** (8 AH). Standing before the assembled leaders of Quraysh who awaited their fate, the Prophet ﷺ asked: *“O assembly of Quraysh, what do you think I shall do with you?”* They replied: *“Good! A noble brother, and son of a noble brother.”* He proclaimed:
  > «اذْهَبُوا فَأَنْتُمُ الطُّلَقَاءُ!»
  > *"Go, for you are the emancipated!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:244).
  The entire tribe embraced Islam, providing the foundational administrative and military leadership for the expansion of the early Caliphate.
