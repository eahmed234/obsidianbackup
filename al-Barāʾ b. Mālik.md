# al-Barāʾ b. Mālik (البراء بن مالك الأنصاري)
*The Fearless Champion of the Anṣār, Hero of the Garden of Death, and the Supplicant whose Oath Allāh Fulfilled*

> [!NOTE] Primary Sourcing & Biographical Provenance
> Sourced directly from *al-Iṣābah fī Tamyīz al-Ṣaḥābah*, 1:413–414 (entry 620).

---

## 1. Lineage & Kinship

al-Barāʾ was an intrepid Anṣārī hero belonging to the clan of Banū al-Najjār of the Khazraj (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:412–413).

- **Full Paternal Lineage**: al-Barāʾ b. Mālik b. al-Naḍr b. Ḍamḍam b. Zayd b. Ḥarām b. Jundub b. ʿĀmir b. Ghanm b. ʿAdī b. al-Najjār al-Anṣārī al-Khazrajī al-Najjārī (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:412).
- **Brotherhood**: He was the brother of the venerable servant of the Prophet ﷺ, **[[Anas b. Mālik]]** ﵁ (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:412).
- **Vocal Gifts & Chanting**: Possessing a resonant, melodious voice, al-Barāʾ chanted camel-driving poetry (*ḥudāʾ*) for the men during expeditions with the Prophet ﷺ (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:413).

---

## 2. Participation in Sīrah Campaigns

al-Barāʾ witnessed the military expeditions alongside the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ following the Battle of Badr, fighting steadfastly at **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]**, **[[Ghazwat al-Khandaq]]**, and subsequent campaigns (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:413). 

He was renowned for an unmatched courage in single combat; it is recorded that in his lifetime of jihad he personally vanquished one hundred warriors in single duel combat, beyond those he slew in collective skirmishes (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:413).

---

## 3. The Prophetic Distinction

The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ singled out al-Barāʾ for an extraordinary spiritual distinction, declaring:
> «رُبَّ أَشْعَثَ أَغْبَرَ لَا يُؤْبَهُ لَهُ، لَوْ أَقْسَمَ عَلَى اللَّهِ لَأَبَرَّهُ، مِنْهُمُ الْبَرَاءُ بْنُ مَالِكٍ»
> *"How many a disheveled, dust-covered soul whom people disregard, if he were to swear an oath upon Allāh, Allāh would surely fulfill it for him; among them is al-Barāʾ b. Mālik!"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:414).

---

## 4. The Garden of Death at al-Yamāmah (12 AH)

During the Wars of Apostasy (**[[Ḥurūb al-Riddah]]**), the Muslim army under [[Khālid b. al-Walīd]] faced the entrenched forces of the false prophet Musaylimah at the Battle of al-Yamāmah (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:413).

- **The Call to Arms**: Khālid commanded al-Barāʾ to rally the Muslims. Mounting his horse, al-Barāʾ proclaimed:
  > «يَا أَهْلَ الْمَدِينَةِ، لَا مَدِينَةَ لَكُمُ الْيَوْمَ، وَإِنَّمَا هُوَ اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ وَالْجَنَّةُ!»
  > *"O people of Medina! There is no Medina for you this day! It is only Allāh alone, and Paradise!"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:413).
- **Breaching the Fortress**: When the enemy fortified themselves within an impregnable enclosed orchard (*Ḥadīqat al-Mawt*), al-Barāʾ demanded:
  > «يَا مَعْشَرَ الْمُسْلِمِينَ، أَلْقُونِي إِلَيْهِمْ!»
  > *"O company of Muslims! Cast me over the wall unto them!"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:413).
- Cast over the parapet into the midst of the enemy, he fought furiously until he unbolted the massive fortress gates from within, sustaining over eighty wounds from arrows and sword strikes (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:413). His heroic sacrifice enabled the Muslims to breach the garden and defeat the apostates (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:413).

---

## 5. Heroism at Tustar & Martyrdom (20 AH)

During the conquest of the Persian fortress city of **Tustar** under the caliphate of ʿUmar, the Persian defenders suspended red-hot iron grappling hooks from chains along the ramparts (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:414).
- One such white-hot chain caught his brother [[Anas b. Mālik]] ﵁. al-Barāʾ scaled the wall and seized the glowing iron with his bare hands, desperately prying the links open until the chain severed, freeing his brother while the burning metal stripped the flesh down to his own bare bones (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:414).
- When the Muslim battle lines experienced a temporary setback, the companions implored: *"O Barāʾ! Swear an oath upon your Lord!"*
- al-Barāʾ beseeched:
  > «أَقْسَمْتُ عَلَيْكَ يَا رَبِّ لَمَّا مَنَحْتَنَا أَكْتَافَهُمْ، وَأَلْحَقْتَنِي بِنَبِيِّكَ ﷺ»
  > *"I swear an oath upon You, O my Lord, that You grant us their shoulders in defeat, and that You unite me with Your Prophet ﷺ!"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:414).
- He charged into the fray, slew the Persian chieftain Marzubān al-Zārah, and fell as a martyr, his oath fulfilled (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:414).
