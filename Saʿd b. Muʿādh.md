# Saʿd b. Muʿādh (سعد بن معاذ الأشهلي) ﵁
*Abū ʿAmr al-Ashhalī al-Awsī — Sayyid al-Aws (Chieftain of the Aws), The Martyr Whose Death Shook the Divine Throne*

## 1. Lineage & Conversion in Medina
He is Saʿd b. Muʿādh b. al-Nuʿmān b. Imriʾ al-Qays al-Ashhalī al-Awsī (kunya: Abū ʿAmr; title: *Sayyid al-Aws*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:192). In pre-Islamic Yathrib, he was the towering young hereditary chieftain of the Banū ʿAbd al-Ashhal clan and one of the supreme leaders of the Aws tribe.

When **[[Muṣʿab b. ʿUmayr]]** ﵁ began preaching in Medina, Saʿd initially dispatched **[[Usayd b. Ḥuḍayr]]** ﵁ to stop him (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:193). When Usayd returned transformed as a Muslim, Saʿd went himself with his lance. Hearing Muṣʿab recite the Qurʾān, faith entered his heart; he bathed, cleansed his garments, and bore witness to Islam (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:194).

Returning to his clan's assembly, he stood tall and asked: *“O Banū ʿAbd al-Ashhal, how do you regard my standing among you?”* They replied: *“Our chieftain, the best of us in judgment and the most noble in descent!”* Saʿd declared:
> *"Then verily, the speech of your men and women is forbidden to me until you believe in Allāh and His Messenger!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:194).
Before that evening arrived, not a single man or woman of Banū ʿAbd al-Ashhal remained except that they had embraced Islam, establishing the foundational stronghold of Islam in Medina (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:194–195).

## 2. Key Sīrah Milestones
- **The Immortal Oath Before Badr (2 AH)**: When the Prophet ﷺ consulted the army concerning engaging the Meccan army at **[[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]]**, Saʿd stepped forward as the spokesman of the Anṣār, delivering the defining address:
  > *"O Messenger of Allāh, we have believed in you, attested to your truth, and given you our solemn covenants! Lead us where you will. For by Him Who sent you with the Truth, if you were to lead us to the sea and plunge into it, we would plunge with you, and not a single man among us would stay behind!... We are patient in battle, truthful upon meeting the enemy; perhaps Allāh will show you from us that which will delight your eyes!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:36; *Ibn Hishām*, 1:615).
  The Prophet's face beamed with joy, and he ordered the advance. Saʿd fought heroically, guarding the Prophet's booth (**[[al-ʿArīsh]]**) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:38, 92).
- **Steadfastness at Uḥud (3 AH)**: Stood firm protecting the Prophet ﷺ during the polytheist breakthrough (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:205).
- **The Defiance at the Trench (5 AH)**: When the Prophet ﷺ contemplated offering one-third of Medina's date crop to Banū Ghaṭafān to lift the siege during **[[Ghazwat al-Khandaq]]**, Saʿd asked whether it was divine command or human strategy (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:373). Learning it was human strategy, Saʿd boldly replied: *“By Allāh, when we were polytheists they never tasted a single date from us except through purchase or hospitality; shall we give them our wealth now that Allāh has honored us with Islam?! By Allāh, we shall give them nothing except the sword!”* and tore up the draft treaty (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:373).
- While guarding the Trench, a polytheist archer, Ḥibbān b. al-ʿAriqah, struck Saʿd with an arrow that severed his medial arm vein (*al-akḥal*). The Prophet ﷺ pitched a special medical tent for him inside the Prophetic Mosque to nurse him closely (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:381). Saʿd prayed:
  > «اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّكَ تَعْلَمُ أَنَّهُ لَيْسَ أَحَدٌ أَحَبَّ إِلَيَّ أَنْ أُجَاهِدَهُمْ فِيكَ مِنْ قَوْمٍ كَذَّبُوا رَسُولَكَ وَأَخْرَجُوهُ ... فَإِنْ كَانَ بَقِيَ مِنْ حَرْبِ قُرَيْشٍ شَيْءٌ فَأَبْقِنِي لَهَا، وَإِنْ كُنْتَ وَضَعْتَ الْحَرْبَ بَيْنَنَا وَبَيْنَهُمْ فَافْجُرْهَا وَاجْعَلْ مَوْتِي فِيهَا، وَلَا تُمِتْنِي حَتَّى تُقِرَّ عَيْنِي مِنْ بَنِي قُرَيْظَةَ!»
  > *"O Allāh, You know that there is no people against whom I love to strive in Your cause more than a people who denied Your Messenger and drove him out! If any war remains against Quraysh, preserve my life for it; and if You have ended the war between us and them, let this wound burst forth and cause my death through it, but do not let me die until You delight my eyes concerning Banū Qurayẓah!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:381; *Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī*, no. 4122).
  His bleeding miraculously ceased immediately.

## 3. The Judgment of Banū Qurayẓah & The Shaking of the Throne
Following the siege of **[[Banū Qurayẓah]]**, the treacherous clan refused all arbiters except Saʿd b. Muʿādh, their pre-Islamic ally (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:396). Brought on a donkey with his arm bandaged, the Prophet ﷺ told the Anṣār: *“Stand up for your master!”* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:396).

Saʿd pronounced his divine verdict: that their fighting men be executed, their wealth divided, and their women and children taken as captives (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:397). The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ exclaimed with awe:
> «لَقَدْ حَكَمْتَ فِيهِمْ بِحُكْمِ الْمَلِكِ مِنْ فَوْقِ سَبْعِ سَمَاوَاتٍ!»
> *"You have judged concerning them with the judgment of the Sovereign King from above the seven heavens!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:398; *Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī*, no. 3804).

With his prayer fulfilled, Saʿd’s wound burst open in his tent inside the Mosque. He was martyred in Dhū al-Qaʿdah 5 AH at the age of thirty-seven (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:400).

When his soul departed, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ proclaimed:
> «اهْتَزَّ عَرْشُ الرَّحْمَنِ لِمَوْتِ سَعْدِ بْنِ مُعَاذٍ!»
> *"The Throne of the Most Merciful shook at the death of Saʿd b. Muʿādh!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:400; *Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī*, no. 3803; *Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim*, no. 2466).
When carrying his bier to **[[Jannat al-Baqīʿ]]**, the companions found the bier astonishingly light; the Prophet ﷺ explained: *“Seventy thousand angels descended who had never trodden the earth before today, carrying him with you!”* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:401).
