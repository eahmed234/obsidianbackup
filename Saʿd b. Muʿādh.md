# Saʿd b. Muʿādh (سعد بن معاذ)
*Abū ʿAmr al-Awsi — Chieftain of the Aws, The Man Whose Death Shook the Throne of Allāh*

## 1. Conversion & Impact
Converted at age thirty-one via **[[Muṣʿab b. ʿUmayr]]** in Medina. His declaration to his clan (*"Speaking to your men and women is unlawful for me until you believe in Allāh and His Messenger"*) caused the entire tribe of Banū ʿAbd al-Ashhal to embrace Islam in a single day.

## 2. Sīrah Milestones
- Delivered the covenant of loyalty on behalf of the Anṣār before **[[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]]**; built and guarded the command booth (**al-ʿArīsh**).
- Severely wounded in the arm at **[[Ghazwat al-Khandaq]]**.
- Arbitrated the siege of **[[Ghazwat Banī Qurayẓah]]**.
- Upon his death, seventy thousand angels descended who had never walked the earth before, and the Prophet ﷺ declared:
  > «اهْتَزَّ عَرْشُ الرَّحْمَنِ لِمَوْتِ سَعْدِ بْنِ مُعَاذٍ!»
  > *"The Throne of the Most Merciful shook at the death of Saʿd b. Muʿādh!"* (*Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī*, no. 3803).
