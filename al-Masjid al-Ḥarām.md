# al-Masjid al-Ḥarām (المسجد الحرام)
*The Inviolable Sanctuary of Allāh, The Mother of Sanctuaries, and the Perpetual Qiblah of the Believers*

## 1. Primacy and Foundation
Al-Masjid al-Ḥarām is the first house established on earth for divine worship (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:143). Al-Ṣāliḥī transmits from ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib ﵁ that while dwellings existed before it, the House was the first sanctuary established exclusively for the worship of Allāh (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:143), confirming the divine word:
> ﴿إِنَّ أَوَّلَ بَيْتٍ وُضِعَ لِلنَّاسِ لَلَّذِي بِبَكَّةَ مُبَارَكًا وَهُدًى لِّلْعَالَمِينَ﴾ (Āl ʿImrān 3:96).

Transmissions recorded by al-Ṣāliḥī relate that when the Throne was upon the water before the creation of the heavens and earth, a foam or crust appeared at the site of the House, from beneath which the earth was spread forth, making it the umbilical center of the earth (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:140–141). Adam ﵊ was guided to its location, where the divine tent and canopy were placed, around which he and the angels circumambulated (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:141–143). Generations later, Prophet Ibrāhīm ﵊ and his son Ismāʿīl ﵊ raised the foundational pillars of the Kaʿbah upon divine instruction (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:144–156).

## 2. Sanctity and the Boundaries of the Ḥaram (*Anṣāb al-Ḥaram*)
The sanctity of the sanctuary is eternal, established by divine decree since the creation of the universe (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:204). On the morning following **[[Fatḥ Makkah]]**, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ formally declared its inviolability:
> «إِنَّ اللَّهَ حَرَّمَ مَكَّةَ يَوْمَ خَلَقَ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضَ، فَهِيَ حَرَامٌ بِحُرْمَةِ اللَّهِ إِلَى يَوْمِ الْقِيَامَةِ، لَمْ تَحِلَّ لِأَحَدٍ قَبْلِي، وَإِنَّمَا أُحِلَّتْ لِي سَاعَةً مِنْ نَهَارٍ، وَقَدْ عَادَتْ حُرْمَتُهَا الْيَوْمَ كَحُرْمَتِهَا بِالْأَمْسِ»
> *"Indeed Allāh made Makkah sacred on the day He created the heavens and the earth, and it is sacred by the inviolability of Allāh until the Day of Resurrection. It was never made lawful for anyone before me, and it was made lawful for me only for an hour of a single day; today its sanctity has returned just as it was yesterday."* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:204).

Hunting its game, uprooting its trees, and shedding blood within its bounds are strictly forbidden (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:204). 

The territorial boundaries of the sanctuary (*Ḥudūd al-Ḥaram*) were originally marked by boundary stones (*anṣāb*) erected by Ibrāhīm ﵊ under the direct guidance of Jibrīl ﵊ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:203). These markers were preserved by Quraysh through Quṣayy b. Kilāb, and following the Conquest of Makkah, the Prophet ﷺ commissioned Tamīm b. Asad al-Khuzāʿī ﵁ to renew and restore every boundary stone across the perimeter (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:203). Al-Ṣāliḥī quotes Imām al-Nawawī and al-Azraqī detailing the radiating distances from the Kaʿbah to the perimeter markers (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:201):
- **North (towards Madīnah)**: At Tanʿīm (near the dwellings of Nifār), at three miles (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:201).
- **South (towards Yemen)**: At Aḍāt Libn, at seven miles (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:201).
- **East (towards Iraq)**: At the pass of the mountain at al-Muqaṭṭaʿ, at seven miles (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:201).
- **North-East (towards al-Jiʿrānah)**: In the defile of the family of ʿAbdullāh b. Khālid, at nine miles (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:201).
- **East (towards al-Ṭāʾif)**: Bordering ʿArafāt at Baṭn Namirah, at seven miles (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:201).
- **West (towards Jeddah)**: At Munqaṭaʿ al-Aʿshāsh, at ten miles (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:201).

## 3. Essential Sanctuaries within al-Masjid al-Ḥarām
Al-Masjid al-Ḥarām encompasses supreme sacred monuments:
- **[[al-Kaʿbah al-Musharrafah]]**: The cubic sanctuary (*al-Bayt al-ʿAtīq*), named for its square form (*tarbīʿ*) and its divine emancipation from tyrants (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:169–170). Prayer inside it grants divine mercy, security, and the forgiveness of sins (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:171–172).
- **Al-Ḥajar al-Aswad**: The Black Stone, a celestial jewel brought from Paradise by Jibrīl ﵊ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:175, 202). The Prophet ﷺ testified: *«الرُّكْنُ وَالْمَقَامُ يَاقُوتَتَانِ مِنْ يَوَاقِيتِ الْجَنَّةِ طَمَسَ اللَّهُ تَعَالَى نُورَهُمَا، وَلَوْلَا ذَلِكَ لَأَضَاءَتَا مَا بَيْنَ الْمَشْرِقِ وَالْمَغْرِبِ»* (*"The Corner and the Station are two sapphires from the sapphires of Paradise whose light Allāh concealed; had He not done so, they would have illuminated all between the east and the west"*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:175).
- **Maqām Ibrāhīm**: The standing stone upon which Prophet Ibrāhīm ﵊ stood while raising the upper courses of the Kaʿbah, designated in the Qurʾān as a station of prayer: ﴿وَاتَّخِذُوا مِن مَّقَامِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ مُصَلًّى﴾ (al-Baqarah 2:125; *Subul al-Hudā*, 1:175).
- **Al-Ḥijr (Ḥijr Ismāʿīl)**: The semi-circular enclosure adjacent to the northern wall of the Kaʿbah, enclosing part of the original foundations laid by Ibrāhīm ﵊ and the burial place of Hājar and Ismāʿīl ﵊ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:167).
- **Biʾr Zamzam**: The miraculous spring gushing beneath the feet of infant Ismāʿīl ﵊ in the barren wilderness, rediscovered by the Prophet's grandfather **[[ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib]]** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:181–190).

## 4. Milestone Sīrah Events at the Sanctuary
Throughout the Prophetic mission, al-Masjid al-Ḥarām remained the central axis of divine manifestation:
1. **The Reconstruction by Quraysh**: During **[[Tajdīd al-Kaʿbah]]**, the young Prophet ﷺ placed the Black Stone into position using his mantle, ending clan strife (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:162–169).
2. **The Nocturnal Journey**: Commencing from the sanctuary enclosure, wherein the Prophet ﷺ was borne by al-Burāq to Bayt al-Maqdis during **[[al-Isrāʾ wal-Miʿrāj]]** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:61–125).
3. **The Direction of Prayer**: The turning of the believers' faces towards the Kaʿbah following sixteen to seventeen months of praying towards Jerusalem during **[[Taḥwīl al-Qiblah]]** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:338; 1:170).
4. **Purification at the Conquest**: In Ramaḍān 8 AH during **[[Fatḥ Makkah]]**, the Prophet ﷺ shattered the 360 idols surrounding the House, purified the sacred courtyard, and offered prayer within the Kaʿbah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:172; 5:240–245).
5. **The Farewell Pilgrimage**: In 10 AH during **[[Ḥajjat al-Wadāʿ]]**, the Prophet ﷺ performed the circumambulation of arrival (*ṭawāf al-qudūm*) and the farewell circumambulation (*ṭawāf al-wadāʿ*), establishing the final rites of the pilgrimage (*Subul al-Hudā*, 8:462–480).
