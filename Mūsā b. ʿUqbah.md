# Mūsā b. ʿUqbah (موسى بن عقبة)
*Author of the Earliest and Most Highly Graded Maghāzī*

## 1. Identity
**Mūsā b. ʿUqbah al-Asadī** (d. 141 AH), a Medinan client of the family of al-Zubayr and a student of
**al-Zuhrī**, from whom he transmitted the campaign material of his ***Maghāzī*** — the earliest such
work of which substantial traces survive, and earlier than [[Ibn Isḥāq]].

## 2. Standing
His *Maghāzī* enjoys unusually high regard among the critics: Mālik b. Anas is reported to have
directed students to it as the soundest of the campaign books, and its transmission through al-Zuhrī
gives it a stronger chain than most of the genre.

Al-Ṣāliḥī cites him precisely where a report's grading matters — for instance in establishing that a
vision preceded the waking encounter with the angel, *"and this comes stated explicitly in the
Maghāzī of Mūsā b. ʿUqbah, from al-Zuhrī"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, Muqaddimah p. 16) — a question bearing
directly on **[[Mabʿath al-Nabī]]**.

He is named among al-Ṣāliḥī's principal authorities in the provenance note of
**[[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]]**, and his testimony is cited in
**[[al-Ḥubāb b. al-Mundhir]]**.
