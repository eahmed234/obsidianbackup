# Mūsā b. ʿUqbah (موسى بن عقبة)
*Author of the Maghāzī, Transmitting from al-Zuhrī*

> [!WARNING] The Book Supplies No Biography
> *[[Subul al-Hudā war-Rashād]]* cites his *Maghāzī* but **gives him no biographical entry** in
> either the matn or the apparatus. His dates, city and standing among the critics are therefore
> **not stated here.**

## 1. What the Book Attests
His ***Maghāzī*** is cited as a source, and specifically as transmitting **from al-Zuhrī**. The
clearest instance bears on **[[Mabʿath al-Nabī]]** — whether a vision preceded the waking encounter
with the angel:

> *"and this comes stated explicitly in the Maghāzī of Mūsā b. ʿUqbah, from al-Zuhrī: that he saw
> that in a dream, and then the angel came to him while awake."*
> (*Subul al-Hudā*, Muqaddimah p. 16)

He is also cited in the apparatus for Companion biographies, where his account is set beside
[[Ibn Isḥāq]]'s — as on a Companion martyred at Ajnādayn, and on one who *"lived on until he was
martyred at the Jordan on the day of Fiḥl, at the beginning of the caliphate of ʿUmar, in the year
thirteen"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:399–400 nn., taḥqīq, citing *al-Iṣābah*).

He is named among the authorities in the provenance note of **[[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]]**, and cited
in **[[al-Ḥubāb b. al-Mundhir]]**.
