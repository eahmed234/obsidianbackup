# Tajdīd al-Kaʿbah (تجديد الكعبة ووضع الحجر الأسود)
*The Reconstruction of the Kaʿbah and the Setting of the Black Stone*

## 1. Overview
When the Prophet [[Muḥammad b. ʻAbdullah|Muḥammad]] ﷺ was thirty-five years of age, severe flash floods damaged the structural walls of the Kaʿbah. Quraysh resolved to rebuild it, strictly funding the construction solely through lawful (*ḥalāl*) wealth, excluding usury (*ribā*), prostitution, or ill-gotten gains.

When the masonry reached the placement of the sacred Black Stone (*al-Ḥajar al-Aswad*), bitter rivalries erupted among the chieftains, bringing them to the brink of civil war (*ḥalaf al-laʿaqah*). The elder Abū Umayyah b. al-Mughīrah al-Makhzūmī proposed that the next person to enter through the Gate of Banū Shaybah should arbitrate. The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ was the first to enter; upon seeing him, the chieftains rejoiced:
> «هَذَا الْأَمِينُ، رَضِينَا، هَذَا مُحَمَّدٌ!»
> *"This is al-Amīn (The Trustworthy)! We are well-pleased; this is Muḥammad!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:173).

## 2. The Prophetic Solomonic Wisdom
The Prophet ﷺ laid his cloak upon the earth, lifted the Black Stone upon it, and instructed the chieftain of each major tribal federation to grip an edge of the fabric. They raised it collectively; when it reached its designated corner, the Prophet ﷺ set the Stone into the masonry with his own blessed hands, resolving the feud peacefully.
