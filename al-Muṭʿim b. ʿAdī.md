# al-Muṭʿim b. ʿAdī (المطعم بن عدي)
*A Qurashī Protector Who Never Embraced Islam*

> [!NOTE] Where the Book Is Silent
> Al-Muṭʿim b. ʿAdī died a polytheist and is not treated as a Companion — he has no entry in
> *al-Iṣābah fī Tamyīz al-Ṣaḥābah*, which is devoted to the Ṣaḥābah specifically. This note therefore
> draws solely on *Subul al-Hudā*, and gives only the two episodes in which he plays a documented role.

## 1. One of the Five Who Annulled the Boycott Document
When Quraysh's unjust document confining **[[Banū Hāshim]]** and Banū al-Muṭṭalib to the Shiʿb had
stood for three years, **Hishām b. ʿAmr** recruited al-Muṭʿim as the third of five men who worked to
undo it, rebuking him: *"O Muṭʿim, are you content that two branches of Banū ʿAbd Manāf should perish
while you stand as a witness to it, in agreement with Quraysh? By Allāh, if you let them succeed
against this, you will find them quick to turn it against you next."* Al-Muṭʿim answered, *"Woe to
you — what can I do? I am only one man"* — and, told a second man had already agreed, asked Hishām to
find a third. Together with Hishām, **Zuhayr b. Abī Umayyah**, **Abū al-Bakhtarī**, and **Zamʿah b.
al-Aswad**, he met by night at Khaṭm al-Ḥajūn and pledged to see the document torn up (*Subul al-Hudā*,
2:413).

The next morning, after Zuhayr and the others spoke against the document before the assembled
Quraysh — over Abū Jahl's objection — **al-Muṭʿim himself went to the document to tear it**, and found
that a termite had already consumed it, sparing only the words *"Bismika Allāhumma"* (*Subul al-Hudā*,
2:414). See **[[al-Ḥiṣār fī Shiʿb Abī Ṭālib]]** for the fuller episode.

## 2. Protection After Riḥlat al-Ṭāʾif
When the Prophet ﷺ returned to Makkah after Thaqīf's rejection at al-Ṭāʾif, with Abū Ṭālib no longer
alive to shield him, al-Muṭʿim b. ʿAdī agreed to grant him protection (*jiwār*) to re-enter the city.
He came out armed alongside six or seven of his own sons and stood beside the Prophet ﷺ, their
sword-belts slung, through the whole of his circuit of the Kaʿbah. When **Abū Sufyān** asked whether he
was granting protection or had himself followed the Prophet's ﷺ religion, al-Muṭʿim answered, *"Rather,
protection,"* and Abū Sufyān conceded the pledge would be honoured (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:441). See
**[[Riḥlat al-Ṭāʾif]]** for the fuller episode.

## 3. Remembered After His Death
Al-Muṭʿim died before **Badr**. When the captives of that battle were brought before the Prophet ﷺ, he
said, recalling this history: *"Had al-Muṭʿim b. ʿAdī been alive and interceded with me for these vile
men, I would have released them for his sake"* — the book noting explicitly that this was **because he
was among those who had stood to annul the unjust document** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:441; 4:54).
