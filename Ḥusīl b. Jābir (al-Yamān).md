# Ḥusīl b. Jābir (حسيل بن جابر) — al-Yamān ﵁
*Father of Ḥudhayfah b. al-Yamān — Confederate of Banū ʿAbd al-Ashhal and Martyr of Uḥud*

## 1. Lineage, Origins & Confederacy (Ḥilf)
- **Ancestry**: Ḥusīl (or Ḥusayl) b. Jābir b. Rabīʿah b. Farwah b. al-Ḥārith b. Māzin b. Qaṭīʿah b. ʿAbs al-ʿAbsī al-Yamānī (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:66, no. 1725). Known universally as **al-Yamān**.
- **Reason for the Epithet al-Yamān**: In pre-Islamic times, he committed a blood transgression (*aṣāba daman*) among his tribe Banū ʿAbs in Najd and fled to Yathrib (Medina). There, he entered into a mutual protection pact (*ḥilf*) with the chieftain ʿAbd al-Ashhal b. Jusham of al-Aws. His northern kinsmen referred to him as *al-Yamān* because he had allied with the Southern/Yemenite Aws tribe (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:66).
- **Progeny**: Father of the keeper of the Prophet's secrets, **[[Ḥudhayfah b. al-Yamān]]** ﵁, and his brothers Ṣafwān and Saʿd (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:66).

## 2. Ghazwat Badr & The Pledge to Quraysh (2 AH)
- Prior to **[[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]]**, Ḥusīl and his son Ḥudhayfah departed Medina and were intercepted on the highway by a cavalry patrol of Quraysh led by Abū Jahl.
- The pagans demanded: *«You intend to join Muḥammad!»* Father and son replied: *«We only intend to reach Medina.»* The Quraysh extracted a solemn covenant from them before Allāh not to fight alongside the Prophet ﷺ at Badr (*Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim*; *al-Iṣābah*, 2:66).
- When they reached the Prophet ﷺ at Badr and recounted the incident, he instructed them strictly: *«Depart to Medina! We shall fulfill their pledge, and we seek the aid of Allāh against them!»* (*Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim*; *al-Iṣābah*, 2:66).

## 3. Martyrdom at Ghazwat Uḥud (3 AH)
- At **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]**, Ḥusīl and his close companion **Thābit b. Waqsh** ﵁ were elderly men left behind with the women and children inside the defensive fortresses (*al-āṭām*) of Medina (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:66).
- As the sounds of battle escalated, one said to the other: *«What are we waiting for? By Allāh, neither of us has much life remaining! Shall we not take our swords and join the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ, so that Allāh may grant us martyrdom?»* (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:66; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:211).
- They took their swords and entered the battle without distinct insignia. Thābit b. Waqsh was killed by the polytheists.
- In the chaotic retreat when Iblīs cried out *«O servants of Allāh, beware your rear!»* (*ay ʿibād Allāh ukhrākum!*), the Muslim front collided with their own rear ranks. Muslim warriors surrounded Ḥusīl believing him to be an enemy warrior (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:66; *Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī*).
- Ḥudhayfah saw his father and shouted in horror: *«O servants of Allāh! My father! My father!»* (*ay ʿibād Allāh! Abī! Abī!*). But amidst the shouting and dust, swords fell upon him before they realized, slaying him (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:66).
- Ḥudhayfah looked upon his fallen father and said with sublime forbearance: *«May Allāh forgive you, and He is the Most Merciful of the merciful»* (*Ghafara Allāhu lakum wa huwa arḥam al-rāḥimīn*).
- ʿUrwah b. al-Zubayr commented: *«By Allāh, there remained an abundance of good in Ḥudhayfah on account of that patience until he met Allāh»* (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:66).
- The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ intended to pay full blood-money (*diyah*) for Ḥusīl from the public treasury, but Ḥudhayfah waived the entire payment, donating it as charity to the Muslims (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:66; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:211).
