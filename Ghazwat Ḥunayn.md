# Ghazwat Ḥunayn (غزوة حنين)
*The Battle of Ḥunayn — The Lesson against Pride in Numbers*

## 1. Overview
Directly following the conquest of Makkah, the proud confederation of **Hawāzin** and **Thaqīf** assembled in vast numbers. The Muslims, numbering 12,000 strong for the first time, felt overconfident: *"We shall never be defeated today on account of small numbers!"*

In the narrow gorge of Ḥunayn, Hawāzin ambushed them with a deadly volley of arrows at dawn, scattering the forward ranks in panic (Qurʾān 9:25).

## 2. Steadfastness of the Prophet ﷺ
While the lines dissolved, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ charged forward alone upon his white mule *Duldul* toward the enemy spears, proclaiming with thunderous courage:
> «أَنَا النَّبِيُّ لَا كَذِبْ ... أَنَا ابْنُ عَبْدِ الْمُطَّلِبْ!»
> *"I am the Prophet without falsehood! I am the son of ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib!"* (*Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī*, no. 4315).

He ordered **[[al-ʿAbbās b. ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib]]** (who possessed a booming voice) to cry out: *"O companions of the Acacia Tree (Aṣḥāb al-Samurah)! O people of Surah al-Baqarah!"* The Companions pivoted mid-flight, shouting *«لَبَّيْكَ! لَبَّيْكَ!»* and turned a catastrophic ambush into overwhelming victory (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:365).
