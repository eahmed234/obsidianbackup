# Usāmah b. Zayd (أسامة بن زيد) ﵁
*Ḥibb Rasūlillāh wa-Ibn Ḥibbih — The Beloved Son of the Beloved, Commander of the Syrian Army*

## 1. Lineage & Prophetic Household Upbringing
He is Usāmah b. Zayd b. Ḥārithah b. Sharāḥīl b. ʿAbd al-ʿUzzā b. Zayd b. Imriʾ al-Qays b. ʿĀmir b. al-Nuʿmān al-Kalbī (kunya: Abū Muḥammad, and also Abū Zayd) (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:202, no. 89). See **[[Banū Kalb]]**.

His father was **[[Zayd b. Ḥārithah]]** ﵁, the adopted son and beloved freedman of the Prophet ﷺ (*Ḥibb Rasūlillāh*). His mother was **[[Umm Ayman]] (Barakah)** ﵂, the devoted Ethiopian nursemaid and freedwoman of the Prophet ﷺ (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:202; *Subul al-Hudā*, 11:406). Born into Islam in Mecca around seven years before the Hijrah, Usāmah was reared directly within the household of the Prophet ﷺ (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:202). 

The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ held immense affection for him, seating Usāmah on one thigh and his grandson **[[al-Ḥasan b. ʿAlī]]** on the other, supplicating:
> «اللَّهُمَّ أَحِبَّهُمَا فَإِنِّي أُحِبُّهُمَا»
> *"O Allāh, love them both, for indeed I love them both!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:114; *Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī*).

He was known across the Muslim community by the honorary title ***Ḥibb Rasūlillāh wa-Ibn Ḥibbih*** (*"The Beloved of the Messenger of Allāh and the Son of His Beloved"*) (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:202).

## 2. Early Sīrah Milestones
- **Inspection at Uḥud & Khandaq**: Presented himself eagerly for military service at **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]** (3 AH) alongside **[[ʿAbdullāh b. ʿUmar]]** ﵁, but the Prophet ﷺ turned him back due to his youthful age (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:185). When he reached the age of fifteen, the Prophet ﷺ permitted him to bear arms at **[[Ghazwat al-Khandaq]]** (5 AH).
- **Steadfastness at Ḥunayn (8 AH)**: When the Muslim vanguard was ambushed in the narrow pass of **[[Ghazwat Ḥunayn]]** and many scattered, Usāmah was among the few resolute believers who stood immovable beside the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ, shielding the Prophet ﷺ with his body against waves of Hawāzin archers (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:328).
- **Riding with the Prophet ﷺ at the Conquest of Mecca (8 AH)**: During **[[Fatḥ Makkah]]**, the Prophet ﷺ honored Usāmah by having him ride behind him on his camel al-Qaṣwāʾ as they made their triumphant entry into Mecca (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:208; 11:115). Furthermore, when the Prophet ﷺ entered the interior of the Kaʿbah, only Usāmah, **[[Bilāl b. Rabāḥ]]** ﵁, and ʿUthmān b. Ṭalḥah entered alongside him (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:240; 11:115).

## 3. The Expedition of Usāmah (*Jaysh Usāmah*, 11 AH)
In late Ṣafar 11 AH, during the Prophet's final days, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ appointed Usāmah supreme commander of a massive expedition to avenge the martyrs of **[[Ghazwat Muʾtah]]** and confront the Byzantine Empire at Ubnā in the region of al-Balqāʾ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:248–254; *al-Iṣābah*, 1:202). The expedition included the most senior Muhājirūn and Anṣār, including **[[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]]** ﵁ and **[[ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb]]** ﵁ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:248).

When certain individuals murmured regarding the appointment of a youth of eighteen or twenty years over the senior Companions, the Prophet ﷺ mounted the minbar with a bound head and delivered a powerful sermon (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:202; 2:497; *Subul al-Hudā*, 6:249):
> «إِنْ تَطْعَنُوا فِي إِمَارَتِهِ فَقَدْ كُنْتُمْ تَطْعَنُونَ فِي إِمَارَةِ أَبِيهِ مِنْ قَبْلِهِ، وَايْمُ اللَّهِ إِنْ كَانَ لَخَلِيقًا لِلْإِمَارَةِ، وَإِنْ كَانَ لَمِنْ أَحَبِّ النَّاسِ إِلَيَّ، وَإِنَّ هَذَا لَمِنْ أَحَبِّ النَّاسِ إِلَيَّ بَعْدَهُ!»
> *"If you criticize his leadership, you have already criticized the leadership of his father before him! By Allāh, his father was truly worthy of command, and he was among the most beloved of people to me; and truly this one is among the most beloved of people to me after him!"*

The Prophet ﷺ fell gravely ill shortly after tying the military banner for Usāmah. When the Prophet ﷺ passed away, Caliph **[[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]]** ﵁ firmly refused all counsel to delay or recall the army despite the eruption of the Riddah (apostasy), declaring: *"By Him besides Whom there is no deity, even if wild beasts were to snatch me, I would not untie a banner tied by the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:249; *al-Iṣābah*, 1:202). Usāmah marched swiftly to Syria, raided Ubnā, decisively defeated the hostile forces, secured the northern borders, and returned to Medina with rich spoils without losing a single soldier (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:250–252).

## 4. Later Life, Caliphal Reverence & Demise
During his Caliphate, **[[ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb]]** ﵁ revered Usāmah and fixed his annual stipend from the public treasury (*dīwān*) at five thousand dirhams, while assigning four thousand dirhams to his own son **[[ʿAbdullāh b. ʿUmar]]** ﵁ (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:202; 2:497). When ʿAbdullāh asked his father about this disparity, ʿUmar explained: *"The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ loved Usāmah more than he loved you, and his father was more beloved to the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ than your father!"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:497).

Following the assassination of **[[ʿUthmān b. ʿAffān]]** ﵁, Usāmah maintained strict political neutrality, resolutely abstaining from participating in internal civil conflicts (*iʿtazala al-fitan*) (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:202). He resided for a period at al-Mizzah near Damascus, then moved to Wādī al-Qurā, and finally returned to Medina (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:203).

He passed away in 54 AH at al-Jurf outside Medina at the age of approximately sixty, during the late caliphate of Muʿāwiyah (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:203). His narrations from the Prophet ﷺ are preserved across the canonical Hadith collections, related by Companions including Abū Hurayrah and Ibn ʿAbbās, and leading Tābiʿūn such as Abū ʿUthmān al-Nahdī and Abū Wāʾil (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:203).
