# Jabal Uḥud (جبل أحد)
*The Mountain of Love and Martyrdom*

## 1. Prophetic Affinity & Sacred Status
A massive reddish-granite mountain standing approximately five kilometers north of **[[al-Madīnah al-Munawwarah]]**. The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ held an intimate spiritual love for the mountain; upon returning from **[[Ghazwat Tabūk]]** and looking upon Uḥud, he proclaimed to his companions:
> «هَذَا أُحُدٌ، جَبَلٌ يُحِبُّنَا وَنُحِبُّهُ»
> *"This is Uḥud, a mountain that loves us and we love it!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:469).

## 2. Topography & Battlefield Alignment at Ghazwat Uḥud
Mount Uḥud formed the defensive anchor during **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]** (7 Shawwāl 3 AH). In deploying the Muslim army against the three-thousand-strong army of Quraysh, the Prophet ﷺ positioned his troops with their backs securely to Mount Uḥud, facing toward Medina (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:189).

To protect the vulnerable rear and western flank of the army, the Prophet ﷺ stationed fifty archers under **[[ʿAbdullāh b. Jubayr]]** ﵁ atop the adjacent outcrop of Mount ʿAynayn (**[[Jabal al-Rumāh]]**), strictly enjoining them:
> «انْضَحُوا الْخَيْلَ عَنَّا، لَا يَأْتُونَا مِنْ وَرَائِنَا، إِنْ كَانَتْ لَنَا أَوْ عَلَيْنَا فَاثْبُتُوا مَكَانَكُمْ لَا نُؤْتَيَنَّ مِنْ قِبَلِكُمْ...»
> *"Fend off the cavalry from us with your arrows, lest they come upon us from our rear. Whether the day is for us or against us, remain firm in your station that we are not attacked from your flank..."* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:190).

When the majority of archers deserted their post in pursuit of camp spoils, the Meccan cavalry under **[[Khālid b. al-Walīd]]** wheeled around the ridge, martyred ʿAbdullāh b. Jubayr and his steadfast companions, and enveloped the Muslim rear (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:195).

## 3. The Ravine, The Rock, and The Sanctuary of Martyrs
During the crisis of the reversal, the Prophet ﷺ was encircled, wounded, and led up into the rugged ravine (**al-Shiʿb**) of Mount Uḥud:
- **The Ascent upon the Rock**: When the Prophet ﷺ sought to ascend an overlooking boulder in the ravine but was weighed down by his two coats of chainmail and fatigue, **[[Ṭalḥah b. ʿUbaydillāh]]** ﵁ knelt down and lifted him onto his back. The Prophet ﷺ declared: «أَوْجَبَ طَلْحَةُ» (*"Ṭalḥah has made Paradise obligatory for himself!"*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:210).
- **Al-Mihrās (المهراس)**: **[[ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib]]** ﵁ brought water from the natural mountain cistern of al-Mihrās in his shield to quench the Prophet's thirst and wash the blood from his face and beard (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:210).
- **Maqbarat al-Shuhadāʾ**: At the foot of the mountain lie the seventy noble martyrs of the battle, foremost among them the Master of Martyrs **[[Ḥamzah b. ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib]]** ﵁ and the standard-bearer **[[Muṣʿab b. ʿUmayr]]** ﵁ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:216–225).
