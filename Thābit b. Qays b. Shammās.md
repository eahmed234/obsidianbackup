# Thābit b. Qays b. Shammās (ثابت بن قيس بن شماس)
*The Orator of the Prophet ﷺ and the Anṣār, Promised Paradise, and Hero of al-Yamāmah*

## 1. Lineage & Early Islam
**Thābit b. Qays b. Shammās** ﵁ belongs to the clan of Banū al-Ḥārith b. al-Khazraj. His genealogical lineage ascends through Thābit b. Qays b. Shammās b. Zuhayr b. Mālik b. Imriʾ al-Qays b. Mālik b. Thaʿlabah b. Kaʿb b. **[[al-Khazraj]]** al-Anṣārī al-Khazrajī (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:511).

Renowned for his powerful voice and eloquence, he was designated as *Khaṭīb al-Anṣār* (the Orator of the Anṣār) and *Khaṭīb Rasūlillāh* (the Orator of the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ) (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:511).

Upon the arrival of the Prophet ﷺ in Medina during the Hijrah, Thābit delivered an address on behalf of the Anṣār, declaring:
> «نَمْنَعُكَ مِمَّا نَمْنَعُ مِنْهُ أَنْفُسَنَا وَأَوْلَادَنَا، فَمَا لَنَا؟ قَالَ: الْجَنَّةَ. قَالُوا: رَضِينَا!»
> *"We shall protect you from all that we protect ourselves and our children from; what then shall be ours? The Prophet ﷺ replied: 'Paradise!' They said: 'We are well pleased!'"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:511).

The Prophet ﷺ praised him with the immortal commendation:
> «نِعْمَ الرَّجُلُ ثَابِتُ بْنُ قَيْسٍ»
> *"What an excellent man is Thābit b. Qays!"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:511).

## 2. Key Sīrah Milestones

### The Oratorical Duel with Wafd Banī Tamīm (9 AH)
When the delegation of **[[Banū Tamīm]]** arrived in Medina in the year 9 AH accompanied by their premier orator ʿUṭārid b. Ḥājib seeking to contest the Muslims in rhetorical precedence, the Prophet ﷺ commanded Thābit b. Qays:
> «قُمْ فَأَجِبِ الرَّجُلَ فِي خُطْبَتِهِ»
> *"Arise and answer the man in his address!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:280, 6:288).

Thābit delivered a soaring sermon praising Allāh as the Creator of the heavens and earth, extolling the dispatch of the Prophet ﷺ and the steadfast devotion of the Muhājirūn and Anṣār, utterly overwhelming the Banū Tamīm delegation and prompting their leaders to acknowledge the superiority of the Prophet's orator and poet (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:288–289).

### Military Campaigns & Steadfastness
Thābit witnessed **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]** and all subsequent campaigns alongside the Prophet ﷺ (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:511). The Prophet ﷺ gave him the glad tidings of Paradise in a renowned narration (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:511).

### Martyrdom at al-Yamāmah & The Unique Bequest
During the Caliphate of **[[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]]** ﵁ at the fierce Battle of al-Yamāmah against Musaylimah al-Kadhdhāb, when lines of the Muslim vanguard wavered, Thābit perfumed himself with embalming fragrances (*taḥannuṭ*), stepped forward, and called out: *"What an evil habit you have taught your peers! O Allāh, I absolve myself before You from what these [apostates] have brought, and from what these [Muslims] have done!"* He fought valiantly until he fell as a martyr (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:511).

Following his death, a Muslim soldier took his valuable suit of chainmail armor. Thābit appeared in a vision to a sleeping Muslim, describing the exact tent, the horse tethered outside, and the pot concealing the armor, instructing him to inform **[[Khālid b. al-Walīd]]** ﵁ to retrieve it and to request Abū Bakr ﵁ to pay his outstanding debts and emancipate his slave (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:511–512). The vision was verified in every detail by Khālid, and Abū Bakr executed the bequest in full, making Thābit b. Qays the only person in Islamic history whose testamentary bequest (*waṣiyyah*) made after death in a dream was lawfully executed and ratified (*al-Iṣābah*, 1:511–512).
