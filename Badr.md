# Badr (بدر)
*The Valley of the Criterion — The Watershed Battlefield of Islam*

## 1. Topography & Significance
An oasis valley blessed with subterranean wells located at the strategic junction of the Syrian trade route and the coastal artery. Immortalized in the Qurʾān as the station where Allāh granted miraculous victory to the 313 believers against 1,000 Meccans on **[[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]]** (*Yawm al-Furqān*).
