# Ubayy b. Khalaf (أبي بن خلف) ☠
*Chieftain of Banū Jumaḥ — The Only Adversary Slain Directly by the Hand of the Prophet ﷺ*

## 1. Identity & Early Hostility
A leading aristocrat of Banū Jumaḥ of **[[Quraysh]]** and brother of **[[Umayyah b. Khalaf]]** ☠ (who was slain at **[[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]]**). In Makkah prior to the Hijrah, Ubayy b. Khalaf had been among the most vitriolic opponents of the Prophet ﷺ.

After being ransomed from captivity following Badr, Ubayy fed a special war-horse named *al-ʿAwd* a measure (*faraq*) of corn every single day, boasting openly in Makkah: *«I shall kill Muḥammad upon this horse!»* When word reached the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ, he declared:
> «بَلْ أَنَا أَقْتُلُهُ إِنْ شَاءَ اللَّهُ تَعَالَى!»
> *"Nay, I shall kill him, if Allāh wills!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:208).

## 2. The Encounter at Uḥud
At **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]**, when the Muslim lines withdrew toward the ravine (*al-shiʿb*) of **[[Jabal Uḥud]]**, Ubayy b. Khalaf galloped forth on *al-ʿAwd*, clad in full iron armor, screaming:
> *"Where is Muḥammad? May I not survive if he survives!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:208).

When Companions moved to block his path, the Prophet ﷺ commanded: *«Leave him alone and clear his path!»* Taking a javelin (*ḥarbah*) from **al-Ḥārith b. al-Ṣimmah** ﵁ (or from **[[al-Zubayr b. al-ʿAwwām]]** ﵁), the Prophet ﷺ made a sudden vigorous motion that sent the Companions scattering from around him like hair flying off a camel's back (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:208).

As Ubayy closed in, the Prophet ﷺ struck him directly in the throat through an exposed gap at the collarbone (*al-tarquwah*) between his helmet and breastplate (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:208).

## 3. Demise and Theological Distinction
Ubayy tumbled repeatedly from his mount, bellowing like an ox (*yakhūru kamā yakhūru al-thawr*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:208). When his fellow polytheists carried him back to their lines, they tried to console him: *«Your heart has fled you! By Allāh, this is but a mere scratch, and there is no real harm upon you!»*

Ubayy wept in terror, retorting:
> *"By al-Lāt and al-ʿUzzā! If this wound upon me were upon all the people of Dhū al-Majāz, they would all perish! He told me in Makkah: 'I shall kill you!' By Allāh, even had he merely spat upon me, he would have killed me!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:208).

The Prophet ﷺ declared regarding him:
> «اشْتَدَّ غَضَبُ اللَّهِ عَزَّ وَجَلَّ عَلَى رَجُلٍ قَتَلَهُ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَآلِهِ وَسَلَّمَ!»
> *"Severe is the wrath of Allāh, Mighty and Majestic, upon a man slain by the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:208).

Ubayy died of the wound on the return journey to Makkah at Sarif (or Baṭn Rābigh) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:208). In the entirety of the Sīrah, he holds the grim distinction of being the **only human being ever killed directly by the hand of the Prophet ﷺ** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:208).
