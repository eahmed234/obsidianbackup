# Umm Salamah (أم سلمة هند بنت أبي أمية) ﵂
*Umm al-Muʾminīn — The Sagacious Matron of the Household and Counselor of Ḥudaybiyah*

## 1. Lineage & Early Islam
She is Hind bint Abī Umayyah Suhayl (famed as *Zād al-Rākib*, "The Provisioner of the Rider", for his generosity) b. al-Mughīrah al-Makhzūmiyyah al-Qurashiyyah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:187). Belonging to the aristocratic Banū Makhzūm, she and her first husband, **Abū Salamah ʿAbdullāh b. ʿAbd al-Asad al-Makhzūmī** ﵁, were among the earliest converts to Islam.

She endured the severe hardships of both migrations, journeying in [[al-Hijrah ilā al-Ḥabashah]] and subsequently undergoing heartbreaking separation from her husband and infant son when migrating to Medina during **[[al-Hijrah ilā al-Madīnah]]** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:382; 3:235).

## 2. Marriage to the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ
Following Abū Salamah's martyrdom from a wound sustained at [[Ghazwat Uḥud]], the Prophet ﷺ visited her and taught her to supplicate:
> «اللَّهُمَّ أْجُرْنِي فِي مُصِيبَتِي، وَأَخْلِفْ لِي خَيْرًا مِنْهَا»
> *"O Allāh, reward me in my affliction and grant me a successor better than it!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:188).
The Prophet ﷺ subsequently proposed to her, and they were married in Shawwāl 4 AH (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:188–189).

## 3. Pivotal Sīrah Milestones
- **The Decisive Counsel at [[Ṣulḥ al-Ḥudaybiyah]] (6 AH)**: When the treaty was finalized and the Prophet ﷺ commanded the companions thrice to slaughter their sacrificial animals and shave their heads, the companions sat motionless in acute grief. Entering her tent perturbed, the Prophet ﷺ sought her counsel. She wisely advised: *“O Messenger of Allāh, go forth and do not speak a word to anyone until you slaughter your camel and call your barber to shave your head.”* When the Prophet ﷺ did so, the companions immediately rushed to emulate him, averting catastrophe (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:54; 11:190).
- **Campaigns**: Accompanied the Prophet ﷺ on major expeditions, including **[[Ghazwat Khaybar]]** (7 AH) where she was his sole accompanying wife (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:115), the siege of al-Ṭāʾif, and **[[Ḥajjat al-Wadāʿ]]**.
- **The Āyah of Purification**: It was in her dwelling that the divine Verse of Purification (Qurʾān 33:33) was revealed, upon which the Prophet ﷺ gathered ʿAlī, Fāṭimah, al-Ḥasan, and al-Ḥusayn under his cloak (*Ḥadīth al-Kisāʾ*), while assuring Umm Salamah: *“You are upon goodness!”* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:6).

## 4. Demise & Legacy
Umm Salamah was renowned for her profound legal discernment, literary eloquence, and custody of Prophetic traditions. She was the last of the Mothers of the Believers (*Ummahāt al-Muʾminīn*) to pass away, dying in Medina around 59 AH (or 61 AH) and being laid to rest in [[Jannat al-Baqīʿ]] (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:192).
