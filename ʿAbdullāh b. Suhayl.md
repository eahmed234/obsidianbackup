# ʿAbdullāh b. Suhayl (عبد الله بن سهيل)
*Hero of Badr, Emigrant to Abyssinia, Protector of His Father at the Conquest, and Martyr of Yamāmah*

## 1. Lineage and Early Conversion

ʿAbdullāh b. Suhayl b. ʿAmr b. ʿAbd Shams b. ʿAbd Wudd b. Naṣr b. Mālik b. Ḥisl b. ʿĀmir b. Luʾayy al-Qurashī al-ʿĀmirī, Abū Suhayl (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:107). 

His father was the renowned orator and chieftain of Quraysh, [[Suhayl b. ʿAmr]] ﵁; his mother was Fākhitah bint ʿĀmir b. Nawfal b. ʿAbd Manāf; and he was the elder brother of [[Abū Jandal b. Suhayl]] ﵁ (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:107).

He was among the early converts in Makkah and participated in the migration to Abyssinia (*[[al-Hijrah ilā al-Ḥabashah]]*) (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:107; *Subul al-Hudā*, 2:369). When he returned to Makkah, his father Suhayl seized him, placed him in fetters, and subjected him to intense torture and persecution until ʿAbdullāh outwardly feigned renouncing Islam to preserve his life, while remaining fiercely devoted to Allah and His Messenger in secret (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:107; *Subul al-Hudā*, 2:369).

## 2. Daring Defection at Badr (2 AH)

When Quraysh mobilized their forces to defend their caravan and march on [[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā|Badr]], Suhayl b. ʿAmr compelled ʿAbdullāh to march in the ranks of the polytheist army (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:107). 

Upon reaching Badr, as the two armies drew up their lines and battle was imminent, ʿAbdullāh b. Suhayl slipped away under cover of the desert dunes, galloped across the sands, and hurled himself into the Muslim camp, declaring his steadfast faith before the Messenger of Allah ﷺ (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:107). 

He fought bravely on the side of the Muslims at Badr, earning the supreme distinction of being counted among the veterans of Badr (*Ahl Badr*), while his father fought among the polytheists and was captured (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:107).

## 3. Witness to the Treaties and Reconnaissance

ʿAbdullāh b. Suhayl participated in all major campaigns alongside the Messenger of Allah ﷺ. 

During the expedition of [[al-Ḥudaybiyah]] in 6 AH, the Prophet ﷺ dispatched ʿAbdullāh b. Suhayl alongside Kurz b. Jābir al-Fihrī on an authorized reconnaissance mission inside Makkah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:48). 

When the terms of [[Ṣulḥ al-Ḥudaybiyah]] were being finalized between the Prophet ﷺ and his father Suhayl b. ʿAmr, ʿAbdullāh witnessed the poignant arrival of his chained younger brother Abū Jandal and signed the historic treaty document as one of the Muslim witnesses (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:107).

## 4. Securing Pardon for His Father at the Conquest (8 AH)

On the day of [[Fatḥ Makkah]], Suhayl b. ʿAmr locked himself inside his home, fearing execution due to his long leadership in opposing the Prophet ﷺ. 

ʿAbdullāh b. Suhayl went directly to the Messenger of Allah ﷺ and pleaded:
> «يا رسول الله، أبي تؤمنه؟»
> *"O Messenger of Allah, will you grant protection to my father?"*

The Prophet ﷺ replied:
> «نعم، هو آمن بأمان الله، فليظهر»
> *"Yes, he is secure with the security of Allah; let him come forth."*

ʿAbdullāh returned swiftly and escorted his father to safety before the Prophet ﷺ, who treated Suhayl with consummate generosity and forbearance, prompting Suhayl to embrace Islam (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:107). Suhayl thereafter would frequently say:
> «قد جعل الله لابني في الإسلام خيرا كثيرا»
> *"Allah has indeed placed great good for my son in Islam."* (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:107).

## 5. Martyrdom at Yamāmah (12 AH)

During the Caliphate of [[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]] ﵁, ʿAbdullāh b. Suhayl marched with the vanguard of the Muslim army under [[Khālid b. al-Walīd]] ﵁ in the [[Ḥurūb al-Riddah|Riddah Wars]] against Musaylimah al-Kadhdhāb (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:107). 

At the fierce Battle of ʿAqrabāʾ in al-Yamāmah in 12 AH, ʿAbdullāh fought with boundless courage until he was slain on the battlefield and achieved martyrdom (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:107). 

When his father Suhayl learned of his son's martyrdom, he wept, praised Allah, and expressed his pride in his son's sacrifice for Islam (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:107).
