# Ḥakīm b. Ḥizām (حكيم بن حزام الأسدي)
*The Noble Chieftain Born Within the Kaʿbah, Master of Genealogies, and Philanthropist of the Sacred House*

> [!NOTE] Primary Sourcing & Biographical Provenance
> Sourced directly from *al-Iṣābah fī Tamyīz al-Ṣaḥābah*, 2:97–98 (entry 1805).

---

## 1. Lineage & Unprecedented Birth

Ḥakīm b. Ḥizām was an esteemed nobleman of Quraysh belonging to the house of Banū Asad b. ʿAbd al-ʿUzzā (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:97).

- **Full Paternal Lineage**: Ḥakīm b. Ḥizām b. Khuwaylid b. Asad b. ʿAbd al-ʿUzzā b. Quṣayy al-Asadī al-Qurashī, Abū Khālid (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:97).
- **Kinship with Khadījah ﵂**: He was the nephew of the Mother of the Believers, **[[Khadījah bint Khuwaylid]]** ﵂ (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:97).
- **Birth Inside the Kaʿbah**: Ḥakīm possesses the unique historical distinction of having been born within the interior of the Sacred Kaʿbah (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:98).
- **Birth Date & Longevity**: Born thirteen years before the Year of the Elephant (*ʿĀm al-Fīl*), five years before the birth of the Prophet ﷺ (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:98). He lived to the age of one hundred and twenty years, spending sixty years in the Jāhiliyyah and sixty years in Islam (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:98).

---

## 2. Pre-Islamic Nobility & Friendship with the Prophet ﷺ

- In pre-Islamic times, Ḥakīm was entrusted with the office of *al-Rifādah* (providing food and provisions to the pilgrims), renowned for upholding ties of kinship and immense charity (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:98).
- He maintained a warm friendship and deep affection for the Prophet ﷺ both before the prophetic commission and throughout the Makkan persecution (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:98).
- He escaped with the survivors of Badr; whenever he took a solemn oath thereafter, he would declare: *«By the One Who saved me on the Day of Badr!»* (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:98).

---

## 3. Conversion at the Conquest of Makkah (8 AH)

Ḥakīm embraced Islam during the Supreme Conquest (**[[Fatḥ Makkah]]**) in the eighth year of the Hijrah (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:98).
- Honouring his dignity and standing among Quraysh, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ declared his residence a general sanctuary:
  > «مَنْ دَخَلَ دَارَ أَبِي سُفْيَانَ فَهُوَ آمِنٌ، وَمَنْ دَخَلَ دَارَ حَكِيمِ بْنِ حِزَامٍ فَهُوَ آمِنٌ»
  > *"Whoever enters the house of Abū Sufyān is safe, and whoever enters the house of Ḥakīm b. Ḥizām is safe!"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:98).
- At **[[Ḥunayn]]**, the Prophet ﷺ bestowed upon him one hundred camels from the spoils of war (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:98). Ḥakīm subsequently embraced Islam with exemplary sincerity and asceticism, resolving never to take anything from anyone again (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:98).

---

## 4. Deeds of Righteousness in Islam

- **Pre-Islamic Deeds**: In an authentic tradition in the *Ṣaḥīḥ*, Ḥakīm inquired: *"O Messenger of Allāh! What think you of deeds of charity, emancipating slaves, and upholding kinship that I performed in the Jāhiliyyah; is there a reward for me in them?"* The Prophet ﷺ replied:
  > «أَسْلَمْتَ عَلَى مَا سَلَفَ لَكَ مِنْ خَيْرٍ»
  > *"You have accepted Islam with all the good you previously performed!"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:98).
- **Sale of Dār al-Nadwah**: Ḥakīm held ownership of the historic municipal council chamber of Quraysh, **Dār al-Nadwah**. He sold it during the caliphate of Muʿāwiyah for one hundred thousand dirhams. When [[ʿAbdullāh b. al-Zubayr]] reproached him for selling a priceless ancestral heritage, Ḥakīm replied: *"O son of my brother! I have purchased with it a home in Paradise!"* and distributed every single dirham to the poor (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:98).

---

## 5. Demise

Ḥakīm b. Ḥizām passed away in Medina around the year fifty-four AH (or sixty AH) at the age of one hundred and twenty years (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:98).
