# Wādī al-Ṣafrāʾ (وادي الصفراء)
*The Valley on the Road Back from Badr*

## 1. Position
A valley on the caravan route between **[[Badr]]** and **[[al-Madīnah al-Munawwarah]]**, through
which the army passed both outward and on return.

## 2. Significance
It was here, on the march back from **[[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]]**, that
**[[ʿUbaydah b. al-Ḥārith]]** ﵁ died of the wound taken in the opening duel — his leg severed by
**[[ʿUtbah b. Rabīʿah]]** ☠ — and here that he was buried, the first of the Muhājirūn to fall in
the battle.

The division of the spoils and the disposition of the seventy captives were also settled on this
return stage of the campaign (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:46–60).
