# Abū Dujānah (أبو دجانة) ﵁
*Simāk b. Kharashah — Bearer of the Red Bandana and the Prophet's Sword at Uḥud, Badri Veteran, and Martyr of al-Yamāmah*

## 1. Lineage, Clan & Pre-Islamic Background
- **Ancestry**: Simāk b. Kharashah (and it was said: Simāk b. Aws b. Kharashah) b. Lawdhān b. ʿAbd Wudd b. Thaʿlabah b. Dhaʿwar b. Sāʿidah b. Kaʿb b. al-Khazraj al-Anṣārī al-Sāʿidī (*al-Iṣābah*, 3:146, no. 3475; 7:99–100, no. 9866). Kunyah: **Abū Dujānah**.
- **Clan**: Banū Sāʿidah of the Khazraj, the kinsmen of **[[Saʿd b. ʿUbādah]]** ﵁.
- **Distinction**: In Jāhiliyyah and Islam, he was famed for his unparalleled martial valour, proud swagger in battle, and the red headband (*al-ʿiṣābah al-ḥamrāʾ*) he tied around his brow whenever he resolved to fight unto death (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:192).

## 2. Early Milestones & Ghazwat Badr
- **Conversion**: Embraced Islam among the early Anṣār of Medina.
- **[[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]] (2 AH)**: Fought valiantly in the ranks of the Anṣār, demonstrating heroic martial prowess (*al-Iṣābah*, 7:100; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:45).

## 3. The Sword of the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ at Uḥud (3 AH)
- **Claiming the Prophetic Sword**: On the morning of **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]**, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ held up a sharp sword and proclaimed: *«Who will take this sword with its right?»* Companions reached out for it—including **[[al-Zubayr b. al-ʿAwwām]]** ﵁ and **[[ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb]]** ﵁—but the Prophet ﷺ withheld it. Then Abū Dujānah stepped forward and inquired: *«And what is its right, O Messenger of Allāh?»* The Prophet ﷺ replied: *«Its right is that you do not strike a Muslim with it, nor flee from an enemy before you until it bends»* (and in Muslim: *«that you strike the faces of the enemy with it until it bends»*) (*Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim*; *al-Iṣābah*, 7:100; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:192).
- **The Red Bandana & Martial Strut**: Receiving the blade, Abū Dujānah bound his renowned red headband around his forehead and began strutting proudly between the ranks. Seeing him walk with such pride, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ remarked: *«Indeed, it is a gait that Allāh detests, except in such a place [the battlefield]»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:192).
- **Cleaving the Enemy Ranks**: He cleaved deeply through the Makkan lines, cutting down enemy warriors until he confronted a disguised pagan warrior inciting the polytheists. Raising his sword above the figure, the warrior cried out in terror, revealing herself to be Hind bint ʿUtbah; Abū Dujānah spared her, declaring that he would not demean the sword of the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ by striking a woman (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:193).
- **Shielding the Prophet ﷺ in the Crisis**: When the Muslim lines fractured and the Quraysh encircled the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ, Abū Dujānah turned his back into a human shield over the Prophet ﷺ. Arrows struck his back repeatedly while he leaned protectively over the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ, refusing to budge until he had sustained dozens of wounds (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:200, 203; *al-Iṣābah*, 7:100).

## 4. Later Campaigns & Martyrdom at al-Yamāmah (12 AH)
- Continued to participate in subsequent campaigns with the Prophet ﷺ, including the siege of Khaybar and Hunayn.
- **The Ridda Wars & al-Yamāmah (12 AH)**: During the caliphate of **[[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]]** ﵁, Abū Dujānah marched under the banner of **[[Khālid b. al-Walīd]]** ﵁ against the false prophet Musaylimah al-Kadhdhāb in the Battle of ʿAqrabāʾ.
- **Assault on the Garden of Death (*Ḥadīqat al-Mawt*)**: Alongside **[[al-Barāʾ b. Mālik]]** ﵁, he breached the fortified orchard where Musaylimah had barricaded himself. In the ferocious close-quarters engagement, Abū Dujānah fell upon Musaylimah alongside **[[Waḥshī b. Ḥarb]]** ﵁, striking him down before attaining martyrdom covered in wounds (*al-Iṣābah*, 7:100).
