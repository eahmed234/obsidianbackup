# ʿAbdullāh b. al-Zubayr (عبد الله بن الزبير بن العوام)
*The Firstborn of the Hijrah, Resolute Lion of the Sanctuary, and Knight of the Righteous*

> [!NOTE] Primary Sourcing & Biographical Provenance
> Sourced directly from *al-Iṣābah fī Tamyīz al-Ṣaḥābah*, 4:78–81 (entry 4700).

---

## 1. Lineage & Illustrious Kinship

ʿAbdullāh was born into the most noble lineage uniting the closest companions of the Prophet ﷺ (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:78).

- **Full Paternal Lineage**: ʿAbdullāh b. al-Zubayr b. al-ʿAwwām b. Khuwaylid b. Asad b. ʿAbd al-ʿUzzā b. Quṣayy al-Asadī al-Qurashī, Abū Bakr (and Abū Khubayb) (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:78).
- **Parents**: His father was the disciple of the Prophet ﷺ, **[[al-Zubayr b. al-ʿAwwām]]** ﵁, and his mother was **[[Asmāʾ bint Abī Bakr]]** (*Dhāt al-Niṭāqayn*) ﵂ (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:78).
- **Aunts & Grandparents**: He was the nephew of **[[ʿĀʾishah bint Abī Bakr]]** ﵂ and grandson of **[[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]]** ﵁; his father al-Zubayr noted that ʿAbdullāh resembled Abū Bakr more than anyone else (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:81).

---

## 2. The Firstborn of the Hijrah (1 AH)

When the Muslim emigrants arrived in Medina, the Jewish factions spread rumors asserting they had placed a sorcerous spell upon the Muslims so that no male child would ever be born to them in their new home (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:80).

- **Birth at Qubāʾ**: Asmāʾ bint Abī Bakr was pregnant with ʿAbdullāh during the perilous journey from Makkah, giving birth to him immediately upon reaching **[[Qubāʾ]]** in the year one AH (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:80).
- **The First Food**: Asmāʾ brought the newborn to the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ; the Prophet ﷺ took him into his lap, chewed a date (*tamrah*), and placed its essence into the child's mouth (*taḥnīk*) (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:80). The very first substance to enter ʿAbdullāh's stomach was the blessed saliva of the Prophet ﷺ (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:80).
- **The Roar of Takbīr**: The Prophet ﷺ stroked his head, named him ʿAbdullāh, and bestowed upon him the kunya Abū Bakr after his grandfather (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:79–80). The Companions throughout Medina erupted in joyous *takbīr* (*Allāhu Akbar!*), refuting the false claims of the hostile factions (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:80).

---

## 3. Youth in the Prophetic Presence

- **The Youthful Pledge**: At seven (or eight) years of age, his father al-Zubayr brought him to the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ to offer the formal oath of allegiance (*bayʿah*); the Prophet ﷺ smiled broadly upon seeing the young boy approach and accepted his pledge (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:80).
- **Drinking the Cupping Blood**: In his youth, the Prophet ﷺ underwent cupping (*ḥijāmah*) and handed the blood to young ʿAbdullāh, instructing him: *«O ʿAbdullāh, go and bury this blood where no one shall see you!»* ʿAbdullāh slipped behind the curtain and drank the cupping blood. When the Prophet ﷺ questioned what he had done with it, he confessed he had concealed it within his own body. The Prophet ﷺ remarked on his intense devotion and bravery (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:81).

---

## 4. Later Stature & Caliphate

- **Courage & Worship**: Renowned among the Companions for unrivaled devotion in nocturnal prayer (*qiyām*) and formidable prowess in battle, having participated in the conquests of North Africa and Persia (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:79).
- **Caliphate**: In sixty-four AH, following the passing of Yazīd b. Muʿāwiyah, the overwhelming majority of the Islamic realms (the Hijaz, Iraq, Yemen, and Khurasan) pledged allegiance to ʿAbdullāh b. al-Zubayr as Amir al-Mu'minin (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:79).
- **Martyrdom (73 AH)**: He remained steadfast defending the Sacred Sanctuary of Makkah until his epic martyrdom in seventy-three AH (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:79).
