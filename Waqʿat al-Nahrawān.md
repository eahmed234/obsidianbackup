# Waqʿat al-Nahrawān (وقعة النهروان)
*The Battle of al-Nahrawān: The Suppression of the Extremist Khawārij, Fulfillment of Prophetic Portents, and the Slaying of Dhū al-Thudayyah (the year thirty-eight AH)*

## 1. Origins of the Secession (al-Khurūj)
Following the armistice and agreement to arbitration at **[[Waqʿat Ṣiffīn]]** (thirty-seven AH), an extremist group of roughly twelve thousand fighters broke away from the army of **[[ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib]]** ﵁ upon their return toward Kūfah. They established an armed encampment at Ḥarūrāʾ, adopting the radical ideological slogan:
> «لَا حُكْمَ إِلَّا لِلَّهِ!»
> *"Judgment belongs to none but Allāh!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 10:148).

Hearing their chant, ʿAlī famously remarked:
> «كَلِمَةُ حَقٍّ يُرَادُ بِهَا بَاطِلٌ!»
> *"A word of truth by which falsehood is intended!"*

The dissidents, known thereafter as the **Khawārij** (or *Ḥarūriyyah*), pronounced apostasy (*takfīr*) upon ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib, ʿUthmān b. ʿAffān, the arbitrators, and anyone who did not endorse their sectarian extremism.

---

## 2. Diplomatic Debates & Extremist Atrocities
### ʿAbdullāh b. ʿAbbās's Historic Debate
ʿAlī dispatched the premier Qurʾānic exegete **[[ʿAbdullāh b. ʿAbbās]]** ﵁ to debate the seceders at Ḥarūrāʾ:
- Ibn ʿAbbās rigorously dismantled their theological misunderstandings regarding human arbitration, citing Qurʾānic verses where Allāh appointed human arbiters to resolve marital discord (4:35) and assess the value of game killed in the sanctuary (5:95).
- Through his persuasive proofs, approximately four thousand of the dissidents renounced their extremism and returned with Ibn ʿAbbās to Kūfah.

### Murder of ʿAbdullāh b. Khabbāb and Terror against Civilians
The intransigent faction elected ʿAbdullāh b. Wahb al-Rāsibī and **Ḥurqūṣ b. Zuhayr al-Saʿdī** (Dhū al-Khuwayṣirah al-Tamīmī) as their chiefs and migrated to **al-Nahrawān**, east of the Tigris river (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:44).

At Nahrawān, their fanaticism turned into murderous violence against peaceful Muslims:
- They encountered the pious Companion **ʿAbdullāh b. Khabbāb b. al-Aratt** ﵁ traveling with his pregnant wife.
- When he refused to curse ʿAlī and ʿUthmān, they tied him and slaughtered him over a stream, then brutally murdered his pregnant wife.
- When news of this atrocity reached ʿAlī as he was marshaling his forces to march toward Syria, the army demanded that the innocent blood be avenged before undertaking any external campaign. ʿAlī demanded the surrender of the murderers; the Khawārij insolently responded: *“All of us killed him, and all of us consider your blood lawful!”*

---

## 3. The Battle of al-Nahrawān (ninth of Ṣafar in the year thirty-eight AH)
Amīr al-Muʾminīn ʿAlī deployed his army outside Nahrawān. Offering them a final chance for clemency, ʿAlī raised a banner of safety with **[[Abū Ayyūb al-Anṣārī]]** ﵁, announcing:
> *“Whoever gathers beneath this banner, or departs to Kūfah or his home, is safe; we have no need of your blood unless you initiate fighting against us.”*
Several thousand more seceders abandoned the extremist camp, leaving roughly two to three thousand fanatical fighters under ʿAbdullāh b. Wahb and Ḥurqūṣ b. Zuhayr (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:44).

On ninth of Ṣafar in the year thirty-eight AH, the Khawārij initiated a frenzied charge, screaming their slogans. ʿAlī's archers and cavalry counter-attacked from both flanks, encircling them completely. Within hours, the extremist army was annihilated; fewer than a dozen of ʿAlī's men fell, while the vast majority of the Khārijite combatants were slain (*Subul al-Hudā*, 10:148).

---

## 4. Discovery of Dhū al-Thudayyah & The Prophetic Portent
Decades prior, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ had explicitly prophesied the emergence and specific distinguishing physical mark of the leader of this renegade faction:
> «يَمْرُقُونَ مِنَ الدِّينِ كَمَا يَمْرُقُ السَّهْمُ مِنَ الرَّمِيَّةِ ... آيَتُهُمْ رَجُلٌ أَسْوَدُ، إِحْدَى عَضُدَيْهِ مِثْلُ ثَدْيِ الْمَرْأَةِ، أَوْ مِثْلُ الْبَضْعَةِ تَدَرْدَرُ!»
> *"They will pass through the religion just as an arrow passes clean through game... Their distinguishing sign is a dark man whose arm has a malformed flesh like a woman's breast, or like a quivering piece of meat!"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:341; *Subul al-Hudā*, 1:44; 10:148).

Following the battle, ʿAlī urgently ordered a search among the slain for this individual. When the body was finally located under a pile of corpses, ʿAlī inspected the deformed arm—bearing hair like the nipple of a breast that quivered when pulled (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:341).

Beholding the miraculous exactitude of the Prophetic prophecy, ʿAlī immediately fell in prostration of gratitude (*sajdat al-shukr*) to Allāh, while the entire army proclaimed the *Takbīr* (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:341; *Subul al-Hudā*, 10:148).

---

## 5. Aftermath & Legacy
Although their primary military host was crushed at Nahrawān, dispersed surviving cells went underground, adopting clandestine terrorism. It was from the surviving ideological remnants of this battle that ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. Muljam al-Murādī emerged two years later to strike down Amīr al-Muʾminīn ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib in the Grand Mosque of Kūfah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:305–306).

The Battle of al-Nahrawān remains the canonical historical benchmark in orthodox Islamic jurisprudence for defining and combating violent rebellion (*qitāl ahl al-baghy*) and religious extremism (*ghuluww*).
