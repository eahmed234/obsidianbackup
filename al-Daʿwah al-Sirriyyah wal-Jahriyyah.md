# al-Daʿwah al-Sirriyyah wal-Jahriyyah (الدعوة السرية والجهرية)
*The Secret Assembly at Dār al-Arqam and the Public Proclamation on Mount al-Ṣafā*

## 1. The Secret Stage: Dār al-Arqam
For three years, the propagation of Islam proceeded privately to protect the nascent community from premature eradication. The home of the youthful convert **al-Arqam b. Abī al-Arqam al-Makhzūmī** ([`Dār al-Arqam`](file:///home/eahmed/Projects/obsidianbackup/Dār%20al-Arqam.md)) near Mount al-Ṣafā served as the clandestine headquarters of worship, brotherhood, and instruction.

## 2. The Public Proclamation: Mount al-Ṣafā
Upon the revelation of:
$$\text{وَأَنذِرْ عَشِيرَتَكَ الْأَقْرَبِينَ}$$
> *"And warn your nearest kinsfolk"* (Qurʾān 26:214),
the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ climbed atop Mount al-Ṣafā and cried: *«يَا صَبَاحَاهْ!»* (*"O morning warning!"*). When Quraysh gathered, he addressed them:
> *"If I were to inform you that enemy cavalry were in the valley behind this mountain preparing to attack you, would you believe me?"*
They answered: *"Yes, we have never experienced anything from you except truthfulness (*al-ṣidq*)!"*
He proclaimed:
> «فَإِنِّي نَذِيرٌ لَكُمْ بَيْنَ يَدَيْ عَذَابٍ شَدِيدٍ»
> *"Then I am indeed a warner to you before an impending severe chastisement!"*

His uncle **[[Abū Lahab]]** spat: *"May you perish this day! Did you gather us for this?!"* Whereupon Allāh revealed [[Sūrat al-Masad]] (111:1–5) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:325).
