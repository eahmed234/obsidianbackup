# Ṣuhayb b. Sinān (صهيب بن سنان الرومي) ﵁
*Abū Yaḥyā al-Rūmī — The Profitable Trader of the Faith and Veteran of Badr*

## 1. Lineage & Early Life
He is Ṣuhayb b. Sinān b. Mālik al-Namarī (kunya: Abū Yaḥyā). Originally of pure Arab stock from Banū Namir b. Qāsiṭ along the Euphrates, his father was a governor for the Persian king (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:77). During a Byzantine raid, Ṣuhayb was captured as a young boy and reared in Byzantine territory, acquiring their speech and dialect, thus earning the epithet *al-Rūmī* ("The Roman") (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:77).

Purchased and brought to Mecca by **[[ʿAbdullāh b. Judʿān]]**, who subsequently manumitted him, Ṣuhayb entered business and accumulated considerable wealth. He accepted Islam in **[[Dār al-Arqam]]** alongside **[[ʿAmmār b. Yāsir]]** on the very same day, becoming one of the earliest believers (*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:359; 3:77).

## 2. The Profitable Bargain (*Rabiḥa al-Bayʿ*)
When Ṣuhayb resolved to migrate during **[[al-Hijrah ilā al-Madīnah]]**, polytheist horsemen of Quraysh pursued him. Ṣuhayb dismounted, emptied his quiver of arrows, and warned them:
> *"O Quraysh, you know well that I am among the sharpest archers among you; by Allāh, you shall not reach me until I shoot every arrow in my quiver, and strike you with my sword as long as it remains in my hand! But if you desire, I shall direct you to all the gold and silver I left in Mecca, and you let me go my way."* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:227).

They accepted the ransom. Reaching the Prophet ﷺ at Qubāʾ, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ greeted him joyfully with the timeless commendation:
> «رَبِحَ الْبَيْعُ أَبَا يَحْيَى! رَبِحَ الْبَيْعُ أَبَا يَحْيَى!»
> *"The transaction has profited, O Abū Yaḥyā! The transaction has profited, O Abū Yaḥyā!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:227).
Allāh confirmed his sacrifice by revealing in Sūrat al-Baqarah:
> «وَمِنَ النَّاسِ مَنْ يَشْرِي نَفْسَهُ ابْتِغَاءَ مَرْضَاتِ اللَّهِ ۗ وَاللَّهُ رَءُوفٌ بِالْعِبَادِ»
> *"And of the people is he who sells himself, seeking the pleasure of Allāh; and Allāh is Kind to His servants."* (Qurʾān 2:207; *Subul al-Hudā*, 3:227).

## 3. Key Sīrah Milestones & Later Life
- **Military Veteran**: Fought beside the Prophet ﷺ at **[[Ghazwat Badr al-Kubrā]]** (4:105), **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]**, and all subsequent campaigns without missing a single battle.
- **Leader of the Prayers**: When the Caliph **[[ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb]]** ﵁ was fatally wounded in 23 AH, he appointed Ṣuhayb to lead the Muslims in public prayer and manage state affairs until the Shūrā council elected the next Caliph (*Subul al-Hudā*, 11:277).

## 4. Demise & Legacy
Ṣuhayb was renowned for his boundless hospitality, generously feeding the needy and narrating Prophetic traditions. He died in Medina in 38 AH and was buried in [[Jannat al-Baqīʿ]] (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:227).
