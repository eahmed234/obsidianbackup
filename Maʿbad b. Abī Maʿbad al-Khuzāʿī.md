# Maʿbad b. Abī Maʿbad al-Khuzāʿī (معبد بن أبي معبد الخزاعي)
*The Chieftain of Khuzāʿah — Whose Stratagem at al-Rawḥāʾ Deterred the Meccan Counter-Attack*

## 1. Tribal Loyalty & Heritage
A respected elder of **[[Banū Khuzāʿah]]** in Tihāmah. In the classical historical tradition synthesized by al-Ṣāliḥī, the tribe of Khuzāʿah — both their Muslims and their polytheists — served as a repository of sincere counsel and confidentiality (*ʿaybat nuṣḥ*) for the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ in the lowlands of Tihāmah, never concealing intelligence from him (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:310).

## 2. Psychological Deterrence at al-Rawḥāʾ
Following **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]**, Maʿbad met the Prophet ﷺ at **[[Ḥamrāʾ al-Asad]]** and offered heartfelt condolences for the martyrs (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:310–311).

Proceeding southward, Maʿbad arrived at **al-Rawḥāʾ**, where the Meccan army under **[[Abū Sufyān b. Ḥarb]]** had halted to organize a return march to Medina, saying: *«We killed the best of his companions, their commanders and notables; shall we return before exterminating the remainder?! Let us wheel back upon them!»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:311).

When Abū Sufyān asked him for news, Maʿbad deployed a brilliant psychological deception:
> *"I have left Muḥammad and his companions marching in pursuit of you with a host the like of which I have never seen! They are burning with rage against you, joined by every warrior of the Aws and Khazraj who remained behind yesterday, having sworn never to return until they overtake you and avenge their fallen!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:311).

When Abū Sufyān gasped in horror, Maʿbad added: *«By Allāh, I do not believe you will even pack your baggage before you see the forelocks of their cavalry!»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:311). He then recited his poem describing the rumbling host of the believers (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:311).

Combined with the counsel of Ṣafwān b. Umayyah, Maʿbad's testimony shattered Meccan morale, causing Abū Sufyān and his army to abandon their proposed counter-assault and hasten back toward Makkah (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:311).
