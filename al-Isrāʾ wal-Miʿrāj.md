# al-Isrāʾ wal-Miʿrāj (الإسراء والمعراج)
*The Miraculous Night Journey from Makkah to Jerusalem and the Heavenly Ascension*

## 1. Overview
Following the tribulations of *ʿĀm al-Ḥuzn* and al-Ṭāʾif, Allāh granted His chosen Prophet [[Muḥammad b. ʻAbdullah|Muḥammad]] ﷺ the supreme physical and spiritual honor of *al-Isrāʾ* (the Night Journey across terrestrial space) and *al-Miʿrāj* (the vertical Ascension through the celestial spheres to the Divine Presence).

Carried upon the celestial mount **al-Burāq** accompanied by **Jibrīl** ﵊:
1. **The Isrāʾ**: Traveled by night from al-Masjid al-Ḥarām in Makkah to **al-Masjid al-Aqṣā** in Jerusalem, where the Prophet ﷺ led all the previous prophets and messengers in prayer as their universal Imām (Qurʾān 17:1; *Subul al-Hudā*, 3:65).
2. **The Miʿrāj**: Ascended through the seven heavens, greeting the prophets (Adam, Yaḥyā, ʿĪsā, Yūsuf, Idrīs, Hārūn, Mūsā, and Ibrāhīm), passing beyond the boundary of created existence at **Sidrat al-Muntahā** (The Lote Tree of the Utmost Boundary) where even Jibrīl could not tread (Qurʾān 53:1–18).

## 2. The Prescription of the Five Daily Prayers
In the Divine Presence, Allāh directly ordained the ritual prayer (*al-Ṣalāh*)—initially fifty prayers daily, reduced upon the advice of Mūsā ﵊ to five daily prayers with the spiritual reward of fifty.

## 3. The Title of al-Ṣiddīq
When Quraysh mocked the claim the following morning, **[[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]]** ﵁ proclaimed: *"If he said it, then he has spoken the truth! For I believe him in news from the heavens that arrives morning and evening!"* For this unshakeable faith, he was honored with the eternal title ***al-Ṣiddīq*** (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:112).
