# Wafd al-Ashʿariyyīn (وفد الأشعريين)
*The arrival of the People of the Ship from Yemen, the prophetic prayer during the maritime storm, and the immortal praise: «The Ashʿarīs among mankind are like a pouch containing musk»*

## 1. Departure from Yemen & The Storm at Sea

When news of the mission of the Prophet ﷺ reached Yemen, **[[Abū Mūsā al-Ashʿarī]]** ﵁ and his two elder brothers, **Abū Ruhm** and **Abū Burdah**, embarked from the Yemeni coastal district of Zabīd aboard a sailing vessel (*safīnah*) alongside **52 or 53 men** of their tribe, seeking to migrate to the Prophet ﷺ (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:135; 6:273).

While at sea, violent tempests battered the vessel until the passengers stood on the very brink of drowning (*ashrafū fī al-baḥri ʿalā al-gharaq*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:273). Far away in Medina, the Prophet ﷺ was granted divine unveiling of their plight; sitting amidst his Companions, he suddenly raised his hands and supplicated:
> «اللهم انج أصحاب السفينة»
> *"O Allāh, deliver the people of the ship!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:273).

After a short pause, the Prophet ﷺ announced: *«Istamaddat»* (*"It has found assistance and safety!"*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:273). Driven off course across the Red Sea, the vessel made landfall on the coast of Abyssinia, where the Ashʿarīs met **[[Jaʿfar b. Abī Ṭālib]]** ﵁ and the Abyssinian emigrants at the court of [[al-Najāshī (Aṣḥamah)|al-Najāshī]] (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:135).

## 2. Arrival at Khaybar & Chanting Love for the Prophet ﷺ

The Ashʿarīs joined Jaʿfar's party aboard two ships provided by the Negus and crossed back to the Ḥijāz, arriving just as the Prophet ﷺ conquered **[[Ghazwat Khaybar]]** in 7 AH (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:135). 

As the Ashʿarī caravan approached, the Prophet ﷺ announced their imminent arrival to the Companions:
> «يَقْدَمُ عَلَيْكُمْ قَوْمٌ هُمْ أَرَقُّ مِنْكُمْ قُلُوبًا»
> *"There approaches you a people who possess softer hearts than you!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:273).

As they drew near the camp, the Ashʿarīs raised their voices in joyful desert cadence (*irtajazū*), chanting:
> غَدًا نَلْقَى الأَحِبَّة ... مُحَمَّدًا وَحِزْبَه!
> *"Tomorrow we shall meet the beloved ones: Muḥammad and his noble party!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:273).

The Prophet ﷺ embraced them warmly and, with the unanimous consent of the Badri veterans of Khaybar, allotted them full shares from the spoils of Khaybar alongside the original participants (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:135).

## 3. Prophetic Commendations of Yemen & The Ashʿarīs

Upon their formal oath of allegiance in Medina, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ delivered a succession of celebrated tributes immortalizing the virtue of the People of Yemen and the Ashʿarīs:

1. **The Gentle Hearts of Yemen**:
> «أَتَاكُمْ أَهْلُ الْيَمَنِ، هُمْ أَرَقُّ أَفْئِدَةً وَأَلْيَنُ قُلُوبًا؛ الإِيمَانُ يَمَانٍ، وَالْحِكْمَةُ يَمَانِيَةٌ!»
> *"The people of Yemen have come to you; they possess the tenderest of hearts and the gentlest of souls. True faith is Yemeni, and wisdom is Yemeni!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:274).

2. **The Fragrance of Musk**:
> «الأَشْعَرِيُّونَ فِي النَّاسِ كَصُرَّةٍ فِيهَا مِسْكٌ»
> *"The Ashʿarīs among mankind are like a pouch containing musk!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:274).

3. **The Blessing upon Zabīd and Zamaʿ**:
When they reported their origin in Zabīd and Zamaʿ, the Prophet ﷺ thrice supplicated: *«Bārak Allāhu fī Zabīd... wa-fī Zamaʿ»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:273).

## 4. Strategic Significance

1. **Opening of Southern Arabia**: The migration of the Ashʿarīs created the earliest organized bridgehead between the Islamic state and the southern tribes of Yemen (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:273).
2. **Exemplary Altruism (al-Ashʿariyyūn)**: Their social tradition—whereby in times of scarcity they pooled all their household food into a single garment and divided it equally—was elevated by the Prophet ﷺ into a defining communal virtue (*«hum minnī wa-anā minhum»*) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 6:274).
3. **Pioneering Judicial & Scholarly Leadership**: The arrival of Abū Mūsā al-Ashʿarī provided the Caliphate with one of its foremost jurists, reciters of the Qurʾān, and military governors (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:181).
