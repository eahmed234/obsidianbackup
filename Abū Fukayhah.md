# Abū Fukayhah (أبو فكيهة)
*A Slave of Ṣafwān b. Umayyah, Tortured for Accepting Islam*

## 1. Identity
Al-Iṣābah gives his name as **Yasār**, mawlā of **Ṣafwān [b. Umayyah]**, known by his kunya Abū
Fukayhah. Ibn Isḥāq names him among those for whom Qurʾān 6:52 was revealed — *"And do not drive away
those who call upon their Lord morning and evening, seeking His Face"* — a verse revealed when Quraysh
objected to the Prophet ﷺ keeping company with such lowly converts (*al-Iṣābah*, 6:536, no. 9364).

## 2. Torture
Al-Ṣāliḥī places him among the persecuted weak believers, accepting Islam at the same time as
**[[Bilāl b. Rabāḥ]]**. **Umayyah b. Khalaf** bound a rope to his leg and had him dragged, then thrown
onto the scorching stones of Makkah's midday heat. As a beetle crawled by, Umayyah mocked, *"Is this
not also your lord?"* — to which Abū Fukayhah answered, *"Allāh is my Lord, who created me and
created you and created this beetle."* Enraged, Umayyah throttled him; his brother **Ubayy b. Khalaf**
urged, *"Increase his torment, until Muḥammad comes and frees him with his sorcery."* They dragged him
out at midday, shackled, onto the burning ground, placed a stone on his belly until his tongue lolled
out, and left him for dead — until **Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq** ﵁ passed by, purchased him, and set him free
(*Subul al-Hudā*, 2:360).
