# Abū Quḥāfah (أبو قحافة عثمان بن عامر التيمي)
*The Patriarch of Banū Taym, Father of the First Caliph, and Inheritor of the Ṣiddīq*

> [!NOTE] Primary Sourcing & Biographical Provenance
> Sourced directly from *al-Iṣābah*, 4:374–375 (entry 5458); *al-Iṣābah*, 7:274 (entry 10414); and *Subul al-Hudā*, 5:232–234.

---

## 1. Lineage & Domestic Background
Abū Quḥāfah was the patriarch of the clan of **Banū Taym b. Murrah** of Quraysh, sharing a common ancestral line with the Prophet ﷺ at Murrah b. Kaʿb (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:374).

- **Full Paternal Lineage**: ʿUthmān b. ʿĀmir b. ʿAmr b. Kaʿb b. Saʿd b. Taym b. Murrah al-Qurashī al-Taymī (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:374).
- **Maternal Lineage**: His mother was Āminah bint ʿAbd al-ʿUzzā b. Ḥarthān b. ʿAwf b. ʿUbayd b. ʿUwayj b. ʿAdī b. Kaʿb (of Banū ʿAdī of Quraysh; also reported as Qaylah) (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:374).
- **Consort & Progeny**: He married his cousin Umm al-Khayr (Salmā bint Ṣakhr b. ʿĀmir al-Taymiyyah), who bore him **[[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]]** ﵁. He was also the father of Muʿtaq, Muʿaytiq, Quraybah, and Umm Farwah (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:374).
- **Four Generations of Companionship**: His family attained a unique historical status in Islam: four continuous generations simultaneously possessed the rank of Companionship (*Ṣuḥbah*)—Abū Quḥāfah, his son Abū Bakr ﵁, his grandchildren (including Asmāʾ, ʿĀʾishah, and ʿAbd al-Raḥmān), and his great-grandchild **[[ʿAbdullāh b. al-Zubayr]]** ﵁.

---

## 2. During the Makkan Era & The Hijrah
Throughout the Makkan period, Abū Quḥāfah remained an adherent of ancestral tradition, grieving that his foremost son Abū Bakr had devoted his life, fortune, and prestige to the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:374).

When the Prophet ﷺ and Abū Bakr emigrated secretly during the **[[Hijrah]]** to Medina (1 AH), Abū Bakr took with him his remaining savings (five or six thousand dirhams) to support the nascent Islamic mission. Abū Quḥāfah—who had lost his sight (*kaffa baṣaruh*) due to advanced age—visited the house of Abū Bakr and lamented to his granddaughter Asmāʾ: *"By Allāh, I fear he has distressed you with his departure and stripped you of all his wealth!"* Asmāʾ reassured him by placing smooth stones into a wall niche where money was kept, covering them with a cloth, and having her blind grandfather touch them, which brought comfort to his heart (*Subul al-Hudā*, 3:242).

## 3. The Day of Conquest & Conversion (*Fatḥ Makkah*)
On 20 Ramaḍān 8 AH, when the Muslim army of ten thousand encamped at Dhū Ṭuwā on the outskirts of Makkah (**[[Fatḥ Makkah]]**), Abū Quḥāfah asked his youngest daughter to lead him up Mount Abū Qubays so he could observe the landscape (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:375; *Subul al-Hudā*, 5:232).

As she described a massive black mass surging through the passes, he realized it was the cavalry of Allāh under **[[Khālid b. al-Walīd]]** ﵁ and told her: *"Take me quickly back to my home!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:232).

### The Scene in the Sacred Mosque
After the Prophet ﷺ entered Makkah and dismounted at the Sacred Mosque, **[[Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq]]** ﵁ took his elderly father by the hand and led him into the presence of the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:375; *Subul al-Hudā*, 5:232).

Upon seeing the frail elder, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ displayed immense compassion and remarked:
> «هَلَّا تَرَكْتَ الشَّيْخَ فِي بَيْتِهِ حَتَّى آتِيَهُ؟»
> *"Why did you not leave the elder in his house so that I might come to him?"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:375; *Subul al-Hudā*, 5:232).

Abū Bakr replied with filial and prophetic courtesy:
> «يَمْشِي هُوَ إِلَيْكَ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ أَحَقُّ مِنْ أَنْ تَمْشِيَ إِلَيْهِ»
> *"He has far greater right to walk to you, O Messenger of Allāh, than that you should walk to him!"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:375).

The Prophet ﷺ seated Abū Quḥāfah before him, gently stroked his chest, and invited him:
> «أَسْلِمْ تَسْلَمْ»
> *"Embrace Islam, and you shall be at peace!"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:375).

Abū Quḥāfah pronounced the Shahādah and embraced Islam in the Prophet's presence (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:375; *Subul al-Hudā*, 5:232).

### The Sunnah of Dyeing Gray Hair
Abū Quḥāfah's hair and beard were completely white, resembling the blooming white *thaghāmah* flower. Observing this, the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ instructed:
> «غَيِّرُوا هَذَا بِشَيْءٍ، وَجَنِّبُوهُ السَّوَادَ»
> *"Change this grayness with something [such as henna or katam], and avoid pure black!"* (*Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim*; *al-Iṣābah*, 4:375).

The eminent Tābiʿī Qatādah recorded two singular legal distinctions for Abū Quḥāfah:
1. **Awwal Makhḍūb**: He was the first person in Islamic history whose hair was dyed in accordance with this explicit prophetic instruction (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:375).
2. **Awwal man Waritha Khalīfah**: He was the first father in Islam to survive and legally inherit from a Caliph of the Muslims (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:375).

## 4. Caliphate of Abū Bakr, Inheritance & Death
When his son Abū Bakr ﵁ was elected Caliph following the Prophet's passing (11 AH), word reached Makkah. Abū Quḥāfah asked who had been chosen; when informed it was his son, he asked: *"Did Banū ʿAbd Manāf and Banū al-Mughīrah consent?"* They replied yes. He remarked: *"None can withhold what Allāh has bestowed."*

When Abū Bakr passed away in Jumādā al-Ākhirah 13 AH, Abū Quḥāfah was still alive in Makkah. Under the Islamic law of succession, he inherited his prescribed Quranic share (one-sixth) of Abū Bakr's estate, which he promptly returned to Abū Bakr's orphaned children (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:375).

- **Passing**: Abū Quḥāfah passed away in Makkah in the year fourteen AH during the caliphate of **[[ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb]]** ﵁, at the venerable age of ninety-seven years (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:375).
