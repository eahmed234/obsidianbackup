# ʿAbdullāh Dhū al-Bijādayn (عبد الله ذو البجادين) ﵁
*al-Awwāh — The Devout Penitent of Muzaynah Whom the Prophet ﷺ Personally Interred in the Sands of Tabūk*

## 1. Lineage, Conversion & The Two Coarse Sheets (*Dhū al-Bijādayn*)
- **Ancestry**: ʿAbdullāh (originally named ʿAbd al-ʿUzzā) b. ʿAbd Nuhm b. ʿAfīf b. Suḥaym b. ʿAdī b. Thaʿlabah b. Saʿd al-Muzanī (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:139, no. 4822). Paternal uncle of the renowned hadith transmitter **ʿAbdullāh b. Mughaffal al-Muzanī** ﵁.
- **Trial of Conversion**: Raised an orphan in the wealthy care of his paternal uncle in the desert of Muzaynah, who lavished camels, horses, and wealth upon him. When ʿAbdullāh resolved to embrace Islam, his uncle threatened to strip him of all earthly possessions. ʿAbdullāh declared: *«By Allāh, I have chosen Islam and the following of Muḥammad ﷺ over all your wealth!»* (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:139; *Subul al-Hudā*, 5:459).
- **The Two Sheets (al-Bijādān)**: His furious uncle stripped him of everything, even tearing off the garments from his back, leaving him naked in the wilderness. ʿAbdullāh went to his mother, who cut a coarse hair-blanket (*bijād*) in two halves: with one half he wrapped his waist (*ittazara nifṣan*), and with the other half he draped his upper torso (*irtadā nifṣan*).
- **Naming by the Prophet ﷺ**: Arriving in Medina before dawn, he prayed Fajr behind the Prophet ﷺ. When the Prophet ﷺ inquired of his identity, he replied: *"I am ʿAbd al-ʿUzzā."* The Messenger of Allāh ﷺ countered:
  > «بَلْ أَنْتَ عَبْدُ اللَّهِ ذُو الْبِجَادَيْنِ! الْزَمْ بَابِي!»
  > *"Rather, you are ʿAbdullāh Dhū al-Bijādayn (the Possessor of the Two Coarse Sheets)! Stay close to my door!"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:139; *Subul al-Hudā*, 5:459).
- **Al-Awwāh (The Fervent Penitent)**: He attached himself to the Mosque Portico (**[[Ahl al-Ṣuffah]]**), raising his voice fervently in recitation of the Qurʾān and weeping in prayer. When ʿUmar questioned whether his voice bordered on display, the Prophet ﷺ corrected him: *«Leave him, O ʿUmar! For indeed, he is one of the fervent penitents (*al-awwāhūn*)!»* (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:139).

## 2. Ghazwat Tabūk & The Longing for Martyrdom (9 AH)
- In Rajab 9 AH, ʿAbdullāh marched in **[[Ghazwat Tabūk]]** (*Jaysh al-ʿUsrah*).
- Prior to reaching Tabūk, he approached the Prophet ﷺ and requested: *«O Messenger of Allāh, supplicate to Allāh that He grant me martyrdom!»* The Prophet ﷺ took a piece of acacia wood, tied it to ʿAbdullāh's arm, and supplicated:
  > «اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أُحَرِّمُ دَمَهُ عَلَى الْكُفَّارِ!»
  > *"O Allāh, I forbid his blood unto the disbelievers!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:459).
- ʿAbdullāh said: *"O Messenger of Allāh, that was not what I sought!"* The Prophet ﷺ replied: *«When you march in the cause of Allāh, if a fever strikes you and you die, you are a martyr; and if your mount throws you and breaks your neck, you are a martyr!»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:459).

## 3. Demise and The Blessed Trench of Tabūk
- While encamped at Tabūk, ʿAbdullāh was struck by a severe fever and passed away in the night (*Subul al-Hudā*, 5:459).
- **The Night Torch**: **[[ʿAbdullāh b. Masʿūd]]** ﵁ narrated:
  > *"I awoke in the pitch darkness of the night at Tabūk and saw a burning torch carried toward the edge of the camp. I followed it and found the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ, Abū Bakr, and ʿUmar. They had dug a grave, and the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ had descended personally into the bottom of the trench! Abū Bakr and ʿUmar lowered ʿAbdullāh's body into the grave, while the Prophet ﷺ said to them:*
  > «أَدْنِيَا إِلَيَّ أَخَاكُمَا!»
  > *'Draw your brother down close to me!'*
  > *He received him into his blessed arms, laid him gently in the niche, and then raised his hands toward heaven and wept, supplicating:*
  > «اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَمْسَيْتُ عَنْهُ رَاضِيًا فَارْضَ عَنْهُ!»
  > *'O Allāh, indeed I have entered upon this evening fully pleased with him, so be You pleased with him!'*
  > *By Allāh, seeing that, I wished with all my soul that I had been the dweller of that trench!"* (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:139; *Subul al-Hudā*, 5:459).
- Authorities note that the Prophet ﷺ personally descended into the grave of only five people throughout his life, among them ʿAbdullāh Dhū al-Bijādayn (*al-Iṣābah*, 4:140).
