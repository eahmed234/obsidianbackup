# Nuʿaym b. Masʿūd (نعيم بن مسعود)
*The Master of Strategic Dissension — Who Broke the Grand Confederacy at the Trench*

## 1. Identity & Secret Conversion
A prominent chieftain of the Ashjaʿ clan of **Ghaṭafān**. He marched to Medina among the confederate forces (*al-Aḥzāb*) during **[[Ghazwat al-Khandaq]]** (5 AH) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:384).

In the midst of the freezing siege, Allāh cast faith into his heart. Under the cover of night, Nuʿaym stole away from the confederate lines, slipped into the Muslim camp, and presented himself before the Prophet ﷺ, professing:
> *"O Messenger of Allāh, I have embraced Islam, and my people know nothing of my conversion. Command me with whatever you wish!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:384).

The Prophet ﷺ replied with the famous military maxim:
> «إِنَّمَا أَنْتَ فِينَا رَجُلٌ وَاحِدٌ، فَخَذِّلْ عَنَّا إِنِ اسْتَطَعْتَ، فَإِنَّ الْحَرْبَ خُدْعَةٌ!»
> *"You are but a single man among us. Dishearten and sow discord among the enemy on our behalf if you are able, for warfare is deception!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:384).

## 2. The Dissension Sown at the Trench
Endowed with tribal credibility across all confederate camps, Nuʿaym executed a three-stage psychological maneuver (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:384–386):
1. **With Banū Qurayẓah**: He visited the Jewish fortress of Banū Qurayẓah (allies from pre-Islamic times) and warned them: *«Quraysh and Ghaṭafān are not like you; this land is your homeland where your wealth, children, and women reside. If they see an opportunity they will seize it, but if warfare turns against them, they will retreat to their distant lands, leaving you exposed to Muḥammad. Do not fight alongside them until you take hostages from their noblemen as a guarantee!»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:384–385). Qurayẓah embraced this counsel.
2. **With Quraysh**: He visited **[[Abū Sufyān b. Ḥarb]]** and the Meccan commanders, whispering: *«You know my affection for you and detachment from Muḥammad. Intelligence has reached me that the Jews of Qurayẓah have regretted breaking their pact with Muḥammad. They have secretly offered to seize seventy noblemen from Quraysh and Ghaṭafān and hand them over to Muḥammad to be beheaded, as proof of their renewed alliance. If they demand hostages of your men, do not surrender a single soul!»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:385).
3. **With Ghaṭafān**: He went to his own kinsmen of Ghaṭafān and repeated the exact warning (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:385).

## 3. The Collapse of Trust
When Abū Sufyān dispatched ʿIkrimah b. Abī Jahl on a Saturday eve urging Banū Qurayẓah to join a coordinated general assault on the morrow, the Jews replied that they could not violate the Sabbath and refused to fight unless given hostages (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:385–386).

Quraysh and Ghaṭafān exclaimed: *«By Allāh, Nuʿaym b. Masʿūd spoke the truth to you!»* Mutual distrust paralyzed the confederacy (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:386). Deprived of cohesive coordination and immediately battered by a freezing divine hurricane, the confederate siege shattered into chaotic retreat.
