# Nuʿaym b. Masʿūd (نعيم بن مسعود)
*The Master of Strategic Dissension — Who Broke the Grand Confederacy at the Trench*

## 1. Lineage & Secret Conversion
Nuʿaym b. Masʿūd b. ʿĀmir b. Unayf b. Thaʿlabah b. Qunfudh b. Khalāwah b. Subayʿ b. Bakr b. Ashjaʿ al-Ashjaʿī, agnomen Abū Salamah, was a prominent chieftain of the Ashjaʿ clan of **Ghaṭafān** (*al-Iṣābah*, 6:363; *Subul al-Hudā*, 4:384). His children included Salamah and Zaynab, both of whom transmitted hadiths from him (*al-Iṣābah*, 6:363).

He originally marched to Medina among the confederate host (*al-Aḥzāb*) during **[[Ghazwat al-Khandaq]]** (5 AH) (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:384). In the midst of the freezing siege, Allāh opened his heart to Islam. Stealing away from the confederate lines under cover of darkness, Nuʿaym entered the Muslim camp, presented himself before the Prophet ﷺ, and declared:
> *"O Messenger of Allāh, I have embraced Islam, and my people know nothing of my conversion. Command me with whatever you wish!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:384; *al-Iṣābah*, 6:363).

The Prophet ﷺ replied with the timeless military maxim:
> «إِنَّمَا أَنْتَ فِينَا رَجُلٌ وَاحِدٌ، فَخَذِّلْ عَنَّا إِنِ اسْتَطَعْتَ، فَإِنَّ الْحَرْبَ خُدْعَةٌ!»
> *"You are but a single man among us. Dishearten and sow discord among them on our behalf if you are able, for warfare is deception!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:384; *al-Iṣābah*, 6:363).

## 2. Strategic Dissension at the Trench (5 AH)
Possessing trusted relationships across all confederate factions, Nuʿaym executed a brilliant three-tier counter-intelligence operation (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:384–386; *al-Iṣābah*, 6:363):
1. **With Banū Qurayẓah**: He visited the Jewish fortresses (his former allies) and cautioned them: *«Quraysh and Ghaṭafān are not like you; if the battle turns against them, they will return to their homelands, leaving you defenseless before Muḥammad. Do not fight alongside them until you demand noble hostages from them as a guarantee!»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:384–385).
2. **With Quraysh**: He met **[[Abū Sufyān b. Ḥarb]]** and the Meccan commanders, confiding: *«The Jews of Qurayẓah have regretted breaking their pact with Muḥammad. They have promised him to seize seventy noblemen from Quraysh and Ghaṭafān to be beheaded in exchange for peace. If they ask you for hostages, do not deliver a single man!»* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:385).
3. **With Ghaṭafān**: He went to his own kinsmen of Ghaṭafān and delivered the exact same warning (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:385).

## 3. The Shattering of the Confederacy
When Abū Sufyān sent ʿIkrimah b. Abī Jahl demanding that Banū Qurayẓah launch a coordinated assault on Sunday, the Jews responded that they could not violate the Sabbath and refused to mobilize without hostages (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:385–386). Quraysh and Ghaṭafān exclaimed: *"By Allāh, Nuʿaym b. Masʿūd spoke the truth to you!"* (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:386). Mutual trust disintegrated overnight, and an icy gale destroyed the confederate camp, precipitating their retreat (*Subul al-Hudā*, 4:386; *al-Iṣābah*, 6:363).

## 4. Demise & Legacy
Nuʿaym narrated several traditions from the Prophet ﷺ, including the report regarding Musaylimah's emissaries: *«Were it not that emissaries are not killed, I would have struck off your necks»* (*al-Iṣābah*, 6:363). Nuʿaym lived in Medina and was killed at the beginning of the caliphate of ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib prior to the Battle of the Camel (or passed away during the caliphate of ʿUthmān) (*al-Iṣābah*, 6:363).
