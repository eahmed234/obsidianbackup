# Mawlid al-Nabī ﷺ (مولد النبي ﷺ)
*The Birth of the Noble Messenger of Allāh ﷺ*

## 1. Overview
The birth of the Prophet [[Muḥammad b. ʻAbdullah|Muḥammad]] ﷺ occurred in the Year of the Elephant (*ʿĀm al-Fīl*), corresponding to approximately 570 or 571 CE in [[Makkah al-Mukarramah]]. He was born an orphan; his father ʿAbdullāh had passed away in Yathrib during a trading expedition prior to his birth. His mother, [[Āminah bint Wahb]], experienced a serene pregnancy marked by celestial portents, and he was delivered with the assistance of [[al-Shifāʾ bint ʿAwf]] (the mother of [[ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. ʿAwf]]).

## 2. Key Portents & Early Nursing
- The illumination of the horizons: A radiant light shone from his mother that illuminated the palaces of Bostra in Syria (*Subul al-Hudā*, 1:340; 2:6).
- The nursing by Ḥalīmah al-Saʿdiyyah among the tribe of Banū Saʿd b. Bakr.
- The Opening of the Breast (*Shaqq al-Ṣadr*) during his early childhood in the desert.

## 3. Related Figures & Events
- **Mother**: [[Āminah bint Wahb]]
- **Grandfather**: ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib (who named him Muḥammad and brought him into the Kaʿbah)
- **Subsequent Event**: Passing of his mother at al-Abwāʾ (at age 6) and of ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib (at age 8), placing him in the guardianship of his uncle [[Abū Ṭālib]].

## 4. Expansion Blueprint (*Subul al-Hudā*)
- Detailed genealogy tracing back to ʿAdnān and Ismāʿīl ﵊ (*Vol. 1*).
- Miracles accompanying the birth: the quaking of the palace of Khosrow and quenching of the sacred fire of Persia (*Vol. 2*).
- Classical Sunni legal discussions regarding the commemoration and sending blessings upon the Prophet ﷺ.
