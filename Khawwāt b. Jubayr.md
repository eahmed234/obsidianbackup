# Khawwāt b. Jubayr (خوات بن جبير الأنصاري)
*Abū ʿAbdillāh al-Awsī — Badri Veteran, Steadfast Knight, and Knight of the Desert Journeys*

## 1. Lineage and Clan Heritage
His genealogy is recorded by Ibn Ḥajar as:
**Khawwāt b. Jubayr b. al-Nuʿmān b. Umayyah b. Imriʾ al-Qays b. Thaʿlabah b. ʿAmr b. ʿAwf b. Mālik b. al-Aws al-Anṣārī al-Awsī**, bearing the agnomens **Abū ʿAbdillāh** and **Abū Ṣāliḥ** (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:291, no. 2303).

He belonged to the prominent Awsī branch of Banū ʿAmr b. ʿAwf (the clan of Qubāʾ). He marched out with the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ toward **[[Badr]]**, but upon reaching **[[Wādī al-Ṣafrāʾ]]**, a flying stone fractured his leg (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:291). The Prophet ﷺ ordered him returned to Medina, yet awarded him a full warrior's share of the spoils and spiritual reward, numbering him canonically among the veterans of Badr (*Ahl Badr*) (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:291).

## 2. Participation in the Campaigns
Following Badr, Khawwāt participated in the Battle of **[[Ghazwat Uḥud]]**, the siege of **[[Ghazwat al-Khandaq]]**, the expedition of **[[Ghazwat Dhāt al-Riqāʿ]]**, the conquest of **[[Khaybar]]**, and the subsequent major campaigns (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:291–292).

During the expedition of **[[Ghazwat Dhāt al-Riqāʿ]]**, his son Ṣāliḥ b. Khawwāt narrated from him the authoritative practical demonstration of the Prayer of Fear (*Ṣalāt al-Khawf*), which became foundational across classical jurisprudence (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:292).

## 3. The Anecdote of the Errant Camel at Marr al-Ẓahrān
A famous lighthearted narrative recorded in Hadith collections describes an encounter during an expedition halt at Marr al-Ẓahrān (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:292). Khawwāt emerged from his tent, saw a group of women conversing, put on his finest mantle, and sat near them. The Prophet ﷺ emerged from his dome-tent, prompting Khawwāt to feel awe and embarrassment. When the Prophet ﷺ asked what he was doing, Khawwāt blurted out an excuse: *«O Messenger of Allāh, a camel of mine has strayed, and I am searching for a hobble for it!»* (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:292).

Later, during journeys, the Prophet ﷺ would playfully tease him: *«O Abū ʿAbdillāh, what became of your straying camel?»* until Khawwāt finally confessed his faith and repentance: *«By Him Who sent you with the truth, that camel has never strayed since I embraced Islam!»* whereupon the Prophet ﷺ prayed for him (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:292).

## 4. Pilgrimage with ʿUmar and Later Life
During the caliphate of **[[ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb]]** ﵁, Khawwāt accompanied ʿUmar on the pilgrimage alongside **[[Abū ʿUbaydah b. al-Jarrāḥ]]** ﵁ and **[[ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. ʿAwf]]** ﵁ (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:292). As they rode through the night, the company urged Khawwāt to chant poetry to ease the journey. ʿUmar encouraged him: *«Let Abū ʿAbdillāh sing from the inspirations of his heart!»* Khawwāt sang until the approach of dawn, when ʿUmar called out: *«Withhold your tongue, O Khawwāt, for dawn has arrived!»* (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:292).

Khawwāt lived to a mature age in Medina, passing away in the year forty AH during the late caliphate (*al-Iṣābah*, 2:293).
